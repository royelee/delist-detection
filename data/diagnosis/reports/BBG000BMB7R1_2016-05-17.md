## 5. What happened

- 2016-05-05: ITT announced a holding company reorganization under Indiana Code 23-1-40-9 ([SEC 8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/216228/000119312516591890/d181502d8k12b.htm)).
- 2016-05-16: ITT merged into ITT LLC; each ITT share was converted on a share for share basis into New ITT common stock with the same rights; New ITT became successor issuer under Rule 12g-3 ([SEC 8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/216228/000119312516591890/d181502d8k12b.htm)).
- New ITT stock is listed on the NYSE under ITT, the same symbol, with new CUSIP 45073V108 (same 8-K12B).
- Fails rows (SEC): old CUSIP 450911201 last row 2016-05-17; new CUSIP 45073V108 from 2016-05-18.
