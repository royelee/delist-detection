# BBG000C7WZ16_2010-10-15 (JNY, Jones Apparel Group / The Jones Group)

## 1. Verdict

The 2010-10-15 ending is not an ending. The company renamed itself The Jones Group Inc. on 2010-10-18, its CUSIP changed (480074103 to 48020T101) and the ticker JNY stayed; the same shares kept trading on the NYSE until the Sycamore Partners cash merger of 2014-04-09 ($15.00 per share). The library's row is wrong: a spurious exchange transfer, and the real 2014 merger is missing.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2014-04-09 item 2.01 reports the merger closing; 8-K 2010-10-18 item 5.03 shows the 2010 event is a rename).
- Successor: filing (merger consideration is cash; Merger Sub merged into the company, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: suspended from trading on 2014-04-09, merger effective before the open, so the last trade is 2014-04-08); fails rows under 48020T101 run to 2014-04-07.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2014-04-09: converted into the right to receive $15.00 in cash).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K and Form 25 notice: $15.00 per share, USD, no stock).

## 5. What happened

- 2010-10-18: name changed to The Jones Group Inc.; CUSIP changed to 48020T 101; the symbol JNY remained ([SEC 8-K 2010-10-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874016/000087401610000050/october1810.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 480074103 under JNY ends 2010-10-15; CUSIP 48020T101 under JNY "JONES GROUP INC" runs 2010-10-19 to 2014-04-07 (SEC fails-to-deliver data, via sec.py fails).
- 2014-04-09: merger with Jasper Merger Sub (Sycamore Partners) closed; each share became $15.00 cash ([SEC 8-K 2014-04-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874016/000119312514137267/d710716d8k.htm)).
- 2014-04-09: NYSE Form 25-NSE, merger effective before the opening on 2014-04-09, security suspended 2014-04-09 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874016/000087666114000166/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- 2014-04-21: Form 15-12B filed ([SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874016/000119312514150740/d715763d1512b.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security (2010: rename, same holders, same shares, new CUSIP); then replaced by cash (2014 merger).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The run's CUSIP history for the security stops at 2010-10-15 (CUSIP 480074103 only), so the fails rows under the new CUSIP 48020T101 were never attached, the ticker JNY appeared to stop, and with no Form 25 the continued-filings fallback (`continued_filings_rule`, crsp 304, `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`) invented an exchange transfer at the last sighting. Flags: `last_trade_date_unconfirmed;no_form25;successor_unknown`. Cause tag: `rename_not_followed` (a CUSIP-changing rename with an unchanged ticker not followed).
