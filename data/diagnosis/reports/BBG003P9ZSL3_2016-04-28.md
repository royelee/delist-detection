## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the three fields the report calls wrong, re-reading the 8-K (0001560385-16-000077), the 25-NSE (0001354457-16-000357) and the run's ticker_history.

- **successor: upheld.** The 8-K items 2.03 and 8.01 state each share was reclassified into and exchanged for three new tracking stocks, the basket being "one share of Series A Liberty SiriusXM, 0.1 of a share of Series A Liberty Braves and 0.25 of a share of Series A Liberty Media". The library names only new LMCA (BBG00BFHDCV6, the 0.25 leg); LSXMA (BBG00BFHD827, the 1.0 leg) is in the run's ticker_history from 2016-04-18, so a single-successor link is incomplete. [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000156038516000077/lmca-20160415x8k.htm)
- **value_rule: upheld, as a judgment.** The filing facts (no cash, one share into three different series) are confirmed. Decision 9 requires "one for one" into the same claim, so a one-to-three exchange is not a continuation and a stock basket is the matching rule. No filing settles whether the operator wants that policy for one-to-many reclassifications; the report already says so.
- **last_trade_date: upheld on the date, with a caveat on "wrong".** The 8-K says the Restated Charter was filed on April 15, 2016 "after the market close" and that the new series began trading on Nasdaq on April 18, 2016 (Monday). So the old line's last session was Friday 2016-04-15, and the library's internal 2016-04-18 (last_sighting) is a sighting, not a trade. The 25-NSE (filed and effective 2016-04-18) gives no suspension date in the text read. The date is inferred from the 8-K, not from an exchange print, so under decision 12 the contract's blank is defensible; the library is wrong in its internal date and the 04-18 end of the old ticker range. 2016-04-15 is right as the true date, not yet as a publishable one.

Not checked: the Braves ticker (the 8-K gives BATRA for Series A Liberty Braves, which supports the report's open check) and any exchange print.
