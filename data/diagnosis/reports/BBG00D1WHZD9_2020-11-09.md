## 5. What happened

- Hertz filed Chapter 11 on 2020-05-22; the NYSE began delisting proceedings on 2020-05-26 and the Company appealed ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2020-10-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000087666120001005/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The appeal committee upheld the delisting on 2020-10-29 and the NYSE suspended trading after the close that day (same notice).
- The shares began trading exclusively OTC on 2020-10-30 as HTZGQ ([SEC 8-K 2020-10-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000165785320000104/htz-20201029.htm)).
- The Form 25 removes the stock from listing at the opening of business on 2020-11-10 (Rule 12d2-2(b)).
- Fails rows show CUSIP 42806J106 under HTZGQ in 2020-11 (18 rows, 2020-11-03..2020-11-30).
- A plan-related 8-K of 2021-06-16 (item 1.03) and 2021-07-07 (items 1.01, 1.02, 3.03, 5.01) follow later; post-bankruptcy equity is a new security under the spec.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: terms

The 8-K filed 2020-10-30 (0001657853-20-000104) says the common stock traded OTC from 2020-10-30 under HTZGQ, and that the NYSE symbol was HTZ. The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-20-001005) confirms trading was suspended after the close on 2020-10-29. The reference rule for otc_print uses the security's own OTC symbol and the session after the last trade, so price_ticker HTZGQ with price_date 2020-10-30 is right. Fails rows for CUSIP 42806J106 exist under HTZGQ from 2020-11-03. One minor error in report section 5: it says 18 fails rows, but the cache shows 36 rows through 2020-12-31. No field depends on that count.
