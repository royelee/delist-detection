# CIK733269-COMMON_2018-09-30 (ACXM, Acxiom -> LiveRamp)

## 1. Verdict

Not an exit. Acxiom Corp's holders got Acxiom Holdings shares one for one on 2018-09-20 (a holding-company merger, same CUSIP, still Nasdaq "ACXM"), and the same common stock then moved from Nasdaq to the NYSE as "RAMP" on 2018-10-02 after the AMS sale to IPG. The library's `exchange` ending is the right kind, but it dates it 2018-09-30 from the predecessor's Form 25-NSE of 2018-09-20 and names no successor; the real exchange transfer is the Nasdaq Form 25 effective at the close on 2018-10-01.

## 5. What happened

- 2018-09-20: stockholders approved a holding-company merger; Acxiom Corp (Nasdaq) became the Predecessor, Acxiom Holdings the Successor with the same ticker ACXM and the same CUSIP ([SEC 8-K12B 2018-09-21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/733269/000119312518279280/d616065d8k12b.htm)).
- The predecessor's Form 25-NSE of 2018-09-20 removed the old registrant, not the stock ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/733269/000135445718000361/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The AMS sale to IPG closed; the Successor was renamed LiveRamp Holdings and its stock traded on the NYSE as RAMP from 2018-10-02; the Form 25 for Nasdaq took effect at the close on 2018-10-01 ([SEC 8-K12B 2018-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/733269/000119312518289748/d612393d8k12b.htm), [SEC Form 25 2018-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/733269/000119312518289670/d612497d25.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 005125109 as ACXIOM CORP to 2018-09-21, as ACXIOM HOLDINGS to 2018-10-03; CUSIP 53815P108 as LIVERAMP from 2018-10-02 (SEC fails data).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: a 1:1 holdco reorganization (continuation) followed by an exchange transfer and rename; no cash was paid for the shares.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It anchored the ending on the 2018-09-20 Form 25-NSE (`last_trade_after_form25_effective:2018-09-30`, `resolved_from_continued_filings`) instead of the later Nasdaq-removal Form 25 of 2018-10-01, and never linked the RAMP line as the continuing security (`successor_unknown`); the 8-K12B was read, but its successor is the same CIK and was not added. Cause tag: `holdco_not_linked`.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `last_trade_date` (the only field the report calls wrong). Result: **upheld**.

- The library's date is not an exchange print of a real ending. Its contract `last_trade_date` is blank and the internal date is 2018-09-30 (a Sunday), from `midas` and anchored on the predecessor's Form 25-NSE (0001354457-18-000361, filed and effective 2018-09-20, Nasdaq, Common Stock, Acxiom Corp). That filing removed the old registrant at the holdco merger. The stock kept trading as ACXM, so it did not end the security.
- The Nasdaq removal of the continuing stock is Form 25 0001193125-18-289670 (filed 2018-10-01, Acxiom Holdings, Inc., Common Stock par $0.10, Rule 12d2-2(c)). The 8-K12B of 2018-10-01 (0001193125-18-289748, item 3.01) says the Form 25 is "effective upon closing of trading on October 1, 2018" and the stock "will trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol RAMP". So the last Nasdaq session is 2018-10-01, with NYSE trading from 2018-10-02.
- Fails data agrees (offline `sec.py fails`): CUSIP 005125109 under ACXM (as ACXIOM HOLDINGS) to 2018-10-03; CUSIP 53815P108 under RAMP from 2018-10-02. The first RAMP row carries $48.48, the library's own `library_last_trade_close`, so that close is the 2018-10-01 close.
- The continuation and successor claims also hold. The 8-K12B of 2018-09-21 (0001193125-18-279280) says the Successor's CUSIP "will remain the same" and the stock stays on Nasdaq as ACXM. The CUSIP changes only at the October rename to 53815P108. No cash is paid in either step, so decision 9 gives a continuation and the transfer rule applies.

Caveat, which does not change the verdict: 2018-10-01 is the last Nasdaq day. Decision 12 allows publishing it only from an exchange print (MIDAS or the 8-K 3.01 text) no later than the Form 25 effective date. It meets the date test, and the library would need such a print to publish it.
