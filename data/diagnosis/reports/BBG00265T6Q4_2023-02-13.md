- Successor: filing (same 8-K: STORE became a subsidiary of the Parent; no continuing public line).

## 5. What happened

- Each share was cancelled and converted into $32.25 cash, without interest ([SEC 8-K 2023-02-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1538990/000119312523024224/d404467d8k.htm)).
- The NYSE notified removal from listing at the open on 2023-02-14 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3); the merger was effective before the open on 2023-02-03 and the security was suspended on 2023-02-03; each share converted into USD 32.25 cash ([SEC 25-NSE 2023-02-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1538990/000087666123000088/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- S-8 POS and POSASR deregistrations filed the same day (SEC filings list, 2023-02-03).
