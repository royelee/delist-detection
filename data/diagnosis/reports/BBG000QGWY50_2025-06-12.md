## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (the only field the report calls wrong). **Upheld.**

- The library publishes 3.00 with no currency. The 8-K of 2025-05-14 ([SEC 8-K 2025-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1293971/000119312525119447/d943903d8k.htm)) says holders may elect (x) $3.00 cash (the "Closing Amount") plus one CVR paying $6.84 in cash on a milestone, or (y) $5.00 per Share in cash. The 8-K of 2025-06-02 ([SEC 8-K 2025-06-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1293971/000119312525132850/d39702d8k.htm)) confirms the final Offer: "between either (but not both)" the Cash and CVR Consideration ($3.00 + CVR) or the "All-Cash Consideration" ($5.00 cash). Both are cash, the security is common stock, and the currency is USD (dollar amounts).
- So the library's 3.00 is only the Closing Amount of one alternative; it drops the $5.00 alternative and the CVR. This is a missing-leg misread, which the reference calls `wrong` even when the rule (cash) agrees.
- Caveat, not a refutation: the filings fix no default package. Publishing 5.00 is a judgement (the certain cash leg), as the report already says (confidence `inferred`). The election tally and the CVR value stay open. A fix that publishes 3.00 + CVR instead would also be defensible; what is wrong is the bare 3.00 without the alternative.
