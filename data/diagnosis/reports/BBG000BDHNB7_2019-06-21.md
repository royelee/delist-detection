## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2019-06-11, items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01: merger completed).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Bemis survives as a wholly owned subsidiary; holders receive Amcor shares, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: suspended from trading 2019-06-11, so last trade 2019-06-10).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K and Form 25-NSE: conversion into Amcor shares).
- Terms: filing (8-K and Form 25-NSE: 5.1 shares per share). AMCR first row in fails data is 2019-06-11.

## 5. What happened

- Merger effective before the open on 2019-06-11; each Bemis common share converted into 5.1 Amcor plc ordinary shares ([SEC Form 25-NSE 0000876661-19-000569](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/11199/000087666119000569/)).
- The 8-K confirms the 5.1 ratio, cash in lieu of fractional shares only, Bemis surviving as a subsidiary of New Amcor ([SEC 8-K 2019-06-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/11199/000110465919034809/a19-11365_18k.htm)).
- The security was suspended from trading on 2019-06-11 (same Form 25-NSE).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old share was exchanged for another company's shares, a merger and not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It did not, except for the fill. The terms gate failed (`terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`, `ftd_close_lagged`): the acquirer's first fails close (about 2019-06-11) was lagged or did not reconcile with the last close, so the library fell back to assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). The 5.1 ratio and AMCR ticker match the filings. Cause tag: `library_right_but_uncertain`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish the stock rule without assumed par; qlib_practice prices 5.1 x AMCR on 2019-06-11 against the last close.
- The sanity gate should not fail a stock deal whose acquirer price only starts on the completion date; no other fact is open.
- Golden case: yes, a well-sourced all-stock merger with a first-day acquirer price.
