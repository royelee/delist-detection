# LMCA (CIK1507934-CLASS-A), 2013-01-16

## 1. Verdict

Not a delisting. On 2013-01-11 Liberty Media Corporation (CIK 1507934) spun off its Sirius/Braves/Live Nation business as a new company and renamed itself Starz; the new company took the ticker LMCA. The old Liberty Capital Series A line kept trading on Nasdaq as Starz (STRZA) under the same issuer. The library's "exchange transfer, no successor, last trade unknown" row is a guess made from the end of fails rows under old CUSIP 530322106.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2013-01-17 Item 1.01/5.03: spin-off, name change only, no exchange removal).
- Successor: fails rows (CUSIP 85571Q102 STRZA starts 2013-01-17; 530322106 stops 2013-01-16) plus the 8-K press release (Starz STRZA, STRZB trade regular way 2013-01-14). No filing read states how the old CUSIP maps to STRZA.
- Last trade date: worked out from the spin-off effective time (5pm 2013-01-11) and the regular-way date; no print or filing states it.
- Payout rule: worked out (no consideration is paid; a continuing issuer).
- Terms: filing (the 8-K: one Spinco share per share held; no cash).

Not backed: the mapping of old Liberty Capital shares to STRZA, and the exact last day of the old symbol. A Nasdaq notice or Starz proxy/10-Q describing the reclassification, or a MIDAS day for LMCA, would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Spin-off completed 2013-01-11 5pm: one Spinco Series A share per Series A Liberty Capital share; Spinco renamed Liberty Media Corporation, the old registrant renamed Starz ([SEC 8-K 2013-01-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1507934/000110465913002999/a13-2797_18k.htm)).
- Item 5.03: the name change from Liberty Media Corporation to Starz "will have no effect on the rights of stockholders" (same 8-K).
- Both companies trade regular way on Nasdaq from 2013-01-14 (STRZA/STRZB; LMCA/LMCB for the new one) (EX-99.1 of the same 8-K).
- Starz stock "did not begin trading until January 14, 2013" ([SEC 10-K FY2012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1507934/000150793413000015/starz_10-kx12312012.htm)).
- No Form 25, 15 or 8-K 3.01 for CIK 1507934 in 2013-01..02 (filings listing).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 530322106 LMCA ends 2013-01-16; STRZA 85571Q102 starts 2013-01-17; LMCA 531229102 (Liberty Media Delaware) starts 2013-01-23.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: the same issuer continued, renamed, with a CUSIP change and a distribution of a new company's shares. At most an ending-with-successor under decision 9; not a merger or a loss.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

No Form 25 existed, so the continued-filings rule (`continued_filings_rule`, flags `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `successor_unknown`) labelled the end of fails rows under the old CUSIP an exchange transfer. It did not see the rename to Starz or that ticker LMCA passed to a different issuer. Cause tag: `successor_not_in_run`.

## 9. Verification

Checked value_rule and ticker_history against the filings (8-K accession 0001104659-13-002999, fails rows).

- **value_rule (report: transfer is wrong, should be continuation): upheld.**
  - The 8-K (filed 2013-01-17, Item 5.03 and EX-99.1) says Liberty Media Corporation (CIK 1507934) renamed itself Starz on 2013-01-11, the certificate "will have no effect on the rights of stockholders", and the new Liberty came from Spinco, a different registrant. The Spin-Off is a dividend of one Spinco share per Series A Liberty Capital share, so holders kept their old share and received a distribution. Nothing was surrendered for cash or other consideration, so it is not a merger and not a terminal event.
  - Fails rows: CUSIP 530322106 LMCA ends 2013-01-16 at about $124; CUSIP 85571Q102 is described "STARZ LIBERTY CAP COM SER A", symbol STRZA, and starts 2013-01-17 at about $15.56. The price drop equals the value spun off, which fits the old line continuing ex-distribution under the new name.
  - The library's "transfer" row (exchange_transfer_zero) has a return of 0 either way, but its value_rule and missing successor are wrong under decision 9: the old line continues as STRZA, not as an exit.
  - Caveat: the CUSIP changes (530322106 to 85571Q102) and no filing read states the old-share to STRZA mapping. The mapping rests on the 8-K rename, the fails description and the price drop, so it stays inferred, as section 4 says.
- **ticker_history (report: LMCA should end about 2013-01-11): upheld, with a correction to the report's reasoning.**
  - The library's LMCA range ends 2013-01-16, the last fails row (settlement lag), not the last day the old line traded as LMCA. The spin-off completed 2013-01-11 at 5pm. Both companies trade regular way from Monday 2013-01-14, the old line as STRZA, so the old LMCA range should end about 2013-01-11 (EX-99.1).
  - Correction: section 2's claim that LMCA belongs to CIK 1560385 from 2013-01-14 is not supported. EX-99.1 shows the new Liberty on LMCAD/LMCBD, and fails rows show CUSIP 531229102 as LMCAD 2013-01-17..22 and as LMCA only from 2013-01-23. The new line took the LMCA symbol on 2013-01-23, not 2013-01-14. The old range still ends too late, so the verdict stands.
- Overall: both fields upheld. The last trade day (2013-01-11) is worked out, not printed, as the report says.
