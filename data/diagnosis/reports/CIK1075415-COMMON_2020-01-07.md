## 5. What happened

- Effective 12:02 a.m. ET on 2020-01-01 the trust changed its name from Senior Housing Properties Trust to Diversified Healthcare Trust; new CUSIP for the common shares 25525P 107; trading as DHC on Nasdaq from the open on 2020-01-02 ([SEC 8-K 2020-01-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1075415/000110465920000436/tm1926716d1_8k.htm)).
- The same 8-K (Items 1.01, 1.02) covers the Five Star transaction agreement; it is not an exchange of the shares.
- Fails rows: CUSIP 81721M109 under SNH 2019-11-01..2020-01-07 (rows dated after the rename are settlement lag); CUSIP 25525P107 under DHC from 2020-01-03 (fails data, `sec.py fails`).
- No Form 25 or Form 15 in the issuer's filings 2019-12-01..2020-03-01 (`sec.py filings`).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: a rename with a CUSIP change, same issuer and holders, one for one.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It found no Form 25, fell back to the continued-filings rule (304, `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`) and treated the end of the SNH CUSIP's fails rows (2020-01-07) as an ending. It did not follow the CUSIP switch 81721M109 to 25525P107 of the same CIK (flags `member_name_mismatch`, `successor_unknown`). Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.
