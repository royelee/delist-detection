## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (report says wrong on `price_date`: library 2026-08-17, should be 2026-08-18). **Refuted.**

- The report's premise is true only of the ticker. Vivmark's own 8-K (CIK 906107, 0001140361-26-033377, Items 2.01/3.03/5.03) says the company shares "will continue to trade on the NYSE and beginning on August 18, 2026 ... under the ticker symbol VMRK", and the closing press release (exhibit to the AVB 8-K 0001104659-26-097833) says the same. The shares themselves are the same security: fails rows for CUSIP 29476L107 run under EQR to 2026-08-18 (priced at the 2026-08-17 close) and under VMRK from 2026-08-19 (priced at the 2026-08-18 close). So the acquirer's shares traded on 2026-08-17, under EQR, on the closing date, after the merger closed.
- The rule (reference.md, "The payout rule"): `price_date` is "the trading day after the last trade (the acquirer's first price after the deal closes)". The last AVB trade is 2026-08-14 (Friday); the next trading day is 2026-08-17, the closing date, when the acquirer's shares (same CUSIP, same `price_sec_id` BBG000BG8M31) already had a post-closing price. The library's 2026-08-17 therefore matches the rule. The report's own corrected block keeps `price_sec_id = BBG000BG8M31`, which prices by line, not by the ticker string, so a VMRK-not-yet-existing objection has no effect.
- The `acquirer_close_lagged` flag is about the library's fails-row lookup lagging (a ticker search on VMRK for the first day), not about an incorrect `price_date`.
- Uncertainty: if the owner of the rule wants the first session under the new ticker, 2026-08-18 would be defensible, but that is not what the rule says. Whether 2026-08-17 or 2026-08-18 is better is a convention call; nothing here shows the library wrong.
- Citation fix: the report attributes "VMRK trades from 2026-08-18" to the AVB 8-K 2026-08-17; it is in Vivmark's 8-K (906107, 0001140361-26-033377) and the press release exhibit to the AVB 8-K.

Other terms upheld: ratio 2.793 Vivmark (formerly Equity Residential) common shares plus cash in lieu of fractions (AVB 8-K 0001104659-26-097833, Item 2.01); no cash leg; USD for fractions. Fields the report calls agree (exit_kind merger, last_trade_date 2026-08-14 consistent with AVB fails rows ending 2026-08-17 at the 184.06 close, issuer 915912, Form 25-NSE signed 2026-08-17) were not contested.

Net: the library's row is right on every field, including `price_date`; the case is `library_right_but_uncertain` (issuer from today's ticker map, CIK 915912 confirmed by the filings), not `terms_misread`. The "golden case" suggestion should not assert 2026-08-18 as the price date.
