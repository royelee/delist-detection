## 5. What happened

- Tender offer launched 2015-05-26 at $5.05 per share in cash; Acquisition Sub accepted all validly tendered shares on 2015-08-14 ([SEC 8-K 2015-08-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/931148/000119312515290818/d32320d8k.htm)).
- The merger became effective 2015-08-17 and each remaining share was converted into $5.05 cash; NYSE suspended trading on 2015-08-17 and removed the class on 2015-08-28 ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2015-08-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/931148/000087666115000362/)).
- Change-in-control 8-K with items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01 filed 2015-08-18 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/931148/000119312515294734/d166379d8k.htm)).
