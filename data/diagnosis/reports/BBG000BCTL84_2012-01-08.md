# PMI (The PMI Group Inc, CIK 935724), case BBG000BCTL84_2012-01-08

## 1. Verdict

A real bankruptcy drop: NYSE halted PMI on 2011-10-21 (the last day it traded on the NYSE), the company filed Chapter 11 on 2011-11-23, NYSE suspended and delisted it, and it then traded OTC as PPMIQ. The library's kind, rule and drop reason are right; its last trade date (2011-11-23, the bankruptcy filing day) is wrong by a month, and its OTC price date follows from that wrong date.

## 4. Why this confidence

Confidence is inferred, not verified: three of the five key fields rest on something other than a filing, so the label stands.

- exit kind: filing. The 8-K of 2011-11-23 (Chapter 11) and the 8-K of 2011-11-29, item 3.01 (NYSE suspension and delisting notice), plus the Form 25-NSE notice that cites the bankruptcy.
- successor: worked out from the cited filings, none of which names a successor or a continuation; no successor filing was searched for in this report.
- last trade date: filing. The 8-K of 2011-11-29, item 3.01, states Oct 21 "was the last day that TPG's common stock traded on the NYSE"; the Form 25-NSE notice agrees on the 2011-10-21 halt.
- payout rule: fails rows (CUSIP 69344M101 under PPMIQ from 2011-11-30), plus worked out from decision 11 (the value is the first OTC print after a bankruptcy delisting). No filing states it.
- terms: worked out from the fails rows (first PPMIQ row 2011-11-30) and the 2011-11-28 suspension date in the 8-K; the price date 2011-11-29 is an estimate, not an observed print.

Not backed: the successor (absence of evidence only), the OTC price date and symbol timing. To make it verified: an OTC print or quote record showing PPMIQ's first session, and a successor search (8-K12B, 8-K12G3) that comes back empty.

## 5. What happened

- 2011-10-21: NYSE halted PMI after Arizona's Director of Insurance took possession of the main subsidiary on 2011-10-20 ([SEC 8-K 2011-10-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/935724/000119312511277918/d246694d8k.htm)).
- 2011-11-23: Chapter 11 petition in Delaware ([SEC 8-K 2011-11-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/935724/000119312511320992/d261060d8k.htm)).
- 2011-11-28: NYSE Regulation notice that the stock is subject to delisting and trading suspended; the 8-K states Oct 21 "was the last day that TPG's common stock traded on the NYSE" ([SEC 8-K 2011-11-29, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/935724/000119312511324479/d262649d8k.htm)).
- 2011-12-29: Form 25-NSE; NYSE removal at the open of 2011-12-27 under Rule 12d2-2(b); notice cites the 2011-11-23 bankruptcy and says trading was halted since 2011-10-21 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/935724/000087666111000571/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 69344M101 under PMI to 2011-11-29, then under PPMIQ from 2011-11-30 to 2012-03-20 (SEC fails-to-deliver data via sec.py fails).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. Holders kept the same shares (same CUSIP) but the exchange listing ended; per decision 11 the value is the first OTC print.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

`last_trade_date_source=ex99_notice` with flag `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`: the library took 2011-11-23 (the bankruptcy date named in the Form 25 notice) as the last trade instead of the notice's and the 8-K's halt date 2011-10-21. The halt-before-suspension pattern (halted, then months later bankruptcy and suspension) is not handled. Cause: other:ex99_notice_date_is_bankruptcy_not_halt. The Shumway fill (-0.30) results from the unconfirmed date.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Last-trade date rule: when the notice or an 8-K says trading was halted on D and it never resumed, the last trade is the trading day before or on D as stated (here 8-K says Oct 21 was the last trading day).
- Open: confirm the first PPMIQ OTC session (about 2011-11-29; fails rows start 2011-11-30); whether Oct 21 itself had prints (8-K says yes, last day traded).
- Golden-worthy: yes, well sourced by two SEC filings and a halt-then-bankruptcy shape.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the three fields the report calls wrong, re-opened through sec.py (8-K 0001193125-11-324479, Form 25-NSE 0000876661-11-000571, fails rows of CUSIP 69344M101).

- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The 8-K filed 2011-11-29 states the NYSE "halted trading of TPG's common stock on October 21, 2011, which was the last day that TPG's common stock traded on the NYSE." The Form 25-NSE notice says trading "has been halted by the NYSE since October 21, 2011" and uses 2011-11-23 only as the bankruptcy date behind the delisting. So the library's 2011-11-23 is a bankruptcy date, not an exchange print, and 2011-10-21 is stated by a filing.
- **terms: upheld, in part.** price_ticker PPMIQ (own OTC symbol, not PMI) is backed: fails rows show CUSIP 69344M101 under PMI 2011-10-03..2011-11-29 and under PPMIQ 2011-11-30..2011-12-30. The library's price_date 2011-11-25 is unsupported (NYSE suspension came 2011-11-28 per the 8-K). The report's 2011-11-29 is only an estimate; the true first OTC session could be 2011-11-28 to 2011-11-30. Read it as unverified, as the report already says.
- **ticker_history: upheld (derived).** It follows last_trade_date, which holds. Whether the range should end at the last print or at the 2011-11-28 suspension is a policy choice not tested here.

Not disputed, not re-checked: exit_kind, drop_reason, continuation, successor, value_rule, issuer.
