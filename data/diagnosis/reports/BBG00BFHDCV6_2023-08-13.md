## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2023-08-03 item 3.03: reclassification, one new share per old share).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: new Series A Liberty Formula One, FWONA) and fails rows (CUSIP 531229771 under FWONA from 2023-08-07).
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K says the Restated Charter was effective 5:00 pm 2023-08-03 and new stock begins trading 2023-08-04, so the old line's last day is 2023-08-03; the filing does not say "ceased trading on D" for the old line.
- Payout rule: filing (continuation, no exit value).
- Terms: filing (0.0428 LLYVA per share and cash for fractions; treated as a distribution, not exit consideration).
- Not backed: the last trade date as a stated print; the continuation-versus-merger call for the Live-share leg is a judgment under decision 9 (Live shares are stock in a new tracking stock of the same issuer, not another company's cash). An exchange print or a Nasdaq notice stating the old line's final session would make the date `verified`.
