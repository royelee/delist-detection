Not an ending. AnnTaylor Stores Corporation changed its name to ANN INC. on 2011-03-15 (same issuer, same ticker ANN, same NYSE listing); the CUSIP changed from 036115103 to 035623107. ## 5. What happened

- Effective 2011-03-15 AnnTaylor Stores Corporation changed its name to ANN INC. by merging a wholly-owned subsidiary into itself, the company surviving ([SEC 8-K 2011-03-16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874214/000119312511068147/d8k.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 036115103 (ANN, AnnTaylor) ends 2011-03-15; CUSIP 035623107 (ANN, ANN INC) starts 2011-03-17 and runs through at least 2012-06 (SEC fails-to-deliver files).
- No Form 25 or Form 15 in 2011; the first Form 25-NSE is 2015-08-25 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874214/000087666115000370/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The Ascena merger became effective 2015-08-21; each share became $37.34 cash plus 0.68 Ascena share (same 25-NSE notice). Form 15-12B filed 2015-09-04.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same issuer, same FIGI and ticker; only the name and CUSIP changed (name_change / rename, not an ending).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The rename gave a new CUSIP, so the old CUSIP's fails rows stopped on 2011-03-15 and the finder saw no Form 25 and continued 10-K/Q filings: `continued_filings_rule` (reason "Continued 10-K/Q filings >180d after delist", code 304), flags `no_form25;delist_date_approx;last_trade_date_unconfirmed`. The rename was not followed through the CUSIP switch, and the 2015 Form 25 was apparently not attached to this security (only one delisting row exists for it). Cause: `rename_not_followed`.

Upheld. The 8-K filed 2011-03-16 (0001193125-11-068147, Item 5.03) records a Section 253 name change in which the company survived. CIK 874214 and the Commission file number are unchanged. No Form 25, 25-NSE or 15 was filed between 2011-03 and 2015-08-25. Fails rows show ANN continuing under the new CUSIP 035623107 from 2011-03-17 to 2012-04-27; the report said 2012-06, which is minor. The 2015 25-NSE (0000876661-15-000370) confirms the $37.34 cash plus 0.68 Ascena share terms, with the merger effective 2015-08-21. The 2011 row is therefore invented and clips ticker_history too early. Not independently confirmed: the exact 2015 last trade day, whether the Ascena ticker is ASN or ASNA (the notice says ASN), and that one FIGI covers both CUSIPs (inferred). Both files were edited as asked.
