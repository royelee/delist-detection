## 5. What happened

- Reorg Merger completed 2014-12-31: Walgreens survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of WBA; each share converted into one WBA share ([SEC 8-K 2014-12-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/104207/000119312514457669/d843793d8k.htm)).
- Holders held the same number of WBA shares and the same ownership percentage immediately after the Reorg Merger, before the Step 2 Acquisition (same 8-K, Item 3.03). The Step 2 Acquisition (Alliance Boots) paid its cash and shares to Boots holders, not to Walgreen holders for their old shares.
- NASDAQ, NYSE and CHX halted WAG before the open on 2014-12-31; WBA began trading on NASDAQ that day (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- Form 25 filed and effective 2014-12-30 ([SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/104207/000119312514457091/d843985d25.htm)); a second Form 25 the same day ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/104207/000119312514457095/d844001d25.htm)).
