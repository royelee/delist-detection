## 1. Verdict

Liberty Capital Series A (LCAPA, CUSIP 53071M302) ended on 2011-09-23, but not as a merger: Liberty Interactive redeemed each share one for one, with no cash, for a share of the same name of its split-off subsidiary Liberty Media Corporation (formerly Liberty CapStarz, CIK 1507934), which trades as LCAPA on CUSIP 530322106 (sec_id BBG000PCNTM2 in the run). That is a continuation into a new issuer (decision 9, R1). The new run's row (`merger`, `stock`, price LCPAD, no successor) is wrong; the base run having no row was wrong too; the added ticker range is right.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (Liberty Interactive 8-K 2011-09-28, item 2.01: redemption of each Series A Liberty Capital share "in exchange for one share of Splitco's Series A Liberty Capital common stock", no cash).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Splitco, "formerly known as Liberty CapStarz", renamed Liberty Media Corporation; Exhibit 99.1 gives LCAPA as the permanent symbol; the run's BBG000PCNTM2 is CUSIP 530322106 LCAPA from 2011-10-05, fails rows).
- Last trade date: filing (Exhibit 99.1: split-off closed 5:00 p.m. Friday 2011-09-23; temporary symbols LCPAD from Monday 2011-09-26; Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2011-09-23).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01: no cash, one for one, so continuation).
- Terms: filing (same sentence: ratio 1 is the redemption ratio; no cash leg, no other security).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced 1:1 by a new line of the same holders (a different issuer, same tracking-stock claim, no cash): continuation with a successor link. Not a merger; nobody paid the holder anything.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The reason string is the continued-filings relabel ("Completed acquisition (8-K item 2.01 filed 2011-09-28, 425 2011-09-21); the registrant kept filing after it"). The 8-K's R1 statement names its target only as "Splitco", a defined term for Liberty Media Corporation (formerly Liberty CapStarz), so the target-name tie (`own_shares.names_target`) cannot match the new issuer and R1 / stage 9 do not link it. The LLM then read the 1:1 redemption as stock consideration priced on LCPAD. Cause: `holdco_not_linked` (a 1:1 reorganization into a new issuer left as a merger).

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat "Splitco" (the defined term for the new issuer) as naming the target when the 8-K's own subsidiary was renamed in the same filing, or link by the issuer's 8-A12B and same-name class within days; the row then becomes exchange / continuation with successor BBG000PCNTM2 and no value.
- Last trade 2011-09-23 should be published from Exhibit 99.1's timing, not left as an unconfirmed closing_day.
- Identity note, not part of this case: BBG000PCNTM2 is named "STARZ CLASS A" in securities.csv (its later issuer name), but its 2011 line is Liberty Capital Series A.
- Good golden case for the rule fix (well sourced).

## 9. Verification

Upheld. Re-read the cached 8-K 0001104659-11-053828: the split-off completed 2011-09-23 at 5:00 p.m. by redeeming each Series A Liberty Capital share "in exchange for one share of Splitco's Series A Liberty Capital common stock". Splitco is Liberty Media Corporation (formerly Liberty CapStarz). No cash is stated. The Form 25-NSE 0001354457-11-000196 names the Series A and B Liberty Capital and Liberty Starz classes and was filed 2011-09-23. The run's security_history gives BBG000PCNTM2 (CIK 1507934) LCPAD from 2011-09-26 and LCAPA from 2011-10-05, so the successor and the last trade day of 2011-09-23 are consistent, and the day is no later than the Form 25 effective date. Both field verdicts survive: delistings.added (neither: the new run's merger row and the base run's missing row are both wrong) and security_history.ranges (new right). The one open point is outside this case: BBG000PCNTM2 is named STARZ CLASS A in securities.csv, an identity-naming matter.
