## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: last_trade_date

The 8-K filed 2011-02-08 (item 3.01) says the common stock stays listed through 2011-02-28 and is delisted 2011-03-01. The Form 25 was filed 2011-02-18 under 12d2-2(c) for NYSE common stock, so it takes effect 10 days later, on 2011-02-28. The date is therefore not after the effective date, and the reference allows item 3.01 as a source. Caveat: this is the stated listing end, not an exchange print, and the short-form merger is only "on or about" 2011-02-28. I did not re-check exit_kind, the 14.25 cash terms or the issuer; the report does not call them wrong.
