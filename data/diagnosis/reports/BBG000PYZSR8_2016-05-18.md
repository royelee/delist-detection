# BBG000PYZSR8_2016-05-18 (CHTR, legacy Charter Class A)

## 1. Verdict

Legacy Charter's Class A shares were exchanged on 2016-05-18 for 0.9042 shares of the new parent's Class A stock (new CUSIP 16119P108, same ticker CHTR), with no cash, as part of the Charter / Time Warner Cable / Bright House combination. That is not one for one, so under decision 9 it is a stock-for-stock exchange (a merger), not a continuation; the library's `exchange` / `continuation=true` is wrong on the rule, though it correctly finds the successor line. The ratio is a technical "parent merger exchange ratio", so an operator who reads it as a pure reorganization could still call it a continuation (see open checks).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the three fields the report calls wrong. Sources re-read through sec.py (cached SEC text).

- **exit_kind (exchange -> merger): upheld.** The 8-K (0001193125-16-596195) says that "as a result of the Third Merger, each share of Legacy Charter Class A Common Stock ... was converted into the right to receive 0.9042 shares of New Charter Class A Common Stock", and the filing states no cash for legacy holders. The press release (0001193125-16-593941) calls 0.9042 "the parent merger exchange ratio" and says legacy holders get "0.9042 shares ... for each share of legacy Charter common stock". Decision 9 requires one for one, so a 0.9042 ratio and a new CUSIP (16119P108) are not a continuation. The new issuer is a different legal entity (CCH I, LLC) that also took in TWC and Bright House. The caveat the report already lists stands: the ratio is a technical adjustment applied to every holder, so an operator could still rule it a reorganization. The rule as written supports "merger".
- **value_rule (continuation -> stock): upheld.** It follows from the exit_kind. The terms the report gives (stock_ratio 0.9042, no cash, price_ticker CHTR) match the 8-K. The price_date of 2016-05-18 is the new line's first trading day under CHTR. Fails rows for new CUSIP 16119P108 start 2016-05-18 (CHTRZZZZ) and then show CHTR from 2016-05-19.
- **ticker_history (old line ends 2016-05-17, new line starts 2016-05-18): upheld, with lower confidence.** The press release says new CHTR begins trading "this morning" (2016-05-18). In the fails data, old CUSIP 16117M305 has a last live CHTR row dated 2016-05-18 at $203.36. The following row (2016-05-19, under CHTRXXXX) shows the same $203.36, so the old line did not trade on 2016-05-18. The library's current ticker_history (old line ends 2016-05-18, new line from 2016-05-19) is therefore one day late. The 05-17 date is worked out and not backed by an exchange print or a Form 25. Fails prices are lagged by one day, and the new CUSIP's 05-18 fails row ($224.91) is not clean evidence of a 05-17 close. The one-day shift rests on the press release plus the stale-price pattern.

Not checked: drop_reason, successor, issuer (the report calls them agree). Overall: all three fields upheld. The merger-versus-continuation call stays subject to the operator ruling named in section 8.
