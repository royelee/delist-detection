## 5. What happened

- Progress Energy was acquired by Duke Energy through the merger of Merger Sub into it; each share was converted into 0.87083 Duke common shares, cash for fractions ([SEC 8-K 2012-07-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094093/000119312512293903/d376434d8k.htm)).
- On 2012-07-02 Progress asked the NYSE to file a Form 25 to remove its common stock ([SEC 8-K 2012-07-03, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094093/000119312512293907/d375065d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2012-07-05, effective 2012-07-15 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094093/000087666112000352/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
