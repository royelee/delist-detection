# FI (Frank's International N.V.), 2021-10-01: case CIK1575828-COMMON_2021-10-01

## 1. Verdict

Not a merger of FI into someone else. Frank's was the legal acquirer in its merger with Expro: it kept its registrant (CIK 1575828), renamed itself Expro Group Holdings N.V., did a 1-for-6 reverse split and began trading as XPRO on 2021-10-04. FI holders kept their shares (no cash, no exchange for another company's stock), so this is a rename plus reverse split of the same issuer, a continuation. The library's "merger, stock 1.212 x XPRO" is wrong: 1.212 is the ratio paid to Expro's shareholders, not to FI's.

## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement dated 2021-03-10; Frank's filed the 425s and the 8-K of 2021-09-15 (shareholder vote) [SEC filings list, CIK 1575828](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=1575828&type=8-K).
- 2021-10-01: Frank's, "formerly named Frank's International N.V.", now Expro Group Holdings N.V., completed the merger with Expro Group Holdings International Limited. Each Expro share became 1.2120 Company shares (7.2720 x the 1-for-6 reverse split ratio). [SEC 8-K 2021-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575828/000119312521289897/d181309d8k.htm)
- The same 8-K: from 2021-10-04 the Company common stock trades on the NYSE post-reverse split as "XPRO".
- Fails rows: CUSIP N33462107 (FI) 2021-09-02..2021-10-01 at about $2.93-2.94; CUSIP N3144W105 (XPRO) from 2021-10-06 at $20.36 (offline `fails`, about 6.9x the FI price, consistent with the 1-for-6 split).
- No Form 25 and no Form 15 for FI in 2021-06..2022-03 (EDGAR filings list): the NYSE listing never ended.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same registrant and listing, new name and ticker, new CUSIP only because of the reverse split; nothing paid to FI holders. Per reference.md this is a rename (plus a reverse split), with a continuation link to the XPRO line if the run adds it.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason: "Completed acquisition (8-K item 2.01 ...); the registrant kept filing after it", the end-of-era resolver's branch 4 (2.01 plus 425 filings) that reads any 2.01 as the target being merged away. It never checks that the registrant is the legal acquirer, so the LLM took Expro's 1.212 ratio as FI's terms (gate failed, assumed par). Cause: `rename_not_followed` (the CUSIP change from the reverse split hid that the same issuer kept trading as XPRO).

- Open: the XPRO sec_id/FIGI is not in this run; the 2021-10-01 last-trade day rests on the 8-K's "first trading day Oct 4" plus fails rows, not an exchange print.
- Golden-worthy: yes. A reverse-split rename where the registrant is the legal acquirer, a clean case for the end-of-era resolver.
