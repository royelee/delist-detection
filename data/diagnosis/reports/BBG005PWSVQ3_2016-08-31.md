Not an ending. WP Glimcher Inc. changed its name to Washington Prime Group Inc. on 2016-08-30 and kept trading
on the NYSE as WPG; the CUSIP changed with the name (92939N102 to 93964W108) and the issuer (CIK 1594686) is the
same. The library's "exchange transfer, 2016-08-31" is a guess from the continued-filings fallback and should be no
row at all; the ticker history should continue past 2016-08-31.

Kept the same security: same issuer, same ticker, same holders; only the name (and CUSIP) changed.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder found no Form 25 and fell back to the continued-filings rule (`continued_filings_rule`, 304,
flags `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `successor_unknown`). It read the last sighting of CUSIP 92939N102 on
2016-08-31 as an exit; the new CUSIP 93964W108 under the same ticker WPG and issuer was not linked, so
`cusip_history` stops at 2016-08-31 and `ticker_history` is clipped. Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the three fields the report calls wrong. All three upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K of 2016-09-02 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594686/000143774916038300/wpg20160901_8k.htm))
  states under Item 5.03 that the name change from "WP Glimcher Inc." to "Washington Prime Group Inc." became effective
  on filing Articles of Amendment on 2016-08-30, with the same Indiana registrant (file no. 001-36252, same CIK 1594686).
  It is an amendment of the same corporation's articles, so no new security or successor. The CIK's filings list for
  2016-06 .. 2017-06 shows no Form 25, 25-NSE, Form 15, 8-K12B or 8-K12G3. The only ending is the library's
  continued-filings guess. No cash and no exchange of shares, so nothing in the decision table makes this an ending.
- **last_trade_date: upheld, with a nuance.** The library's value is blank, so it is not a wrong date. It is wrong
  only in that no ending exists, so no last trade date should be published. Fails rows: old CUSIP 92939N102 (WPG) ends
  2016-08-31 at $13.75, plus 2 rows under WPGXXXX on 2016-09-01/02 at $13.75 (old line's tail). New CUSIP 93964W108
  (WASHINGTON PRIME GROUP INC) starts 2016-09-01 at $13.74 under WPG and runs to 2017-03-29 (95 rows). The prices
  are continuous across the CUSIP switch.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** `output/ticker_history.csv` has BBG005PWSVQ3, WPG, 2014-05-30 .. 2016-08-31 (clipped,
  source observation), while WPG kept trading under the new CUSIP after 2016-08-31 (fails rows above) and the
  registrant filed a 10-Q on 2016-11-03. The clip is wrong.
- Minor wording point: section 4 says the name change was "effective on filing after the 2016-08-30 vote". The 8-K
  says the filing itself was on 2016-08-30. This does not affect the verdict.
- Not independently re-checked: NYSE listing continuity beyond what fails rows show; no web evidence was used or needed.
