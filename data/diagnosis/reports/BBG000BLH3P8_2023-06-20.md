The 8-K filed 2023-06-08 (0000045876-23-000029, item 5.03) says the stock keeps trading, as HSC through June 18 and as NVRI on the NYSE from June 19, and that the change does not affect shareholders' rights. The same CIK 45876 kept filing 10-Qs and 8-Ks through 2023-09 (`filings 45876`), and no Form 25 or Form 15 appears from 2023-05 to 2023-09.
