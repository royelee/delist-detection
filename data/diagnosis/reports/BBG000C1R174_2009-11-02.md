## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2009-10-26 item 2.01: HLTH merged into WebMD, HLTH ceased to exist).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: WebMD survives; Class A shares unchanged, keep trading as WBMD).
- Last trade date: worked out from the Form 25 effectiveness date 2009-10-23, the 8-K's 2009-10-23 closing date and fails rows ending 2009-10-26; no filing states the last trading day.
- Payout rule: filing (DEFM14A and 8-K: conversion into WebMD common stock).
- Terms: filing (0.4444 in the DEFM14A and the 8-K); price date worked out as the next session after the last trade.

Not backed: the last trade day itself. An exchange print (Nasdaq or MIDAS volume, or a notice stating the last day) would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- HLTH and WebMD signed the merger agreement 2009-06-17; HLTH merges into WebMD, each HLTH share converts into 0.4444 WebMD shares, fractions paid in cash ([SEC DEFM14A 2009-09-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009575/000095012309043127/g19631ddefm14a.htm)).
- Merger completed 2009-10-23; WebMD Class A shares unchanged and continue on Nasdaq as WBMD ([SEC 8-K 2009-10-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326583/000095012309053180/g20916e8vk.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE for HLTH filed and effective 2009-10-23, with Form 15-12G the same day ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009575/000135445709000292/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows: HLTH 2009-01-02..2009-10-26; WBMD (CUSIP 94770V102) rows continue past 2009-10-27 (fails rows).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: HLTH shares were exchanged for 0.4444 shares of another registrant, not one for one; a merger, not a continuation (even though HLTH was the accounting acquirer).
