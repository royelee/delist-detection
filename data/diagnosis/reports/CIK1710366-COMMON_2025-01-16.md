# CEIX (CONSOL Energy) 2025-01-16: a rename to Core Natural Resources (CNR), not an ending

## 1. Verdict
CONSOL Energy was the acquirer in its merger of equals with Arch Resources. It kept its shares, listing and CIK, changed its name and ticker to Core Natural Resources / CNR and began trading under a new CUSIP on 2025-01-15. The library's "merger, 1.326 CEIX shares" row is wrong: 1.326 is what Arch holders received, not CEIX holders. There is no ending.

## 5. What happened
- Merger of equals closed 2025-01-14; Merger Sub merged into Arch, Arch survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, which was renamed Core Natural Resources, Inc. (formerly CONSOL Energy Inc.) [SEC 8-K 2025-01-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1710366/000119312525007135/d836647d8k.htm).
- Each Arch share became 1.326 shares of Company Common Stock; the stock continued to trade on NYSE, ticker CEIX to CNR, new CUSIP 218937100, trading under the new ticker and CUSIP from the open 2025-01-15 (same 8-K).
- Item 5.03: charter amendment filed 2025-01-14 changing the name (same 8-K).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 20854L108 (CEIX) rows 2025-01-02..01-16; CUSIP 218937100 (CNR) rows 2025-01-15..02-27, first close $99.61 equal to CEIX's last (fails data).
- No Form 25 was found for CEIX (library flag no_form25).

The end-of-era resolver saw 8-K item 2.01 plus a 425 and called it a merger (`resolved_from_continued_filings`), and the LLM read the Arch exchange ratio as the CEIX holders' terms (`terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`). Cause: rename_not_followed.

## 8. Fix and open checks
- Follow the CUSIP/ticker change 20854L108 to 218937100 and CEIX to CNR on one sec_id; drop the delisting row.
- Guard: an 8-K 2.01 where the filer is the acquirer (its own shares are not exchanged) is not an ending.
- Open facts: none. Good golden case (acquirer renamed after a merger of equals, new CUSIP).

## 9. Verification
Re-read the cited 8-K ([SEC 8-K 2025-01-15, acc. 0001193125-25-007135](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1710366/000119312525007135/d836647d8k.htm)) and the fails rows (`sec.py fails --cusip 218937100`).
- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K says the registrant (Core Natural Resources, "formerly known as CONSOL Energy Inc.") is the acquirer; at the Effective Time it "changed its name", the common stock kept "continuing to trade on the New York Stock Exchange", the symbol went CEIX to CNR and the stock "is trading through a new CUSIP (218937100)", from the open on 2025-01-15. No CEIX share was exchanged, so there is no merger ending. `reference.md` (rename / ticker change; `rename_not_followed`) treats a rename as no ending; same holders, same claim (decision 9), so the new CUSIP does not make a different security.
- **value_rule: upheld.** Nothing replaced a CEIX share, so no stock or cash payout; transfer (0) is right. The library's `stock` rule rests on the same misread merger.
- **terms: upheld.** The 8-K says the 1.326 "Exchange Ratio" converts Arch Class A and Class B shares into Company Common Stock. It is the Arch holders' ratio, so applying it to CEIX holders is wrong (the gate's `fail_sanity` agrees). Fails rows for 218937100 (CNR, 13 rows 2025-01-15..2025-02-27, first $99.61) fit a continuing line.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** `output/ticker_history.csv` and `cusip_history.csv` end at 2025-01-14 (CEIX, 20854L108); the security continues as CNR / 218937100 from 2025-01-15, so that range and CUSIP are missing.
- Not re-checked: the "Merger Sub merged into Arch" detail; it does not bear on these fields. No uncertainty remains on the four fields.
