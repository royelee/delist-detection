# MYL (Mylan N.V.) -> Viatris, 2020-11-16

## 1. Verdict

Mylan N.V. combined with Pfizer's Upjohn into Viatris on 2020-11-16; each Mylan share was exchanged one for one for a Viatris common share, with no cash to Mylan holders. Under decision 9 this is a continuation (successor VTRS, BBG00Y4RQNH4), not a merger: the library's `merger` label, its 2020-11-26 end date and its blank last trade date are wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2020-11-19, Item 3.03: each Mylan share exchanged for one Viatris share; the $12 billion cash went to Pfizer, not to Mylan holders).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Viatris common; Viatris CIK 1792044) plus the run's own VTRS line.
- Last trade date: not stated by a filing; worked out from the 8-K (closing and Nasdaq suspension request on 2020-11-16, Form 25-NSE effective 2020-11-16) and VTRS line starting 2020-11-17 in ticker_history.

- Terms: filing (1:1).
- Not backed: the last trading day as an exchange print. A Nasdaq notice or MIDAS row naming 2020-11-16 as the final Mylan session would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- On 2020-11-16 Pfizer's Upjohn was spun off and combined with Mylan; Mylan merged into Mylan II B.V., an indirect Viatris subsidiary, and ceased to exist as a separate entity ([SEC 8-K 2020-11-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1623613/000119312520298224/d91045d8k.htm)).
- Each Mylan share outstanding was exchanged for one share of Viatris common stock (same 8-K, Item 3.03).
- Viatris paid Pfizer $12 billion in cash as partial consideration for the Upjohn contribution (same 8-K); this is not paid to Mylan holders.
- Mylan asked Nasdaq on 2020-11-16 to suspend trading and delist; Nasdaq filed the Form 25 the same day ([SEC 8-K Item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1623613/000119312520298224/d91045d8k.htm), [SEC Form 25-NSE 2020-11-16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1623613/000135445720000696/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15 filed 2020-11-27 ([SEC 15-15D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1623613/000119312520304601/d15980d1515d.htm)); the library's 2020-11-26 date looks like the Form 25's filing plus 10 days, not the last trade.
- The run's own cusip_history shows VTRS (92556V106) beginning 2020-11-17.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced 1:1 by a new line of the same holders (the Viatris holdco): a continuation, like BHI -> BHGE and the 1:1 holdco reorganizations. Pfizer holders' receipt of shares does not alter what Mylan holders got.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The classifier fired its M&A trigger on 8-K items 2.01+3.01+5.01 and called it a merger (CRSP 231) with a stock leg of 1.0 VTRS but no price date. It did not link the Viatris successor as a holdco reorganization, and its end date (2020-11-26) is the Form 25 filing plus 10 days rather than 2020-11-16, so there was no last trade date and the payout gate failed (`no_acq_price`). Cause tag: `holdco_not_linked`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat a 1:1, no-cash share exchange into a holdco as a continuation: exit_kind exchange, successor BBG00Y4RQNH4, no value; clip MYL at 2020-11-16.
- Open check: an exchange print for the last Mylan session (2020-11-16).
- Golden case: yes, a reverse-Morris-Trust-style combination that is 1:1 for the listed company's holders.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the three fields the report calls wrong. Sources re-opened through sec.py (8-K 0001193125-20-298224, Form 25-NSE 0001354457-20-000696, the CIK 1623613 filing list, offline fails rows) and the run's own ticker_history and contract security_history.

- **exit_kind (merger -> exchange, continuation): upheld.** The 8-K Item 3.03 says "each Mylan Share issued and outstanding immediately prior to the consummation of the Combination was exchanged for one share of Viatris common stock (subject to any withholding taxes)": one for one, no cash for Mylan holders. The only cash in the filing is the $12 billion Viatris paid Pfizer "as partial consideration for the Contribution" (Introductory Note), which is paid to Pfizer for Upjohn and not to Mylan holders for surrendering their shares, so the BHI/BHGE ruling in reference.md does not make it merger consideration. Mylan's holders keep a claim in the successor line (about 43% of Viatris). Decision 9 and the "replaced 1:1 by a new line" row therefore apply. Residual doubt: the transaction is a Reverse Morris Trust and Mylan holders end with a minority stake, so a reader who treats the combination as an acquisition of Mylan would call it a merger. The reference rules ask what the holder received for the old share, so the report's reading is the one the rules support.
- **value_rule (stock 1.0 x VTRS -> continuation, no value): upheld.** The library's `terms_gate=failed:no_acq_price` and blank price date are consistent with there being no price to apply: a continuation publishes no value. The ratio 1.0 is correct but moot.
- **ticker_history (MYL ending 2020-11-26 -> 2020-11-16): upheld.** The 8-K Item 3.01 says that on 2020-11-16 Mylan asked Nasdaq to suspend trading and remove the listing, and Nasdaq filed the Form 25 that day. The Form 25-NSE header shows FILED AS OF DATE 20201116 and EFFECTIVENESS DATE 20201116, so 2020-11-26 is not the Form 25's own date; it is the library's filing + 10 days. Offline fails rows put VTRS (CUSIP 92556V106) first on 2020-11-17, and the run's ticker_history already starts VTRS on 2020-11-17. MYL fails rows run to 2020-11-18 (settlement lag after the last trade), which does not contradict a 2020-11-16 last session. The remaining gap is the one the report already names: 2020-11-16 as last trade is inferred and not shown by an exchange print.

Result: all three fields upheld; the last trade date stays `inferred`, not verified. The report's secondary remark that the library's date "looks like the Form 25's filing plus 10 days" is correct as to the arithmetic (filing 2020-11-16 + 10 = 2020-11-26), though the 25-NSE header itself lists effectiveness on 2020-11-16.
