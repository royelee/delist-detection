# PX (Praxair) -> Linde plc, 2018: case BBG000CGQ6M4_2018-11-10

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger: on 2018-10-31 each Praxair share became one Linde plc ordinary share, and Praxair became an indirect subsidiary of Linde plc, which also took over Linde AG. The library's kind, date, rule and terms are all right; the row is uncertain only because its payout gate (a sanity check against the fails-based close) failed and left assumed par (0.0) as the fill.

- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Praxair became a subsidiary of Linde plc; no continuing Praxair line).

## 5. What happened

- 2018-10-31: Praxair and Linde AG combined under Linde plc; in the Merger each Praxair share was converted into the right to receive one Linde plc ordinary share ([SEC 8-K 2018-10-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884905/000119312518313154/d646523d8k.htm)).
- The shares were suspended from the NYSE at the close on 2018-10-30 ([same 8-K, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884905/000119312518313154/d646523d8k.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2018-10-31 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884905/000087666118001152/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); a Form 25 for the notes followed 2018-11-01.
- Fails rows of CUSIP 74005P104 end 2018-11-01 (last close 164.50 as the library holds it). Linde plc (LIN) is in the run from 2018-10-31.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced by stock of another company. It is not a continuation: Praxair holders did not keep their claim; they exchanged it for shares of a new parent that also owns Linde AG, which is a business combination, not a holding-company reorganization. No cash was paid in the exchange.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It did not; only its evidence is thin. Flags `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity` and `merger_at_par` made the value assumed par (0.0) (`uncertain_reasons = assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). The terms (1:1, LIN) are right, so the sanity check failed on the price relationship, not on a misread term. Cause: `library_right_but_uncertain`.
