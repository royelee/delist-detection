# CIK708819-COMMON_2018-05-08 (MDR, McDermott International)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending. McDermott was the acquirer of CB&I; on 2018-05-09 it did a 3-to-1 reverse stock split (new CUSIP
580037703, same ticker MDR, same issuer) and kept trading on the NYSE until its 2020 bankruptcy. The library's
"merger" row, its stock leg (0.82407 shares, which is the ratio CB&I holders received) and its missing last trade date
are all wrong: the security continues.

## 4. Why this confidence

- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2018-05-11 Items 2.01, 5.03: McDermott is the acquirer; the split is its own charter amendment).
- Successor: filing (same registrant, CIK 708819, kept filing 8-Ks and 10-Qs under the same name).

## 5. What happened

- McDermott filed on 2018-05-02 an amendment effecting a 3-to-1 reverse split, effective 11:59 PM on 2018-05-09
  ([SEC 8-K 2018-05-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/708819/000119312518160205/d569261d8k.htm), Item 5.03; 8-K 2018-05-02 items 5.03, 5.07 [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/708819/000119312518148800/d650093d8k.htm)).
- On 2018-05-10 McDermott completed the Combination with CB&I, issuing about 84.5 million shares to CB&I holders; the
  NYSE closing price on 2018-05-09 is cited as the reference ([SEC 8-K 2018-05-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/708819/000119312518160205/d569261d8k.htm), Item 2.01).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 580037109 under MDR to 2018-05-08 (last $6.65); CUSIP 580037703 "COM NEW" under MDR from
  2018-05-14 (first $22.01), 246 rows to 2019-12-31 (fails data, `sec.py fails`).
- McDermott filed 10-Qs and 8-Ks as the same registrant afterward (10-Q 2018-07-31, 10-Q 2018-10-30). No Form 25, 15 or 8-K12B in 2018.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same issuer, same ticker, a reverse split that changed the CUSIP and nothing else.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The resolver found an 8-K Item 2.01 plus a 425 and read the new CUSIP's appearance and the old CUSIP's last fails row as
the end of the security (`resolved_from_continued_filings`, no Form 25). The stock leg 0.82407 is the CB&I
holders' exchange ratio, attributed to the acquirer's own holders by the LLM extractor (`terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`;
last close 22.01 is a post-split price, which is why the gate failed). The CUSIP switch from a reverse split was not
followed. Cause tag: other:reverse_split_cusip_change_read_as_merger (acquirer role misread).

## 9. Verification

Re-checked against the 8-K filed 2018-05-11 (0001193125-18-160205), the fails data and the EDGAR filing list.

- exit_kind (merger -> none): upheld. Item 2.01 says "McDermott and CB&I completed the Combination"; McDermott issued about 84.5 million of its own shares to CB&I holders, so McDermott is the acquirer and its holders surrendered nothing. Item 5.03 confirms the 3-to-1 reverse split by an amendment filed 2018-05-02 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/708819/000119312518160205/d569261d8k.htm)). A reverse split of the same issuer and ticker is not an ending (reference.md decision table).
- last_trade_date: upheld. Fails rows show MDR under the new CUSIP 580037703 ("COM NEW") from 2018-05-14 (30 rows to 2018-06-28 in the checked window); 10-Qs followed on 2018-07-31 and 2018-10-30. No Form 25 or 15 appears from 2018-05 to 2018-12. There is no last trade to date.
- value_rule (stock -> continuation): upheld, since there is no exit.
- terms (0.82407 x MDR): upheld. The ratio belongs to the exchange offer for CB&I shares, not to MDR holders.
- ticker_history clipped at 2018-05-08: upheld. MDR keeps trading past that date.
- Caveat: the report's 246 fails rows through 2019-12-31 were not re-run; the May-June 2018 rows suffice for the conclusion.
