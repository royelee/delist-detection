# BBG000DY8ZS4_2019-05-15 (WFT, Weatherford International plc)

## 1. Verdict
The NYSE suspended trading in the ordinary shares before the open on 2019-05-13 ("abnormally low" price, NYSE 802.01D) and the shares traded OTC afterwards; the Form 25 came late (2020-04-17). It is a real ending, `dropped`, last trade 2019-05-10, valued at the first OTC print. The library's exit kind and last trade date are right; its drop reason (guidelines vs price), OTC symbol/date and delist date (2019-05-15, "approximate") are not.

## 5. What happened

- Plan effective 2019-12-13: Old Ordinary Shares cancelled; 69,999,954 New Ordinary Shares and warrants issued to noteholders and old holders [SEC 8-K 2019-12-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000110465919073841/tm1925198-1_8k.htm).
- Company withdrew its NYSE appeal; delisting via Form 25; OTC trading continues as WFTLF [SEC 8-K 2020-04-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000110465920046579/tm2015956-1_8k.htm).
- NYSE filed 25-NSE on 2020-04-17 [SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000087666120000340/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Lost: delisted for a deficiency (abnormally low price), then OTC. Post-bankruptcy equity is a new security, so no continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
It found no Form 25 (filed 11 months after the suspension), fell back to the 8-K 3.01 notice and read it as a compliance failure with code 570 and a guessed delist date 2019-05-15 (flags `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`). Exit kind and date landed right; drop reason is a deficiency subtype (price, not guidelines), and the OTC price ticker is WFT, not WFTIF, dated 2019-05-13 (one day early). Cause: late_form25.

## 9. Verification
Field checked: **terms** (price_ticker, price_date). Result: **upheld**.
- Re-read [SEC 8-K 2019-05-16, acc. 0001603923-19-000067](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000160392319000067/a8-kdelistingnotice.htm): "trading of the Company's ordinary shares on the OTC Bulletin Board or 'pink sheets' market commenced on May 14, 2019 under the symbol 'WFTIF.'" The NYSE suspension was effective prior to the open on May 13, 2019. The library's price_ticker WFT (the NYSE symbol) and price_date 2019-05-13 (a day the shares did not trade on the NYSE) therefore disagree with the filing.
- The otc_print rule in reference.md (price_ticker = the security's own OTC symbol, price_date = the session after the last exchange trade) gives WFTIF and 2019-05-14, since the last exchange trade is Fri 2019-05-10. The report applied it correctly.
- Not refuted: no filing found that gives a different OTC symbol or start date. The later WFTLF symbol (2020 8-K) is a different, later line and does not affect the first print.
- Side check (not the field under test): drop_reason "price" rests on the 8-K's "abnormally low" price levels under NYSE 802.01D. The 8-K also says the NYSE considered the RSA, so price vs guidelines is a judgement. The reference maps 550/552 to price and 570/584 to guidelines, so price is the closer reading, but I rate it plausible, not certain. The report's own status table puts terms, not drop_reason, in the record's wrong fields.
