## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2007-07-30 item 2.01-type text: merger completed; Form 25-NSE 2007-07-25).
- Successor: filing (8-K: ServiceMaster is the surviving corporation, an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Holdings; holders get cash only).

- Terms: filing (same 8-K and the Form 25 notice: $15.625 in cash, without interest).

## 5. What happened

- On 2007-07-24 Acquisition Co. merged into ServiceMaster, which survived as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of
  a CD&R-led Holdings; each share became $15.625 cash ([SEC 8-K 2007-07-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1052045/000110465907057227/a07-20197_18k.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-07-25: the merger became effective July 25, each share converted into $15.625 cash,
  the security was suspended from trading July 25, removal from listing at the open on 2007-08-06
  ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1052045/000087666107000641/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2007-08-06 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1052045/000110465907059155/a07-21182_11512b.htm)).
- The CUSIP 81760N109 and ticker SVM appear in the library only to 2007-07-24 (cusip_history, ticker_history).
- The caller's observations list SVM 2008-01-16 through 2009-06-08 (7 rows, status `after_delisting`): a stale snapshot,
  and the origin of `seeds_outside_history`. The registrant did keep filing (8-Ks through 2008) because of its new debt,
  not because the stock kept trading.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check against the cited filings (re-read through sec.py).

- **last_trade_date 2007-07-24: upheld.** The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-07-000641) says the security "was suspended from
  trading on July 25, 2007", so the last trade is the prior trading day, 2007-07-24 (reference.md, decision 12). The 8-K
  (0001104659-07-057227) names July 24, 2007 as the Closing Date. The two sources disagree on the merger's effective date
  (July 24 vs July 25) but both give the same last trade day.
- **exit_kind merger, continuation false, no successor: upheld.** The 8-K says each share "was converted into the right to
  receive $15.625 in cash"; the Form 25 notice cites Rule 12d2-2(a)(3) and the same $15.625 cash conversion. No stock leg.
- **value_rule cash, terms 15.625: upheld.** Both filings state $15.625 in cash, without interest.
- **cash_currency blank (status missing): upheld.** The filings state dollars ("$15.625"), so USD is the correct currency
  and the source row leaves it blank. Minor point: the reference's `currency_missing` tag is defined for non-USD cash, so
  the report's tag `library_right_but_uncertain` is reasonable, with the blank currency a small separate gap.
- **Uncertainty from stale seeds and the continued-filings label: upheld.** The source row's reason text reads "Change in
  control (8-K item 5.01 ...); the registrant kept filing after it" with `resolved_from_continued_filings`, while the
  delisting has a real 25-NSE and `last_trade_date_source=ex99_notice`; `seeds_outside_history:1 from 2008-01-16` matches
  the report.
- Not re-checked: the 2008-2009 observation rows, ticker_history ranges and the 15-12B (not needed for any disputed field).
