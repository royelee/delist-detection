## 9. Verification

Re-read [SEC 8-K 2023-06-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/912752/000119312523158935/d530850d8k.htm), `output/ticker_history.csv` and the fails rows for both CUSIPs. All four fields the report calls wrong are upheld.

- **exit_kind (merger -> exchange continuation): upheld.** The 8-K's explanatory note states a holding company reorganization. Effective 12:00 am on 2023-06-01, each SBG Class A share was exchanged one for one for a New Sinclair Class A share. Management and board are unchanged. New Sinclair is the Rule 12g-3 successor issuer. Item 3.01 says the new shares trade "on an uninterrupted basis" under SBGI. Item 5.01 is the holdco's change in control, not an acquisition. This is reference.md's 1:1 holdco case (Alphabet, ANAT).
- **value_rule (stock -> continuation): upheld.** The holder received only the same-class New Sinclair share, with no cash and no other consideration. The 1.0 ratio is the exchange ratio into the holder's own successor line, so the library's `stock` rule (1 x SBGI) treats the successor as acquirer stock. The only cash in the 8-K is the $0.25 quarterly dividend declared 2023-05-03, record date 2023-05-30, payable 2023-06-15. New Sinclair pays it to holders of the new shares, so under the 2026-10-03 BHI/BHGE ruling it is a distribution on the successor and does not make a merger. The report does not mention it. It should be recorded as such.
- **terms (stock_ratio 1.0, price_ticker SBGI -> none): upheld.** There is no stock leg to price. The 1.0 is the exchange ratio, and the library's own formula "1 x price(SBGI, ?)" has no price date.
- **ticker_history (new line starts 2023-06-05 -> 2023-06-01): upheld.** `ticker_history.csv` has BBG000F2XXP2 SBGI to 2023-05-31 and BBG01GJ3NY88 SBGI from 2023-06-05, which leaves a gap. The 8-K shows trading was uninterrupted from the 2023-06-01 effective time. Fails rows for the new CUSIP 829242106 begin 2023-06-05 (first $14.84, the same figure as the old line's last close). The fails rows for the old CUSIP 829226109 end 2023-05-31. The 06-05 start is a fails-data settlement lag, not a trading break. The 06-01 date rests on the 8-K, not on an exchange print.

Caveats, unchanged from sections 4 and 8. The last trade date (2023-05-31) is worked out from the effective time. No Nasdaq Form 25 notice was checked. Neither affects the four fields above.
