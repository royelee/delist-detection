## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2013-11-13, Items 2.03/3.01/3.03/5.01: NYX merged into NYSE Euronext Holdings and ceased to exist).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: shares converted into the right to receive ICE Group shares and cash; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: suspended from trading on 2013-11-13, merger effective before the open, so the last trade was 2013-11-12).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: Standard Consideration of 0.1703 ICE Group Share plus $11.27 cash).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K; the alternatives were $33.12 cash or 0.2581 ICE Group shares, subject to proration).

## 5. What happened

- Merger closed before the open on 2013-11-13; each share converted into 0.1703 ICE Group share plus $11.27 cash (standard election), alternatives $33.12 cash or 0.2581 shares ([SEC 8-K 2013-11-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1368007/000119312513440079/d627265d8k.htm)).
- NYSE's Form 25-NSE: security suspended from trading on 2013-11-13 ([SEC 25-NSE 2013-11-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1368007/000087666113000823/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- S-8 POS and POSASR deregistrations filed the same day.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old share was exchanged for cash plus another company's stock, a merger and not a continuation.
