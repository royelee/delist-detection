## 9. Verification

Checked the two fields the report calls wrong, exit_kind and value_rule, against the filings.

- **exit_kind (blank -> exchange): upheld.** The 8-K item 3.01 of 2026-08-26 (the item the report had not read in full) says the Company "determined to voluntarily withdraw the listing of the Company's common stock ... from The Nasdaq Global Select Market and transfer the listing to the New York Stock Exchange", and that the stock "will continue to trade under the stock symbol KHC" ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637459/000163745926000057/khc-20260824.htm)). The 8-A12B (0001637459-26-000061) registers the same class (common stock, $0.01 par) on the NYSE "in connection with the transfer of the listing", incorporating the existing description with Nasdaq replaced by NYSE. The Form 25 (0001637459-26-000062) is a 12d2-2(c) voluntary withdrawal of "Common Stock, par value $0.01 per share" from Nasdaq, dated 2026-09-08. Class, issuer and ticker match, so this is reference.md's "Kept: same CUSIP, other exchange" row (exchange, successor = itself), not an unknown ending. The senior notes stay on Nasdaq, so the Form 25 covers only the common stock.
- **value_rule (unknown -> transfer): upheld.** A transfer of the same shares has no consideration, so `transfer` (the same shares, 0 return) is the rule per reference.md's payout table. Nothing in the filings suggests a cash or stock leg.
- **Still open, as the report says:** the actual last Nasdaq session (2026-09-11 is the filings' stated expectation, not a print) and that NYSE trading began 2026-09-14. No fails rows or MIDAS were checked. A new CUSIP was not checked; the 8-A12B says the description of the common stock is unchanged, which supports the same CUSIP. None of this changes the exit kind or the rule.
