## 5. What happened

- Each share was cancelled and converted into $72.50 cash at the merger closing; NYSE was asked to halt trading after the close on 2013-06-07 ([SEC 8-K 2013-06-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513258009/d555504d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2013-06-12, removal at the open 2013-06-24, rule 12d2-2(a)(3), merger with Berkshire Hathaway effective ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000087666113000400/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2013-06-24 and again 2013-08-12 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513269041/d559453d1512b.htm)).
- NYSE re-filed the notice as 25-NSE (0000876661-13-000542) and 25-NSE/A (0000876661-13-000540) on 2013-07-18, identical text ([SEC 25-NSE/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000087666113000540/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Heinz kept filing (8-K 2013-10-21 item 5.03) because it still had public debt, which is why the continued-filings rule fired.
