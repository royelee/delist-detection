## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- exit kind: filing, NYSE Form 25-NSE EX-99.25 (0000876661-07-000794): merger with FirstGroup Acquisition Corporation effective before the opening on 2007-10-01.
- successor: filing, same notice; shares converted to cash, no successor line.
- last trade date: filing, same notice: suspended from trading on 2007-10-01, so the last trade was the prior trading day, 2007-09-28 (a Friday). The 8-K of 2007-09-28 reports HSR early termination that day, allowing the close.
- payout rule: filing, same notice: "converted into $35.25 in cash per share".
- terms: filing, same notice: 35.25 cash, currency USD (US issuer, US notice).

## 5. What happened

- [SEC 8-K 2007-09-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/737874/000129993307005654/htm_22870.htm): agreement with eleven state attorneys general on the FirstGroup acquisition.
- [SEC 8-K 2007-09-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/737874/000129993307005684/htm_22899.htm): DOJ granted early termination of the HSR waiting period, permitting the close.
- [SEC 25-NSE 2007-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/737874/000087666107000794/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml): merger effective before the open 2007-10-01, $35.25 cash per share, suspended 2007-10-01, removal at the open 2007-10-11.
- [SEC 15-12G 2007-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/737874/000095010307002410/dp07080_15-12g.htm): deregistration the same day.
