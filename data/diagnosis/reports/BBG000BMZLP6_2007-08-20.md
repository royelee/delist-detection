## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2007-08-13, items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01: merger closed, change in control).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: aQuantive became a wholly owned subsidiary of Microsoft; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out, not read. The 8-K gives the closing and the Nasdaq removal notice as 2007-08-10, the
  Form 25-NSE is dated 2007-08-10, and fails rows under a deleted symbol start 2007-08-10 (settlement of earlier
  trades). No filing states the last trading day.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: converted into the right to receive $66.50 in cash).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K, $66.50 cash; USD is implied by the US deal, the filing says "$").
- Not backed: the last trade day. An exchange print (MIDAS does not cover 2007) or Nasdaq's trading-halt or
  delisting notice stating the last day would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement dated 2007-05-17 among aQuantive, Microsoft and Arrow Acquisition Company
  ([SEC 8-K 2007-08-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1071806/000089102007000238/v32899e8vk.htm)).
- Closing on 2007-08-10; each aQuantive share converted into $66.50 cash; aQuantive became a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Microsoft (same 8-K, Items 2.01 and 5.01).
- 2007-08-10: aQuantive notified the Nasdaq Global Market of its intent to remove the stock (same 8-K, Item 3.01);
  Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE the same day
  ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1071806/000135445707000241/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12G filed 2007-08-13
  ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1071806/000089102007000237/v32870e15v12g.htm)).
- Fails rows (CUSIP 03839G105): under AQNT through 2007-07-11 (last $65.78); under a deleted symbol 2007-08-10 to
  2007-08-14 (fails rows, `sec.py fails`).
- No filings by the issuer after 2007-08-20 other than the EFFECT notice; the observations of 2008-2009 in the
  caller's snapshots are a stale ticker list.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: each share became cash from another company; a merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The Form 25 was found (filed 2007-08-10, effective 2007-08-20) and classified as a merger (CRSP 231) from the 8-K,
but no exchange print gave the last trade (`last_trade_date_source` blank, `dlret_method=needs_last_trade`, flags
`no_last_trade_date`, `no_last_close`), so the contract holds no date and no return. The ending date and history
end are the Form 25 effective date (2007-08-20) rather than the closing day. The security is also uncertain because
a 2008-01-16 observation lies outside its history (`seeds_outside_history`). The cash currency is blank. Cause tag:
`no_last_trade_print`.

## 9. Verification

- **ticker_history (library: AQNT 2004-08-24 .. 2007-08-20): upheld, with a caveat on the exact end.**
  - The 2007-08-20 end is wrong. The merger closed on 2007-08-10: every share was converted into $66.50 cash and
    aQuantive notified Nasdaq the same day ([8-K, Items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1071806/000089102007000238/v32899e8vk.htm)).
    The Form 25-NSE was filed 2007-08-10 (0001354457-07-000241, `sec.py filings`). 2007-08-20 is only the Form 25
    effective date (filing + 10 days), so no AQNT share traded on it. The history end came from the Form 25 date
    because no exchange print gave the last trade.
  - Caveat: the report's 2007-08-10 is the closing day, not a verified last trading day. The 8-K of 2007-08-09
    (0000950134-07-017496) says only that shareholders approved the merger and that closing was expected "within two
    business days". The fails rows under a deleted symbol start 2007-08-10 at $66.39 (`sec.py fails --cusip
    03839G105`); fails rows are dated one day after the trade they price, so that row suggests a close on
    2007-08-09, and a trade on 2007-08-10 itself is not shown. The true last trading day is 2007-08-09 or
    2007-08-10. Either way it is no later than 2007-08-10, so the 2007-08-20 end is wrong.
  - Rule applied: reference.md, `last_trade_date` row: never after the Form 25 effective date and published only
    from an exchange print; the library's own end date is a guess, as the report says.
- **last_trade_date (report: missing, expected 2007-08-10): upheld as "missing", with the date itself uncertain.**
  Same caveat: no source states the last trading day; 2007-08-10 is an upper bound and 2007-08-09 is possible.
  The report's own confidence of `inferred` is right.
- **terms (report: missing currency): upheld.** The 8-K says "$66.50 in cash"; the library's `cash_per_share` is
  66.500000 and `cash_currency` is blank (source.csv). USD is inferred from "$" and a US deal; reference.md says to
  give the currency the filing states.
- Not re-checked (the report calls them "agree"): exit_kind, successor, issuer, value_rule. The 8-K text supports
  merger, cash and no successor.
- Minor: Sections 4 and 5 call the 8-K "2007-08-13"; the document itself is dated 2007-08-10. This changes no field.
