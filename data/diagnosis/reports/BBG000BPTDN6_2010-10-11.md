- Successor: filing (same notice and DEFM14A: shares converted to cash, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25 notice: effective before the open on 2010-10-01, suspended 2010-10-01, so the last trade is 2010-09-30).

## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement dated 2010-07-15 among NBTY, Alphabet Holding Company and Alphabet Merger Sub; stockholders approved it on 2010-09-22 ([SEC 8-K 2010-09-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/70793/000119312510214841/d8k.htm)).
- Holders receive $55.00 in cash per share if the merger completes ([SEC DEFM14A 2010-08-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/70793/000119312510195297/ddefm14a.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2010-10-01: the merger became effective before the open on 2010-10-01, each share converted into $55.00 cash, the security was suspended from trading on 2010-10-01 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/70793/000087666110000366/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2010-10-12 ([SEC Form 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/70793/000110465910051992/a10-19258_11512b.htm)). NBTY filed an S-4 in 2011-03 (exchange offer for its notes), which is probably what made the filings continue.

Cause tag: `continued_filings_guess`.
