## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The LLM extractor read the DEFM14A headline "$41.18 per share" (total proceeds of the two asset sales) as the merger consideration. Its gate failed against the 4.09 close (`llm_gate_failed`), so the value fell to `assumed_par`. The consideration actually paid at the 2022 closing is $4.11. Cause: `terms_misread`. Secondary: the issuer after 2021-09-22 is CIK 1845290, but the library holds 1289790 throughout.

## 9. Verification
- **terms: upheld.** The 8-K of 2022-07-21 (accession 0001104659-22-081423, CIK 1845290), Item 2.01, says each common unit "was canceled ... and converted into the right to receive $4.11 in cash without interest"; its Exhibit 99.1 repeats "$4.11 per unit in cash". There is no stock leg. The library's 41.18 is the DEFM14A's two-sale proceeds headline, not the closing consideration (the $52.50 total the press release cites is cumulative proceeds, also not the 2022 payout). Rule applied: cash, USD, `4.11 / last_close - 1`. Matches the report.
- **issuer: upheld.** The 8-K12B of 2021-09-22 (0001104659-21-118160, CIK 1845290) states the Common Units trade from 2021-09-23 "on an uninterrupted basis under the trading symbol MIC and with CUSIP 55608B105", with a one-for-one conversion, so the post-2021-09-22 issuer is Macquarie Infrastructure Holdings, LLC (CIK 1845290). `output/contract/security_history.csv` has a single row for BBG000M34GG1 with issuer 1289790 for 2008-03-04 to 2022-07-20, so the library does not split at the change.
- Minor inconsistency, not a field check: the report cites Form 25-NSE 0000876661-22-000576, while the library's row uses 0000876661-22-000571. This does not affect the verdict.
