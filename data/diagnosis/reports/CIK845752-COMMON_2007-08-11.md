## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement with Jaguar Acquisition Sub Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of BAE Systems, Inc.; holders receive $88.00 cash per share ([SEC DEFM14A 2007-06-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/845752/000095013607004457/file1.htm)).
- Stockholders approved on 2007-07-25 (71.0% of shares outstanding); closing expected about 2007-07-31 ([SEC 8-K 2007-07-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/845752/000095013607005115/file1.htm)).
- The merger became effective 2007-07-31; each share converted to $88.00 cash; the NYSE suspended trading 2007-08-01 ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2007-08-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/845752/000087666107000664/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2007-07-31 ([SEC Form 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/845752/000095015707000963/form15-12b.htm)).
- The security's own history: CUSIP 042260109, AH on NYSE 2004-08-05 to 2007-07-31 (library tables).
- The seeds for AH dated 2008-01-16 through 2009-01-23 (name "ARMOR HOLDINGS INC") come from the caller's observations; no filing supports trading after 2007-07-31 (observation_map status after_delisting).
