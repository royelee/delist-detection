## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2016-07-12, items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01: the Merger closed July 12, 2016; Hatteras merged into Annaly's subsidiary).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: shares converted into cash/Annaly stock, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: suspended from trading on July 12, 2016, so the last trade was July 11).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2.01: Mixed Consideration, All-Cash, All-Stock elections).
- Terms: filing (8-K 2.01: $5.55 cash and 0.9894 Annaly shares; dollars).

## 5. What happened

- Annaly's tender offer completed and the merger closed July 12, 2016; each share was exchanged for $5.55 + 0.9894 NLY, or $15.85 cash, or 1.5226 NLY, subject to proration ([SEC 8-K 2016-07-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1419521/000119312516646386/d225444d8k.htm)).
- The 8-K Item 3.01 says the NYSE was asked to file a Form 25 for the common and preferred shares (same 8-K).
- The NYSE's Form 25-NSE, filed 2016-07-12, states the security was suspended from trading on July 12, 2016 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1419521/000087666116001088/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Hatteras preferred shares became Annaly Series E preferred one for one (not this security; same 8-K).
