# CAL (Continental Airlines) 2010-10-14

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger: each Continental share became 1.05 shares of UAL Corporation, which renamed itself United Continental Holdings at the closing on 2010-10-01. The library's kind, date and ratio are right, but it prices the stock leg under the wrong ticker. It publishes UAUA for 2010-10-01, and UAUA stopped trading on Nasdaq at the close of 2010-09-30. From 2010-10-01 the acquirer's stock traded on NYSE as UAL (BBG000M65M61). Because UAUA had no price that day, the gate failed and the row took assumed par.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified. All five key fields rest on SEC filings.
- Exit kind: filing (CAL 8-K 2010-10-01 items 3.01, 3.03 and 5.01; UAL 8-K 2010-10-01 item 2.01: Continental became a wholly owned subsidiary of UAL).
- Successor: filing (the same 8-Ks: holders received another issuer's stock, so there is no continuing CAL line).
- Last trade date: filing (UAL 8-K item 2.01 calls September 30, 2010 "the last trading day before the closing of the Merger"; the Form 25-NSE notice says trading was suspended on 2010-10-01).
- Payout rule: filing (CAL 8-K item 3.03 and UAL 8-K item 2.01: 1.05 shares of UAL Common Stock, fractions in cash).
- Terms: filing. The ratio 1.05 is in both 8-Ks. The acquirer is UAL Corporation, CIK 100517, renamed United Continental Holdings at the closing (UAL 8-K introductory note). The 8-K's item 3.01 also states its symbol on the price date: NYSE "UAL" from 2010-10-01. The price security BBG000M65M61 is a look-up in the library's own tables: securities.csv lists it for issuer 100517, common, and its CUSIP 910047109 and ticker UAL both start on 2010-10-01.

The first diagnosis marked this inferred because the price ticker rested on fails rows only. The UAL 8-K now names the symbol, and it shows the library's UAUA is wrong for the price date.

## 5. What happened

- The merger completed. Each share of Continental Common Stock was converted into 1.05 UAL shares, with fractions paid in cash ([CAL 8-K 2010-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/319687/000119312510222187/d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE: the merger became effective before the opening on 2010-10-01, each share was converted into 1.05 United Continental Holdings (successor to UAL Corporation) shares, trading was suspended on 2010-10-01, and the shares were removed from listing at the open on 2010-10-14 ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2010-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/319687/000087666110000370/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- UAL's own 8-K of the same day ([UAL 8-K 2010-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100517/000119312510222185/d8k.htm)):
  - "UAL changed its name from UAL Corporation to United Continental Holdings, Inc."
  - UAL Common Stock closed at $23.66 on NASDAQ on September 30, 2010, "the last trading day before the closing of the Merger".
  - Item 3.01: UAL told NASDAQ it would voluntarily delist and "transfer the listing of UAL Common Stock to the NYSE beginning on October 1, 2010". It filed a Form 25 on 2010-09-30 to end Nasdaq trading when NYSE trading began, and "UAL Common Stock commenced trading on the NYSE under the ticker symbol 'UAL' on October 1, 2010".
- UAL's Nasdaq Form 25 ([0000950157-10-001887](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100517/000095015710001887/form25.htm)) and its NYSE 8-A12B ([0000950157-10-001888](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100517/000095015710001888/form8a12b.htm)) were both filed on 2010-09-30.
- EDGAR lists CIK 100517 as UAL CORP /DE/ until 2010-09-30 and as United Continental Holdings after that.
- The LLM read the terms from filing 0001193125-10-191980: stock deal, ratio 1.05, acquirer "UAL Corporation", ticker "UAUA", confidence high (cache/llm).
- SEC fails data (a row dated D carries the close of the trading day before D):
  - The old CUSIP 902549807 trades under UAUA. Its trading rows run to 2010-09-30 ($23.41, the 09-29 close). After a gap, settlement rows run from 2010-10-08 to 2010-10-15.
  - The new CUSIP 910047109 trades under UAL from 2010-10-01. Its 2010-10-01 row shows $0.01, because the CUSIP had no prior close. The 2010-10-04 row carries the 2010-10-01 close, $24.70.

Replaced: old share exchanged for another company's stock, a merger.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The kind, date and ratio are right. The price security is wrong.
- The LLM read the acquirer ticker as UAUA. That was UAL Corp's Nasdaq symbol, and it was right only until the close of 2010-09-30.
- The gate prices the acquirer by that ticker on the trading day after the last trade (2010-10-01). UAUA has no fails row on that day, so the gate failed (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_price`) and the row took assumed par. The flag is `merger_at_par`, and the uncertain reason is `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`.
- `_add_acquirers` only reads terms that passed the gate, so no acquirer lookup ran. If one had, `find_acquirer` would have mapped the old CUSIP 902549807 through OpenFIGI, and OpenFIGI did not resolve it.
- The run holds UAL Corp as the placeholder CIK100517-COMMON (UAUA, CUSIP 902549807). It is not linked to BBG000M65M61, although the 8-K says the same stock moved its listing. The placeholder also carries `ended_without_delisting` and `form25_unmatched` (ambiguous class) on its Nasdaq Form 25.
- The contract therefore publishes price_ticker UAUA and a blank price_sec_id.
- `resolved_from_continued_filings` is a separate label. It fires because the Form 25 is dated 13 days after the merger.

Cause tag: `other:acquirer_price_security_null` (stale acquirer ticker UAUA; the acquirer traded as UAL on the price date).

## 8. Fix and open checks

The fix has four steps. For CAL each step is backed by the evidence above.

1. **Link an issuer's lines across a ticker and CUSIP change (security master).** CIK100517-COMMON (UAUA, CUSIP 902549807, to 2010-09-30) and BBG000M65M61 (UAL, CUSIP 910047109, from 2010-10-01) are one stock, for three reasons:
   - The 8-K says the listing of the same UAL Common Stock moved from Nasdaq to NYSE, and the company renamed itself.
   - EDGAR's name for CIK 100517 changes on 2010-09-30.
   - No bankruptcy filing falls in the window.

   Link them as a continuation. UAUA's Nasdaq Form 25 (0000950157-10-001887) becomes an exchange continuation with successor BBG000M65M61. That clears the placeholder's `ended_without_delisting` and `form25_unmatched` rows, and clips its observed ticker_history (now to 2010-10-15) at the transfer.

   The general rule must not link a bankruptcy emergence. UAL's own 2006 emergence had the same CIK and new shares, and the old shares were cancelled. Require all of these:
   - the same CIK and class;
   - the old line stops and the new one starts within days;
   - the issuer renamed at the switch, or a filing states a transfer of the same stock;
   - no bankruptcy (8-K 1.03) in the window.
2. **Find the acquirer for every published stock leg, including terms that failed the gate.** For CAL, `resolve("UAUA", 2010-09-30, name="UAL Corporation")` should return CIK 100517. Accept it because the LLM's acquirer name "UAL Corporation" matches the EDGAR former name UAL CORP /DE/, and the CIK is not CAL's own (319687). Then take CIK 100517's common line on 2010-10-01. After step 1 the only one is BBG000M65M61.
3. **Publish and price by the security (contract and gate).**
   - Publish price_sec_id BBG000M65M61, with price_ticker UAL, its ticker_history value on 2010-10-01.
   - The gate prices CUSIP 910047109's close on 2010-10-01: $24.70, from the fails row dated 2010-10-04. Skip the 2010-10-01 row's $0.01.
   - Then 1.05 × $24.70 = $25.94 against the last close of $24.53, a 5.7% gap inside the 15% tolerance. The gate passes, the return is measured, and `merger_at_par` and `assumed_par_after_failed_gate` go away.
   - This needs the acquirer lookup to run before the gate.
   - The new test passes here: (UAL, 2010-10-01) is in BBG000M65M61's ticker_history.
4. **Skill rule.** The rule change isn't needed for this case, because the 8-K names the symbol. Add one check to the diagnosis instead: the price ticker must match the acquirer's ticker_history on the price date. The first diagnosis accepted UAUA as "agree (ticker unverified)".

Open:
- Even after steps 1–3, CAL stays in uncertain.csv because of `resolved_from_continued_filings`, which none of the four steps touches.
- No skeptic pass has checked the `terms` finding yet. This record now says the library is wrong.
- Golden case: yes. It is a clean stock-only merger whose acquirer changed ticker, CUSIP and exchange on the price date.
