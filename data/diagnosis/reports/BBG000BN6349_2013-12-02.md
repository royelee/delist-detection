## 1. Verdict

There was no ending on 2013-12-02: that day First Marblehead did a 1-for-10 reverse split, the CUSIP changed and the stock kept trading as FMD on the NYSE. ## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The row's reason is "Listing deficiency notice (8-K 2014-01-16), no merger evidence; the registrant kept filing after it", flags `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`. The ending was anchored on the end of the FTD CUSIP 320771108 (2013-12-02), an old CUSIP that disappeared in a reverse split, and the 8-K 3.01 of 2014-01-16 was read as a deficiency (it reports regained compliance). The 2016 Form 25 for the same security was never used. Cause tag: `continued_filings_guess` (a split's CUSIP change read as an ending; the real ending is later).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check from the filings (sec.py grep/fails). All four fields upheld.

- **exit_kind (upheld).** 8-K 0001193125-13-459228: on 2013-12-02 a Certificate of Amendment effected a 1-for-10 reverse split; every ten shares were "reclassified and combined into one share of New Common Stock", same par value, same issuer. Not an ending. Fails rows: CUSIP 320771207 trades as FMD from 2013-12-04 ($7.49), 2013-12-03 under the interim symbol FMDZZZZ ($1.00), continuing through 2016-08-24 (last $5.05). The 2016 ending is real: 8-K 0001193125-16-687599 item 2.01 (merger consummated 2016-08-22, each share converted to cash), item 3.01 (suspended before the open on 2016-08-23, Form 25 requested).
- **last_trade_date (upheld).** 2016-08-22 follows from item 3.01 (trading suspended before the open on 2016-08-23). The fails rows ending 2016-08-24 are settlement lag and do not contradict it. 2013-12-02 is only the old CUSIP's last row.
- **value_rule (upheld).** Item 2.01 and 3.03: each share "automatically converted into the right to receive $5.05 in cash, without interest"; no stock leg. Rule cash, cash_per_share 5.05, USD.
- **ticker_history (upheld).** output/ticker_history.csv stops FMD at 2013-12-02 and cusip_history holds only 320771108 (source ftd, to 2013-12-02); FMD under 320771207 traded to 2016-08-22, so the range should extend.

Residual uncertainty: none material. The Form 25-NSE of 2016-08-23 was not re-opened here; the 8-K already settles the facts.
