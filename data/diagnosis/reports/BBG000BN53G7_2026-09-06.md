Inferred, because the last trade date is not a stated exchange print.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2026-08-26 items 2.01/3.01/5.01; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (8-K: Merger Sub merged into LEG, which survives as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; holders get Parent shares).
- Last trade date: fails rows plus worked out from the filed closing date 2026-08-26 (the Form 25 notice gives the conversion date, not a suspension date).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: 0.1455 Parent shares, cash only in lieu of fractions).
- Terms: filing for the ratio; SGI ticker from SGI's 425 cover page (NYSE: SGI).
- Not backed: the last exchange day. An NYSE/MIDAS print or a filing stating "last day of trading" would make it verified.
