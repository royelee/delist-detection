# IAC (CIK891103-COMMON), case CIK891103-COMMON_2014-06-30

## 1. Verdict

The 2014-06-30 ending is not a real event: it is the last observation date (the caller's data stops there). The company kept filing and the stock kept trading. The real ending is 2020-06-30, when Old IAC common stock was delisted from Nasdaq in the IAC/Match separation: each Old IAC share became one New IAC share plus 2.1584 shares of Old IAC Class M (renamed Match Group, MTCH). The library's row (exchange_transfer, 304, date 2014-06-30, no value) is wrong in date, kind and value.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2020-07-02, Items 2.01 and 3.01: Old IAC common withdrawn from Nasdaq; Form 25).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K names New IAC common and Old IAC Class M).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K Item 3.01: Form 25 filed 2020-06-30, trading suspended before the open on 2020-07-01).

- Terms: filing (8-K Item 2.01: one New IAC share and 2.1584 Class M shares per Old IAC share).

## 5. What happened

- 2014-2019: IAC filed ordinary 8-Ks and 10-Ks; no Form 25 or Form 15 in 2014-2015 (checked by sec.py filings 2014-01..2015-12). No fails rows under CUSIP 44919P300 after 2014-06.
- 2020-06-30: Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE for Old IAC common ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000135445720000290/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Old IAC holders received, per share, one New IAC share and 2.1584 Old IAC Class M shares; Old IAC was renamed Match Group; trading suspended before the open on 2020-07-01 ([SEC 8-K 2020-07-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000110465920080606/tm206790d25_8k.htm)).
- That 8-K also says New Match would file Form 15 for the Old IAC common.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: old shares were exchanged for shares of two companies (New IAC and Match). Not a continuation (decision 9: not one successor line, one for one). Arguably a split-up; recorded as `merger`, stock leg.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The observations stop at 2014-06-30 and the finder found no Form 25 in its window, so the continued-filings fallback (`continued_filings_rule`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`, `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`) labelled the last sighting an exchange transfer. The real Form 25 is in 2020, six years later. Cause tag: `continued_filings_guess`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Do not end the security in 2014: when the observations end and the issuer kept trading, the ending should be unknown, not an invented transfer; with a window to 2020 the Form 25 of 2020-06-30 gives the ending above.
- Add New IAC and Match as securities in the run so the successor links and prices exist.
- Check that Class M traded as MTCH from 2020-07-01 (not read from a filing here).
- Golden case: yes, but only once the caller's window covers 2020 (a two-successor stock reorganization).

## 9. Verification

Re-checked through sec.py (filings, grep) against the filings the report cites.

- **exit_kind (merger): upheld.** 8-K filed 2020-07-02 (0001104659-20-080606), Item 2.01: holders of Old IAC common stock received, for each share, one New IAC common share and 2.1584 Old IAC Class M shares. Item 3.01: Old IAC asked Nasdaq to withdraw Old IAC Common Stock. Holders got stock of two companies, so it is not a one-for-one continuation (decision 9). Caveat: the report itself calls it arguably a split-up; `merger` is the closest bucket, not a certain one.
- **last_trade_date (2020-06-30): upheld.** 25-NSE 0001354457-20-000290 filed and effective 2020-06-30 (Nasdaq, Common Stock, IAC/INTERACTIVECORP, CIK 891103). The 8-K Item 3.01 says trading was suspended before the open on 2020-07-01, so the last trade was 2020-06-30, no later than the Form 25 effective date (decision 12). No exchange print was checked; the date comes from the filing text.
- **The 2014-06-30 ending being an artifact: upheld.** 10-Qs and a 10-K were filed 2014-07-31, 2014-11-03, 2015-02-27 and 2015-05-01 under CIK 891103. No 25, 25-NSE, 15, 8-K12B or 8-K12G3 appears 2014-07-01..2019-12-31. The fails check under symbol IAC for 2014-07..2014-12 returned no rows, which is weak evidence alone (fails rows are sparse); the filings are the real support.
- **value_rule (stock): upheld with a caveat.** The terms are stock only, no cash to Old IAC common holders (Item 2.01). The caveat: the contract's `stock_ratio` holds one leg, and this deal has two (1.0 New IAC plus 2.1584 MTCH), so `stock_ratio=1.0` with `price_ticker=IAC;MTCH` stretches the schema. Both legs are verified against the 8-K text.
- **ticker_history (IAC continues to 2020-06-30): upheld.** It follows from the filings above: the Form 25 is the first removal after 2014, effective 2020-06-30. Not independently confirmed from price sightings.
- **Open check resolved:** the 8-K (0001104659-20-080606) states the company's common stock began trading as MTCH on Nasdaq on 2020-07-01. A Form 15-12B was filed 2020-07-10 (0001104659-20-082738), consistent with the Form 15 the 8-K announced.

Result: all four fields upheld; the only caveat is the single-leg schema fit for value_rule.
