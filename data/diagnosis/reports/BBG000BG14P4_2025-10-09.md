## 9. Verification

Checked by re-reading the cited filings through `sec.py` and the fails rows. Both claims survive; one detail inside `terms` (the price date) is uncertain and is flagged, not refuted.

- **value_rule (library's `otc_print` is wrong, correct rule is `stock`): upheld.** The 8-K of 2025-09-30 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895419/000119312525224251/d22768d8k.htm)) item 3.02 says all Old Common Stock was cancelled and "existing equity holders received their pro rata share of approximately 1,306,896 shares of New Common Stock"; item 5.01 says the same. The 25-NSE of 2025-09-29 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895419/000087666125000713/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)) says holders of Old (CUSIP 977852102) "will receive shares of Common Stock of the reorganized Wolfspeed, Inc. 'New' (CUSIP - 97785W106)" and that the new stock stays listed on the NYSE. Holders were paid in new listed shares, not an off-exchange print, so `stock` is right under reference.md's payout table. The old CUSIP has no OTC rows in September 2025 (last fails row 2025-09-26, $1.85).
- **terms (library's empty terms are wrong; ratio 0.008352, price_ticker WOLF, price_date 2025-09-30): upheld, with one caveat.**
  - Ratio: 1,306,896 / 156,479,390 = 0.0083518; both figures are in the 8-K. Upheld. The 871,287-share contingent distribution (8-K item 3.02) is correctly left out of the base ratio and noted as open.
  - price_ticker WOLF (new CUSIP 97785W106): upheld. Fails rows show the new CUSIP under WOLF from 20250930 (22 rows, first $22.10) and the old CUSIP 19 rows to 20250926 (last $1.85). The library's 22.10 last close is the new CUSIP's first price, as the report says.
  - price_date 2025-09-30: **uncertain.** It rests on the first fails row date, not on a filing. Reference.md defines `price_date` as the trading day after the last trade, which here is Monday 2025-09-29, the day the old stock was suspended and the plan took effect. A fails row dated D can carry D-1's close (trading_calendar convention), so the new stock may have traded on 2025-09-29. No filing states the first session. Treat 2025-09-29 vs 2025-09-30 as unresolved; the rule, ratio and ticker do not depend on it.
- **Other fields.** Not challenged. Spot check: the 25-NSE says suspended 2025-09-29, consistent with a 2025-09-26 last session.
