## 5. What happened

- Merger Agreement dated 2019-11-05; the merger completed 2020-03-27 and Aircastle became an affiliate of MM Air Limited and Marubeni Aviation Holding ([SEC 8-K 2020-03-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1362988/000114036120007240/form8k.htm)).
- The company asked the NYSE to suspend trading and file a Form 25 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2020-03-30: each share exchanged for USD 32.00 cash, suspended from trading on 2020-03-30 ([SEC 25-NSE 0000876661-20-000258](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1362988/000087666120000258/)).
- The Form 25 takes effect 10 days after filing, about 2020-04-09, which is the library's delist_date.
