## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2008-07-30: items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (8-K: shares converted to cash or CC Media Class A; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out from the Form 25 notice (shares converted 2008-07-30, removal at the open on 2008-08-11) and fails rows (last CCU row dated 2008-07-31). No filing states "last traded on D".
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 3.03 / 5.01: $36.00 cash).
- Terms: filing ($36.00 USD). The election's proration and the stock leg are not in the 8-K I read.

Not backed: the last trade date as an exchange print. An NYSE/MIDAS print (MIDAS starts 2012) or a press release stating the final trading day would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- The merger became effective 2008-07-30; each share not held by the Sponsors or dissenters was converted into $36.00 cash or one CC Media Class A share ([SEC 8-K 2008-07-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/739708/000095013408013693/d58839e8vk.htm)).
- Item 3.01: the company requested on 2008-07-18 that NYSE file a Form 25 (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2008-07-31: shares came to evidence other securities on 2008-07-30; removal at the open on 2008-08-11 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/739708/000087666108000303/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2008-08-11 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/739708/000095013408014790/d58840e15v12b.htm)).
- Fails rows under CUSIP 184502102 / CCU run 2008-01-15 .. 2008-07-31, last priced 35.98; the CUSIP 204429104 rows under CCU from 2008-09 are a different company (Cervecerias Unidas ADS), a ticker takeover (fails rows).
