## 5. What happened

- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2016-07-13, Rule 12d2-2(a)(3), removal effective 2016-07-25 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1316360/000087666116001092/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
