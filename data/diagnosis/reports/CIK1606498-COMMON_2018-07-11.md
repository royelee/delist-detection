# CIK1606498-COMMON_2018-07-11 (HYH, Halyard Health)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending. Halyard Health renamed itself Avanos Medical effective 2018-06-30 and the same shares kept trading as AVNS from 2018-07-02. The library's `exchange` ending (continued-filings fallback, CRSP 304) is a rename it failed to follow; there should be no ending row, and the ticker history should continue under AVNS.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2018-06-01, item 5.03, name change; the registrant kept filing 10-Qs as Avanos).
- Successor: fails rows (AVNS rows 2018-07-02..2018-08-24 under a new CUSIP; the issuer is the same CIK 1606498).
- Last trade date: worked out (rename effective Saturday 2018-06-30, so the last HYH day was Friday 2018-06-29; HYH fails rows end 2018-07-11 as settlement lag).
- Payout rule: filing (no exchange of shares, no consideration in the 8-K).
- Terms: none needed.
- Not backed: no filing states the first AVNS trading day or that the CUSIP changed with the name (05350V106 comes from fails rows). An NYSE notice or the company's press release (Exhibit 99.1 of the 8-K) naming the 2018-07-02 ticker switch would make it `verified`.

## 5. What happened

- Board amended the charter on 2018-05-29 to rename Halyard Health, Inc. to Avanos Medical, Inc., effective 2018-06-30 ([SEC 8-K 2018-06-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1606498/000160649818000053/hyhform8k_060118.htm)).
- Fails rows: HYH (CUSIP 40650V100) 2018-06-04..2018-07-11; AVNS (CUSIP 05350V106, AVANOS MEDICAL INC) 2018-07-02..2018-08-24 (fails data, `sec.py fails`).
- The same registrant filed its Q2 10-Q as Avanos on 2018-08-07 ([SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1606498/000160649818000068/avns2q2018form10q.htm)).
- No Form 25 was filed in the window 2018-05..2018-09 (`sec.py filings`).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same issuer, same holders, new name and ticker.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The library saw HYH stop on 2018-06-29 with no Form 25 and the issuer still filing, so the continued-filings rule (`continued_filings_rule`, flags `no_form25`, `successor_unknown`) labelled it an exchange transfer. It did not see AVNS as the same security because the CUSIP changed with the name. Cause: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Follow the rename through the 8-K 5.03 (same CIK, name change) or the AVNS fails rows; drop the ending and continue ticker_history under AVNS.
- Check that the run's FIGI for AVNS equals this security's composite FIGI (the placeholder CIK1606498-COMMON may differ).
- Golden case: yes, a rename with a CUSIP change and no Form 25.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check through sec.py (filings list 2018-05..2018-09, 8-K text, offline fails rows). Result: all three fields upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K of 2018-06-01 (0001606498-18-000053, items 5.03/8.01/9.01) says the certificate of incorporation was amended to change the name from "Halyard Health, Inc." to "Avanos Medical, Inc." effective June 30, 2018, with no exchange of shares and no consideration. The filings list for 2018-05-01..2018-09-30 holds no Form 25, Form 15, 8-K12B or 8-K12G3, and no 8-K 2.01/3.01/5.01 after 2018-05-01. (The 2018-05-01 8-K with item 2.01 is an asset sale by the registrant, not a delisting.) The same CIK 1606498 filed an 8-K (2018-08-07) and its Q2 10-Q (avns2q2018form10q.htm) as Avanos. The 2018-06-21 8-K (item 7.01) is an investor-day deck. A rename is not an ending.
- **value_rule: upheld.** `transfer` is wrong because there is no exit and no row. Per the reference (rename: not an ending, same security) the rule is `continuation`. No filing carries cash, ratio or price terms, so none are owed.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** Fails rows: HYH (CUSIP 40650V100) 2018-06-04..2018-07-11, last price $57.25. AVNS (CUSIP 05350V106, AVANOS MEDICAL INC COM) 2018-07-02..2018-08-24, first price $57.25, so the same price carries across the symbol change and the line continues. The library's history stops at 2018-06-29, too early.
- **Caveats.** The CUSIP changed (40650V100 to 05350V106); with a charter name change by the same registrant this is a rename, not an ending. No filing states the first AVNS trading day (2018-07-02 rests on fails rows), so confidence stays `inferred`.
