Cause: `holdco_not_linked`.

## 9. Verification

Re-read the 8-K of 2025-12-15 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000110465925121236/tm2533345d1_8k.htm)) through sec.py, plus `output/ticker_history.csv` and `output/securities.csv`. Result: all four fields upheld.

- **exit_kind (merger -> exchange continuation): upheld.** Item 2.01: the split-off was "a redemption ... of each outstanding share of its Liberty Live common stock ... in exchange for one share of the corresponding series of Liberty Live Group common stock ... of Liberty Live Holdings". No cash term appears in the exchange (grep for cash payment, in cash and fractional found nothing else). Exhibit 2.1 states the same one-for-one by series (LLYVK -> one New LLYVK). This is decision 9's test: same holders, same claim, one for one, no cash. The library's "Completed acquisition" reading rests only on Item 2.01 plus a 425, and the 2.01 here is the split-off, not a sale to an acquirer. Caveat: a split-off moves the business to a separate issuer, so a stricter reading could call it a spin-type event. By the reference's rule the holders keep the same claim, so continuation stands.
- **value_rule (stock -> continuation): upheld.** A continuation has no value. The library's stock rule has no price ticker or date (`terms_gate=failed`, `no_acq_ticker`) because there is no acquirer.
- **terms (ratio 1.0, no price): upheld.** The ratio 1.0 is correct as a 1:1 redemption, but as a payout it does not apply. It is a continuation ratio with no price leg and no cash, so the library's stock terms are wrong as published.
- **ticker_history (end 2025-12-25): upheld.** Item 3.01 says delisting was requested effective 2025-12-15 following the 4:05 p.m. Effective Time. The shares ceased to exist then. The library's 2025-12-25 is the Form 25 filing date plus 10 days. `output/ticker_history.csv` has BBG01YYX1Z14 LLYVK from 2025-12-17 (CIK 2078416, `securities.csv`), so the old line should end 2025-12-15, and its overlap with the new line (`ticker_overlap`) goes away.
- Residual uncertainty: the new line's first trading day is not confirmed (the run's first observation is 2025-12-17), and the Form 25's own effective date was not re-checked. Neither changes the four verdicts.
