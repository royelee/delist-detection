## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K12B 2017-10-02, Item 2.01).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K12B: BKI succeeds under Rule 12g-3; 1:1 conversion).
- Last trade date: worked out from the 8-K12B (closing 2017-09-29, BKI trading from the open 2017-10-02) and the library's MIDAS print; no filing states BKFS's last day, and no Form 25 was found.
- Payout rule: filing (no cash; 1:1 conversion).

Not backed: the last day by a filing. A Form 25 notice or exchange statement of the final BKFS session would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement of 2017-06-08 completed 2017-09-29. Class A shares and New BKH shares converted one for one into New Black Knight shares ([SEC 8-K12B 2017-10-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1627014/000110465917060272/a17-22780_18k12b.htm)).
- New Black Knight lists on the NYSE as BKI, trading from the open 2017-10-02, new CUSIP 09215C105 (same 8-K12B).
- New Black Knight is the successor issuer under Rule 12g-3(a) and files under BKFS's file number 001-37394 (same 8-K12B); a duplicate 8-K12B was filed 2017-09-29 under CIK 1704177 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1704177/000110465917060002/a17-22686_18k12b.htm)).
- The 8-K12B text has no cash consideration to Class A holders.
- Fails/cusip history: CUSIP 09214X100 ends 2017-09-29; 09215C105 starts 2017-10-02 (library tables).
