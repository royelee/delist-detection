## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (25-NSE EX-99.25 notice: merger effective 2016-07-01, shares converted into cash; 8-K of 2016-07-01 items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01).
- Successor: filing (same notice: shares converted into $66 cash, no substituted security).

- Payout rule: filing (EX-99.25: "converted into $66 cash").
- Terms: filing (same sentence).

## 5. What happened

- The merger between AGL Resources and AMS Corp., a wholly owned subsidiary of The Southern Company, became effective 2016-07-01; each AGL share was converted into $66 cash; the NYSE suspended the security on 2016-07-01
  ([SEC 25-NSE 2016-07-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1004155/000087666116001060/)).
- The same day AGL filed an 8-K with items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01, 5.02, 5.03, 8.01
  ([SEC 8-K 2016-07-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1004155/000095015716002019/form8k.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the shares became cash, a merger, not a continuation. AGL (renamed Southern Company Gas) kept filing for its debt, which is why the continued-filings rule fired.

It did not, beyond the blank currency. The row is uncertain because the end-of-era resolver relabelled a
continued-filings ending from the 5.01 8-K (`resolved_from_continued_filings`), though a Form 25-NSE exists. Cause tag: `library_right_but_uncertain`.
