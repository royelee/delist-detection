# TERP (TerraForm Power, BBG006KY8KV2): ending dated 2017-10-17

## 1. Verdict

The 2017-10-17 ending is not an ending. Brookfield bought SunEdison's 51% stake; the public Class A shares kept
trading on Nasdaq as TERP (CUSIP 88104R100 became 88104R209). The real ending is 2020-07-30: Brookfield Renewable
acquired the public shares for BEPC shares or BEP units at 0.47625 each. The library's row is wrong.

## 5. What happened

- 2017-10-16/17: Brookfield Holdco merged into TERP and held 51% of Class A; SunEdison's Class B went away. "The Company's Class A Shares will continue to be traded on the Nasdaq Stock Market under the symbol TERP"; CUSIP changed to 88104R209 ([SEC 8-K 2017-10-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599947/000156761917002200/s001920x1_8k.htm)).
- TERP kept filing 10-Ks for 2017, 2018, 2019 ([SEC 10-K 2020-03-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599947/000159994720000012/terp-20191231.htm)); fails rows show TERP under CUSIP 88104R209 from 2020-07-02 to 2020-08-05 (fails).
- 2020-07-31: the reorganization with BEP and BEPC closed; TERP stopped trading before the open on 2020-07-31; public holders got BEPC Exchangeable Shares or BEP Units at 0.47625 ([SEC 8-K 2020-07-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599947/000114036120017178/brhc10013911_8k.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2020-07-31 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599947/000135445720000370/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15-12B on 2020-08-11.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

2017: kept the same security (change of control of the majority holder, same Class A shares, a new CUSIP; no
exchange of public shares). 2020: replaced by another company's stock, a merger.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Its reason: "Change in control (8-K item 5.01 filed 2017-10-17); the registrant kept filing after it", flagged
`no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `resolved_from_continued_filings`. The end-of-era resolver's branch 3 (a 5.01
change in control read as a merger) fired although the stock kept trading, and the real Form 25 of 2020-07-31 was
never used. The LLM then read the Brookfield stake purchase (9.52 cash, 1 BAM) as deal terms and failed its gate.
Cause tag: `continued_filings_guess`.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check against the filings (sec.py). All five disputed fields upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The [8-K of 2017-10-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599947/000156761917002200/s001920x1_8k.htm) (items 1.01, 2.01, 3.03, 5.01, 5.02, 5.03, 8.01) says Merger Sub merged into TERP, TERP survived, Brookfield Holdco then held 51% of Class A, and "The Company's Class A Shares will continue to be traded on the Nasdaq Stock Market under the symbol TERP", CUSIP changed to 88104R209. The Sep-Dec 2017 filing list shows no Form 25 and no item 3.01. Public shares were not exchanged, so 2017 is not an ending (decision table: kept the same security). The real ending is the [8-K of 2020-07-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599947/000114036120017178/brhc10013911_8k.htm): each Public TERP Share exchanged for BEPC Exchangeable Shares or BEP Units; Item 3.01 suspension and removal; Form 15-12B filed 2020-08-11. Fails rows for CUSIP 88104R209 run 2020-06-01..2020-08-05, so it traded until 2020.
- **last_trade_date: upheld.** Item 3.01: "The TERP Common Stock ceased being traded prior to the opening of the market on July 31, 2020", so the last trade day is 2020-07-30 (an 8-K 3.01 exchange-print source, decision 12). The library's blank/2017 guess has no support.
- **value_rule: upheld.** Item 2.01 gives no cash leg: consideration is BEPC Exchangeable Shares or BEP Units only, so `stock`, not `cash_plus_stock`. The 9.52 cash + 1 BAM belongs to the 2017 Brookfield/SunEdison stake purchase, not to public holders.
- **terms: upheld.** Item 2.01: "The Adjusted Exchange Ratio is equal to 0.47625", the product of 0.381 and (0.25 + ...), same ratio for shares or units at the holder's election. Price security BEPC and price date 2020-07-31 (day after the last trade) follow the rule. Caveats: the election has no stated default, and BEPC's sec_id is not in the run; neither changes the verdict.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** Follows from the above: TERP traded under CUSIP 88104R209 until 2020-07-30, so a range ending 2017-10-17 is wrong.

Refuted: none. Uncertain: the Form 25-NSE of 2020-07-31 cited in section 5 did not appear in the August 2020 filing list I pulled (the 8-K Item 3.01 and the Form 15-12B suffice).
