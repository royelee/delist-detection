## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2007-08-24 items 3.01/5.01: merger completed, shares converted to cash).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: all shares cancelled for cash, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE EX-99.25: suspended from trading 2007-08-27, Monday; last trading day is Friday 2007-08-24, the merger date).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 5.01: cash).
- Terms: filing (8-K item 5.01 and the EX-99.25 notice: $42.00 per share in cash).

## 5. What happened

- National Grid completed its merger with KeySpan on 2007-08-24; each share was converted into $42.00 cash, and KeySpan ceased to be publicly traded ([SEC 8-K 2007-08-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1062379/000106237907000033/ks8k-merger82407.txt)).
- KeySpan filed Form 15-12B the same day ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1062379/000106237907000042/ksform15-82407.txt)).
- The NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-08-28, removal effective at the open 2007-09-07 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3), shares came to evidence only the right to $42.00 cash; trading suspended 2007-08-27 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1062379/000087666107000739/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Output tables: one CUSIP 49337W100, ticker KSE 2004-11-15 to 2007-08-24; observation_map has KSE seeds on 2008-01-16 through 2009-01-23, all `after_delisting`.

The row's `observed_after_delisting` flag is the same fact. Cause tag: `library_right_but_uncertain`.
