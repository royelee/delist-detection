# GRUB (Grubhub Inc., BBG001KWG293), delisting 2021-06-25

## 1. Verdict

A real stock merger: each Grubhub share became 3.355 Just Eat Takeaway.com (JET) American Depositary Shares (cash only for fractions). The library's exit kind, ratio and price date are right. Its row is uncertain because the acquirer's price security is `NULL` (JET's ADSs trade OTC and the run has no ticker for them), so the payout gate failed and the value fell back to assumed par. The last-trade date (06-15 vs 06-14) is also not settled by the filings.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2021-06-15 item 2.01, Form 25-NSE notice).
- Successor: filing (no successor line; holders receive JET ADSs).
- Last trade date: MIDAS (library) and fails rows; the 8-K says only "suspended on June 15, 2021", which does not say whether the last print was 06-14 or 06-15.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 3.01/2.01: exchange for ADSs).
- Terms: filing for 3.355 (8-K; the DEFM14A gives 0.6710 ADSs at one ADS per ordinary share, restated to one-fifth at closing); the ADS's price symbol rests on fails rows only.
- Not backed: the last trading day and the ADS's trading symbol. An exchange print, an NYSE notice stating the last day, or JET's closing press release naming the ADS ticker would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- The Mergers closed on 2021-06-15 and each share became 3.355 JET ADSs, each ADS one-fifth of an ordinary share ([SEC 8-K 2021-06-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594109/000119312521190684/d155950d8k.htm)).
- NYSE trading in GRUB was suspended on June 15, 2021, per the same 8-K item 3.01.
- The merger agreement fixed 0.6710 ADSs per share at one ADS per ordinary share ([SEC DEFM14A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594109/000114036121016935/nt10016123x20_defm14a.htm)). 0.6710 ordinary shares equals 3.355 one-fifth ADSs, so the two agree.
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2021-06-15: the shares came to evidence other securities in substitution ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594109/000087666121000886/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15-12B filed 2021-06-25 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594109/000095015721000691/form15-12b.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 400110102 last row 2021-06-15 at $59.89, one GRUBXXXX row 2021-06-16; JET ADS CUSIP 48214T305 has rows from 2021-06-16 (symbol GRUB; JETKD earlier in June) (fails files, offline).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Mostly it did not: the type and ratio are right. Its acquirer lookup returned `NULL` as the price ticker (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_price`), so the value became assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`); the `last_trade_date_conflict` flag is the 8-K's "suspended June 15" against MIDAS. Cause tag: other:acquirer_price_security_null (JET's ADSs are OTC; no ticker or sec_id was found).
