A real cash merger, not an exchange transfer: TXU Corp (now Energy Future Holdings) was bought by the KKR/TPG-led Texas Energy Future Holdings LP, and each common share became $69.25 cash on 2007-10-10. ## 5. What happened

- Form 25-NSE filed by the Chicago Stock Exchange 2007-10-12 for CIK 1023291 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1023291/000087688207000010/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the five fields the report calls wrong. All five upheld; one is now better supported.

- **exit_kind (exchange -> merger): upheld.** The 8-K filed 2007-10-11 (0000950134-07-021173, Item 3.01) states that on October 10, 2007 each common share "was converted into the right to receive $69.25, without interest" and that NYSE and CSE were asked to file Form 25. Item 5.01 records the change in control. The NYSE's own 25-NSE (0000876661-07-000841, filed 2007-10-23, Rule 12d2-2(a)(3)) says the shares came to evidence only a right to cash. That is a merger, not a transfer. The only later 10-Ks (2008-03-31, 2009-03-03) are the issuer filing on its public debt; nothing shows the common stock moving to another venue.
- **last_trade_date (blank -> 2007-10-10): upheld, and stronger than the report says.** The report cites only the CSE 25-NSE (2007-10-12) and the 8-K. The NYSE 25-NSE EX-99.25 (2007-10-23) also says the merger "became effective on October 10, 2007" and that the security "was suspended from trading on October 11, 2007". By the reference rule ("suspended before the open on D" means the last trade was the previous trading day), the last trade is 2007-10-10 (a Wednesday). That is an exchange-notice source the contract accepts, so the report's open check is answered and confidence for this field could be `verified`. Fails rows for CUSIP 873168108 end 2007-10-11 (58 rows, last price $69.24), consistent with settlement lag. The date is not after the Form 25 effective date (2007-10-23).
- **value_rule (transfer -> cash): upheld.** The 8-K and the NYSE notice both give cash only, $69.25 per share, no stock leg.
- **terms (none -> 69.25 USD cash): upheld.** The same $69.25 appears in the 8-K Item 3.01 and the NYSE 25-NSE ("converted into $69.25"). Currency USD is implied. No stock ratio or acquirer security exists since the acquirer is a private partnership.
- **ticker_history (TXU 2008-01-16..2009-06-08 -> should end 2007-10-10): upheld.** `output/ticker_history.csv` has one row for BBG000BVW841, source `observation`, 2008-01-16 to 2009-06-08, all after the merger. The stock stopped trading 2007-10-10 and NYSE removal was set for 2007-11-02, so the observations are stale and the range is wrong.
- **Not re-checked (agree rows):** drop_reason, continuation, successor, issuer. The 8-K supports them (cash paid, private acquirer, same CIK 1023291).

Net: the diagnosis stands.
