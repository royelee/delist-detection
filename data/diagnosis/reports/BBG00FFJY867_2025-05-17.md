## 5. What happened

- Plan of arrangement effective 2025-05-06; Old Lionsgate renamed Starz Entertainment Corp.; New Lionsgate became Lionsgate Studios Corp. ([SEC 8-K 2025-05-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/929351/000119312525114510/d864362d8k.htm)).
- Initial exchange: each LGEC Class B share for one New Lionsgate Class B share plus one Class C preferred share; second exchange: each Class C preferred share for one New Lionsgate Common Share and one Starz Common Share ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/929351/000119312525114510/d864362d8k.htm)).
- Following the second exchange the Starz Common Shares were consolidated 15 to 1 ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/929351/000119312525114510/d864362d8k.htm)).
- After the transactions LION trades on the NYSE and STRZ on Nasdaq ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/929351/000119312525114510/d864362d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2025-05-07: shares came to evidence other securities; suspended from trading May 07, 2025; removal at the open on May 19, 2025 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/929351/000087666125000296/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check, re-reading the cited filings through sec.py.

- **value_rule (library `unknown`/assumed par is wrong; right answer `stock`, two legs): UPHELD.** The 8-K of 2025-05-07 (0001193125-25-114510) says each LGEC Class B share became one New Lionsgate Class B share plus one Class C preferred share (Initial Share Exchange). Each such Class B share plus Class C preferred share then became one New Lionsgate Common Share and one Starz Common Share (Second Share Exchange). "Following the Second Share Exchange, the Starz Common Shares were consolidated on a 15-to-1 basis". So one LGFB share is 1 LION + 1/15 STRZ, with no cash. The NYSE 25-NSE (0000876661-25-000296) agrees: plan effective May 6, 2025, and the Class B line converts to one Lionsgate Studios and one Starz share (its Starz count is before the consolidation, which the 8-K supplies, so no conflict). Replacement by two lines, one a different issuer, is a merger, not a continuation (decision 9). The library's assumed par is a fill with no payout rule.
- **terms (missing; 1 LION + 0.0666667 STRZ): UPHELD.** The ratios match the 8-K. The price date 2025-05-07 is an assumption the report already lists as an open check; I did not confirm the first trading session of LION and STRZ.
- **last_trade_date 2025-05-06: UPHELD.** The 25-NSE says suspended from trading on May 07, 2025, so the last trade was May 6 (decision 12).
- **exit_kind merger, continuation false, issuer 929351:** no contradicting evidence found.

Verdict: the report survives. Residual uncertainty is only the LION/STRZ first-trade date and the two-leg price field.
