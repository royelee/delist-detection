- Liberty Interactive completed the Split-Off at 4:01 p.m. on 2018-03-09, redeeming each Series A Liberty Ventures share (LVNTA) for one share of GLIBA, with no LVNTA remaining outstanding ([SEC 8-K 2018-03-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1355096/000110465918017857/a18-8242_18k.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE for Liberty Interactive's security, effective 2018-03-09 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1355096/000135445718000053/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

- Ticker history: the placeholder CIK1355096-COMMON also carries LVNTA fails rows, which causes `ticker_overlap:LVNTA`.
