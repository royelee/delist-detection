## 5. What happened

- Restated charter filed 2023-08-03, effective 5:00 pm New York time. Each Formula One share was reclassified into one share of the corresponding new series plus 0.0428 of a Liberty Live share, cash in lieu of fractions ([SEC 8-K 2023-08-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000110465923087380/tm2320270d12_8k.htm)).
- New shares were expected to trade on Nasdaq from 2023-08-04 under FWONA/FWONK; Series B moved to OTC as FWONB (same 8-K).
- Nasdaq filed a Form 25-NSE on 2023-08-03, effective the same day, for the old Series A/C Formula One and SiriusXM series ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000135445723000564/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); a Form 8-A12B for the new classes was filed 2023-08-01 ([SEC 8-A12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000110465923086344/tm2320270d11_8a12b.htm)).
- The CUSIP changed from 531229854 to 531229755 (cusip_history, library fails rows).
