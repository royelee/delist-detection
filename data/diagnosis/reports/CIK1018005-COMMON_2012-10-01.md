# CIK1018005-COMMON_2012-10-01 (CWTR, Coldwater Creek)

## 1. Verdict

The library's ending is invented. Coldwater Creek kept trading on Nasdaq for 18 more months. Its real ending is a Chapter 11 delisting: Nasdaq suspended trading at the open on 2014-04-23 (last exchange trade 2014-04-22), the Form 25-NSE followed on 2014-05-02, and the shares moved to OTC as CWTRQ. The row (exchange transfer, 2012-10-01, no last trade date) is wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 1.03 of 2014-04-11; 8-K 3.01 of 2014-04-17; Form 25-NSE of 2014-05-02).
- Successor: filing (a wind-down; no merger, reorganization or successor registration in the 2014 filing list).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K 3.01: suspended at the opening of business on 2014-04-23, so the last trade was 2014-04-22). The Form 25 notice text itself was not read for a suspension date; it rests on the 8-K plus fails rows (CWTR rows end 20140423, CWTRQ rows begin 20140424).
- Payout rule: worked out from the decision table (bankruptcy then OTC is `otc_print`).
- Terms: fails rows only (the OTC symbol CWTRQ, CUSIP 193068202). Not backed by a filing.
To reach verified: the Nasdaq/Form 25 notice stating the suspension date and an OTC quote source naming CWTRQ.

## 5. What happened

- 2014-01-02 Nasdaq bid-price deficiency letter, cure until 2014-07-01 ([SEC 8-K 2014-01-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000101800514000005/cwtr-201401028xk.htm)).
- 2014-04-11 Coldwater Creek and its domestic subsidiaries filed Chapter 11 in Delaware for an orderly wind-down ([SEC 8-K 2014-04-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000094787114000216/ss209556_8k.htm)).
- 2014-04-14 Nasdaq staff decided to delist because of the Chapter 11 filing; trading to be suspended at the open on 2014-04-23; no appeal ([SEC 8-K 2014-04-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000101800514000008/cwtr-20144178k.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE filed 2014-05-02 by Nasdaq ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000135445714000078/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 193068202 traded as CWTR 2013-01-02..2014-04-23, then as CWTRQ 2014-04-24..2014-06-30 (fails data, `sec.py fails`). The pre-2013 CUSIP 193068103 appears only on 2012-10-01; the CUSIP changed by 2013-01-02 (most likely a reverse split; the cause is not read from a filing).
- The company kept filing 10-Qs and a 10-K through 2013, so 2012-10-01 is just the last observation.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. The holders kept shares of the debtor, which traded off-exchange; the exit is valued at the first OTC print.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The observations stopped on 2012-10-01 and the finder found no Form 25 in its window, so it fell to the continued-filings fallback (304, `continued_filings_rule`, `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`), using the last sighting as the date. The 2014 Form 25 is 19 months later and was not matched, and the CUSIP change in 2013 left the security's CUSIP history ending at 2012-10-01. Cause tag: `continued_filings_guess`.

## 9. Verification

Second reader, against cached/live SEC filings and fails rows. All four disputed fields upheld; one detail in sections 2 and 5 is corrected here (earlier sections left unchanged).

- **exit_kind (upheld).** The [8-K 3.01 of 2014-04-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000101800514000008/cwtr-20144178k.htm) says that on 2014-04-14 Nasdaq Listing Qualifications Staff decided to delist the common stock because of the 2014-04-11 Chapter 11 announcement (discretionary authority, Rules 5101, 5110(b), IM-5101-1), the company will not appeal, and a Form 25-NSE will be filed. The [Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000135445714000078/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml) (0001354457-14-000078) was filed 2014-05-02; the [8-K 1.03 of 2014-04-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000094787114000216/ss209556_8k.htm) confirms the filing. The filing list for 2012-09..2014-07 shows continuous periodic filings and no successor registration or merger filing. So `dropped` / `bankruptcy` fits the decision-table row "Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC", and no filing supports the library's 2012-10-01 transfer.
- **last_trade_date (upheld).** The 8-K 3.01 states trading "will be suspended at the opening of business on April 23, 2014", so the last trade is 2014-04-22. Fails rows agree: CUSIP 193068202 under CWTR through 20140423 (fails rows are dated a day after the trade), under CWTRQ from 20140424. 2014-04-22 is before the Form 25 effective date (2014-05-02 + 10 days), so decision 12 allows it. Caveat: the 25-NSE notice text was not shown to state a suspension date; the date rests on the 8-K, so "inferred" is right.
- **value_rule (upheld).** Bankruptcy then off-exchange trading as CWTRQ (fails rows 2014-04-24..2014-06-30, 25 rows) is `otc_print` per the reference payout table, price_ticker CWTRQ, price_date 2014-04-24 (the session after the last trade). The filings read show no cash, stock or recovery terms. The library's `transfer` is wrong.
- **ticker_history (upheld, with a correction).** The library stops at 2012-10-01, but the security listed until 2014-04-22. Correction: the CUSIP change is not "by 2013-01-02". The [FY2012 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018005/000101800513000010/cwtr2012-10k.htm) says a 1-for-4 reverse stock split was effected on 2012-10-04, and fails rows show CUSIP 193068202 under CWTR from 20121005 (111 rows to 20130430; the old CUSIP 193068103 only on 20121001). So the new CUSIP starts 2012-10-05, caused by the split (now confirmed from a filing, closing the open check on the split date). A CUSIP change under the same ticker is not an ending, which explains the library's stale 2012-10-01 date.
- **Not re-checked.** The Form 25 notice's own suspension date and any OTC print (prices are out of scope). The `bankruptcy` drop_reason versus Nasdaq's discretionary-authority rationale is a labelling choice; the Chapter 11 trigger is explicit in the 8-K, so it stands.
