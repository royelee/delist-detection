## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred. The label was corrected from verified: the last trade date is worked out, not read from a filing.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2016-03-18: merger closed, each common share became $137.50 cash; Form 25-NSE 2016-03-18: shares came to evidence other securities).
- Successor: filing (the same 8-K: EXOR's subsidiary is the acquirer, holders receive cash, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out from the 8-K closing date 2016-03-18 (suspension requested that day) and the fails rows (last row dated 2016-03-18, priced at the 03-17 close). No filing states the last trading day.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2016-03-18: cash, without interest).
- Terms: filing (8-K 2016-03-18: $137.50 per common share, in USD).

Not backed: the last trade date 2016-03-17. Evidence that would make it verified: a filing or exchange notice that states the last trading day, or an exchange print on 2016-03-17.

## 5. What happened

- On 2016-03-18 the merger with Pillar Ltd (EXOR S.p.A.'s subsidiary) closed; each common share was converted into $137.50 cash, without interest ([SEC 8-K 2016-03-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/911421/000095010316011958/dp64290_8k.htm)).
- Item 3.01: the company told the NYSE on 2016-03-18 of the completion and asked for trading in the common shares to be suspended (same 8-K).
- The NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2016-03-18 for the entire class of Common Shares, removal effective at the open on 2016-03-29, because the shares came to evidence other securities (cash) on 2016-03-18 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/911421/000087666116000866/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

Whether listed preferred shares explain that is not checked.
