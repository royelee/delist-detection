## 5. What happened

- Exchange offer began 2014-10-07 for $16.00 cash + 0.8998 Amec Foster Wheeler securities per share, or elect $32.00 cash or 1.7996 securities, subject to proration; it expired 2014-11-12 23:59 NY and completed 2014-11-13, with about 99.03% tendered ([SEC 8-K 2014-11-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1130385/000114420414067433/v393901_8k.htm)).
- On 2014-11-13 Foster Wheeler told Nasdaq it breached Rule 5605(c)(2)(A) (audit committee) after directors resigned, and the board decided to withdraw the listing (same 8-K).
- Form 25 filed 2014-11-24 under 12d2-2(c), voluntary withdrawal ([SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1130385/000114420414070496/v394968_form25.htm), [SEC 8-K 2014-11-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1130385/000114420414070512/v394964_8k.htm)). Effective 10 days later, 2014-12-04.
- Fails rows for CUSIP H27178104 under FWLT run 2014-11-03 to 2014-12-04 (offline fails data; they do not show the last print).
- AMEC renamed Amec Foster Wheeler with ticker AMFW from 2014-11-14 ([web](https://www.maritimeprofessional.com/news/foster-wheeler-delist-from-nasdaq-260744)).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The terms were read from the all-cash election ($32.00) instead of the default package, so the stock leg is missing and `value_rule` is cash; the currency is blank. The date is blank because the Form 25 is a voluntary 12d2-2(c) filing with no suspension date, and the 8-K gives no last trading day (flags `no_last_trade_date`, `no_last_close`). Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: value_rule
- Upheld: terms

The 8-K of 2014-11-13 (0001144204-14-067433) sets the Offer Consideration at $16.00 cash plus 0.8998 Amec Foster Wheeler securities per share. The $32.00 cash and the 1.7996 securities are only elections, subject to proration. The library's published cash 32.00 is therefore the election, not the default package. The Form 25 (0001144204-14-070496) is a voluntary 12d2-2(c) filing and no filing states a last trade date. Not confirmed: the AMFW ticker (it rests on web only), the $1.6B aggregate-cash detail (my search of the 8-K did not find it), and the realised package after proration (the 8-K does not state it).
