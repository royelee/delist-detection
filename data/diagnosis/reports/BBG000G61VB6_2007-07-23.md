## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Terms only: the LLM extractor published stock_ratio 0.35, the ratio of the June 2007 amendment, not the final 0.375 of the 2007-07-06 amendment. Its gate passed against a lagged close (flags `ftd_close_prior:2`, `acquirer_close_lagged`), so the wrong ratio was not caught. The uncertainty came from `security_uncertain` (seeds outside history), not from the ending. Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (stock_ratio; the report calls the library's 0.35 wrong, evidence 0.375). **Upheld.**
- [SEC 8-K 2007-07-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1161448/000119312507150679/d8k.htm) (re-read through sec.py): the amendment to the agreement of Dec 20 2006, May 11 and June 14 2007 gives each CBOT Class A holder "0.3750 shares of CME Holdings Class A common stock"; no cash is mentioned.
- [SEC Form 25-NSE 2007-07-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1161448/000087666107000614/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml) (re-read): merger effective 2007-07-12; each Class A share "was converted into 0.375" of a CME Class A share. Same security class (Class A, CIK 1161448), same ratio, and it is the completed conversion, so no later amendment can supersede it.
- The library's 0.35 therefore does not match the final filed terms. Not checked: which amendment first set 0.35; the report's "June 2007 amendment" attribution in section 7 is unverified, but it does not affect the ratio claim.
- Other fields (exit_kind, date, rule, issuer) were not called wrong and were not re-examined.
