## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the five fields the report calls wrong. Re-opened the 8-K of 2025-12-15 (0001104659-25-121236, CIK 1560385) and the offline fails rows for LLYVA.

- **exit_kind (merger -> exchange): upheld.** Item 2.01 states the Split-Off was "a redemption ... of each outstanding share of its Liberty Live common stock ... in exchange for one share of the corresponding series of Liberty Live Group common stock ... of Liberty Live Holdings." One for one, same series, no cash. Under reference.md decision 9 (a 1:1 reorganization into a successor line) this is a continuation, not a merger. Caveat: a tracking stock becoming the equity of an independent company changes the economic claim, so a stricter reader could call it a distribution; the reference rule as written does not.
- **successor (none -> BBG01YY256K1): upheld.** Fails rows: CUSIP 531229748 under LLYVA to 2025-12-16, CUSIP 530909100 "LIBERTY LIVE HLDGS INC SER A" under LLYVA from 2025-12-17. `securities.csv` has BBG01YY256K1 (CIK 2078416, Class A); `cusip_history.csv` has 530909100 from 2025-12-16. The link rests on the filing text plus the matching series and ticker, not on timing alone.
- **value_rule (stock -> continuation): upheld.** A continuation has no value; the library's `stock` rule has no price_ticker and no price_date and cannot be priced.
- **terms (ratio 1.0, gate failed -> none): upheld.** The 1.0 ratio is the redemption ratio, but it is not an exit consideration, and the library's own gate failed (`no_acq_ticker`) because there is no acquirer.
- **ticker_history (old line to 2025-12-25 -> end 2025-12-15): upheld.** Item 3.01 says Nasdaq was notified on 2025-12-15 to delist LLYVA and LLYVK after the 4:05 p.m. Effective Time. The old line's `ticker_history` row ends 2025-12-25 (the Form 25 effective date, not a trade date), while the new line starts 2025-12-17 (`ticker_history.csv`). The last old-line fails row is dated 2025-12-16, so the last old-line close is 2025-12-15. Minor precision: the new line's fails rows start 2025-12-17, implying a first priced close of 2025-12-16.

Not re-checked: the Form 25-NSE page itself (only its accession, 0001354457-25-001270, is in the library row).
