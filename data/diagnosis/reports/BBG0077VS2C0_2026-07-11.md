## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement with QXO signed 2026-04-18 ([SEC 8-K 2026-04-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633931/000110465926045245/bld-20260418x8k.htm)).
- QXO completed the acquisition on 2026-07-01. Each share converted, at the holder's election and subject to
  proration, into $505.00 cash or 20.200 QXO shares; no election meant stock. About 91.0% of shares elected cash,
  so after proration every share received about $249.67 cash and 10.212 QXO shares
  ([SEC 8-K 2026-07-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633931/000110465926079876/tm2618991d10_8k.htm)).
- NYSE trading in BLD was suspended prior to the open on 2026-07-01 (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2026-07-01, effective 2026-07-01 (so delisting effective date 2026-07-11 by the 10-day rule;
  [SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633931/000087666126000589/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
