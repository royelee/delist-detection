## 5. What happened

- Google implemented the holding company reorganization on 2015-10-02; Alphabet became the successor issuer ([SEC 8-K 2015-10-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000119312515336550/d56649d8k.htm)).
- Item 3.03: each Google share converted into an equivalent Alphabet share with the same designations, rights and preferences (same 8-K).
- Item 3.01: Nasdaq suspends the Google shares after the close on 2015-10-02; Alphabet Class A and Class C commence trading on 2015-10-05 as GOOGL and GOOG (same 8-K).
- Alphabet's 8-K12B establishes it as successor issuer under Rule 12g-3(a) ([SEC 8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000119312515336577/d82837d8k12b.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed a Form 25-NSE for Class A and Class C on 2015-10-02 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000135445715000182/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Google also filed Forms 15-12G and 15-15D the same day.
- Fails rows: old CUSIP 38259P508 last seen 2015-10-02; new CUSIP 02079K305 from 2015-10-05 (library's cusip_history).
