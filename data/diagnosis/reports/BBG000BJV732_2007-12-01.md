# FRK (Florida Rock Industries), case BBG000BJV732_2007-12-01

## 1. Verdict

A real merger: Vulcan Materials acquired Florida Rock on 2007-11-16. Each FRK share became $67 cash or 0.63 VMC shares at the holder's election, prorated to 70% cash / 30% stock in aggregate. The library's exit kind (merger) is right, but its terms add both election legs ($67 plus 0.63 VMC), which double-counts, and its last trade date (2007-11-14, Nasdaq halt feed for an NYSE stock) is not confirmed by any filing I found.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the field the report calls wrong (`terms`), plus one related finding on `last_trade_date`.

- **terms: upheld.** The Vulcan 8-K Ex 99.2 (accession 0000950123-07-015875) says the elections "were subject to proration so that, in the aggregate, 70% of the Florida Rock shares ... were converted into the right to receive $67 in cash per share ... and 30% ... into the right to receive 0.63 of a share of common stock of Vulcan Materials". The deal closed 2007-11-16. The NYSE Form 25-NSE (0000876661-07-000905) states the same either/or election (0.63 VMC, $67.00 cash, "OR a combination"). So each holder gets cash OR stock, not both, and the library's `67 + 0.63 x VMC` double-counts. The aggregate arithmetic is right: 0.70 x 67 = 46.90 USD, 0.30 x 0.63 = 0.189 VMC per FRK share. The class is common stock (Form 15-12B, 0000950123-07-015670, "Common Stock $0.10 Par Value"). Reference.md allows the default or aggregate package for an election deal, and the report says which it chose. Blank currency is also confirmed (the filings state dollars, USD).
- **last_trade_date: the report's "unknown" is refuted; the date can be pinned.** The Form 25-NSE notice (0000876661-07-000905) says "this security was suspended from trading on November 19, 2007". Per decision 12, the last trade was the trading day before: Friday 2007-11-16 (the closing date). The report said the notice carried no suspension date; it does. The library's 2007-11-14 (Nasdaq halt feed) is therefore wrong, not just unconfirmed, and the report's 2007-11-15 alternative is excluded. 2007-11-16 is before the Form 25 effective date (2007-11-21), so it is publishable. The report's price_date of 2007-11-19 (the session after) is the right value for the corrected row.
- **Not rechecked** (no dispute raised): exit_kind merger, no continuation, issuer 37651, `value_rule = cash_plus_stock` as the aggregate label.

Net: the verdict on terms stands. The report should be amended so last_trade_date = 2007-11-16 (source: Form 25 notice) and confidence can rise toward verified.
