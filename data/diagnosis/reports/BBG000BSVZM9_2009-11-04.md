## 4. Why this confidence

- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2009-11-04, items 2.01/5.03).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: SP common converted into cash and New Merck stock).
- Last trade date: worked out — the closing date 2009-11-03 from the 8-K, and fails rows (SGP last row 2009-11-04 priced $28.15; MRK 58933Y105 first row 2009-11-05).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01).
- Terms: filing (8-K item 2.01: $10.50 and 0.5767); price_date 2009-11-04 worked out from the closing date.
- Not backed: an exchange print or filing stating SGP's last trading day (no Form 25 for the common stock was found; the only Form 25-NSE, 2010-08-13, is a later one). A NYSE notice or MIDAS (2012+ only, so none) would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Closing on 2009-11-03: Merger Sub 1 merged into Schering-Plough, Merger Sub 2 into Old Merck; Schering-Plough was renamed Merck & Co., Inc. ([SEC 8-K 2009-11-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/310158/000119312509223917/d8k.htm)).
- Each Schering-Plough common share became $10.50 cash and 0.5767 New Merck share; the 6.00% mandatory convertible preferred stayed outstanding one for one (same 8-K, item 2.01).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 806605101 (SGP) ends 2009-11-04; CUSIP 58933Y105 (MRK) starts 2009-11-05 at $32.64 (SEC fails-to-deliver).
- Old Merck holders received New Merck shares one for one (a separate security, BBG000BPD168, which the library's MRK line covers).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: cash plus another line's shares for the old SGP share — a merger, not a continuation.
