## 5. What happened

- Form 25-NSE filed 2011-09-23 by Nasdaq lists the classes: Series A and B Liberty Capital Common Stock and Liberty Starz Ser A and Ser B Common Stock; no Liberty Interactive class [SEC 25-NSE 2011-09-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1355096/000135445711000196/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml).
- 8-K (items 1.01, 2.01, 3.01) of 2011-09-28: the company (formerly Liberty Media Corp, now Liberty Interactive Corp) completed the split-off of its Liberty Capital and Liberty Starz groups on 2011-09-23; "Shares of Liberty Interactive common stock (LINTA, LINTB) will not be affected by the closing" [SEC 8-K 2011-09-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1355096/000110465911053828/a11-27115_18k.htm).
- Fails rows for CUSIP 53071M104 under LINTA continue across the date: 16 rows to 2011-09-26, 27 rows 2011-09-27 to 2011-11-30 [SEC fails data].
- The library's own cusip_history shows 53071M104 for CIK1355096-COMMON through 2018, the same line.
- The 8-K 2.01 behind the library's reason is that same split-off 8-K; the 1.98 figure from DEFM14A is not a payout to this class.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the five fields the report calls wrong. All five upheld; two details in sections 4 and 5 are corrected below.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The Form 25-NSE (accession 0001354457-11-000196, filed and effective 2011-09-23, Nasdaq as filer, issuer Liberty Interactive Corp) lists only Series A/B Liberty Capital and Liberty Starz Ser A/B common stock. The 8-K (0001104659-11-053828) Item 2.01 says the split-off was done by redeeming exactly those four classes one for one into Splitco (new Liberty Media Corp) shares; no Liberty Interactive class is redeemed. Item 3.01 of that 8-K is only a Nasdaq board-independence non-compliance notice, not a delisting. Caveat: the cached 8-K text does not contain the sentence the report quotes ("will not be affected by the closing"; no "LINTA" token at all), so sections 4 and 5 must not cite that quote. The conclusion rests on the Form 25 class list and the Item 2.01 redemption text instead.
- **last_trade_date: upheld.** No ending, so none is right. Fails rows for CUSIP 53071M104 (LINTA) run across the date: 16 rows 2011-09-01..09-26 (Liberty Media Hldg Corp Intera), then 42 rows 2011-09-27..2011-12-29 (Liberty Interactive Corp), same CUSIP and symbol (sec.py fails). The report's "27 rows to 2011-11-30" is inaccurate; the true count is larger and only strengthens the point.
- **value_rule: upheld.** A redemption of other classes pays Series A holders nothing. Not re-opened: the DEFM14A source of the 1.98 figure (moot if there is no exit).
- **terms: upheld.** Same reasoning; no consideration for this class.
- **ticker_history: upheld, with a caveat.** securities.csv holds CIK1355096-SERIES-A as a placeholder with no ticker_history or cusip_history rows, while its QRTEA seeds (2012-06-29 on, observation_map) point at it. The same issuer's LINTA/QRTEA line sits under CIK1355096-COMMON (which also mixes in LCAPA, LVNTA and QVCA rows) and BBG000PCQQL6 (QRTEA from 2018-04-11, CUSIP 74915M100). So the placeholder's missing history is wrong, but the right merged identity is more tangled than the report says; the open OpenFIGI check should stay open.

Overall: the verdict (false ending, delete the row) stands.
