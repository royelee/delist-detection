## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2010-10-15 items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01: merger completed, Abraxis a Celgene subsidiary).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: holders receive cash, Celgene stock and a CVR; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K says Abraxis asked Nasdaq on 2010-10-15 to suspend trading; the Form 25 is dated 2010-10-15; fails rows run to 2010-10-15 (priced at the prior close). No filing states the last session. Taken as 2010-10-14.

- Terms: filing (8-K: $58.00 cash, 0.2617 Celgene share, one CVR). The price date follows from the worked-out last trade.
- To make it verified: an exchange print or a filing that states the last trading day (MIDAS, Nasdaq halt feed).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The issuer step took CIK 1141399 for the name ABRAXIS BIOSCIENCE INC, which is that CIK's former EDGAR name (before the Nov 2007 spin-off), so the era resolved to APP, the wrong issuer. It then found APP's 2008 Form 25 and the placeholder `CIK1141399-COMMON` stands for APP. The flags `member_name_mismatch` and `no_last_trade_date` show the doubt. The 2010 Celgene ending was never found, and the observations after 2008-09-20 show as `after_unconfirmed_delisting`. Cause tag: `identity_wrong`.

## 9. Verification

Checked value_rule, terms, issuer and ticker_history. All four upheld.

- **terms: upheld.** [SEC 8-K 2010-10-15, Abraxis CIK 1409012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1409012/000119312510230214/d8k.htm) (re-read): each share became $58.00 cash, 0.2617 Celgene share and one CVR. The library's $23.00 comes from APP's Fresenius merger: the [APP 8-K 5.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141399/000119312508196737/d8k.htm) says Fresenius completed the APP acquisition on 2008-09-10.
- **value_rule: upheld.** Cash plus a Celgene stock leg (plus a CVR) is cash_plus_stock, not cash. Leaving the CVR unvalued matches the report's rule.
- **issuer: upheld.** The [25-NSE of 2008-09-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141399/000135445708000253/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml) names CIK 1141399 as "APP Pharmaceuticals, Inc." (former name Abraxis BioScience, Inc. (ABBI), changed 2006-04). Fails rows: APPX = APP (00202H108), 2007-11-19..2008-09-17. ABII = "ABRAXIS BIOSCIENCE INC NEW" (00383Y102) runs to 2010-10-15. All seven ABBI observations carry the name ABRAXIS BIOSCIENCE INC, and four fall after APP's end (2008-11-18 to 2009-06-08), when only the new Abraxis existed. The library itself marks those `after_unconfirmed_delisting`.
- **ticker_history: upheld, one correction.** The library's ABBI 2008-01-16..2008-09-20 is APP's span, while ABII is the line that traded. Correction to section 5: a symbol search shows ABII fails rows from 2007-11-26, not 2008-09-08 (the end 2010-10-15 stands). ABBI was the old company's pre-spin ticker, so it is a stale alias in the observations. The verdict is unchanged.
- **Still open, not refuted:** the last trade date 2010-10-14 is worked out, not stated; the 8-K says suspension was requested 2010-10-15. Confidence stays inferred.
