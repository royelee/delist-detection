# BBG000FJJW82_2008-12-06 (ABI, Applera Corp - Applied Biosystems Group)

## 1. Verdict

A real cash-and-stock merger: Invitrogen (renamed Life Technologies) acquired Applied Biosystems on 2008-11-21. The
default package per share is $17.10 cash plus 0.4543 Invitrogen share (elections: $38.00 cash or 0.8261 shares,
prorated). The library's kind, date and terms agree with the filings; only the currency is missing, and its price
security IVGN is a symbol that stopped existing with the name change.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2008-11-28 items 2.01/3.01/5.01; Form 25-NSE notice).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2008-11-28: Applied Biosystems survives as a subsidiary; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out from the Form 25-NSE notice's merger-effective date 2008-11-21; no filing states the last session.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2008-11-28 election terms).
- Terms: filing (8-K 2008-11-28: $17.10 + 0.4543; $38.00; 0.8261). The currency is USD by the filing's dollar sign.
- Not backed: the last trade day as an exchange print, and the first trading day and symbol of the combined company
  (the filing says only that Invitrogen changed its name to Life Technologies). A MIDAS-era print or a Life
  Technologies filing stating the first trading day would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Merger Agreement: each share converts, at election, to (1) $17.10 cash + 0.4543 Invitrogen share, (2) $38.00 cash, or (3) 0.8261 shares; cash elections were prorated; Invitrogen renamed Life Technologies
  ([SEC 8-K 2008-11-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77551/000134100408002994/form8k.htm)).
- On 2008-11-21 the company confirmed consummation with the NYSE and asked for delisting (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE: the merger with Invitrogen (Nasdaq: IVGN) became effective 2008-11-21; shares came to evidence other securities or a cash payment
  ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2008-11-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77551/000087666108000491/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12G filed 2008-12-08 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77551/000119312508249359/d1512g.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the share was exchanged for cash plus another company's shares, so a merger and not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Mostly it did not. Its row is right on kind, date and the two terms. The gate failed (`llm_gate_failed`,
`merger_at_par`), probably because the stock leg is priced off a stale IVGN symbol and the check compared a
mixed package with the last close, so the fill stayed assumed par and the row is uncertain. Currency is blank. Cause
tag: `currency_missing` (with a stale price symbol, an acquirer renamed at closing).

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish `cash_currency=USD`; give the price security by FIGI or as Invitrogen/Life Technologies rather than the retired IVGN symbol.
- Confirm the first trading day and symbol of the combined company (the library's price_date 2008-11-24 is the next trading day after 2008-11-21).
- Confirm the last NYSE session was 2008-11-21 (an exchange print).
- Unexamined: the security-level flag `seeds_outside_history:1 from 2009-06-08` (an observation of ABI after the end, probably another issuer reusing the ticker).
- Golden case: yes, a well-sourced election deal with a prorated cash option, once the last-trade print is confirmed.
