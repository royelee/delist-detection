## 5. What happened

- CCE shares suspended on the NYSE at the opening of trading on 2016-05-31 (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2016-05-31 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3): shares came to evidence other securities and the right to cash; removal at the opening of 2016-06-13 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1491675/000087666116000998/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2016-06-27 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1491675/000094787116001303/ss199_1512b.htm)).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The last trade date comes from MIDAS (`last_trade_date_source=midas`) and landed on 2016-05-31, the day the new CCEP shares (same ticker CCE) traded; the 8-K states suspension at that day's open, so the MIDAS volume is most likely the successor's under the reused ticker (not verified). The stock leg has no price ticker (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`), which is why the gate failed and par was assumed. Cause tag: `other:midas_date_from_reused_ticker_and_acquirer_ticker_missing`.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the three fields the report calls wrong. Sources re-read: 8-K 2016-06-01 (accession 0001193125-16-610069, via sec.py grep) and the fails rows for symbol CCE (sec.py fails, 2016-05..06); library row from source.csv and output/ticker_history.csv.

- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The 8-K Item 3.01 says the shares "were suspended from trading on the NYSE effective as of the opening of trading on May 31, 2016." Under the reference rule (suspended before the open on D means the last trade was the prior trading day) and the calendar (05-28/29 weekend, 05-30 Memorial Day) the last trade is 2016-05-27. Fails rows agree: CUSIP 19122T109 (CCE) ends with a row dated 2016-05-31 at $51.55, which is the prior session's close (rows are priced at D-1), matching the library's own last close of 51.55. The library's 05-31 is the suspension date, not a trade date. Plausible cause: CUSIP G25839104 (CCEP) appears under symbol CCE from 06-01 with a 05-31 close, so CCEP shares did trade on 05-31 under the reused ticker, which would explain MIDAS volume that day (the mechanism itself is not verified).
- **terms: upheld.** Cash $14.50 and one share per CCE share match the 8-K Item 2.01 text ("one (1) ... Share ... and $14.50 in cash"), so the library's cash and ratio are right. What is wrong or missing is as the report says: the library's price ticker is blank (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`), its currency is blank, and its price date (2016-06-01) is one trading day late, because it is derived from the wrong last trade date. With a last trade of 05-27 the next trading day is 05-31. The ticker CCE for the acquirer's shares is supported by the fails rows (G25839104 under symbol CCE, "COCA-COLA EUROPEAN PARTNERS PL"). Note the 8-K text calls the acquirer's shares "Orange Shares" (the acquirer's pre-closing name), not CCEP, so the name-to-ticker link rests on the fails rows and not on this filing. Currency USD is an inference from the dollar sign, not stated in words.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library ends CCE at 2016-05-31 (output/ticker_history.csv, source observation). It follows the last trade date, so it should end 2016-05-27. Dependent on the first field and fails with it.

Not refuted: nothing. Residual uncertainty: CCEP's first trading day and symbol still come from fails rows, not a filing; this does not affect the three verdicts above.
