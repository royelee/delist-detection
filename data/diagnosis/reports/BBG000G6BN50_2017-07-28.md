## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2017-07-18, items 2.01/3.01/5.01: merger completed, shares converted to cash).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Panera became a subsidiary of Parent and JAB; no successor line).
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K says the closing was 2017-07-18 and Nasdaq was notified that day; the library's MIDAS print says 2017-07-17. No filing states the last trading day.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K, $315.00 cash).
- Terms: filing (8-K, $315.00 in cash per Class A and Class B share).
- Not backed by a filing: the last trade day. An exchange print, or a press release or Nasdaq notice stating the last day, would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- On 2017-07-18 the merger became effective; each Class A and Class B share was converted into $315.00 cash, about $7 billion in total ([SEC 8-K 2017-07-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/724606/000119312517229854/d425559d8k.htm)).
- Panera notified Nasdaq on the closing date and asked it to file a Form 25 (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- Nasdaq filed the Form 25-NSE on 2017-07-18, effective 2017-07-18 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/724606/000135445717000134/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Panera filed a Form 15-12G on 2017-07-31 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/724606/000119312517241443/d432964d1512g.htm)).
- The agreement was dated 2017-04-04 and stockholders approved it on 2017-07-12 (SEC 8-K, item 5.07, accession 0001193125-17-227046).
