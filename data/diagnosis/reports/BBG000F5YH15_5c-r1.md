## 5. What happened
- [SEC 8-K 2023-09-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091907/000119312523233499/d519698d8k.htm): Merger Sub merged into WWE, WWE a wholly owned TKO subsidiary, then converted to WWE LLC and contributed to TKO OpCo for 49%; Endeavor subsidiaries got TKO Class B (51% voting) for par; each WWE Class A/B share became one TKO Class A share.
- [SEC 25-NSE 2023-09-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091907/000087666123000718/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml): NYSE removal under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3), "merger ... effective before market open on September 12, 2023", one TKO share per WWE share, suspended 2023-09-12.
- [SEC 15-12G 2023-09-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091907/000119312523240930/d515925d1512g.htm): registration terminated.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Replaced by stock of another company (a different security, TKO, whose claim covers WWE plus UFC and has a controlling Endeavor holder). Not a 1:1 holding-company reorganization: the holders are not the same and the business is not the same, so decision 9's "same holders, same claim" fails. The one-for-one ratio is the deal's ratio, not a reorganization.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
Reason "Continuation (R1): each share became one of TKO ... successor by new issuer", flags `r1_continuation`. The 5c rule links any one-for-one no-cash transfer to a successor and does not test that the successor is a pure reorganization (no third party's business or holders merged in). Cause: `holdco_not_linked` inverted, tagged `other:r1_continuation_overreach_third_party_merger`.
