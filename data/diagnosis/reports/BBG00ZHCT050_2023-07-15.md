## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2023-07-05 Item 2.01: merger completed, shares cancelled for cash).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Company survives as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; no new line).
- Last trade date: worked out (the filing gives the Closing Date 2023-07-05 and the Form 25 notice came the same day, but no filing text states "ceased trading at the close on D"; fails rows end 2023-07-06).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K Item 2.01).
- Terms: filing (8-K Item 2.01: $8.40 per Ordinary Share, without interest).

Not backed: the last trade date. A Nasdaq halt/MIDAS print or the Form 25 notice stating the suspension date would make it `verified`.

## 5. What happened

- On 2023-07-05 Merger Sub merged into Diversey; Diversey survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (Platinum Equity / Solenis affiliates) ([SEC 8-K 2023-07-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1831617/000110465923078137/tm2320294d3_8k.htm)).
- Each ordinary share (except Rollover Shares and Bain Shares) was cancelled for $8.40 cash, without interest (same 8-K, Item 2.01).
- The company notified Nasdaq on the Closing Date and asked it to file a Form 25 (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2023-07-05 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1831617/000135445723000453/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); the library took 2023-07-15 (filing + 10 days) as `delist_date`.
- Fails rows under DSEY (CUSIP G28923103) run 2023-06-01 to 2023-07-06 and then stop (fails data, offline).
- DEFM14A of 2023-05-15 carried the merger terms ([SEC DEFM14A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1831617/000110465923060078/tm2311231-5_defm14a.htm)); shareholders approved on 2023-06-08 (8-K Item 5.07).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Kind, rule and amount are right. The finder found the Form 25 but no exchange print: `last_trade_date_source` blank, `dlret_method=needs_last_trade`, flags `no_last_trade_date;no_last_close`. The 8-K Item 3.01 does not state a trading end date and no MIDAS/halt day was found, so the date stayed blank. The currency is also blank (always USD here). Cause tag: `no_last_trade_print`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Set the last trade date to 2023-07-05 (closing/Form 25 date; Nasdaq halts on a closing day normally follow the last session). Publish `cash_currency=USD`.
- Open check: confirm the final Nasdaq session via a halt record or the Form 25's exchange notice.
- Golden case: yes, a plain cash take-private with a clear filing trail whose only gap is the exchange print.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: last_trade_date
- Upheld: terms

8-K 0001104659-23-078137 Item 3.01 states trading on Nasdaq was halted following the close on the Closing Date (2023-07-05), and the Form 25-NSE effectiveness date is 2023-07-05, so last_trade_date 2023-07-05 holds. Item 2.01 confirms $8.40 cash per ordinary share (Bain shares $7.84), so currency USD is right. The report's section 4 claim that no filing states the last session is wrong (the evidence is stronger, so confidence could be verified), but the corrected values stand.
