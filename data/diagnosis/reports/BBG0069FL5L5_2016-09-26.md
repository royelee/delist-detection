## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K of 2016-09-16, Items 2.01/3.01/5.01: merger completed, MRD delisted).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: MRD survives only as a Range subsidiary; holders get Range common stock).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K Item 3.01: trading suspended before the open on 2016-09-16, so the last trade was 2016-09-15).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K Item 2.01: 0.375 Range share per MRD share).
- Terms: filing (same; no cash except fractional shares).

## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement dated 2016-05-15 among MRD, Range and Merger Sub; MRD survived as a Range subsidiary ([SEC 8-K 2016-09-16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599222/000119312516711718/d212881d8k.htm)).
- At the Effective Time each MRD share was converted into 0.375 Range common shares; fractions were paid in cash at 0.375-fraction x $41.0766 (5-day VWAP) (same 8-K, Item 2.01).
- Restricted MRD stock vested and received the same consideration (same 8-K).
- NASDAQ trading in MRD was suspended before the open on 2016-09-16 (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- NASDAQ filed Form 25-NSE on 2016-09-16 ([SEC 25-NSE 0001354457-16-000495](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599222/000135445716000495/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15-12B filed the same day.
