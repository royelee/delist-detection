# CIK73887-COMMON_2013-06-28 (ticker ERA, observed 2013-06-28)

## 1. Verdict

There was no ending. Ticker ERA on 2013-06-28 was Era Group Inc. (CIK 1525221), spun off from Bristow in February 2013 and still listed afterwards. The library gave the observation the name "BRISTOW GROUP INC", which is the name Era Group took in 2020. It then matched that name to the old Bristow (CIK 73887, ticker BRS) and read the old Bristow's continued filings as an exchange transfer. The row is wrong: wrong issuer, so a made-up ending.

## 5. What happened

- Era Group filed a Form 10-12B in 2012 and completed the spin-off from Bristow in early 2013 ([SEC 8-K 2013-02-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1525221/000152522113000014/era8-kxcompletionofspinxof.htm)).
- Era Group kept filing 10-K, 8-K (2013-02-28 to 2013-11-13) and had no Form 25 in 2013 ([SEC filings list, CIK 1525221](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=1525221&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=40)).
- The old Bristow (CIK 73887) kept filing 10-Qs and 8-Ks through 2013 and filed no Form 25 either ([SEC 10-Q 2013-08-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/73887/000007388713000006/brsq110-q06302013.htm)).
- On 2020-06-11 Era Group completed its combination with Bristow, and the registrant (CIK 1525221) was renamed BRISTOW GROUP INC. ([SEC 8-K 2020-06-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1525221/000114036120014075/form8k.htm)). That is the name on the 2013 observation.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: nothing happened to ERA holders in 2013. This is an identity error, not a delisting.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The resolver took the observation name "BRISTOW GROUP INC" through the name tier (`library_cik` source `name_search`) to CIK 73887, the issuer that held that name in 2013, instead of CIK 1525221, whose later name it is. With no Form 25 for CIK 73887, the continued-filings fallback (`continued_filings_rule`, 304) produced an exchange transfer with an unconfirmed date. Cause tag: `identity_wrong`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- The observation's issuer should be CIK 1525221 (name tier should prefer the issuer whose own name at the observation date or ticker ERA matches; here the ticker ERA belongs to Era Group, which the 10-12B and 8-Ks confirm). The row then drops out: no delisting for ERA in 2013.
- Open: whether Era Group's own history ends with the 2020 rename (same CUSIP) or a later event; not examined here.
- Golden case: yes, a name-search issuer resolution that picks an old namesake over the renamed company.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the four fields the report calls wrong. All four upheld; one factual slip in the report noted below.

- **issuer: upheld.** Era Group's FY2012 10-K (CIK 1525221, 0001525221-13-000021) says its common stock is listed on the NYSE under the symbol "ERA", with regular-way trading from 2013-02-01. So ticker ERA on 2013-06-28 is CIK 1525221, not CIK 73887 (old Bristow, ticker BRS). The 2020 8-K (0001140361-20-014075) confirms Era Group Inc. was renamed Bristow Group Inc. and its ticker became VTOL, while old Bristow became Bristow Holdings U.S. Inc. Both registrants carried the name "Bristow Group Inc." at different times, so the name tier's pick of 73887 is the ambiguity the report describes.
- **exit_kind: upheld.** No Form 25 or Form 15 exists for CIK 1525221 from 2013 through 2025 (`filings` with forms 25, 25-NSE, 15, 15-12B, 8-K12B returned nothing). CIK 73887's only Form 25-NSE (0000876661-19-000511, 2019-05-29) and Form 15-12B filings (2019-10-31, 2020-01-09) are all in 2019-2020, none in 2013. The library's 2013-06-28 exchange transfer (304) rests only on the continued-filings fallback with no Form 25, so it is not a real ending for ERA.
- **value_rule: upheld.** It follows from exit_kind: with no ending there is no payout rule, so "transfer" is wrong.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** ERA 2013-06-28 under CIK73887-COMMON is attached to the wrong issuer. The correct ERA line is Era Group (1525221). The report's end point "2020 rename" is consistent with the 2020 8-K (ticker became VTOL on 2020-06-11); not examined further, as the report itself says.
- **Factual slip (does not change any verdict):** sections 1 and 5 say Era Group was spun off from Bristow. The 10-K shows the spin-off was from SEACOR (effective January 2013); the 2013-02-01 8-K cited in section 5 is that completion. Bristow was not the parent. The identity conclusion is unaffected.
