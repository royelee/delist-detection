## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2008-07-30, Items 2.01/3.01: merger completed, Form 25 requested).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Sirius is the acquirer, XM survives as its subsidiary; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: fails rows plus the filing's completion date (2008-07-28); worked out, not an exchange print or a filing that states the last trading day.

## 5. What happened

- Merger with Vernon Merger Corporation (Sirius subsidiary) completed 2008-07-28; each common share converted into 4.6 Sirius common shares ([SEC 8-K 2008-07-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091530/000095012308008563/y64329e8vk.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE for the Class A common stock under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3), dated 2008-07-28, filed and effective 2008-07-29 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091530/000135445708000198/)).
- Form 15-12G filed 2008-07-29 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091530/000095012308008405/y63964ffe15v12g.htm)).
- Fails rows for CUSIP 983759101 (XMSR) run 2008-01-03 to 2008-07-29, none after (fails rows, offline).
- XM kept filing a 10-Q on 2008-11-12 as a Sirius subsidiary with public debt, which is what the library's continued-filings rule read ([SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091530/000119312508232894/d10q.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old shares were exchanged for another company's stock, not one for one in a continuing line. A merger.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the four fields the report calls wrong. Re-opened the 8-K of 2008-07-30 (0000950123-08-008563), the 25-NSE (0001354457-08-000198), the filing list for 2008-07 to 2008-09, and the fails rows for CUSIP 983759101.

- **exit_kind (merger): upheld.** 8-K Item 2.01: on July 28, 2008 Merger Co. merged into XM, which survived as a wholly owned Sirius subsidiary; each common share was converted into 4.6 Sirius common shares (also Item 3.03). Item 3.01: XM asked NASDAQ to file Form 25 for the Common Stock. This is replacement by another company's stock (decision table), not a continuation or an exchange transfer. The library's continued-filings guess has no Form 25 and reflects only the subsidiary's later public-debt filings.
- **last_trade_date (2008-07-28): upheld, as an inferred date.** The 25-NSE (filed and effective 2008-07-29, Rule 12d2-2(a)(3), Class A Common Stock, signed 2008-07-28) matches the observed share class. Merger completion was 2008-07-28, and the last fails row is 2008-07-29 (34 rows, 2008-01-03 to 2008-07-29, last price $8.17, priced at the prior close). The date is not after the Form 25 effective date, but no exchange print or filing states the last trading day (EX-99.25 text unread). Right on evidence, not verified.
- **value_rule (stock) and terms (4.6 x SIRI, price_date 2008-07-29): upheld.** Ratio 4.6 is stated twice in the 8-K (Items 2.01, 3.03), with no cash leg. Acquirer is Sirius (SIRI). price_date is the session after the last trade, per the reference rule. The library's transfer rule with a 0.0 fill is wrong for a merger.
- **ticker_history (should end 2008-07-28): upheld, with a caveat.** The Class A line ended in the merger and no fails rows follow 2008-07-29, so a range to 2009-06-08 is not supported by the filings. I did not inspect the observations behind the 2009-06-08 end, so the stale-snapshot explanation is unconfirmed.

Result: all four fields upheld.
