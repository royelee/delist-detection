# MIR (Mirant Corp), case BBG000DQV8M1_2010-12-17

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger: on 2010-12-03 each Mirant share became 2.835 shares of RRI Energy, which renamed itself GenOn Energy that day and traded as GEN from 2010-12-06. The library's kind, date, ratio and issuer are right; its price security is wrong (RRI stopped trading on 2010-12-03, so the leg cannot be priced, the gate fails and the row falls back to assumed par).

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified. It was inferred until 2026-10-03, when the acquirer's own closing 8-K was read.
- exit kind: filing (8-K 2010-12-07 items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01; Form 25-NSE).
- successor: filing (8-K: RRI Energy is the acquirer, "renamed GenOn Energy, Inc. on the closing date").
- last trade date: filing (Form 25 EX-99.25: securities converted on 2010-12-03; removal at open 2010-12-17).
- payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01: converted into 2.835 RRI Energy shares).
- terms: filing. The ratio is in Mirant's 8-K. The acquirer is RRI Energy (CIK 1126294), renamed GenOn Energy at the closing. Its closing 8-K ([0000950123-10-111604](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1126294/000095012310111604/c09394e8vk.htm)) states the symbol and its first session:
  - "On December 6, 2010, our ticker symbol was changed from 'RRI' to 'GEN,' and our common stock continues to trade on the New York Stock Exchange".
  - The press release (EX-99.1) says GenOn "will begin trading on the New York Stock Exchange on Monday, December 6 under the stock symbol GEN".

## 5. What happened

- [SEC 8-K 2010-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1010775/000119312510275719/d8k.htm): on 2010-12-03 Merger Sub merged into Mirant; each share was converted into 2.835 shares of RRI Energy common stock; RRI Energy renamed GenOn Energy on the closing date; NYSE notified under item 3.01.
- [SEC Form 25-NSE 2010-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1010775/000087666110000453/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml): NYSE removes the class at the open on 2010-12-17 (Rule 12d2-2(a)(3)); securities came to evidence other securities on 2010-12-03.
- [SEC Form 15-12B 2010-12-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1010775/000095012310115511/c10049e15v12b.htm) follows.
- Fails rows (offline `sec.py fails`): RRI CUSIP 74971X107 under RRI 2010-11-01..2010-12-03; GenOn CUSIP 37244E107 under GEN from 2010-12-06.
  - GenOn's 2010-12-06 row shows $0.01, because the CUSIP had no prior close.
  - The row dated 2010-12-07 carries the 2010-12-06 close, $3.73.
- GenOn's closing 8-K: the merger completed 2010-12-03, RRI Energy was renamed GenOn Energy, and on December 6, 2010 "our ticker symbol was changed from 'RRI' to 'GEN'" ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1126294/000095012310111604/c09394e8vk.htm)).
- In the run, RRI Energy is the placeholder CIK1126294-COMMON (RRI, to 2010-12-03). No GenOn line exists for 2010-12-06. The GEN line in the run, BBG000BH2JM1 from 2022-11-09, is Gen Digital, a later company that reused the ticker.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The LLM read the acquirer as RRI (its name in the 2010 merger agreement); after the rename the fails closes under RRI end 2010-12-03, so `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_price`, `merger_at_par` and `dlret_method=assumed_par` followed (`assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause: terms_misread.

## 8. Fix and open checks

The same four steps as CAL (BBG000BDXVW8_2010-10-14) apply. The acquirer renamed itself and changed ticker and CUSIP at the closing.
1. **Link lines.** Carry RRI Energy's line (CIK1126294-COMMON, CUSIP 74971X107, to 2010-12-03) forward as GenOn (CUSIP 37244E107, GEN from 2010-12-06). The guard holds:
   - same CIK;
   - a rename at the switch;
   - the 8-K says the stock "continues to trade on the New York Stock Exchange";
   - no bankruptcy.

   The run has no GenOn line, so step 1 must also add it, from the fails rows under CUSIP 37244E107. The RRI case (its own continuation report) is the same link seen from the other side.
2. **Find the acquirer for every stock leg, including a failed gate.** Resolve RRI on 2010-12-03 to CIK 1126294. Its name "RRI Energy" matches its EDGAR names, and it is not Mirant's CIK 1010775. Then take its common line on 2010-12-06.

   Do not take GEN's ticker_history on the price date. In the run it belongs only to Gen Digital (BBG000BH2JM1, from 2022-11-09).
3. **Publish and price by the security.**
   - Publish price_ticker GEN with the GenOn line's sec_id.
   - The gate prices CUSIP 37244E107's 2010-12-06 close, $3.73, from the row dated 2010-12-07. Skip the $0.01 first row.
   - Then 2.835 × $3.73 = $10.57 against MIR's last close of $10.01, a 5.6% gap. The gate passes, and assumed par (`assumed_par_after_failed_gate`, the row's only uncertain reason) goes away.
4. **Skill rule:** not needed; the filing names the symbol.

Golden candidate: yes (well-sourced rename-after-merger case).

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: terms

8-K 0001193125-10-275719 confirms 2.835 RRI Energy shares per MIR share and that RRI was renamed GenOn on the closing date, 2010-12-03. Fails rows show RRI CUSIP 74971X107 under RRI only through 2010-12-03, and GenOn CUSIP 37244E107 under GEN from 2010-12-06. So the library's RRI price ticker cannot be priced on 2010-12-06, and GEN is right. The symbol GEN rests on fails rows, not a filing, so the report's confidence stays inferred.
