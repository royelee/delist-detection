## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2016-10-03 items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01: merger completed; Form 25-NSE: shares converted into $60.00 cash).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: surviving company a wholly owned Duke subsidiary; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: suspended from trading on 2016-10-04, so last trade 2016-10-03).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: each share converted into the right to receive $60.00 cash).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K; USD is the filing's dollar).

## 5. What happened

- At the Effective Time each Piedmont share was converted into $60.00 cash, without interest ([SEC 8-K 2016-10-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78460/000110465916148229/a16-19209_18k.htm)).
- The merger with Forest Subsidiary, Inc., a Duke Energy subsidiary, became effective 2016-10-03; the shares were suspended from trading on 2016-10-04 ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2016-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78460/000087666116001312/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows under CUSIP 720186105 (PNY) run 2016-09-12..2016-10-05, last at $60.02 (fails data, offline).
