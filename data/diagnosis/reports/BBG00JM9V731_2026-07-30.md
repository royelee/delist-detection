## 9. Verification

Re-checked against the 8-K12B (0001628280-26-048761), the filing list for CIK 1718512 and the fails data. Fields checked: exit_kind, value_rule, ticker_history. All three upheld.

- **exit_kind (upheld).** The 8-K12B says holders of Old Gates ordinary shares "received common shares ... in New Gates in exchange for their Old Gates Shares on a one-for-one basis", and Old Gates became a wholly-owned subsidiary of New Gates. The filing mentions no cash paid in the exchange, so this is the reference's 1:1 holding-company reorganization (decision 9): a continuation, `exchange`, not a blank/unknown ending. The 25-NSE (2026-07-20) and 15-12G (2026-07-30) are the old line's housekeeping. One difference from the report: the fails data shows a new CUSIP (G39104107, "GATES INDL CORP LTD COM SH(BMU") from 2026-07-21, replacing G39108108 (GBR). A new CUSIP does not break a 1:1 holdco continuation under the reference (Alphabet, ANAT, Xerox), so the verdict stands.
- **value_rule (upheld).** A 1:1 exchange with no cash leg means "continuation: not an exit", so `unknown` / assumed par 0.0 is the wrong rule. The 8-K12B's 253,151,170 New Gates Shares issued to holders at the Scheme Record Time agrees with stock_ratio 1.0.
- **ticker_history (upheld).** The 8-K12B states the New Gates Shares begin trading on the NYSE at the open on 2026-07-20 under GTES, the same symbol. Fails rows show GTES trading under the new CUSIP from 2026-07-21 to 2026-08-27 (about $25.70 to $26.67), and the old CUSIP's last row is 2026-07-20 at $26.61. So the library's clip at 2026-07-17 is wrong. The report's open check (does the composite FIGI survive, and is a successor sec_id missing from the run) is unresolved and I did not test it. It does not change the three fields above.
