## 1. Verdict

The library's 2019-04-22 "ending" is not an ending: it is the 1-for-20 reverse split of 2019-04-18 (new CUSIP, same company, still on the NYSE). The real ending is Rite Aid's chapter 11 on 2023-10-16, the NYSE suspension and Form 25-NSE of 2023-10-31, then OTC trading as RADCQ. The library's row is wrong.

## 5. What happened

- 2019-04-18: 1-for-20 reverse split effective 5:00 p.m., to meet the NYSE $1 minimum price ([SEC 8-K 2019-04-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/84129/000110465919022372/a19-8544_18k.htm)). Fails rows: CUSIP 767754104 under RAD to 20190422 (last $0.49), CUSIP 767754872 under RAD from 20190423 (first $10.75).
- 2023-10-16: Rite Aid filed chapter 11; the restructuring term sheet cancels existing equity with no recovery ([SEC 8-K 2023-10-16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/84129/000110465923109236/tm2328505d1_8k.htm)).
- 2023-10-31: NYSE Form 25-NSE (Rule 12d2-2(b), section 802.01D); the common was suspended after the 2023-10-16 determination and removed at the open on 2023-11-13 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/84129/000114331323000135/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows: RAD to 20231017, then RADCQ on the same CUSIP 767754872 from 20231018.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. The holders kept the same shares, which traded off-exchange under RADCQ; the listing ended.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder found no Form 25 for the first listing run and used the continued-filings fallback (reason "Continued 10-K/Q filings >180d", flags `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`). The CUSIP change from the reverse split (767754104 to 767754872) ended the security's CUSIP history at 2019-04-22 and was read as the end. The real Form 25 is four years later. Cause: `continued_filings_guess`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat a reverse split's CUSIP change as the same security (not an ending); extend cusip_history with 767754872 and ticker_history to 2023-10-16; end the security at the 2023-10-16 bankruptcy drop with `otc_print` on RADCQ.
- Open: an exchange print for the last RAD trade day (2023-10-16 vs 2023-10-13 if trading was halted at the open on 10-16). The fails rows suggest 10-16.
- Golden case: yes, a reverse split with a CUSIP change that the library read as an ending, with a later bankruptcy drop.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic pass: tried to refute each field the report calls wrong, from SEC filings and the fails data (sec.py).

- **exit_kind (dropped): upheld.** The 2019 event is a reverse split, not an ending: 8-K 2019-04-18 items 3.03/5.03 states a 1-for-20 reverse split effective 5:00 p.m. ET on 2019-04-18 (0001104659-19-022372). No Form 25 or Form 15 appears in Rite Aid's filings from 2019-04-01 to 2019-06-30. Fails rows show the stock still listed as RAD under the new CUSIP 767754872 (RAD rows 2020-07-01..2020-07-31, $15-17). The real ending is the NYSE removal: the Form 25-NSE of 2023-10-31 (0001143313-23-000135, EX-99.25 notice) removes the common stock at the open on 2023-11-13 under Rule 12d2-2(b), Section 802.01D, "after the Company's October 16, 2023 disclosure" of the chapter 11 petitions, noting the term sheet gives equity "no recovery". This matches the decision table's bankruptcy row (`dropped` / `bankruptcy`). One imprecision in section 4: the Form 25 face page does not mention chapter 11; the EX-99.25 notice does. The conclusion is unchanged.
- **last_trade_date: upheld that the library is wrong; the replacement date 2023-10-16 is inferred, not confirmed.** The EX-99.25 notice says the NYSE "determined" on 2023-10-16 that the stock "should be suspended from trading" and does not say whether that was before the open. Under decision 12, a pre-open suspension would make 2023-10-13 the last trade. Fails data favour 2023-10-16: CUSIP 767754872 has RAD rows through 20231017 (priced at the 10-16 close) and RADCQ rows from 20231018 (priced at the 10-17 close), with no RADCQ row dated 20231017. This is indirect evidence. Without an exchange print the contract date stays unpublished, so "inferred" is the right confidence. The library's 2019-04-22 guess is clearly wrong.
- **value_rule (otc_print on RADCQ, price_date 2023-10-17): upheld.** The library's `transfer` / 0.0 fill is wrong because the holders lost the listing and the term sheet gives equity no recovery. Decision 11 gives the first off-exchange print. The same CUSIP 767754872 trades as RADCQ from 20231018 (34 rows to 20231229), so the symbol is right. The price date depends on the last-trade caveat above.
- **ticker_history (RAD 2007-12-18..2023-10-16, CUSIP 767754872 from 2019-04-23): upheld.** The library's ticker_history ends 2019-04-22 and its cusip_history holds only 767754104 (to 2019-04-22). Fails rows show 767754104 under RAD to 20190422, then 767754872 under RAD from 20190423 with description "RITE AID CORP COM PAR $1.00", continuing to 2023-10-17. A same-issuer CUSIP change from a reverse split is the same security. The end date shares the 10-16 versus 10-13 caveat.
- Open items unchanged: an exchange print or NYSE notice stating the last RAD trading day; the first RADCQ print. The overall verdict stands (cause `continued_filings_guess`).
