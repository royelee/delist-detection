# BBG000DST2V3_2012-06-04 (EP, El Paso Corp)

## 1. Verdict

A real merger: El Paso was acquired by Kinder Morgan (KMI) effective before the open on 2012-05-25, so the last trade was 2012-05-24. Each share became cash plus KMI Class P shares plus KMI warrants (election deal). The library's kind, date and rule are right; its terms (14.53 cash, 0.4231 KMI) do not match the package any filing I read states (mixed: 14.65 cash, 0.4187 KMI, plus 0.640 warrants), and its 0.0 is only an assumed-par fill.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (the report calls the library's 14.53 / 0.4231 wrong). Result: refuted in its main claim, partly upheld on the gaps.

- Refuted: sections 1, 4 and 7 say 14.53 / 0.4231 is "not in the filings read" and a `terms_misread`. The Form 8-K of 2012-05-30 filed for the successor (El Paso Holdco LLC, CIK 1066107, accession 0001193125-12-253764, [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066107/000119312512253764/d360854d8k.htm)) gives the final election results: 76.4% of shares elected stock, 0.8% cash, 7.9% mixed, 14.9% no election (they receive the mixed package). "Because it was oversubscribed, the Stock Consideration underwent a proration adjustment, and holders ... electing the Stock Consideration will receive, on a rounded basis, 0.4231 of a share of Kinder Morgan Class P common stock, $14.53 in cash without interest and 0.640 of a warrant". So the library's figures are the final prorated stock-election package, a real filed number, not an LLM misreading. The report's open check (is 14.53 / 0.4231 the final proration?) is answered: yes, for stock electors, about 76% of shares.
- The report's own figures are right but are the other package: the 8-K of 2012-05-24 (0001193125-12-246141) states 0.4187 KMI + USD 14.65 + 0.640 warrant for the mixed election, which after proration applies to mixed and no-election holders (about 22.8% of shares). Naming it the correct term and the library's wrong is not supported; the deal has several packages and the dominant final one is the library's.
- Upheld: the library's `cash_currency` is blank (the filing says USD), and the 0.640 KMI warrant leg has no field, so the payout leaves out part of what a holder received.
- Not re-opened: exit kind, last trade date, rule and issuer. The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-12-000250) shows effectiveness date 2012-05-25, consistent with the report.

Corrected `terms` status: cash and ratio agree (final prorated stock-election package); currency missing; warrant leg missing. Cause `terms_misread` is refuted; `currency_missing` fits better. The report's fix item "publish 14.65 / 0.4187" should not be applied. Why `terms_gate` failed is not explained by a misread and needs a separate look at the gate.
