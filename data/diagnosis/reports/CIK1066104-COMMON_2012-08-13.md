# CIK1066104-COMMON_2012-08-13 (EXBD, The Corporate Executive Board Co)

## 1. Verdict
Not a delisting. The Corporate Executive Board Company kept the same common stock (CUSIP 21988R102) and changed its ticker from EXBD to CEB on 2012-08-14. The library's ending (exchange transfer, 304, from the continued-filings rule) is invented: no Form 25 exists and the issuer never stopped trading or filing.

- Exit kind: fails rows (CUSIP 21988R102 continues under CEB) plus filing (FY2012 10-K says shares trade under "CEB"); the date of the symbol change is not read from a filing.
- Successor: filing (10-K: same company, same common stock); no successor line exists.
- Last trade date: not applicable; the symbol switch date comes from fails rows.

- Terms: none.
Not backed: a filing or press release stating the symbol change date (and whether the exchange moved from Nasdaq to NYSE; the 2012-08-02 8-K press release still says "NYSE: EXBD", the 10-K cover lists NYSE). An 8-K or press release of Aug 2012 announcing the CEB symbol would make it verified.

No Form 25 was found, so the continued-filings rule (`continued_filings_rule`, flags `no_form25;delist_date_approx;successor_unknown`) invented a 304 exchange transfer at the ticker's last sighting. The rename was not followed through the fails rows. Cause: `rename_not_followed`.

## 9. Verification
Skeptic re-check of the four fields the report calls wrong. Sources: `sec.py filings 1066104` (2012-06..2013-06), `sec.py fails --cusip 21988R102`, `sec.py grep` on the 10-Q and 10-K.
- **exit_kind: upheld.** No Form 25, 25-NSE or Form 15 appears in the issuer's filings 2012-06..2013-06. Fails rows show CUSIP 21988R102 under EXBD 2012-07-02..2012-08-13 and under CEB 2012-08-14..2012-09-28 (last $53.35), so the same security kept trading. The FY2012 10-K says the common stock is listed on the NYSE as "CEB" ([SEC 10-K 2013-03-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066104/000119312513087660/d459933d10k.htm)). A rename is not an ending (reference.md, "Rename / ticker change"). The 10-K also says EXBD was NYSE-quoted before the change, so there was no exchange move either.
- **value_rule: upheld.** With no ending there is no payout; `transfer` (0.0) should not be published.
- **last_trade_date: upheld, with a correction to the report's detail.** The library's 2012-08-10 is not a real last trade of the security, because the security kept trading, so the correct published value is blank. The report's "EXBD last traded 2012-08-13" is refuted. The 10-K Item 5 states "Since August 13, 2012, our common stock has been quoted on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol 'CEB.'" Fails rows dated D are priced at the close of D-1 (trading_calendar), so the first CEB row dated 2012-08-14 reflects trading on 2012-08-13. The last EXBD day is 2012-08-10, which matches the library's date as the last day under the old ticker.
- **ticker_history: upheld, with the same correction.** The library clips the history at 2012-08-10 with no CEB range, which is wrong. The corrected history is EXBD to 2012-08-10, then CEB from 2012-08-13 (the 10-K's date), on the same sec_id. The report's "EXBD to 2012-08-13, CEB from 2012-08-14" is one trading day late. Section 3's `effective_date` of 2012-08-14 should read 2012-08-13, and the date is now filing-backed (10-K Item 5), not only fails-row-backed.

Overall: the verdict (not a delisting, a rename on the same CUSIP) stands. Confidence is better than the report's `inferred` for the symbol-change date, which the 10-K now states. The cause tag `rename_not_followed` stands.
