# BBG000BTN971_2012-10-20 (SUN, Sunoco Inc.)

## 1. Verdict

A real cash-and-stock merger: Energy Transfer Partners (ETP) acquired Sunoco on 2012-10-05, and each SUN share became $25.00 cash plus 0.5245 ETP common units (the Standard Mix). The library's kind, date, rule and terms are right; only the cash currency is missing, and the 0.0 return is an assumed-par fill that its failed payout gate left in place.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2012-10-11 item 2.01: ETP completed the acquisition on 2012-10-05).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Merger Sub merged into Sunoco, holders received cash and units; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out (MIDAS last volume 2012-10-04 per the library, one day before the 2012-10-05 effective time); no cited filing states the last trading day.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01, Standard Mix).
- Terms: filing (8-K item 2.01: $25.00 and 0.5245 ETP common unit).
- Not backed by a filing: the last trade day. An exchange print or the 8-K / notice stating the last day would make it verified. ETP's first price day 2012-10-05 follows the effective date.

## 5. What happened

- ETP completed the acquisition on 2012-10-05; each share converted into $25.00 cash and 0.5245 ETP common unit (Standard Mix); elections of $50.00 cash or 1.0490 units were available, subject to proration ([SEC 8-K 2012-10-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/95304/000119312512421137/d421330d8k.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2012-10-10, removal at opening of business 2012-10-22; shares came to evidence other securities (the right to cash) ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/95304/000087666112000632/)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2012-10-22 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/95304/000119312512430261/d427012d1512b.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Terms and rule match the filing. The row is uncertain because the payout gate failed (`payout_gate_failed:25`, `llm_gate_failed`, `merger_at_par`): the regex payout saw only the $25 cash leg against a 46.75 close, and the LLM terms' gate did not reconcile (the library has no ETP close for it, `terms_gate=failed`), so it fell back to assumed par (0.0). The cash currency is also blank. Cause tag: `currency_missing` (closest; the gate failure is the visible symptom).
