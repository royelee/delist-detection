## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2011-04-08, items 2.01/3.01/5.01: acquisition completed, shares converted into cash and a CVR).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Genzyme is the surviving corporation, a subsidiary of sanofi-aventis; no new line).
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K and the Form 25-NSE (filed and effective 2011-04-08) state no last-trade day; fails rows end 2011-04-01. Not backed by an exchange print.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: $74.00 cash plus one CVR per share).
- Terms: filing (8-K) for cash and CVR; the currency (USD) follows from the US$ price.
- To make it verified: an exchange print or a filing/notice stating the final Nasdaq trading day (the suspension date in the Form 25's EX-99.25 text, MIDAS does not cover 2011).

## 5. What happened

- On 2011-04-08 sanofi-aventis completed its acquisition of Genzyme through an exchange offer, then a merger; shares not tendered were converted into $74.00 cash plus one CVR, as tendering holders received ([SEC 8-K 2011-04-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/732485/000095012311034216/b85960e8vk.htm)).
- A top-up option for 16,245,894 shares at $76.33 was exercised the same day (same 8-K).
- Nasdaq filed the notification of removal from listing on 2011-04-08 at Genzyme's request ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/732485/000135445711000080/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml); same 8-K, Item 3.01). Its effectiveness date is stated as 2011-04-08.
- Fails rows for CUSIP 372917104 run to 2011-04-01 (last about $76) (fails data, offline).
