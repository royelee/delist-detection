# CIK314808-CLASS-A_2019-04-11 (ESV, Ensco plc Class A)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending. CIK 314808 (Ensco, renamed Ensco Rowan plc, then Valaris plc) is the ACQUIRER of Rowan Companies, not the target. Its Class A ordinary shares kept trading, first as ESV, then as VAL on the NYSE. The library read the acquirer's own 8-K item 2.01 (the completion of the Rowan deal) as the end of ESV and published a merger with 2.75 x ESV, which is Rowan holders' ratio. The row is wrong and should not exist.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (the 8-K of 2019-04-11 says "the Company acquired ... Rowan", so 314808 is the buyer; the 8-K of 2019-07-31 shows the same registrant, same file number 1-8097, as Valaris plc with Class A shares on the NYSE).
- Successor: filing (same CIK, EIN and class, now VAL).
- Last trade date: not applicable; the day ESV became VAL is not stated in anything I read.
- Payout rule: filing (no consideration for 314808 holders in the 2.01 text).
- Terms: filing (the 2.75 ratio belongs to Rowan holders).
- Not backed: the exact date the symbol changed ESV to VAL and whether the CUSIP changed. Fails rows under G3157S106 for 2019-07/08 returned nothing. An 8-K or exchange notice giving the first VAL trading day would settle it.

## 5. What happened

- Rowan acquisition closed 2019-04-11 by scheme of arrangement; Ensco acquired all Rowan shares ([SEC 8-K 2019-04-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314808/000110465919020867/a19-7374_18k.htm)). The registrant was already named Ensco Rowan plc.
- Name change to Valaris plc, ticker VAL on the NYSE for the Class A ordinary shares ([SEC 8-K 2019-07-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314808/000031480819000134/form8k_item801namechange.htm)).
- No Form 25 or Form 15 by CIK 314808 between 2019-03 and 2019-09 (filings list showed only 8-Ks).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same CIK and Class A shares, renamed and re-ticked. The deal was an acquisition BY this issuer.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The end-of-era resolver ("Completed acquisition (8-K item 2.01 ...), DEFM14A; the registrant kept filing after it", `resolved_from_continued_filings`) treats any 2.01 plus merger filings as the issuer being acquired, without checking whether the issuer is the buyer. The LLM then read Rowan's exchange ratio (2.75 ESV per Rowan share) as ESV's own terms (`terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`). Cause tag: `other:acquirer_read_as_target`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Resolver: for 8-K 2.01 / DEFM14A, require the issuer to be the target (the 2.01 text says "the Company acquired" means acquirer); otherwise no ending. Follow the rename to VAL so ticker_history continues.
- Confirm the first VAL trading day and the CUSIP (not a price).
- Golden case: yes. Acquirer's own 2.01 plus a rename must not become a merger.

## 9. Verification

Re-opened the 2019-04-11 8-K (0001104659-19-020867), the 2019-07-31 8-K (0000314808-19-000134) and the CIK's filing list 2019-03 to 2020-12 through sec.py. Result: every field the report calls wrong is upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K item 2.01 says "the Company consummated the transactions ... the Company acquired the entire issued and to be issued ordinary share capital of Rowan", so CIK 314808 (registrant Ensco Rowan plc, file no. 1-8097) is the buyer. No Form 25, 25-NSE, 15 or 8-K12B by this CIK from 2019-04-12 to 2020-12-31 (only 8-Ks). The library's rule (2.01 plus DEFM14A means the issuer was acquired) misreads the buyer's own 2.01.
- **successor: upheld.** The 2019-07-31 8-K shows the same registrant (1-8097, EIN 98-0635229) as Valaris plc with Class A ordinary shares listed as VAL on the NYSE. Same CIK, same file number, same class: the security continues (successor = itself).
- **last_trade_date: upheld, with a caveat.** The library's blank is consistent with "no ending" in that no last trade exists; the report marks it wrong because the row should not exist. The ESV to VAL switch day is still unconfirmed (the 7-31 8-K only shows VAL as the ticker by then). Not a refutation, an open check.
- **value_rule: upheld.** The 8-K says each Rowan Class A share became 2.750 Class A ordinary shares of the Company. 2.75 is Rowan holders' ratio, not ESV holders' terms; ESV holders get no consideration.
- **terms: upheld.** Same text: ratio 2.75 belongs to Rowan holders. The library's `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity` was the gate catching a wrong reading.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** ESV/Class A is not clipped at 2019-04-11. Its range runs on under VAL (NYSE, 2019-07-31 8-K).
- **Side note, not a refutation.** The 2019-07-31 8-K shows Class A par value $0.40, against $0.10 in the 2019-04-11 8-K. The shares were consolidated (4 to 1) in between, so a price series across this period needs a split adjustment. The security is still the same line, so the verdict stands.
