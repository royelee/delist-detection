Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2015-07-24, items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01: merger completed, Nasdaq delisting requested).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: DIRECTV merged into a subsidiary of AT&T; no continuing line).

Not backed: the last trading day by a filing. An exchange print or a filing stating it would make this verified.

## 5. What happened

- At the Effective Time DIRECTV merged into Steam Merger Sub LLC (now DIRECTV Group Holdings), a wholly owned AT&T subsidiary; each share became 1.892 AT&T shares plus $28.50 cash ([SEC 8-K 2015-07-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1465112/000090951815000217/mm07-2415_8k.htm)).
- Item 3.01: DIRECTV asked NASDAQ to file Form 25; the surviving company would file Form 15 (same 8-K).
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2015-07-24 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1465112/000135445715000135/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows for CUSIP 25490A309: DTV through 2015-07-27 (last price $93.55), then deleted symbol A309PS on 2015-07-28 (SEC fails-to-deliver data via `sec.py fails`).
