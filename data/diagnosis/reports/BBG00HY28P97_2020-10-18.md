# BBG00HY28P97_2020-10-18: Garrett Motion (GTX), NYSE delisting after Chapter 11

## 1. Verdict

A real bankruptcy drop: Garrett filed Chapter 11 on 2020-09-20, the NYSE suspended GTX on 2020-09-21 (last trade 2020-09-18) and removed it by Form 25-NSE. The old common stock was later cancelled and replaced by new common stock under the plan. The library's exit kind, drop reason, last trade date and value rule are right; the price symbol (GTX, should be the OTC symbol GTXMQ), the Shumway fill and the ticker history that runs on past the ending are the problems.

## 5. What happened

- Chapter 11 petitions filed 2020-09-20, with a restructuring support agreement ([SEC 8-K 2020-09-21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1735707/000119312520249717/d35574d8k.htm)).
- NYSE indefinitely suspended trading on 2020-09-21; Garrett did not appeal ([SEC 8-K 2020-10-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1735707/000119312520267359/d928862d8k.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2020-10-08 under rule 12d2-2(b), removal at the opening of business 2020-10-19, citing Section 802.01D ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1735707/000087666120000900/ruleprovisionnotice.htm)).
- Fails rows under GTXMQ (CUSIP 366505105) run 2020-09-24 to 2020-10-30 (fails rows).
- Plan effective 2021-04-30: existing common stock cancelled; holders not electing the Cash-Out Option received new common stock one for one; Series A preferred issued ([SEC 8-K 2021-04-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1735707/000119312521145249/d172869d8k.htm)).
- New common listed on Nasdaq under GTX (8-A12B 2021-05-03) ([SEC 8-A12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1735707/000119312521146478/d354704d8a12b.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost (bankruptcy, then OTC): the old shares were suspended, traded OTC as GTXMQ and were later cancelled; the post-plan stock is a new security.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The ending itself is right. Uncertainty comes from `issuer_from_todays_ticker_map`, a `last_trade_date_conflict` flag (text 2020-09-21 vs MIDAS 2020-09-18), and the price symbol: the rule is priced on GTX rather than the OTC symbol GTXMQ, and the dlret is a Shumway fill (-0.30) until the OTC print is applied. The ticker history keeps one sec_id through the OTC period and the 2021 Nasdaq relisting. Cause tag: `other:price_ticker_not_otc_symbol_and_history_past_ending`.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: terms (the one field the report calls wrong).

- terms: **upheld**. The library publishes price_ticker GTX; the reference rule for otc_print says price_ticker is the security's own OTC symbol and price_date the session after the last exchange trade. The 8-K of 2020-10-09 ([SEC 8-K 2020-10-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1735707/000119312520267359/d928862d8k.htm)) states that trading "commenced, effective as of September 21, 2020, in the over-the-counter market under the symbol GTXMQ". This is stronger than the report's fails-row basis, and it also supports price_date 2020-09-21 (Monday after the 2020-09-18 last trade). Fails rows for CUSIP 366505105 agree: GTX 2020-08-05..2020-09-22, then GTXMQ 2020-09-24..2020-11-25.
- Correction to section 5: the GTXMQ fails rows run to 2020-11-25, not 2020-10-30. This does not affect the finding.
- Confidence note: the symbol is now filing-backed, not only fails-backed, so the terms line could be rated filing rather than fails rows. The OTC print itself remains qlib_practice's.
- Not re-checked: ticker_history (report marks it unknown; the library's rows GTX to 2020-09-23, GTXMQ to 2021-05-03, GTX Nasdaq from 2021-05-04 under one sec_id match the report's description).
