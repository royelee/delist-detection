## 5. What happened

- The merger became effective before the market open on 2024-11-27; each Vista Outdoor share was exchanged for one Revelyst share and USD 25.75 in cash ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2024-11-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616318/000087666124001115/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The 8-K confirms the conversion into the right to receive one Revelyst share and USD 25.75 cash per share ([SEC 8-K 2024-11-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616318/000161631824000122/vsto-20241127.htm)).
- Revelyst began trading on the NYSE as "GEAR" on 2024-11-27; its sale to SVP for an all-cash USD 1.125 bn enterprise value was agreed on 2024-10-04 and expected to close by January 2025 (same 8-K filing's exhibit).
- Form 15-12G was filed 2024-12-09 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616318/000095015724001657/15-12g.htm)).
- The Vista Outdoor stockholders approved the merger on 2024-11-25 ([SEC 8-K 2024-11-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616318/000161631824000116/vsto-20241125.htm)).

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (the only field the report calls wrong). **Upheld.**

- Cash leg: the 8-K item 2.01 of 2024-11-27 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616318/000161631824000122/vsto-20241127.htm)) says each Vista Outdoor share "was converted into the right to receive (a) one fully paid and non-assessable share of common stock ... of Revelyst and (b) $25.75 in cash". The Form 25-NSE ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1616318/000087666124001115/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)) says "One (1) share of Revelyst, Inc. Common Stock and USD 25.75". Cash 25.75 and ratio 1.0 match the library. The currency is USD (stated in the Form 25); the library's `cash_currency` is blank.
- Price security: the library's `price_ticker` and `price_sec_id` are blank (source row), so the formula reads `price(?, 2024-11-27)`. The 8-K names Revelyst as "NYSE: GEAR". The stock leg therefore needs GEAR, and the library publishes none. This is consistent with `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker` and the assumed-par fill (decision 4).
- Date: the 25-NSE says the merger became effective before the market open on 2024-11-27 and the security was suspended on 2024-11-27, so `price_date` 2024-11-27 and a last trade of 2024-11-26 are consistent with the reference rule.
- Class and rule: the consideration is common stock plus cash with no election, so `cash_plus_stock` is right. Revelyst is a distinct company's stock (spin-off), so this is not a continuation.
- Not independently checked: the exact first-trade date of GEAR (the report relies on the press-release exhibit). It does not change the terms, because `price_date` follows from the last-trade rule.

No field is refuted. The cause tag `terms_misread` is reasonable; `terms_not_extracted` (a missing leg) would also fit.
