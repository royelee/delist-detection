# ROVI (Rovi Corp), case BBG000BJ9D07_2016-09-08

## 1. Verdict

Not a cash or stock merger for Rovi holders: Rovi merged with a subsidiary of a new holding company (TiVo Corporation, formerly Titan Technologies) and each Rovi share became one share of that holdco, with no cash. By decision 9 this is a continuation (1:1 holdco reorganization) with a successor link. The library's row (merger, stock rule, 1 x TIVO, assumed par) is wrong in kind and rule.

## 5. What happened

- 2016-04-28 merger agreement among Rovi, TiVo Inc., Parent (Titan Technologies, renamed TiVo Corporation), Nova and Titan; on 2016-09-07 Nova merged into Rovi and Titan into TiVo, both surviving as subsidiaries ([SEC 8-K 2016-09-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424454/000119312516704222/d234513d8k.htm)).
- Each Rovi share converted into the right to receive one share of Parent common stock (same 8-K, supplemental indenture recital).
- Rovi's convertible notes became convertible into Parent stock at the same rate, guaranteed by Parent (same 8-K).
- Rovi filed Form 15-12G on 2016-09-09 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424454/000119312516706532/d256054d1512g.htm)); no Form 25 by Rovi.
- Fails rows: ROVI CUSIP 779376102 to 2016-09-09 (last $22.42); TIVO CORPORATION CUSIP 88870P106 from 2016-09-09; old TiVo Inc. (888706108) ends 2016-09-08 (sec.py fails).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the three fields the report calls wrong, against the cited Rovi 8-K ([SEC 8-K, accession 0001193125-16-704222](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424454/000119312516704222/d234513d8k.htm)) and `reference.md` (decision 9).

- **exit_kind (merger -> exchange): upheld.** Item 5.01 states that on September 7, 2016 Nova merged into Rovi (Rovi surviving) and Titan into TiVo, and that "each issued and outstanding share of Rovi Common Stock was converted into one fully paid and nonassessable share of Parent Common Stock"; Rovi and TiVo each became wholly owned by Parent (TiVo Corporation, formerly Titan Technologies Corporation). The text names no cash leg for Rovi holders. By the reference's "no cash in the exchange" test and its 1:1 holdco rule, this is a continuation, which maps to `exchange`. TiVo Inc.'s holders' cash is a different security and does not bear on Rovi's.
- **successor (none -> TiVo Corporation, not in the run): upheld.** The same 8-K names Parent (TiVo Corporation) as the issuer of the replacement share. The library has no successor link. The CUSIP 88870P106 and the fails-row start of 2016-09-09 were not re-checked here and rest on the report's fails lookup.
- **value_rule (stock -> continuation): upheld.** A 1:1 holdco exchange has no value to publish, so `continuation` replaces the library's `stock` (1 x TIVO, failed gate, assumed par). The ratio 1.0 itself is correct.
- **Not re-verified:** `last_trade_date` stays "unknown" as the report says, since no filing states the last ROVI session; the exchange print the report asks for is still open.

Residual doubt: nothing in the filing gives Rovi holders anything beyond the one-for-one share, so the continuation reading holds even though Rovi was a party of comparable size.
