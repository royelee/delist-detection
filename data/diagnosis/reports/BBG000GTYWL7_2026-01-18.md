## 9. Verification
Checked by a skeptic against the filings through sec.py (filings list, Form 25 text, 20-F text, offline fails rows).

- **exit_kind (no ending): upheld.** Both Form 25-NSE filings (0000876661-26-000016 of 2026-01-08, 0000876661-26-000033 of 2026-01-09) name Common Shares on the NYSE, filed by the exchange under 12d2-2(b). The 20-F FY2025 (0001015820-26-000018, filed 2026-03-20) still lists "Common Shares ... QGEN New York Stock Exchange" on its cover and says the shares "are listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol QGEN". It describes the synthetic share repurchase (capital repayment plus reverse split) announced 2025-12-18 and executed 2026-01-08. Fails rows: QGEN N72482206 (17 rows, 20251201..20260108), then QGEN N72482156 "SYNTHETIC SH REPURCH" (29 rows, 20260109..20260317). The symbol kept trading after both Form 25s, so no ending.
- **successor (the security itself): upheld.** Same symbol, issuer and listing; only the CUSIP changed. The reference allows "not an ending, or exchange with successor = itself".
- **last_trade_date (none): upheld.** The library's 2026-01-07 is an ex99_notice date for the old-CUSIP line only; the symbol traded on. With no ending, no last trade date applies.
- **value_rule (continuation, no payout): upheld with a caveat.** No share was surrendered for cash and the shares remain listed, so no exit value applies. The roughly $496.7M capital repayment (20-F) is a distribution to all holders of the continuing shares, but decision 9's "same holders, one for one, no cash" is not literally met (reverse split with cash, new CUSIP). Recording "not an ending, capital repayment is a distribution" is safer than asserting `continuation=true`. The conclusion that the library's `unknown`/assumed-par row is wrong is unaffected.
- **Not backed (as the report says):** why the NYSE filed the Form 25 (the rule-provision box is not readable in the text); the 6-K press release was not re-read. Confidence `inferred` stands.

Result: all four fields upheld; the `continuation=true` label carries the caveat above.
