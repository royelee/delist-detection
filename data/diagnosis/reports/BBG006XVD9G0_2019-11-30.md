# GCI (old Gannett Co., Inc., CIK 1635718) – case BBG006XVD9G0_2019-11-30

## 1. Verdict

A real merger: New Media Investment Group acquired Gannett on 2019-11-19; each Gannett share became $6.25 cash plus 0.5427 New Media shares. New Media then took the name Gannett Co., Inc. and the ticker GCI, which is why the ticker overlaps. The library's kind, date, cash and ratio are right; only the stock leg's price security is missing (it reads `price(?)`), which is why the payout gate failed and the fill is assumed par.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2019-11-20 item 2.01 completion of the merger; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (8-K: Gannett survives as a subsidiary of New Media; holders receive cash and shares, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: suspended from trading on Nov 20, so the last trade was Nov 19).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01 merger consideration).
- Terms: filing (8-K: 0.5427 share plus $6.25; EX-99.1: New Media ticker becomes GCI on Nov 20).

## 5. What happened

- Effective Time was 12:20 pm Eastern on 2019-11-19; each Gannett share became 0.5427 New Media share plus $6.25 cash; New Media renamed itself Gannett Co., Inc., and Gannett became Gannett Media Corp. ([SEC 8-K 2019-11-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1635718/000119312519295996/d722337d8k.htm)).
- New Media (NYSE: NEWM) traded as GCI from 2019-11-20 (same 8-K, EX-99.1).
- The NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2019-11-20: shares converted to $6.25 and 0.5427 New Media share; suspended from trading Nov 20 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1635718/000087666119001159/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
