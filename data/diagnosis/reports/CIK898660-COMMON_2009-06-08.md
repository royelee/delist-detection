## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2007-11-08, items 2.01-style merger text under 3.01/3.03/5.01).
- Successor: filing (cash merger, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out, not read. The 8-K says the merger closed and NYSE was notified 2007-11-07; fails rows for CUSIP 857689103 end 2007-11-08 at $90.00. No filing states the last session.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: converted into the right to receive $90.00 cash).
- Terms: filing (same sentence).
Not backed: the exact last trade day. A Form 25-NSE notice date or an exchange print would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- The merger closed 2007-11-07; shares converted to $90.00 cash, NYSE asked to file Form 25 ([SEC 8-K 2007-11-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/898660/000110465907081288/a07-28636_18k.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-11-08 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/898660/000087666107000873/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows for Station Casinos CUSIP 857689103 under STN end 2007-11-08 at $90.00; STN rows from 2008-08 are Stantec (CUSIP 85472N109) (fails data).
- The issuer kept filing afterward because of public debt (10-K/Q), which triggered the continued-filings fallback.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced by cash (going private / merger), not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The caller's observations of STN start 2008-01-16, after the Form 25 (2007-11-08), so the finder never saw the real Form 25 and fell back to `continued_filings_rule` (304) on the last sighting 2009-06-08 (flags no_form25, delist_date_approx, last_trade_date_unconfirmed). The 2008-2009 sightings are stale or another security (Stantec). Cause tag: other:form25_before_first_sighting.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- When a security's first sighting follows a Form 25 for its issuer's same class, place the ending at that Form 25 (2007-11-08, effective ~2007-11-18), not at the last sighting; flag the later observations as after_delisting. Publish cash 90.00 USD.
- Open: the exact last session (expected 2007-11-07); the identity of the 2008-2009 observations (Stantec vs caller's stale name).
- Golden case: yes, a going-private merger with observations only after the delisting.

## 9. Verification

Re-checked through sec.py (filings list, 8-K text, Form 25-NSE header, fails rows) and the library's own tables.

- **exit_kind (library: exchange): upheld.** The 8-K filed 2007-11-08 (accession 0001104659-07-081288, items 1.01, 1.02, 2.03, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01, 5.02, 5.03, 9.01) says Merger Sub merged into the Company on 2007-11-07 (Item 5.01) and each common share was converted into the right to receive $90.00 without interest (Items 3.01, 3.03). That is a cash merger, not an exchange transfer or continuation. The library's 2009-06-08 ending rests only on the continued-filings fallback. Minor error in section 4: the 8-K has no Item 2.01; the merger evidence is Items 3.01, 3.03 and 5.01.
- **value_rule (library: transfer; report: cash $90.00 USD): upheld.** The $90.00 cash, no stock leg, is stated in the same 8-K. Fails rows for CUSIP 857689103 (STN, 13 rows, 2007-03-01..2007-11-08) end at $90.00.
- **last_trade_date (library: blank, internal 2009-06-08 guess; report: 2007-11-07): upheld that the library's date is wrong, with a caveat.** The 8-K says the NYSE was notified on 2007-11-07; Form 25-NSE 0000876661-07-000873 was filed 2007-11-08. The last fails row is dated 2007-11-08 (carries the 2007-11-07 close, $90.00). No filing states the last session, so 2007-11-07 is inferred, not an exchange print; under decision 12 the contract would publish it blank. Also, sections 7 and 8 say the Form 25 was "effective ~2007-11-18"; the 25-NSE header shows EFFECTIVENESS DATE 20071108. That does not change the conclusion.
- **ticker_history (library: STN 2008-01-16 .. 2009-06-08, source observation): upheld.** `output/ticker_history.csv` holds that single row for CIK898660-COMMON. CUSIP 857689103 stops after 2007-11-08; STN fails rows from 2008-08-05 to 2009-06-30 are STANTEC INC. COM, CUSIP 85472N109. The seven observations (2008-01-16 on, name STATION CASINOS INC, no CUSIP) are stale or another security; the Station Casinos listing ended in 2007.

Left open: whether the 2008-2009 observations are Stantec (section 8). The issuer kept filing 10-K/8-K through 2009-11, consistent with the report's explanation of the fallback.
