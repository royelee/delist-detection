# EXE (Chesapeake Energy, CIK 895126), case BBG00Z6DX554_2020-07-30

## 1. Verdict

A real bankruptcy drop: Chesapeake filed Chapter 11 on 2020-06-28, the NYSE suspended the old common stock on 2020-06-29 (last exchange trade 2020-06-26) and it moved to OTC Pink as CHKAQ. The ending, kind and date are right; the library prices the OTC print on `CHK`, which is the post-bankruptcy stock (a different security), instead of `CHKAQ`, and its security line runs from the old stock through the new one.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2020-06-30 item 3.01 and the Form 25-NSE: NYSE delisting under 802.01D after the Chapter 11 filing).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2021-02-09: all prior equity interests cancelled without any distribution; New Common Stock issued to creditors).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K item 3.01: NYSE suspended trading 2020-06-29; Form 25 notice says the same; trading day before is 2020-06-26).

- Terms: filing (the same 8-K gives the symbol and start date); fails rows show CHKAQ rows from 2020-07-02.

## 5. What happened

- Chesapeake and subsidiaries filed Chapter 11 on 2020-06-28; the NYSE determined on 2020-06-29 to delist and indefinitely suspended trading that day; trading began 2020-06-30 on OTC Pink as CHKAQ ([SEC 8-K 2020-06-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895126/000089512620000131/a8-k2020x06x298xkreini.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2020-07-20 for the common stock and notes, removal effective at the open on 2020-07-31, citing Manual 802.01D ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895126/000087666120000569/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails-to-deliver rows exist under CHKAQ from 2020-07-02 to 2021-02-19 (SEC fails data, via sec.py).
- On emergence (2021-02-09) all equity interests outstanding before the effective date were cancelled without any distribution, and New Common Stock was issued to lenders and noteholders; an 8-A12B for the new stock was filed 2021-02-09 ([SEC 8-K 2021-02-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895126/000089512621000033/chk-20210209.htm), [SEC 8-A12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895126/000089512621000035/form8-axemergence.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. Old holders kept the old shares on OTC and they were later cancelled; the post-emergence stock is a new security.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The ending row is right, but the security `BBG00Z6DX554` was merged across the old CHK stock (CUSIP 165167107, to 2021-02-11) and the post-bankruptcy stock (CUSIP 165167735, from 2021-02-12) and later EXE, so its ticker_history runs 2007 to 2024 and the OTC print rule is priced on `CHK` from 2020-06-29, the new stock's ticker and a non-trading date. Flags `resolved_by_current_ticker_map`, `member_name_mismatch` and the issuer-from-today's-map reason reflect the same name/identity weakness. The `last_trade_date_conflict` flag has no bearing: the filing supports 2020-06-26. The dlret fill is a Shumway -0.30 although an OTC print exists. Cause tag: `identity_wrong`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Open: which composite FIGI the old stock holds (the run's one sec_id spans both).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the two fields section 2 calls wrong (SEC filings re-read through sec.py; fails data offline).

- **terms: upheld, with a qualification on the date.**
  - Ticker: the 8-K of 2020-06-30 (accession 0000895126-20-000131) says the NYSE suspended trading on 2020-06-29 and that trading "commenced, effective as of June 30, 2020, on the OTC Pink Market ... under the symbol CHKAQ". Fails rows under CHKAQ run 2020-07-02 to 2021-02-19 (CUSIP 165167743), so the security's own OTC symbol is CHKAQ, which the `otc_print` rule (reference.md) calls for. The library's `CHK` is wrong.
  - Correction to the report's reason: `CHK` is not only the post-bankruptcy stock. Fails rows show the old stock under CHK to 2020-07-01 (CUSIP 165167743) and the new stock under CHK from 2021-02-12 (CUSIP 165167735). The ticker is ambiguous between two securities.
  - Date: reference.md defines price_date as "the session after the last exchange trade" (2020-06-29, what the library publishes) and also as "the first OTC session" (2020-06-30, what the report publishes). The 8-K supports no OTC session on 06-29. The date is a convention call, not clearly wrong; the ticker is the defect.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library's own tables (`output/ticker_history.csv`, `cusip_history.csv`, `securities.csv`) hold one sec_id, BBG00Z6DX554, with CHK 2007-12-17 to 2024-10-02 then EXE, CUSIP 165167107 to 2021-02-11 then 165167735 from 2021-02-12, listed today. The 8-K of 2021-02-09 (0000895126-21-000033, Item 1.02) says pre-effective-date equity interests "were cancelled, released and extinguished without any distribution", so the post-2021 stock is a new security (decision 9). One line spanning both is wrong.
  - Small errors in the report's details: the old stock's final CUSIP was 165167743, not 165167107 (fails rows show 165167107 under CHK to 2020-04-15, then 165167743, after a 2020 CUSIP change); the new line's first fails row is 2021-02-12. Neither changes the verdict.
  - Open, as the report says: BBG00Z6DX554 may belong to the new stock only, so the old stock's own FIGI is unresolved.
- The fields marked agree were not re-examined beyond the 8-K text above, which supports the suspension on 2020-06-29.

Result: both wrong fields upheld; the terms finding is narrowed to the ticker.
