## 9. Verification

Skeptic check; all three fields upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The filings list for CIK 1336917 (2016-01-01..2026-12-31, forms 25, 25-NSE, 15, 15-12B, 15-12G, 8-A12B) holds only the 8-A12B of 2016-03-21 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336917/000119312516511236/d149367d8a12b.htm)); no Form 25 or 15 exists. The FY2026 10-K cover lists "Class C Common Stock UA New York Stock Exchange" ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336917/000133691726000073/ua-20260331.htm)). Fails rows for CUSIP 904311206 run 2016-04-12..2016-12-06 under UAC and 2016-12-08..2017-01-30 under UA, with no gap at 2016-06-30. A live, never-delisted class has no ending, so the library's exchange transfer is wrong.
- **value_rule: upheld.** With no ending there is no payout rule; the library's `transfer` (0.0 return, `exchange_transfer_zero`) is a fill on a non-event. Depends on exit_kind.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library has one day, UAC-C 2016-06-30..2016-06-30, on a placeholder sec_id. The fails rows show the class trading as UAC from 2016-04-12 and as UA from 2016-12-08, continuing to today per the 10-K. The one-day range is wrong. The exact FIGI was not checked.

Minor: the 8-A12B registers the new class; it is not a Form 25, so the transfer label has no Form 25 basis either. No web search was needed.
