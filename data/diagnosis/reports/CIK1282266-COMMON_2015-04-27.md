# CIK1282266-COMMON_2015-04-27 (WIN, Windstream Holdings)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending. On 2015-04-27 Windstream Holdings spun off Communications Sales & Leasing (CS&L) and did a 1-for-6 reverse split; WIN kept trading on Nasdaq under the same ticker, with a new CUSIP. The library's "exchange transfer" ending (continued-filings guess, no Form 25) is wrong: the security continues and the row should not exist.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2015-04-27 Items 1.01, 5.03: spin-off and 1-for-6 reverse split, no delisting; no Form 25 or Form 15 in the issuer's 2015-03 to 2015-07 filings list).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K; no successor registration, the issuer is unchanged).
- Last trade date: not applicable; fails rows show WIN under CUSIP 97382A200 trading 2015-04-29 to 2015-12-31.
- Payout rule: filing (the 8-K describes a pro rata distribution and a split, no consideration for the shares).
- Terms: filing (8-K: one CS&L share per five WIN shares, record date 2015-04-10, Nasdaq ex-date 2015-04-27).

## 5. What happened

- 2015-04-27: Holdings completed the spin-off of CS&L, one CS&L share per five Holdings shares, Nasdaq ex-date 2015-04-27 ([SEC 8-K 2015-04-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1282266/000119312515147334/d910911d8k.htm)).
- A Certificate of Amendment effected a 1-for-6 reverse split at 8:00 pm ET on 2015-04-26 (same 8-K, Item 5.03).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 97382A101 under WIN to 2015-04-27, then under the deleted symbol WINXXXX through 2015-05-01; new CUSIP 97382A200 under WIN from 2015-04-29 (first $11.91) to at least 2015-12-31 (SEC fails-to-deliver data).
- The issuer kept filing: 10-Q 2015-05-07, 8-K 2015-05-19 (Item 5.07) ([SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1282266/000128226615000021/a2015033110q.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same issuer, same ticker, same exchange. Only the CUSIP changed (reverse split) and a spin-off distribution was paid.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder found no Form 25 and fell back to the continued-filings rule (`continued_filings_rule`, crsp 304, flags `no_form25;delist_date_approx;last_trade_date_unconfirmed`). It read the CUSIP switch 97382A101 to 97382A200 and the retired symbol WINXXXX as the security ending, and placed the end on the date of the spin-off. Cause tag: `continued_filings_guess`. (A sibling effect: the new CUSIP was not followed, as in a rename; the CUSIP history lacks 97382A200.)

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Suppress the ending: a reverse split with a CUSIP change and the same ticker still trading is not a delisting. Extend `ticker_history` to the later last sighting and add CUSIP 97382A200 from 2015-04-29.
- The spin-off distribution (1 CS&L per 5 WIN) is a dividend-type event for the return; qlib_practice should adjust the price series, not treat it as an exit.
- Windstream's real later ending (2019 bankruptcy, delisted from Nasdaq) is outside this case and not checked here.
- Golden case: yes. A well-sourced reverse split plus spin-off with a new CUSIP and no Form 25 that a rule fix should flip to no ending.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the three fields the report calls wrong. All three upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K of 2015-04-27 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1282266/000119312515147334/d910911d8k.htm), Items 1.01, 2.01, 2.03, 5.02, 5.03, 8.01) confirms a 1-for-6 reverse split effective 8:00 pm ET 2015-04-26 and a spin-off of one CS&L share per five Holdings shares (record date 2015-04-10, Nasdaq ex-date 2015-04-27). The new CUSIP 97382A200 is stated in the filing. It reports no delisting. The filings list for 2015-03 to 2016-06 shows no Form 25, 25-NSE or Form 15. The issuer kept filing 10-Qs on 2015-05-07, 2015-08-06 and 2015-11-05. The 2015-09-18 8-A12B and 8-K (Items 1.01, 3.03, 5.03) is a Section 382 rights plan (rights dividend), not a new listing of the shares. Under reference.md, the same issuer, ticker and exchange with only a CUSIP change is not an ending. The library's row (304, `continued_filings_rule`, no Form 25) is a guess.
- **value_rule: upheld.** The row's `transfer` / `exchange_transfer_zero` fill assumes an exit that did not happen. The CS&L distribution and the reverse split are not consideration for surrendered shares, so no exit rule applies and `continuation` / no row is right. One wording gap in the report: the 8-K also shows a $0.0659 per share cash dividend (ex-date 2015-04-27) and cash in lieu of fractional shares. Both are distributions or split mechanics, not exit consideration, and do not change the verdict.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library's range ends 2015-04-27. Offline fails rows show CUSIP 97382A200 under WIN from 2015-04-29 (first $11.91, 165 rows to 2015-12-31), then under WIN from 2016-01-04 to 2018-05-29 (498 rows), and CUSIP 97382A309 under WIN from 2018-05-30 to 2018-12-27. The report's "to at least 2015-12-31" is therefore understated: the continuation runs to at least 2018-12-27. The library's `cusip_history` also lacks 97382A200 and 97382A309 (a second reverse split in 2018).

Net: the verdict (not an ending, the row should not exist) holds. Caveat: the report did not name the 2018 CUSIP change, and WIN's later real ending (2019) stays out of scope.
