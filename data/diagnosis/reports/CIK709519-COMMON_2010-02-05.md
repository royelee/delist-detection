## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2010-01-29, items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03: merger completed 2010-01-26).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Sun survives as a wholly owned subsidiary of Oracle, holders get cash).
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K says trading on NASDAQ was suspended on completion of the merger on 2010-01-26 and gives no last-trade day; the Form 25-NSE (filed 2010-01-26) shows effectiveness 2010-01-26. Not backed: an exchange print or a filing that states the last day (MIDAS starts 2012, FTD has no SUNW rows). A NASDAQ halt record or press release would make it `verified`.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01 and 3.03: $9.50 in cash, without interest).
- Terms: filing (same text; USD is the filer's currency, not stated beside the figure).

## 5. What happened

- Oracle completed the merger on 2010-01-26; each Sun share was converted into the right to receive $9.50 in cash ([SEC 8-K 2010-01-29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/709519/000119312510017049/d8k.htm)).
- Sun notified NASDAQ of completion; trading on the NASDAQ Global Select Market was suspended and NASDAQ filed Form 25 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- NASDAQ's Form 25-NSE was filed 2010-01-26 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/709519/000135445710000021/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Sun filed a Form 15-12G on 2010-02-05 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/709519/000119312510022780/d1512g.htm)). The library's delist_date 2010-02-05 is this date, not the Form 25.

## 9. Verification

Re-opened the 8-K (0001193125-10-017049) and the 25-NSE (0001354457-10-000021) through sec.py; checked the filings list for 2010-01-15..02-20.

- **last_trade_date (library blank; report calls it missing): upheld.** The library's `last_trade_date` is blank and the source row has `no_last_trade_date`. No filing names a last trading day. The 8-K item 3.01 says only that "in connection with the completion of the Merger on January 26, 2010, Sun has notified NASDAQ ... trading ... has been suspended". The report's 2010-01-26 is a plausible inference, not established: the 8-K does not say whether suspension was before the open on 2010-01-26 (which, by the reference rule, would make the last trade 2010-01-25) or after the close. It should stay marked inferred and not be published as an exchange-print date.
- **Correction to section 4 (not a field in dispute):** the 25-NSE does not show an effective date of 2010-01-26. 2010-01-26 is the filing and signature date. A Form 25 filed by the exchange takes effect 10 days later, which is consistent with the library's delist_date 2010-02-05 (the section 5 wording that this is the Form 15 date is also unsafe; the delist date matches the Form 25 effective date). This does not change the verdict.
- **exit_kind merger, drop_reason none, continuation false, successor none: upheld.** The 8-K item 2.01 says Oracle completed the acquisition on 2010-01-26 and Sun survived as a wholly owned subsidiary.
- **value_rule cash, cash_per_share 9.50, no stock leg: upheld.** The 8-K says each share was "converted into the right to receive $9.50 in cash, without interest". The USD currency is implied, not stated beside the figure.
- **issuer 709519: upheld.** The 8-K and the 25-NSE are both filed under CIK 709519 (Sun Microsystems).
- **ticker_history: stays unknown.** It was not re-checked.

Overall: the verdict (right kind and terms, only the last trade date missing, cause no_last_trade_print) is upheld. The one weak point is that the proposed date 2010-01-26 is unconfirmed.
