# CIK716006-COMMON_2010-10-04 (YRCW, YRC Worldwide Inc)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending. YRC Worldwide did a 1-for-25 reverse split effective on NASDAQ on 2010-10-01; the shares kept
trading on NASDAQ under YRCW with a new CUSIP (984249102 to 984249300). The library read the CUSIP change as the
end of the security and labelled a listing-deficiency drop (570); its row is wrong and should not exist.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2010-09-30 item 5.03: reverse split 1:25 effective on NASDAQ on 2010-10-01; no Form 25 in
  EDGAR for the issuer from 2010-08 through 2011-12; 8-K 2011-10-27 item 3.01 shows the stock still NASDAQ-listed and
  contesting delisting at a hearing, so the 2010 notices did not end the listing).
- Successor: filing (same issuer, same ticker, no successor registration).
- Last trade date: not applicable; the security did not stop trading (fails rows corroborate).
- Payout rule: filing (a reverse split pays nothing but cash for fractions).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K 5.03: ratio 1:25).

## 5. What happened

- 2010-03-03 NASDAQ sent a deficiency letter; 8-K item 3.01 of 2010-09-07 updates it
  ([SEC 8-K 2010-09-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/716006/000119312510205775/d8k.htm)).
- Board approved a 1-for-25 reverse split on 2010-09-29; certificate filed 2010-09-30; effective on NASDAQ on
  2010-10-01, fractions paid in cash
  ([SEC 8-K 2010-09-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/716006/000119312510220769/d8k.htm)).
- Fails rows (offline): CUSIP 984249102 as YRCW 2010-08-02 to 2010-10-04 (last price $0.25); CUSIP 984249300 as
  YRCW 2010-10-29 to 2011-12-02 (first price $4.57), a 1:25 rescaling.
- The stock was still NASDAQ-listed in 2011: staff determination 2011-07-22 and a hearings panel 2011-09-21
  ([SEC 8-K 2011-10-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/716006/000119312511284365/d248521d8k.htm)).
- A further 1-for-300 reverse split followed in December 2011
  ([SEC 8-K 2011-12-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/716006/000119312511327484/d263760d8k.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: a reverse split changes the share count and CUSIP, not the holder's claim; ticker and
exchange stay the same. No ending.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Its reason is the continued-filings fallback resolved from the 8-K 2010-09-07 listing-deficiency notice ("no merger
evidence; the registrant kept filing"); flags `no_form25`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`, `delist_date_approx`.
The security's only CUSIP in the run (984249102) stopped in the fails data on 2010-10-04 because the reverse split
issued a new CUSIP, and the run did not link 984249300 to the same issuer and ticker. The cause is the missed
CUSIP switch, read as the security ending. Cause tag: `other:reverse_split_cusip_change_read_as_ending`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat a same-ticker, same-issuer CUSIP switch at a dated reverse split (8-K 5.03) as the same security; extend
  its CUSIPs to 984249300 and keep the ticker history running. The 2010-10-04 row disappears.
- Open check: the security's real ending is after this case (the 15-12G of 2012-02-28,
  [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/716006/000119312512083443/d281462d1512g.htm), not examined here).
- Golden case: yes, a reverse split with a CUSIP change and a listing-deficiency notice that must not be read as a drop.

## 9. Verification

All four disputed fields upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** [SEC 8-K 2010-09-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/716006/000119312510220769/d8k.htm) item 5.03 says a one-to-25 reverse split "will be effective on NASDAQ on October 1, 2010", fractions cashed out. The EDGAR filing list for CIK 716006 (forms 25, 25-NSE, 15, 15-12G, 15-12B, 2010-08 to 2012-04) holds only the 15-12G of 2012-02-28, so no Form 25 and no deregistration in 2010. The 8-K of 2011-10-27 shows the common stock still NASDAQ-listed in 2011. A reverse split on the same ticker and exchange is no ending under the decision table. The 570 drop has no basis.
- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The fails rows show 984249102 under YRCW to 2010-10-04 (44 rows, last $0.25) and 984249300 from 2010-10-05 (261 rows YRCW 2010-10-29 to 2011-12-02, plus 17 rows 2010-10-05 to 2010-10-28 under YRCWD). Trading never stopped, so no last trade date applies. The rescale is consistent ($0.25 x 25 = $6.25 against a first new-CUSIP price of $5.44).
- **value_rule: upheld.** With no ending, `otc_print` has nothing to price. A reverse split pays cash only for fractions. `continuation` / not an exit stands.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The security trades past 2010-10-04 on the same issuer and ticker, so the library's range ending 2010-10-04 is too short.

Correction to section 5, not to the verdict: the new CUSIP 984249300 first traded under the temporary symbol YRCWD (2010-10-05 to 2010-10-28, the Nasdaq marker for a recent reverse split) and moved to YRCW on 2010-10-29. This does not change any field's status. It does help explain why the library's ticker/CUSIP link missed the switch.
