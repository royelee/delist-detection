## 5. What happened

- Merger Sub merged into AmeriCredit on 2010-10-01; each share converted into $24.50 cash without interest; shareholders approved on 2010-09-29 ([SEC 8-K 2010-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804269/000119312510221701/d8k.htm)).
- The company was to be renamed General Motors Financial Company, Inc. (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2010-10-01: merger effective at 12:01 A.M. on 2010-10-01, each share converted into $24.50 cash ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804269/000087666110000360/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The company kept filing afterwards (8-Ks 2010-10-21, 2010-11-09, 2010-12-14) because of its public debt, which is why a continued-filings rule fired.
