## 9. Verification

Upheld. Re-read the Form 25-NSE 0000876661-20-000026 (filed and effective 2020-01-14) through sec.py: the EX-99.25 notice says the NYSE "on December 27, 2019, determined that the Common Stock ... should be suspended" and "Trading in the Common Stock was suspended at the close of the market on December 27, 2019" (market-cap standard under 802.01B). Common stock, CIK 1669812, matches the security. 2019-12-27 is a Friday and is no later than the Form 25 effective date plus 10 days (2020-01-24), so it is publishable under decision 12; the next session is Monday 2019-12-30 (price_date). The earlier report's 2019-12-26 read "effective immediately" as the prior day, which the notice contradicts. All three field verdicts survive; the missed filing shows the earlier report wrong.
