# NCRA (CIK1308161-CLASS-A), delisting 2013-06-28

## 1. Verdict

Not a merger. On 2013-06-28 News Corporation (CIK 1308161) spun off its publishing business as the new News Corp (NWSA, 1 new share per 4 old) and renamed itself Twenty-First Century Fox (FOXA). Old Class A holders kept their shares, which moved to a new CUSIP: a one-for-one continuation, not an exit. The library's `merger`, stock 0.25 NWSA, is wrong. The ticker NCRA is also not this company's symbol (it is Nocera Inc in fails data), so the observation is a vendor name-only mapping.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2013-07-03: Separation, name change; holders keep their shares).
- Successor: fails rows (FOXA / 90130A101 rows from 2013-07-03, first price 29.21 equal to the last NWSAXXXX row); the 8-K states the name change but not the CUSIP.
- Last trade date: worked out (filing: separation effective 4:30 pm ET 2013-06-28; no exchange print or Form 25 states the day).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K Item 2.01: distribution of 1 NWSA per 4 shares, cash in lieu of fractions; no consideration for the old share).
- Terms: filing (same).
- Not backed: the last trading day under the old CUSIP, and the identity of NCRA. An exchange print (MIDAS) or a Nasdaq notice would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Separation completed 2013-06-28 at 4:30 pm ET by distributing 1 News Corp Class A share per 4 old Class A held on 2013-06-21, cash for fractions ([SEC 8-K 2013-07-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1308161/000119312513281456/d562317d8k.htm)).
- The same 8-K reports the name change to Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc. (Item 5.03, same CIK 1308161).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 65248E104 as NWSA to 2013-07-01, then NWSAXXXX 2013-07-02..03 at 29.40; CUSIP 90130A101 as FOXA from 2013-07-03 at 29.21 (fails output, `sec.py fails`).
- Fails symbol NCRA 2012-07-03 is NOCERA INC (CUSIP 655186104), not News Corp.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept: the holder keeps the same company shares one for one (rename, new CUSIP); the NWSA shares are an extra distribution. Continuation per decision 9.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason string: "Completed acquisition (8-K item 2.01 ...)": the end-of-era resolver read the spin-off's Item 2.01 as an acquisition, and the LLM read 0.25 NWSA as stock consideration (`terms_gate_failed:no_last_close`). The security CIK1308161-CLASS-A also came from a name-only observation with a wrong ticker and is a placeholder duplicating CIK1308161-COMMON. Cause tag: `wrong_exit_kind`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat a spin-off (Item 2.01 "distributing to its stockholders") as not merger consideration; rename plus CUSIP switch is a continuation to the FOXA line.
- Merge CIK1308161-CLASS-A into CIK1308161-COMMON (the same old Class A line, CUSIP 65248E104) and add FOXA as successor.
- Check the last trading day under CUSIP 65248E104 (MIDAS or a Nasdaq notice).
- Golden case: yes, after the identity is settled (spin-off plus rename plus CUSIP change).
