- Manor Care asked the NYSE to suspend trading effective at the opening of the business day after the closing ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878736/000095015707001450/form8k.htm)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2007-12-21 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878736/000095015707001447/form15-12b.htm)); Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2007-12-26 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878736/000087666107000970/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Proxy and terms: DEFM14A 2007-09-14 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878736/000095012307012617/y27078ddefm14a.htm)).
