## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (State Street 8-K 2007-07-02; Form 25-NSE "Acquired by State Street Corporation").
- Successor: filing (DEFM14A: holders receive STT stock; STT survives).
- Last trade date: worked out (closing 2007-07-02 per 8-K, Form 25 effective 2007-07-02, so the last session was the prior trading day 2007-06-29; fails rows under IFIN end 2007-06-11 and say nothing more).
- Payout rule: filing (DEFM14A: all-stock).
- Terms: filing (DEFM14A: 0.906 of a share of STT per IFIN share, no cash).

Not backed: the last trade date. A filing, Nasdaq halt notice or exchange print stating the last IFIN session would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement: each IFIN share converts into 0.906 STT shares ([SEC DEFM14A 2007-05-29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949589/000119312507124565/ddefm14a.htm)).
- IFIN stockholders approved the merger; Federal Reserve approval reported ([SEC 8-K 2007-06-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949589/000110465907048748/a07-17108_18k.htm)).
- State Street completed the acquisition on 2007-07-02 ([SEC 8-K 2007-07-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93751/000110465907051750/a07-17984_18k.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-07-02, effectiveness 2007-07-02, reason "Acquired by State Street Corporation" ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949589/000135445707000196/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- IFIN filed Form 15-12G on 2007-07-10 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949589/000095015707000867/form15-12g.htm)).
