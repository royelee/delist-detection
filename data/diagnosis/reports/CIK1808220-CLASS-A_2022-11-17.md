# GoHealth (GOCO), case CIK1808220-CLASS-A_2022-11-17

## 1. Verdict

Not a delisting. GoHealth did a 1-for-15 reverse stock split effective 11:59 p.m. ET on 2022-11-17; the Class A stock stayed on the Nasdaq Global Market under GOCO and got a new CUSIP (38046W204). The library's "compliance failure, 570" ending is wrong; the right result is no ending (a corporate action on the same security).

## 5. What happened

- Stockholders approved a reverse split range of 1:5 to 1:15 on 2022-10-24; the board set 1:15, effective 11:59 p.m. ET on 2022-11-17 ([SEC 8-K 2022-11-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1808220/000119312522288595/d341111d8k.htm)).
- Nasdaq had notified GoHealth on 2022-05-25 of a minimum bid price deficiency (Rule 5450(a)(1)); on 2022-11-22 it granted a further 180 days to 2023-05-22 ([SEC 8-K 2022-11-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1808220/000180822022000114/goco-20221122.htm)). Not a delisting.
- No Form 25 or Form 15 in 2022-2025; the issuer kept filing 8-Ks with GOCO on Nasdaq.
- Fails rows: CUSIP 38046W105 (GOCO, $0.49 last) to 2022-11-17, then 38046W204 "CL A NEW" (GOCO, $12-19) from 2023-07 to 2024-02 (SEC fails-to-deliver files).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security (rename-like corporate action; the CUSIP changed only because of the split).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The new CUSIP made the old CUSIP's fails rows end on 2022-11-17 and no Form 25 existed, so the continued-filings fallback read the 8-K 3.01 deficiency notice as a compliance-failure ending (570; flags no_form25, delist_date_approx). The security's ticker history was clipped, and the new CUSIP is not in the run. Cause tag: `other:reverse_split_cusip_change_read_as_delisting`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat a reverse split (8-K 5.03 reverse stock split, same ticker continuing) as not an ending; extend ticker_history and add the new CUSIP.
- A 3.01 deficiency notice with an extension or regained compliance is not a delisting evidence by itself.
- Golden case: yes, a reverse split with a CUSIP change, still listed.

## 9. Verification

Re-checked from the SEC filings list and filing text (sec.py `filings`, `grep`, `fails`).

- **exit_kind (upheld).** The 8-K of 2022-11-18 (0001193125-22-288595, item 5.03) states a 1-for-15 reverse split, effective 11:59 p.m. ET on 2022-11-17, with cash only for fractions. Its cover lists Class A on The Nasdaq Global Market under GOCO. The 8-K of 2022-11-22 (0001808220-22-000114, item 3.01) gives a bid-price extension to 2023-05-22, not a delisting. The 2022-10-01 to 2023-12-31 filings list has no Form 25, 25-NSE or 15; 8-Ks continue through 2023-11. Fails rows: 38046W105 GOCO to 2022-11-17, then 38046W204 "CL A NEW" GOCO from 2023-07-14 to 2024-02-14. The ending is not real.
- **value_rule (upheld).** With no ending, `otc_print` is unsupported; the right rule is continuation/none. No OTC symbol appears in the evidence.
- **terms (upheld).** The library's Shumway -0.55 fill and last close 0.49 describe an exit that did not happen. The only terms are the 1:15 ratio and cash for fractions. Section 3's `stock_ratio = 0.0667` is a split factor for the same security, not a payout leg (the JSON record holds null); a presentation quirk, not an error in the verdict.
- **ticker_history (upheld).** GOCO continues past 2022-11-17 (fails rows to 2024-02-14, 2023 8-Ks list Nasdaq). The library's range ending 2022-11-17 is clipped wrongly, and the new CUSIP 38046W204 is missing from the run.
