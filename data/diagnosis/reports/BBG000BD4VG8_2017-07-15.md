# BHI (Baker Hughes Inc), case BBG000BD4VG8_2017-07-15

A one-for-one holding-company reorganization: on 2017-07-03 each BHI share became one share of Baker Hughes, a GE company (BHGE, CIK 1701605, new CUSIP 05722G100), with a $17.50 special dividend declared by BHGE on the new shares. ## 4. Why this confidence

Verified. All five key fields rest on the 8-K12B filings, so the label follows the rule.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K12B 2017-07-03: each BHI share converted one for one into a BHGE Class A share, then BHI was delisted).
- Successor: filing (8-K12B 2017-07-03 filed by BHGE, CIK 1701605, names it as the successor registrant; the library's own security table supplies its FIGI, BBG00GBVBK51).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K12B 2017-07-03: BHI traded on NYSE until the close on 2017-07-03).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K12B: a one-for-one conversion of the same holders, so continuation; the $17.50 is a dividend on BHGE shares).
- Terms: filing (8-K12B: one BHGE Class A share per BHI share, no cash consideration).

## 5. What happened

- The Mergers closed 2017-07-03; each outstanding Baker Hughes share was converted into the right to receive one BHGE Class A share. BHI traded on NYSE until the close on 2017-07-03 and was then delisted; BHGE Class A began trading under BHGE on 2017-07-05 ([SEC 8-K12B 2017-07-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1701605/000119312517220852/d343521d8k12b.htm)).
- BHGE declared a $17.50 per share special dividend to record holders of Class A immediately after the second merger (same 8-K12B).
- Baker Hughes (renamed Baker Hughes a GE Co LLC, CIK 808362) also filed an 8-K12B the same day ([SEC 8-K12B 2017-07-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/808362/000119312517220863/d343454d8k12b.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2017-07-05 (effectiveness date field 2017-07-05; the notice text states removal at the opening of 2017-07-17) ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/808362/000087666117000381/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The new line has CUSIP 05722G100 (fails rows from 2017-07-06), the old 057224107 ends 2017-07-03 (library cusip_history).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced 1:1 by a new line of the same holders (successor 8-K12B, holdco merger at one for one): reorganization, `exchange`, continuation true, successor named. Caveat: the $17.50 special dividend (a large cash distribution, about 30% of the price) is declared by the successor after the exchange; under decision 9 ("no cash") a strict reading could call this a merger with cash. The filing frames it as a dividend on the new Class A shares, so continuation is chosen; flagged for the person deciding.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder labelled it an exchange transfer from the Form 25 (bucket exchange_transfer, 304), `resolved_from_continued_filings`, with flag `successor_unknown`: the successor stage did not link the 8-K12B registrant (BHGE, CIK 1701605) to BBG00GBVBK51 although that security exists in the run and starts within days. Cause: `holdco_not_linked` (the 8-K12B successor is in the run but not linked).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic pass on the field the report calls wrong: `value_rule` (library `transfer`, report `continuation`).

- **value_rule: upheld.**
  - Re-read the 8-K12B (CIK 1701605, 0001193125-17-220852): each BHI share "was converted into the right to receive one share of BHGE's Class A common stock", BHI traded on NYSE to the close of 2017-07-03, and BHGE declared the $17.50 "to the holders of record of the Class A Common Stock immediately following the effective time of the Second Merger", that is, a dividend on the new shares after the exchange. Class A trades from 2017-07-05.
  - The 424B3 (0001193125-17-187201) says the same: the exchange is one for one, no gain or loss on the Mergers, and the Special Dividend is "characterized as a dividend" paid on Class A.
  - The reference's decision table names BHI to BHGE as a continuation, and its operator ruling (2026-10-03) settles that a successor's dividend after the exchange is not "cash in the exchange". `transfer` ("the same shares, 0 return") is not the published rule for a security replaced by a new CUSIP line, so the library's value is wrong under the contract. Both the BHGE security (BBG00GBVBK51, CIK 1701605, history from 2017-06-30) and the missing successor link are confirmed in the run's tables.
  - Residual doubt, not a refutation. (1) The 424B3 lists the Special Dividend as a term of the Transaction Agreement that holders voted on, funded by GE's $7.4bn contribution. The reference's exception ("transaction documents make the payment part of the consideration for the old shares") could be argued, but the documents call it a dividend on new Class A shares and the operator has ruled. (2) The deal also gave GE about 62.5% of BHGE LLC (BHI holders keep 37.5%), so it is not a pure holdco reorganization, and the "same claim" test is arguable. The reference's own example classes BHI as a continuation, so the rule applies as written. Confidence in the field is high but not absolute.
- **continuation / successor (same root cause): upheld.** Same evidence; the 8-K12B filer CIK 1701605 is the registrant named as successor.
