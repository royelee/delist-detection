## 5. What happened

- On 2014-06-30 NRF LP merged into Old NorthStar Realty, which then merged into NorthStar Realty (formerly NRFC Sub-REIT Corp.); each common and preferred share was exchanged for one share of the corresponding new security ([SEC 8-K12B 2014-07-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1273801/000127380114000018/nrf8-k712014.htm)).
- Right after, NorthStar Realty did a one-for-two reverse split of its common stock (same filing, Item 5.03).
- At 11:59 pm on 2014-06-30 it distributed one NSAM share per post-split NRF share (tax-free spin-off). This is a distribution on the successor after the exchange, so it does not make the exchange a merger (same filing).
- Common and preferred stock continue on the NYSE, uninterrupted, under NRF and the same preferred symbols; new CUSIP 66704R704 (same filing).
- Fails-to-deliver history: 66704R100 to 2014-07-01, then 66704R704 from 2014-07-02 (`output/cusip_history.csv`).
