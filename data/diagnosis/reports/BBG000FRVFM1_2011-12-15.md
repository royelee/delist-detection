## 5. What happened

- The merger with Jaguar Merger Sub closed on 2011-12-05; PPDI became an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Jaguar Holding Company II ([SEC 8-K 2011-12-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1003124/000119312511330317/d265257d8k.htm)).
- Each share was cancelled and converted into $33.25 cash, without interest ([same 8-K, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1003124/000119312511330317/d265257d8k.htm)).
- Nasdaq trading was suspended after the close of trading on 2011-12-05, and Nasdaq filed the Form 25 that day ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1003124/000119312511330317/d265257d8k.htm)).
- The 25-NSE 0001354457-11-000270 was filed 2011-12-05 with effectiveness date 2011-12-05 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1003124/000135445711000270/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
