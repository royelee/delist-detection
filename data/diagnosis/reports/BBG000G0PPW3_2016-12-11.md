## 5. What happened

- NASDAQ filed Form 25-NSE on 2016-12-01, effective that day ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895930/000135445716000565/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15-12B followed 2016-12-13 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895930/000119312516791569/d366446d1512b.htm)).

- Note: the 8-K's Item 3.03 wording reads one-for-one at an intermediate step (Merger1 into Holdings); the 0.334 is the final exchange into Envision.

Its LLM-read terms (`terms_source=llm`) most likely took the intermediate 1:1 conversion as the stock ratio (1.0 instead of 0.334); the sanity gate then failed (`terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`) and the return fell to assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: terms (the report's claim that stock_ratio is 0.334, not the library's 1.0). **Refuted.**

- The cited 8-K (2016-12-07, accession 0000895930-16-000327, Items 2.01 and 3.03) says "the Company" is AmSurg. "Holdings" is Envision Healthcare Holdings, Inc., the other party. It reads: "each share of the Company's common stock was converted into one share of Envision common stock ... and each share of Holdings common stock was converted into 0.334 shares of Envision common stock."
- So 0.334 is the ratio for the former Envision Holdings holders. AmSurg holders got 1.0 share of Envision Healthcare Corporation (formerly New Amethyst Corp., "Newco"). Item 2.01 says "the Company merged with and into Envision, with Envision surviving (Merger 1)", and Item 3.03 calls Envision "successor to the Company by operation of law".
- The report's section 5 note had it backwards: Merger 1 (AmSurg into Envision) is the AmSurg holders' conversion, at 1:1, not an intermediate step. The 0.334 conversion belongs to Merger 2 and applies to Holdings holders only.
- The library's stock_ratio 1.0 for EVHC therefore matches the filing. The report's cause tag `terms_misread`, its `library_wrong` and its "wrong (ratio)" status are unsupported.
- Open question, not decided here: AmSurg's holders received the same shares in a new holding company at 1:1. That may be a reorganization or continuation (reference.md: "Replaced 1:1 by a new line of the same holders") rather than a merger with a priced stock leg. The exit_kind, continuation and successor fields were not asked about and were not re-checked. The failed sanity gate may come from price scaling rather than a misread ratio, and that was not investigated.
