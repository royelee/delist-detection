## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2008-05-14 Item 3.01: shares converted into the right to receive the Offer Price in cash).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Takeda America Holdings, Inc. is the parent; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out, not read. No filing states the last trading day; the merger became effective 2008-05-14 and Nasdaq filed the Form 25 that day.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2008-05-14: $25.00 per share net in cash).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K; SC TO-T/A 2008-05-14).

Not backed: the last trade date. An exchange print (MIDAS is not available before 2012), a Nasdaq halt record or a press release stating the final trading day would make it `verified`. Fails rows exist for MLNM until 2008-06-10 but are settlement lag, not trading.

## 5. What happened

- Merger Agreement with Takeda America Holdings and Mahogany Acquisition Corp. signed 2008-04-10; tender offer for $25.00 net cash per share commenced 2008-04-11 ([SEC 8-K 2008-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002637/000110465908033222/a08-14414_48k.htm)).
- Initial offering period ended 2008-05-08, subsequent offering period ended 2008-05-13; 295,628,495 shares (about 90.3%) tendered and accepted ([SEC SC TO-T/A 2008-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002637/000110465908032996/a08-14414_2sctota.htm)).
- Short-form merger effective 2008-05-14; untendered shares converted into $25.00 cash ([SEC 8-K 2008-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002637/000110465908033222/a08-14414_48k.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed the Form 25-NSE on 2008-05-14 at Millennium's request because the merger left the numerical listing requirements unmet ([SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002637/000135445708000134/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15 filed the same day ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002637/000110465908033213/a08-14414_11512g.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: holders' shares were exchanged for cash from another company. A merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It did not get the kind wrong. The Form 25 (filed 2008-05-14) carries no suspension date and the 8-K item 3.01 text states no last trading day; no MIDAS (pre-2012) or halt print, so `last_trade_date` is blank (`no_last_trade_date`, `needs_last_trade`, `no_last_close`). Also `member_name_mismatch` is raised on the observations. The ticker_history end uses the Form 25 effective date (2008-05-24) as a fallback. Cause tag: `no_last_trade_print`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish `cash_currency=USD`.
- Date the last trade: a Nasdaq-published or press-release statement of the final trading day (probably 2008-05-14 or 2008-05-13). Not a market price.
- Ticker_history end should follow the last trade date, not the Form 25 effective date.
- Golden case: yes, a simple tender-offer cash deal whose only gap is the print date; usable once the date is sourced.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: ticker_history

25-NSE 0001354457-08-000134 is effective 2008-05-14. The 8-K 0001104659-08-033222 press release says Nasdaq trading ends after the close of market on 2008-05-14. The library's ticker_history end of 2008-05-24 is the Form 25 date plus 10 days, so it is wrong by 10 days. Side note: a filing does state the last trading day, so the report's claim that none does is too weak. Section 9 was appended to the report, and a verification object was added to the record JSON.
