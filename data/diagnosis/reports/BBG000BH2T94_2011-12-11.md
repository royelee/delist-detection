## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The row has no last trade date (`no_last_trade_date`, `no_last_close`, `dlret_method=needs_last_trade`) although the 8-K item 3.01 text states the suspension date. The internal delist_date 2011-12-11 is the Form 25 filing date plus 10 days. The Form 25 notice here carries no suspension date, MIDAS (2012+) does not cover it, and the 8-K item 3.01 date was not picked up. Cause tag: `no_last_trade_print`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Read the date from 8-K item 3.01 ("suspended after the close of trading on December 1, 2011") as 2011-12-01, which is no later than the Form 25 effective date; publish `cash_currency=USD`.
- No other check; qlib_practice prices 8.00 against the 2011-12-01 close.
- Golden case: yes, a plain cash merger whose date comes only from the 8-K item 3.01.

## 9. Verification

Re-opened [SEC 8-K 2011-12-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895663/000119312511327509/d263973d8k.htm) and the Form 25-NSE header (accession 0001354457-11-000259) through the skill's sec.py helper.

- **last_trade_date (missing, should be 2011-12-01): upheld.** Item 3.01 says "The trading of the Common Stock on NASDAQ will be suspended after the close of trading on NASDAQ on December 1, 2011", so the last session is 2011-12-01. The Form 25-NSE header gives EFFECTIVENESS DATE 20111201 and FILED 20111201, so the date is not after the Form 25 effective date (decision 12 holds). The source row has a blank `last_trade_date` and `no_last_trade_date`, so "missing" is right. Small caveat: the 8-K wording is forward-looking ("will be suspended"); the same-day Form 25 effectiveness agrees.
- **terms / currency (missing): upheld.** The source row has `cash_per_share=8.000000` and a blank `cash_currency`. Item 2.01 states "per share consideration of $8.00 per share" (about $936 million aggregate) and Item 1.01 repeats $8.00 for the debenture conversion, with no stock leg, so 8.00 is right and the currency is the dollar the filing states (USD). The 8-K does not spell out "USD", but nothing in it points elsewhere.
- The fields the report called "agree" (exit_kind merger, value_rule cash, 8.00) were also consistent with the 8-K (Items 2.01, 5.01: cash merger completed 2011-12-01).
