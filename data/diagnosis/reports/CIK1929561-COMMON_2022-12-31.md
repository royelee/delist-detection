## 7. Why the library got it wrong
No Form 25 exists. The `continued_filings_rule` (304) fired because the issuer kept filing after the last sighting (2022-12-31) and invented an exchange transfer dated at the observation date; flags `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`. The real problem is the ticker "RXO-WI" being treated as a final ticker rather than a when-issued symbol of the listed RXO. Cause: continued_filings_guess.

## 8. Fix and open checks
- Do not emit an ending for a security whose only sighting is a when-issued symbol while the issuer is still listed; map the "-WI" ticker to RXO (ticker_history RXO from 2022-11-01, WI before).
- Open check: exact when-issued trading dates (a `web` fact, not retrieved). Not needed for the verdict.
