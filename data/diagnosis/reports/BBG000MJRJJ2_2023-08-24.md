## 5. What happened

- NYSE Form 25-NSE (2023-08-14) removes only HHC's listing, not the registration of HHH's common stock; trading suspended before the open on 2023-08-14
  ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498828/000087666123000651/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- HHC filed a Form 15-12G on 2023-08-25 ([SEC Form 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498828/000110465923095350/tm2324775d7_1512g.htm)).

## 9. Verification

Checked by a skeptic against the cited filings, re-read through sec.py.

- **value_rule (library `transfer`, evidence `continuation`): upheld.** The 8-K of 2023-08-11 (0001104659-23-090461, Item 1.01) says that at 4:01 p.m. ET on 2023-08-11 each outstanding HHC common share "was automatically converted into one share of common stock ... of Holdco ... having the same designation, rights, powers, and preferences", and Holdco (Howard Hughes Holdings Inc.) replaced HHC as the NYSE-traded company. One for one, same class, no cash in the exchange, no bankruptcy: decision 9's holdco continuation. The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-23-000651) was filed and effective 2023-08-14, consistent with a 2023-08-11 last trade. HHH is in the run as BBG01HTMDZ54 (`ticker_history`: HHH NYSE from 2023-08-14; `cusip_history`: 44267T102 from 2023-08-14), so the link does not rest on timing alone. The library's `transfer` (same shares, zero return) is the wrong rule, since a continuation is not an exit.
- **continuation (false vs true) and successor (missing): upheld**, on the same evidence.
- Not contested: exit_kind, last_trade_date, issuer. The `delist_date` 2023-08-24 open check stays open.
