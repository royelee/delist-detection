# CIK1498301-COMMON_2020-08-21 (ASNA, Ascena Retail Group)

## 1. Verdict

Not a merger. Ascena filed Chapter 11 on 2020-07-23; Nasdaq suspended the common stock at the open on 2020-08-04 (last exchange trade 2020-08-03) and it moved to OTC Pink as ASNAQ. The 8-K 2.01 of 2020-11-24 is an asset sale in bankruptcy (to Sycamore), not an acquisition of the shares. The library's kind (merger), its assumed par and its empty value rule are wrong; the last trade date is right.

## 5. What happened

- 2020-07-23 Ascena and subsidiaries filed Chapter 11 in E.D. Va. ([SEC 8-K 2020-07-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498301/000149830120000065/asna-form8xkxnasdaqand.htm)).
- Nasdaq notified the company on 2020-07-24 that trading would be suspended at the open on 2020-08-04 under Rules 5101, 5110(b), IM-5101-1 (same 8-K).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2020-08-11, removal effective at the open on 2020-08-21; no appeal ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498301/000135445720000408/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The stock began trading on OTC Pink as ASNAQ on 2020-08-04; equity holders expected to lose everything, the restructuring support agreement contemplates cancelling the common stock ([SEC 10-K 2020-12-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498301/000149830120000105/asna-20200801.htm)).
- 2020-11-23 the company sold most assets; reported under item 2.01 on 2020-11-24 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498301/000110465920128647/tm2036043d1_8k.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. Holders kept the same shares, which left the exchange for OTC Pink and were later to be cancelled.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder's reason is "Completed acquisition (8-K item 2.01 ...); the registrant kept filing" via the continued-filings end-of-era resolver (`resolved_from_continued_filings`, flag `merger_at_par`). The 2.01 was an asset sale in Chapter 11; the resolver has no bankruptcy branch (8-K 1.03 / 3.01 citing Chapter 11 precedes it). Cause tag: `resolver_no_bankruptcy_branch`.

## 9. Verification

Re-opened the 8-K of 2020-07-28 (0001498301-20-000065), the 8-K item 2.01 of 2020-11-24 (0001104659-20-128647) and the 10-K of 2020-12-11 (0001498301-20-000105).

- **exit_kind (merger -> dropped / bankruptcy): upheld.** The 2020-07-28 8-K item 3.01 says Nasdaq suspended trading "as a result of the Chapter 11 Cases" at the opening of business on 2020-08-04, and the stock was expected to trade on OTC Pink. The 10-K says it began trading on OTC Pink as "ASNAQ" on 2020-08-04, and that the restructuring support agreement cancels the existing common equity. The 8-K 2.01 (2020-11-24) describes a sale of certain assets under sections 105, 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code, so it is not an acquisition of the shares. Per reference.md ("Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC"), this is dropped / bankruptcy.
- **value_rule (unknown, assumed par -> otc_print): upheld.** Decision 11 values a move to OTC at the first off-exchange print within 10 trading days. The own OTC symbol is ASNAQ and the first session is 2020-08-04, as the report states. The 10-K (equity cancelled, no consideration) gives no cash or stock terms, so there is no other rule to apply.
- **Correction (does not change any field):** the report's verdict and section 5 say the 8-K 2.01 sale was "to Sycamore". The 2.01 text names Justice Brand Holdings LLC (Bluestar Alliance) as the purchaser, for $71 million plus assumed liabilities. The Sycamore APA is a separate later agreement (10-K Note 24). Neither is a sale of the shares.
- Open check left as the report states: the first ASNAQ print date is not confirmed here.
