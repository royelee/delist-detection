## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- exit kind: filing (8-K 2017-05-30 items 2.01/3.01/5.01; Form 25-NSE).
- successor: filing (none; JHG is the acquirer, 8-K item 2.01).
- last trade date: worked out from the 8-K/Form 25 effective date 2017-05-30 and the 2017-05-29 market holiday; the filings do not state the last trading day. MIDAS (the library's source) agrees.
- payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01, Form 25 notice).
- terms: filing (0.47190 ratio; the Form 25 notice says 0.4719 of a JHG ordinary share, equal to 4.7190 Henderson shares).
- Not backed by a filing: price_date 2017-05-30 (first JHG trading day) is not read from SEC text here. A filing or notice stating the last NYSE day and JHG's first NYSE trading day would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- [SEC 8-K 2017-05-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065865/000110465917036203/a17-14258_18k.htm): Merger Sub merged into JCG; at the Effective Time each JCG share converted into 0.47190 Janus Henderson ordinary shares plus cash in lieu of fractions; Item 3.01: JCG asked the NYSE on 2017-05-30 to delist.
- [SEC 25-NSE 2017-05-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065865/000087666117000283/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml): merger effective 2017-05-30; each JNS share converted into 0.4719 JHG share (4.7190 Henderson shares); effectiveness date 2017-05-30.
- The library's delist_date 2017-06-09 is the Form 25 filing plus 10 days.
