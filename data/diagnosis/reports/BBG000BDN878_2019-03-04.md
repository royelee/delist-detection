# TAHO (Tahoe Resources) — BBG000BDN878, delisting 2019-03-04

## 1. Verdict

A real cash-and-stock merger: Pan American Silver (PAAS) acquired Tahoe by plan of arrangement, effective after the close on 2019-02-21. Each Tahoe share became, at the default (prorated share-election) package, US$0.67 cash plus 0.1929 PAAS share, plus one CVR exchangeable for 0.0497 PAAS share. The library's row is wrong: it leaves the exit kind blank and publishes no payout rule. Its last trade date (2019-02-21) is right.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: merger effective after the close on 2019-02-21; shares substituted by other securities).
- Successor: filing (same notice: holders receive PAAS shares and CVRs; no continuing Tahoe line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: suspended from trading 2019-02-22, so last trade 2019-02-21).
- Payout rule: filing (Tahoe 6-K of 2019-02-22: election with proration, default package cash plus PAAS shares).
- Terms: filing (same 6-K: approx. 19.7% cash US$0.67 and 80.3% shares 0.1929 PAAS; election terms US$3.40 or 0.2403 in the 6-K of 2018-11-27).

## 5. What happened

- 2018-11-14: Tahoe and Pan American agreed an arrangement; holders may elect US$3.40 cash or 0.2403 PAAS per share, prorated to a US$275M cash cap and 56M share cap, plus a CVR for 0.0497 PAAS ([SEC 6-K 2018-11-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1510400/000106299318004823/form6k.htm)).
- 2019-02-22: Pan American completed the acquisition; non-electors deemed share-electors receive about US$0.67 cash and 0.1929 PAAS per share; cash-electors get all cash; one CVR per share ([SEC 6-K 2019-02-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1510400/000106299319001045/form6k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2019-02-22: the merger became effective after the close on 2019-02-21; the security was suspended from trading on 2019-02-22 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1510400/000087666119000146/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2019-03-06 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1510400/000134100419000173/form15-12b.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old share was exchanged for cash plus another company's shares and a CVR. A merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The row's reason is "Delisted/deregistered without merger or distress evidence" (`no_evidence_default`, `unknown_exit_kind`), with flags `no_evidence_default;no_last_close`. The Form 25-NSE notice itself states the merger and the terms, but the library found no 8-K (Tahoe is a foreign private issuer filing 6-Ks, so the 8-K item rules had nothing to read) and did not use the notice text or the 6-K. Cause tag: `wrong_exit_kind`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Classify from the Form 25 notice text ("merger ... became effective", "receive ... shares") and from 6-K news releases for foreign private issuers; publish `cash_plus_stock` with USD 0.67, 0.1929, PAAS, 2019-02-22.
- Open check: the CVR (0.0497 PAAS, contingent on Escobal restart) is not in the payout rule; decide whether to ignore it. The 0.67 / 0.1929 package is the proration for share electors; cash electors got US$3.40 (a holder's actual package is not observable from filings).
- Golden case: yes — a foreign-private-issuer (6-K) election deal with a Form 25 notice that states the terms.

## 9. Verification

- **exit_kind (library blank, evidence merger): upheld.** The Form 25-NSE notice (0000876661-19-000146, filed and effective 2019-02-22) states "The merger between Tahoe Resources Inc. and Pan American Silver Corp. became effective after the closing on February 21, 2019", that the class came to evidence other securities in substitution, and that holders receive a PAAS CVR plus the election consideration. Cash and another company's shares, so a merger and not a continuation. Suspension 2019-02-22 is consistent with last trade 2019-02-21.
- **value_rule (library unknown, evidence cash_plus_stock): upheld.** The Tahoe 6-K of 2019-02-22 (0001062993-19-001045) says holders who elected or were deemed to elect shares, subject to proration, "will receive consideration per Tahoe share of approximately 19.7% in cash (US$0.67) and approximately 80.3% in Pan American shares (0.1929 of a Pan American share)"; cash electors get all cash (US$3.40 / 0.2403 share election; caps US$275M and 56.0M shares). Non-electors were deemed share electors, so the default package matches the report's terms (0.67 USD, 0.1929, PAAS). price_date 2019-02-22 follows the rule. Caveats already in section 8, not refuting: the CVR (0.0497 PAAS, contingent on the Escobal restart) is excluded, and a cash elector's actual package differs.
