## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement 2015-03-04, amended 2015-03-22: AbbVie acquires Pharmacyclics through an exchange offer ([SEC 8-K 2015-05-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949699/000110465915040558/a15-12704_18k.htm)).
- Default consideration per share: $152.25 cash plus AbbVie shares equal to $109.00 / AbbVie Trading Price; alternatives $261.25 cash or $261.25 of AbbVie stock, prorated (same 8-K).
- The offer expired as scheduled at 5:00 p.m. New York time on Friday 2015-05-22 with 67,408,824 shares tendered, about 87% ([SEC SC TO-T/A 2015-05-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949699/000110465915040557/a15-12705_2sctota.htm)).
- On 2015-05-26 the merger closed and Pharmacyclics asked Nasdaq to file Form 25 (8-K item 3.01); Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2015-05-26 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949699/000135445715000099/)).
- Form 15-12G filed 2015-06-05 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949699/000110465915043562/a15-13497_11512g.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: shares were exchanged for cash plus another company's stock - a merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The library read only the cash leg (`terms_source=llm`, `value_rule=cash`, 152.25, no ratio); the stock leg is a floating ratio ($109 / VWAP) that the LLM extraction did not turn into a rule. Its own check failed (`llm_gate_failed`), so it filled assumed par (`merger_at_par`, 0.0 fill), hence `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`. Cause tag: `terms_not_extracted`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish `value_rule=cash_plus_stock`, cash 152.25 USD, stock leg as 109.00 / AbbVie Trading Price in ABBV, price date 2015-05-26. A contract needs a way to carry a dollar-denominated stock leg (a value over a VWAP) instead of a fixed `stock_ratio`; qlib_practice computes the ratio from ABBV prices.
- Confirm the last trading day from the Form 25 notice or an exchange print (expected 2015-05-22).
- Golden case: yes - an exchange-offer election deal with a floating exchange ratio.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the two fields the report calls wrong. Both upheld.

- **value_rule (library `cash`, report `cash_plus_stock`): upheld.** The 8-K filed 2015-05-26 ([SEC 8-K, 0001104659-15-040558](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949699/000110465915040558/a15-12704_18k.htm)) states each accepted Pharmacyclics share "was exchanged for the right to receive: $152.25 in cash; and a number of shares of AbbVie common stock equal to $109.00 divided by" the 10-day ABBV VWAP (the "AbbVie Trading Price"). That default package has a stock leg, so a cash-only rule is incomplete. The $261.25 all-cash and all-stock elections are alternatives subject to proration, and the report correctly records the default package, as reference.md directs for election deals. The library's `terms_gate=failed` and `assumed_par` fill agree with the cash-only reading being wrong.
- **terms (library 152.25, no currency, no stock leg; report 152.25 USD + $109.00 / AbbVie Trading Price in ABBV, price date 2015-05-26): upheld.** Cash 152.25 and the 109.00 numerator match the 8-K. The currency is blank in the library, and the filing states dollars. The stock leg is missing from the library. The report is right to leave `stock_ratio` blank, because the ratio depends on a VWAP that is a market figure. The offer expired 5:00 p.m. Friday 2015-05-22 with 67,408,824 shares tendered ([SEC SC TO-T/A, 0001104659-15-040557](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949699/000110465915040557/a15-12705_2sctota.htm)), consistent with the report's dates.
- **Minor uncertainty (does not change the verdict).** The price date 2015-05-26 is the closing date rather than "the trading day after the last trade" (2015-05-26 after the 2015-05-25 holiday). The two coincide here. The report's cause tag `terms_not_extracted` is reasonable. `terms_misread` would also be defensible, since the library published a cash leg only.
