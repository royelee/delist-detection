# SLE (Sara Lee Corp) 2012-07-13: rename and 1-for-5 reverse split, not an ending

## 1. Verdict
Sara Lee Corp (CIK 23666) did not end: on 2012-06-28 it spun off its coffee business, did a 1-for-5 reverse split, and was renamed The Hillshire Brands Company (NYSE: HSH, new CUSIP 432589109). The same shares, same issuer, same exchange line. The library's row (exchange, continuation to BBG000BT4T69) points at the right successor, but it is a split of one security into two sec_ids, not an ending.

## 4. Why this confidence
Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2012-07-05 items 2.01, 3.03, 5.03: name change and reverse split, shares continue on NYSE).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: "will continue to trade on the New York Stock Exchange, under the new symbol HSH").
- Last trade date: filing (name change and split effective prior to the open on 2012-06-29, so last SLE day 2012-06-28; matches MIDAS).
- Payout rule: filing (no cash or other security received for surrendering the old share).
- Terms: filing (1-for-5 split; the $3.00 special dividend is paid by CoffeeCo).

## 5. What happened
- 2012-06-28: Sara Lee distributed 100% of CoffeeCo to holders; CoffeeCo paid a $3.00 special dividend and merged into D.E MASTER BLENDERS 1753 ([SEC 8-K 2012-07-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/23666/000119312512295170/d377100d8k.htm)).
- Before the open on 2012-06-29 the 1-for-5 reverse split took effect and the company became The Hillshire Brands Company (same 8-K).
- New CUSIP 432589109; shares continue on NYSE under "HSH" (same 8-K, item 5.03).
- Library: cusip_history has 803111103 to 2012-06-28 under a placeholder sec_id and 432589109 from 2012-07-03 under BBG000BT4T69; no Form 25 was found (flags no_form25, delist_date_approx).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Kept the same security (rename; the CUSIP changed only because of the reverse split, same issuer, same listing).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The CUSIP change from the reverse split made the library treat the two CUSIPs as two securities; with no Form 25, the continued-filings rule (304) invented an exchange-transfer ending at the approximate date 2012-07-13, then linked the successor. The SLE era never joined the HSH FIGI. Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks
- Merge the SLE era into BBG000BT4T69 (CUSIP switch 803111103 -> 432589109 by a split with the same issuer and a name change) so ticker_history reads SLE then HSH and no ending row exists.
- No facts left to confirm.
- Golden case: yes, a rename plus reverse split with a spin-off and a CUSIP switch.

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: exit_kind
