# SXCI (SXC Health Solutions) 2012: case CIK1363851-COMMON_2012-07-24

## 1. Verdict
Not an ending. SXC was the acquirer of Catalyst Health Solutions; it only changed its name to Catamaran Corporation (ticker SXCI to CTRX, new CUSIP 148887102), same CIK, same holders, same claim. The library's merger row with 28.00 + 0.6606 terms took Catalyst's holders' consideration as SXC's own; the row is wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence
Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (10-K FY2012 states SXC unveiled a new name, Catamaran Corporation, after the Catalyst merger).
- Successor: filing (same registrant, CIK 1363851, files the 10-K as Catamaran).
- Last trade date: fails rows (CTRX rows under the new CUSIP from 2012-07-11); no filing states the ticker switch day. Not needed if it is a rename.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2012-07-06: shareholders approved the name change; Catalyst holders, not SXC holders, got the consideration).
- Terms: filing (8-K 2012-07-06 states the 0.6606 and $28.00 are per Catalyst share).
Not backed: the exact first day under CTRX and why the CUSIP changed (fails rows show 78505P100 then 148887102; no filing read on the CUSIP change). A Nasdaq notice or an 8-K12G3/8-A naming the new CUSIP would make it verified.

## 5. What happened
- SXC acquired Catalyst; effective time 4:01 pm ET 2012-07-02; each Catalyst share got $28.00 cash + 0.6606 SXC shares; Catalyst stock ceased trading on Nasdaq at that close ([SEC 8-K 2012-07-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1363851/000119312512296100/d375927d8k.htm)).
- SXC shareholders approved the share issuance and an amendment to the articles of continuance changing the name from SXC Health Solutions Corp. to Catamaran Corporation (same 8-K).
- Common shares began trading as CTRX on Nasdaq and CCT on TSX ([SEC 10-K FY2012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1363851/000136385113000007/ctrx10k123112.htm)).
- A 2-for-1 split of Catamaran shares followed, distributed 2012-10-01 ([SEC 8-K 2012-09-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1363851/000136385112000042/ctrxstocksplit8k.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 78505P100 SXCI to 2012-07-24; CUSIP 148887102 CTRX from 2012-07-11 (fails, fails rows).
- No Form 25 or Form 15 for SXC in 2012-2013; the issuer kept filing (SEC filings list).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Kept the same security: a name and ticker change by the same issuer, not a delisting. (The CUSIP changed; a 1:1 continuation at worst.)

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
No Form 25 existed, so the continued-filings fallback ran and the end-of-era resolver read the 2.01 item and merger filings (425, S-4) as "SXC was acquired", but SXC was the acquirer. The LLM read the Catalyst consideration as SXC's. Flags: `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `merger_at_par`, `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`; reasons `resolved_from_continued_filings`. Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks
- Resolver: an item 2.01 / 425 that names the registrant as acquirer (filer is the offeror) must not be a merger; follow the rename (5.03 / articles amendment, new ticker, new CUSIP in fails rows) and extend ticker_history SXCI to CTRX.
- Open: confirm the day CTRX trading began and the CUSIP change reason from a filing.
- Golden case: yes, an acquirer's rename misread as a target merger.

## 9. Verification
Skeptic pass; all five contested fields upheld. Evidence re-read through sec.py.
- **exit_kind: upheld.** 8-K 2012-07-06 (0001193125-12-296100) Item 2.01 and 5.07: Catalyst merged into a subsidiary of "the Company" (SXC, CIK 1363851), so SXC was the acquirer; the same 8-K lists the 2012-07-02 vote to change the name from "SXC Health Solutions Corp." to "Catamaran Corporation". The filing list for 2012-06 to 2012-09 shows no Form 25 or 15, and the registrant files its 10-Q (0001363851-12-000037) as CTRX, so there is no ending. Nuance: the CUSIP changed (78505P100 to 148887102), so decision 9's "unchanged CUSIP" shortcut does not apply, but one-for-one, same holders and same claim still gives a continuation.
- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The library's 2012-07-10 (MIDAS) is not a last trade: fails rows show CUSIP 78505P100 SXCI through 2012-07-24 (28 rows, last $85.80) and CUSIP 148887102 CTRX from 2012-07-11 (35 rows to 2012-08-31, last $85.73). The security traded on. Corrected value: none.
- **value_rule: upheld.** The 8-K states the 0.6606 share and $28.00 cash are paid per share of Catalyst Common Stock. SXC holders surrendered nothing, so cash_plus_stock is wrong; continuation or none is right.
- **terms: upheld.** 28.00 and 0.6606 are Catalyst holders' consideration (same 8-K, Item 2.01). The library read them as SXC's own, and its gate failed (terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker).
- **ticker_history: upheld.** SXCI ending 2012-07-10 is wrong; same CIK, trading continues as CTRX (10-Q: Nasdaq "CTRX", TSX "CCT"). One continuous history, SXCI then CTRX.
- Still open (as in section 8): the first CTRX day and the CUSIP change reason from a filing. Neither changes a verdict above.
