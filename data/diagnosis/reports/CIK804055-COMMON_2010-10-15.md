# CCE (old Coca-Cola Enterprises, CIK 804055), 2010

## 1. Verdict

A real merger: on 2010-10-02 each old CCE share became one share of New CCE (renamed Coca-Cola Enterprises, ticker CCE, new CUSIP 19122T109) plus $10.00 cash. The library's kind, date and cash leg are right; its stock leg names the wrong security (KO instead of the new CCE), so the terms are misread and the assumed-par fill is wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred, only because of the price date.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2010-10-07 items 2.01/3.01/3.03/5.01; DEFM14A).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2010-10-07: the old company became a wholly owned TCCC subsidiary; holders receive New CCE shares and cash).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K Item 3.01: suspended before the open on 2010-10-04; Form 25-NSE filed 2010-10-05).
- Payout rule: filing (DEFM14A merger consideration; 8-K Item 3.03).
- Terms: filing for 1 share + $10.00; the price date 2010-10-04 is worked out from the suspension date and fails rows (new CUSIP 19122T109 under CCE first appears 2010-10-05, priced at the 2010-10-04 close). Would be verified by a filing or exchange notice stating the first New CCE trading day.

## 5. What happened

- Each old CCE share outstanding at the effective time converted into one New CCE share and $10.00 cash ([SEC DEFM14A 2010-08-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804055/000119312510197473/ddefm14a.htm)).
- The merger closed 2010-10-02; the old company was renamed Coca-Cola Refreshments USA and New CCE (International CCE Inc.) was renamed Coca-Cola Enterprises ([SEC 8-K 2010-10-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804055/000134100410001684/form8k.htm)).
- The NYSE suspended the old shares before the open on 2010-10-04 and filed Form 25 on 2010-10-05 (same 8-K, Item 3.01; [Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804055/000087666110000372/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15-12B followed 2010-10-15.
- Fails rows: CUSIP 191219104 under CCE through 2010-09, CUSIP 19122T109 (COCA-COLA ENTERPRISES INC NEW) under CCE from 2010-10-05 (fails data).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: holders surrendered the old share for a new company's share plus $10.00 cash paid in the exchange. By the "no cash in the exchange" ruling that is a merger, not a continuation, although the ticker and economic business partly carry on.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The LLM read the stock leg as one TCCC (KO) share; the holders received New CCE shares (the acquirer TCCC paid the cash). `terms_gate=failed:fail_sanity` and the flags `merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate` follow, leaving a 0.0 fill. Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish price_ticker CCE for the new security (and its sec_id if added to the run; the ticker was taken over, so it needs a distinct line from the old CCE) and cash_currency USD.
- Open: confirm the first regular-way New CCE trading day (expected 2010-10-04).
- Golden case: yes. A spin-and-merge where the stock leg is a new line under the same ticker, a trap for ticker-keyed price lookups.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (report says wrong: price security KO instead of the new CCE, currency missing). **Upheld.**
- Stock leg: [SEC 8-K 2010-10-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804055/000134100410001684/form8k.htm) says each old CCE share was converted into "one share of New CCE common stock and $10.00 in cash, without interest". The [DEFM14A 2010-08-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804055/000119312510197473/ddefm14a.htm) says the same. Neither names TCCC (KO) shares as consideration. Ratio 1.0 and cash 10.00 match the library; only the price security (KO) is wrong.
- New CCE was renamed Coca-Cola Enterprises, Inc. (same 8-K), so its ticker is CCE. `price_ticker = CCE` is consistent, but a ticker-keyed lookup must use the new CUSIP (19122T109), not the old line.
- Currency: the filings give "$10.00" (USD); the library publishes none. Upheld.
- The rule (`cash_plus_stock`, per reference.md) is correctly applied.

Not fully backed, as the report already says: `price_date` 2010-10-04 is worked out. The 8-K confirms suspension before the open on 2010-10-04 but not New CCE's first trading day.

Minor correction to section 7: the DEFM14A says the $10.00 cash is "funded by New CCE", not paid by TCCC. This does not change the verdict or the cause tag `terms_misread`.
