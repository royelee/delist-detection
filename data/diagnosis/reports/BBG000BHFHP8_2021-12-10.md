## 5. What happened

- On 2021-11-30 Domtar completed its merger with a subsidiary of Karta Halten B.V.; each share converted into $55.50 cash, without interest ([SEC 8-K 2021-11-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1381531/000119312521343066/d217948d8k.htm)).
- The NYSE filed Form 25 and suspended trading before the open on 2021-11-30 (same 8-K, Item 3.01; [SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1381531/000087666121001666/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2021-12-13 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1381531/000119312521354173/d270837d1512b.htm)).
