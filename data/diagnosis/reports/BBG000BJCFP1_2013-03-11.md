## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2013-03-01 items 2.01/3.01/5.01; Form 25-NSE effective 2013-03-01).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Jefferies became an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Leucadia).
- Last trade date: worked out. The filings give the 2013-03-01 closing and Form 25 effectiveness, not the last print; the library's MIDAS print (2013-02-28) agrees. An exchange print or a filing stating the last day would make it verified.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: 0.81 Leucadia Common Shares per share).
- Terms: filing (ratio 0.81; 8-K item 3.01 repeats it). LUK's first price date 2013-03-01 is worked out from the closing date.

## 5. What happened

- Under the two merger agreements of 2012-11-11, Merger Sub One merged into Jefferies, then New Jefferies merged into a Leucadia subsidiary; each New Jefferies share converted into 0.81 Leucadia common shares plus cash for fractions, and preferred into Leucadia 3.25% Series A preferred ([SEC 8-K 2013-03-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1084580/000119312513087969/d495163d8k.htm)).
- Item 3.01: the NYSE was notified that each Jefferies common share converted into 0.81 Leucadia Common Shares (same 8-K).
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2013-03-01 for the common stock ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1084580/000087666113000083/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old shares were exchanged for another company's shares at 0.81, not one for one of a successor line of the same holders. A merger.
