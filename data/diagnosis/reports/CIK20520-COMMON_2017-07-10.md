# FTR (Frontier Communications), case CIK20520-COMMON_2017-07-10

## 1. Verdict

The 2017-07-10 ending is not an ending: it is a 1-for-15 reverse stock split of the same common stock (new CUSIP, same ticker FTR, same issuer). The library's row is wrong. The security's real ending is the Chapter 11 bankruptcy: Nasdaq suspended FTR at the open on 2020-04-24 (last exchange trade 2020-04-23), Form 25-NSE filed 2020-04-29, and the stock traded OTC as FTRCQ until it was cancelled for nothing on 2021-04-30.

## 5. What happened

CUSIP 35906A108 became 35906A306 (fails rows); the library read the CUSIP end as a delisting.
- 2020-04-14: Frontier and subsidiaries filed Chapter 11 ([SEC 8-K 2020-04-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20520/000114036120008876/form8k.htm)).
- 2020-04-17: Nasdaq determined to delist; trading to be suspended at the opening on 2020-04-24, no appeal ([SEC 8-K 2020-04-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20520/000114036120009130/brhc10011071-8k.htm)).
- 2020-04-29: Form 25-NSE, removal effective at the opening on 2020-05-08 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20520/000135445720000170/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 35906A306 under FTR 2020-04-01..04-24, under FTRCQ 2020-04-27..2021-04-30 (sec.py fails).
- 2021-04-30: plan effective; all Old Common Stock cancelled and extinguished ([SEC 8-K12G3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20520/000114036121015200/brhc10023786_8k12g3.htm)); new equity trades as FYBR (BBG010MVVVW7, a separate security).

The real 2020 Form 25-NSE was never matched to this security, and its ticker_history is clipped at 2017.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic pass over the four fields the report calls wrong, re-opened through sec.py (cache or live SEC).

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The [SEC 8-K 2017-07-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20520/000119312517224772/d394967d8k.htm) says each fifteen shares were converted into one share of common stock, no change in par, and trading continues on Nasdaq under "FTR" as a "new listing" with new CUSIP 35906A 306. Same holders, same claim, same issuer and ticker: a same-security event, not an ending. The real ending is 2020: [SEC 8-K 2020-04-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20520/000114036120009130/brhc10011071-8k.htm) item 3.01 cites the Chapter 11 cases as the basis for Nasdaq's delisting determination, and a Form 25-NSE was filed 2020-04-29 (sec.py filings). Decision table: lost, bankruptcy then OTC, so dropped/bankruptcy.
- **last_trade_date: upheld, with a caveat.** The 8-K says trading "will be suspended at the opening of business on April 24, 2020" unless appealed, and "does not intend to appeal". Per decision 12 that gives 2020-04-23. Fails rows for CUSIP 35906A306 show FTR through 20200424 (a row dated D is priced at D-1's close, so the last priced close is 04-23) and FTRCQ from 20200427. Caveat: this is an 8-K item 3.01 statement of a planned date, not an exchange print. It is a permitted source, and it is before the Form 25 effective date (2020-05-08). The "inferred" confidence is right.
- **value_rule: upheld.** A move to OTC after a bankruptcy delisting is `otc_print` (decision 11). The library's `transfer` rests only on the continued-filings guess. Minor note: the 8-K expected the OTC symbol "FTRQ", but the fails rows show the actual symbol FTRCQ under the same CUSIP, so price_ticker FTRCQ is right. The first OTC session is 2020-04-24 (the 04-27 row is priced at the 04-24 close), so price_date 2020-04-24 holds. No price was checked, as the skill forbids it.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** output/ticker_history.csv holds only `CIK20520-COMMON,FTR,2008-08-01,2017-07-10`. Since 2017-07-10 is no ending and FTR traded on Nasdaq until 2020-04-23, the range is clipped about three years early.

Still open: an exchange print or MIDAS for 2020-04-23, and the first FTRCQ print. Overall: all four fields upheld.
