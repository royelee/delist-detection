# CIK1469372-CLASS-A_2015-10-02 (MSG)

## 1. Verdict

Not a delisting. On 2015-09-30 The Madison Square Garden Company (CIK 1469372) spun off its sports business as a new company and renamed itself MSG Networks Inc. (MSGN); the same registrant and shares kept trading, with a CUSIP change. The library's exchange-transfer ending with no successor is wrong: the right reading is a rename plus spin-off, so the line continues as BBG000NS03H7 (MSGN). The new Madison Square Garden Co (CIK 1636519, BBG007FG0C23) took over the ticker MSG.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2015-10-06: same registrant renamed, Distribution of the spun-off company).
- Successor: fails rows (CUSIP 553573106 under MSGN from 2015-10-06 in `cusip_history`) and the 8-K naming the same CIK; the CUSIP change itself is worked out, not read from a filing.
- Last trade date: not applicable; the 2015-10-02 date is a fails row, not backed by an exchange print.
- Payout rule: filing (spin-off is a distribution, no exchange of the old share).
- Terms: filing (1 new share per 3 old).
Not backed: the old to new CUSIP change and the old ticker's final day. A filing or CUSIP source stating the CUSIP change at the rename would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- On 2015-09-30 the registrant distributed all MSG (formerly MSG Spinco) common stock, one new Class A share for every three old Class A shares, record date 2015-09-21 ([SEC 8-K 2015-10-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1469372/000146937215000015/msgnetworks8-k1062015spino.htm)).
- Same 8-K, item 5.03: the registrant changed its name from The Madison Square Garden Company to MSG Networks Inc. at 11:59 p.m. on 2015-09-30 (same file number 1-34434).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 55826P100 under MSG through 2015-10-02, then three rows under deleted symbol MSGZZZZ 2015-10-05 to 2015-10-07 (sec.py fails).
- Library cusip_history: MSGN's FIGI BBG000NS03H7 holds CUSIP 553573106 from 2015-10-06, issuer 1469372; the new MSG, BBG007FG0C23, holds 55825T103 from 2015-10-05.
- No Form 25 for CIK 1469372 appears in its filing list from 2015-08 to 2015-12.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same registrant and shares, renamed; the spin-off is a distribution. A rename/ticker change with a CUSIP change, so no ending.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder found no Form 25 and fell back to the continued-filings rule (304), reading the ticker MSG's end as a transfer. The new company's ticker takeover and the rename's CUSIP change hid that the issuer is the same as MSGN's. The old line has a placeholder sec_id although BBG000NS03H7 holds the same issuer and class (`cusip_handoffs` linkage missing). Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Follow the rename: link or merge CIK1469372-CLASS-A into BBG000NS03H7 (same CIK, 8-K 5.03 name change, CUSIP switch); the new MSG's takeover of the ticker is a ticker takeover, not a continuation.
- Open: a source stating the CUSIP change; the final trading day under the old CUSIP is moot once merged.
- Golden case: yes, a spin-off with rename where the parent keeps the CIK and the spun company takes the ticker.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic pass: each field the report calls wrong was re-checked against the 8-K and the fails rows. All four are upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K filed 2015-10-06 (0001469372-15-000015, items 2.01, 5.03) is signed by the same registrant (file no. 1-34434, CIK 1469372), now MSG Networks Inc., "formerly The Madison Square Garden Company". Item 5.03: the name change took effect at 11:59 p.m. on 2015-09-30. Item 2.01: it distributed all the stock of the new MSG (formerly MSG Spinco), one new Class A share per three old, record date 2015-09-21. No Form 25 was filed 2015-08 to 2015-12 (`sec.py filings 1469372`). Holders kept their shares and received a distribution, so the library's 304 "continued filings" ending is not a real ending. One wording point: block 3 keeps `exit_kind = exchange` with `continuation = true`, the contract's form for a continuation row; the report's better fix is no row at all.
- **successor: upheld.** `sec.py fails`: CUSIP 55826P100 traded as MSG through 2015-10-02 (17 rows in 2015-09/10), then as MSGZZZZ 2015-10-05 to 2015-10-07 (3 rows). CUSIP 553573106 traded as MSGN ("MSG NETWORK INC CL A") from 2015-10-06, first $19.57. The library's `cusip_history.csv` agrees: BBG000NS03H7 holds 553573106 from 2015-10-06, and BBG007FG0C23 (new MSG) holds 55825T103 from 2015-10-05. The link rests on the same CIK and the 8-K name change, not on timing alone. The CUSIP change at the rename is inferred from fails rows and not stated in a filing, as the report says, so `inferred` stands.
- **value_rule: upheld.** The one-for-three MSG share is a spin-off distribution on the old shares. It is not paid for surrendering them and involves no cash, so by decision 9 there is no merger consideration. The library's `transfer` (0.0 return) is wrong as a label; the fails closes fell from $72.14 to $19.57 across the spin, which the distribution explains. That is an observation only; qlib_practice prices the return. `continuation` with no value is the right label.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library has MSG 2010-02-16 to 2015-10-02 on placeholder CIK1469372-CLASS-A. `output/ticker_history.csv` also has BBG007FG0C23 as MSG from 2015-10-05 (new company) and BBG000NS03H7 as MSGN from 2015-10-06. Per the filing the old registrant is the MSGN line, so the old MSG range should merge into BBG000NS03H7, and MSG after 2015-10-05 belongs to the new company. The "split into two sec_ids" reading is correct.

Not re-checked, and not among the wrong fields: `last_trade_date` (unknown, moot for a continuation). Open: a source stating the CUSIP change.
