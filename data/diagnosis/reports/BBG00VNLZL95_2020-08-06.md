# BBG00VNLZL95_2020-08-06 (TMUSR, T-Mobile US subscription rights)

## 1. Verdict
TMUSR were transferable subscription rights issued to T-Mobile US holders on 2020-06-26 and listed on Nasdaq; they expired at 5:00 p.m. ET on 2020-07-27 and the Form 25-NSE was filed and effective the same day. The library's kind (expiration) is right, but its ending date (2020-08-06) and ticker_history end are about ten days too late, no Form 25 was matched, and the last trade date is blank (it should be 2020-07-27).

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (the 8-K of 2020-08-04 says the offering expired in accordance with its terms; the Form 25-NSE for "Subscription Rights Expiring 7/27/2020").
- Successor: filing (the 8-K of 2020-06-26: exercise yields TMUS common, a separate security; the rights have no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (the 424B5 of 2020-06-24 states trading until 4:00 p.m. ET on 2020-07-27; the 8-K of 2020-08-04 says the offer was not extended; the Form 25-NSE is effective 2020-07-27).

- Terms: filing (the 8-K of 2020-06-26 and the 424B5: 0.05 TMUS share per right at $103.00 per whole share).

## 5. What happened
- Rights offering: one transferable right per share held at 5:00 p.m. ET on 2020-06-25; each buys 0.05 TMUS share at $103.00 per whole share; expiration 5:00 p.m. ET on 2020-07-27 [SEC 8-K 2020-06-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1283699/000114036120014904/nt10012922x6_8k.htm).
- Rights expected to trade on Nasdaq as TMUSR from 2020-06-24 until 4:00 p.m. ET on 2020-07-27 [SEC 424B5 2020-06-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1283699/000114036120014592/nt10012922x4_424b5.htm).
- Form 25-NSE for "Subscription Rights Expiring 7/27/2020", Nasdaq, filed and effective 2020-07-27 [SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1283699/000135445720000356/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml).
- The offering "expired in accordance with its terms at 5:00 p.m. ET on July 27, 2020" (no extension) [SEC 8-K 2020-08-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1283699/000114036120017381/nc10013758x2_8k.htm).
- Fails rows for CUSIP 872590112 (TMUSR) run 2020-06-26..2020-07-20 (16 rows), none later [SEC fails-to-deliver, offline].

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Expired: a right that lapsed. Holders either exercised into TMUS common (a different security that already trades), sold, or let it lapse. Not a continuation or a merger.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The kind is right (CRSP 600, expiration). The end date 2020-08-06 is the last observation, not an exchange print; no Form 25 was matched, so no last-trade date could be published (`no_last_trade_date`, `no_last_close`). Cause: `no_last_trade_print`.
