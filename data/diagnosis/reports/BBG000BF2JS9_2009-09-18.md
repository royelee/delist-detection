## 5. What happened

- Colonial Bank was closed on 2009-08-14, FDIC appointed receiver; the company said it does not believe any recovery will be realized ([SEC 8-K 2009-08-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/92339/000119312509179032/d8k.htm)).
- On 2009-08-17 NYSE suspended CNB immediately as no longer suitable for listing after the closure ([same 8-K, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/92339/000119312509179032/d8k.htm)).
- BancGroup filed Chapter 11 on 2009-08-25 ([SEC 8-K 2009-08-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/92339/000119312509181323/d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2009-09-08; the notice states the suspension announcement at the close of the 2009-08-17 session ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/92339/000087666109000352/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)). Two more 25-NSE (09-08, 09-10) cover the other securities.
- Fails rows under CNB run 2009-01-05 to 2009-08-19, last price $0.09 (fails data).
- A web result says NYSE suspension was effective 2009-08-18 ([web](https://res-certification.cboe.com/resources/regulation/circulars/regulatory/RG09-087.pdf)); consistent with a last trade on 08-17.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: delisted by the exchange for the bank's failure, then Chapter 11; filings show nothing paid to shareholders.

## 9. Verification

Checked: `exit_kind` (the report's one wrong field: drop reason `sec_order` should be `bankruptcy`). Result: **upheld**, with a caveat.

- `sec_order` is wrong. The library's reason is "SEC REVOKED registration filed 2010-12-20". That REVOKED entry (accession 9999999997-12-008050) comes 15 months after the 2009-08-17 NYSE suspension and the 2009-09-08 Form 25-NSE (0000876661-09-000352, effective 2009-09-08), so it cannot be what ended the listing. The reference ties 573/585 to an SEC order, which did not remove the stock from the exchange here.
- `dropped` with a bankruptcy reason is supported. The 8-K of 2009-08-20 (0001193125-09-179032) has Item 1.03 (bank closed 2009-08-14, FDIC receiver) and Item 3.01 (NYSE suspended CNB on 2009-08-17). The 8-K of 2009-08-26 (0001193125-09-181323) has Item 1.03: Chapter 11 petition filed 2009-08-25, M.D. Ala., after the FDIC receivership of the Bank, the company's "primary asset". This fits the reference's "Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC" row (8-K 1.03 plus 3.01 suspension).
- Caveat: the 3.01 text gives NYSE's reason as the securities being "no longer suitable for listing ... due to the aforementioned closure of the Bank". It was not the Chapter 11 filing itself, which came eight days after the suspension. `guidelines` (570/584, a listing deficiency) is a defensible alternative reading, but the receivership and the Chapter 11 are one failure sequence, so `bankruptcy` is the better fit. The report states this correctly in section 5 and does not claim the 3.01 cited bankruptcy.
- Not rechecked: the OTC symbol and first print date (report status `unknown`). The 2009-08-17 last trade date stands on the 3.01 text, "announced ... suspended immediately" on 2009-08-17; this agrees with section 5.

Confidence in the exit_kind correction: high that `sec_order` is wrong, fairly high that `bankruptcy` is right.
