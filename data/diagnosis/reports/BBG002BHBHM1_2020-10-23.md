# MNK (Mallinckrodt plc), case BBG002BHBHM1_2020-10-23

## 1. Verdict

A real delisting after a Chapter 11 filing: the NYSE suspended MNK before the open on 2020-10-12, so the last exchange trade was 2020-10-09. The library's kind (dropped / bankruptcy) and rule (otc_print) are right; its last trade date (2020-10-12) is one trading day late, and its price date (2020-10-13) and price ticker (MNK) are therefore wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2020-10-13 items 1.03, 3.01; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (none named; equity left in place pending the plan).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25 notice: suspended prior to market open on 2020-10-12, so the trading day before, 2020-10-09), worked out by the suspension rule.
- Payout rule: filing (decision 11 applies; the 8-K leaves equity treatment to the plan).

Not backed: the OTC symbol and the first-print session. A FINRA/OTC symbol record or a press release naming MNKKQ would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- NYSE determined on 2020-10-12 to suspend MNK and file for removal; trading was suspended prior to market open that day ([SEC 25-NSE 2020-10-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1567892/000087666120000911/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The company filed Chapter 11 on 2020-10-12 and disclosed NYSE Regulation's delisting proceedings; trading was suspended on 2020-10-12; the company would not appeal ([SEC 8-K 2020-10-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1567892/000119312520267836/d942107d8k.htm)).
- Noteholders are to receive 100% of the new ordinary shares; equity's treatment is left to a proposed resolution ([SEC 8-K 2020-10-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1567892/000119312520267836/d942107d8k.htm)).
- Fails rows for CUSIP G5785G107: MNK to 2020-10-14, then MNKKQ from 2020-10-15 (fails rows, sec.py fails).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. Same shares, now off-exchange; valued at the first OTC print.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Its reason is "Bankruptcy (8-K item 1.03 filed 2020-10-13)" with `last_trade_date_source=ex99_notice` and flag `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`; it took the notice's suspension date (2020-10-12) as the last trade instead of the trading day before. The price date and ticker follow from that date, and the MNK ticker is used rather than the OTC symbol. Dlret is a Shumway fill (-0.30) pending the OTC print. Cause tag: other:suspension_date_taken_as_last_trade.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Apply "suspended before the open on D means D-1" to the Form 25 notice: last_trade_date 2020-10-09, price_date 2020-10-12, price_ticker MNKKQ, cash_currency USD.
- Confirm the MNKKQ symbol from a non-fails source.
- Golden case: yes, a well-sourced bankruptcy suspension where the date is off by one.

## 9. Verification

Checked each field the report calls wrong against the cited filings (re-read through sec.py) and reference.md.

- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-20-000911) says the NYSE determined on 2020-10-12 to suspend the Ordinary Shares and that "on October 12, 2020 ... trading of the Ordinary Shares was suspended prior to market open". The 8-K (0001193125-20-267836) confirms the petition date was 2020-10-12. 2020-10-12 was a Monday and a NYSE trading day, so the trading day before is Friday 2020-10-09. reference.md (decision 12) says "suspended before the open on D" means D-1. The library's 2020-10-12 (source `ex99_notice`, flag `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`) is one trading day late.
- **terms: upheld.** The price date follows from the corrected last trade: the session after 2020-10-09 is 2020-10-12, not the library's 2020-10-13 (reference.md, otc_print). Fails rows for CUSIP G5785G107 show MNK for 9 rows 2020-10-01..10-14, then MNKKQ for 24 rows 2020-10-15..11-30. The same CUSIP under a Q symbol supports MNKKQ as the post-delisting symbol. The report's own caveat stands: this rests on fails rows only, and no non-fails source confirms the symbol or the first print session. Confidence in the date is high, in the ticker moderate. The missing currency (USD) is as the report says.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The range end derives from the last trade date, so it moves to 2020-10-09 with it.
- **Not refuted: kind and rule.** Chapter 11 on 2020-10-12 with NYSE removal under 802.01D is a bankruptcy drop valued at an OTC print; the report's exit_kind, drop_reason and value_rule stand.
