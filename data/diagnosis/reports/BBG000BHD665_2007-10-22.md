Cause: `no_last_trade_print`. The seed after the ending is a caller-side stale-snapshot issue (`seeds_outside_history`).

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `ticker_history` (the report calls the library wrong).

- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library's row carries `last_end` 2007-10-22, the Form 25 effective date (`delist_date` 2007-10-22), as the end of CDWC's history. The 8-K of 2007-10-18 (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/899171/000119312507221351/d8k.htm) says "On October 12, 2007 (the 'Closing Date'), all of the outstanding common stock of CDW Corporation ... was acquired", and its header gives the earliest event as October 12, 2007. October 12, 2007 was a Friday. The fails data for CUSIP 12512N105 (`sec.py fails`) show 19 rows, 2007-01-26 to 2007-10-15, last price $87.72, which fits a last close on 2007-10-12 settling on the next business day. After the closing every share had become $87.75 cash, so no exchange trading could continue to 2007-10-22. Per the reference (decision 12 and the `ticker_history` clipping rule) the listing history ends at the last trade, not the Form 25 effective date, so ending at 2007-10-22 overshoots by ten days.
- Caveat: no filing states the last trading day, so 2007-10-12 is an inference from the closing date, the same-day Form 25 and the fails rows. The correct end could in principle be a day earlier, but not later than 2007-10-12. The report's `inferred` confidence is right.
- The other fields the report marks as missing (`last_trade_date`, currency) were not in the scope of this check.
