- Last trade date: worked out, not read. The 8-K says the merger closed the evening of 2007-12-03; the Form 25-NSE was filed and effective 2007-12-04; the last fails row under CKFR is dated 2007-12-04.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `ticker_history` (report: wrong; should end 2007-12-03, library ends 2007-12-14). **Upheld**, with two corrections to the report's reasoning.

- Upheld: the 8-K ([SEC 8-K 2007-12-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949341/000089882207001430/closing8k.htm), Item 7.01) says Fiserv completed the acquisition "on the evening of December 3, 2007" and each share became $48.00 cash. CKFR therefore stopped trading after 2007-12-03. The offline fails rows for CUSIP 162813109 end 2007-12-04 (a row dated D carries D-1's close) with last close $47.51, which fits a last trade on 2007-12-03. A `ticker_history` ending 2007-12-14 (library: CKFR, NASDAQ, 2004-12-31 .. 2007-12-14) runs 11 days past the last trade, so it is wrong.
- Correction 1: the report says the Form 25 was "effective 2007-12-04". The Form 25-NSE ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949341/000135445707000376/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)) was filed 2007-12-04 and signed 2007-12-03. A Form 25 under Rule 12d2-2(d) takes effect 10 days after filing, so effectiveness is about 2007-12-14, not 12-04. The conclusion is unaffected: 2007-12-03 rests on the 8-K and fails rows, not on the Form 25 effective date.
- Correction 2: the report blames the Form 15 for the library's 2007-12-14 date (verdict and section 7, "Form 25 ... was not used as the anchor"). The library row's `delist_filing_form` is 25-NSE with accession 0001354457-07-000376, so the Form 25 was the anchor, and 2007-12-14 is most likely its filing date plus 10 days. It coincides with the Form 15 date ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/949341/000119312507265241/d1512g.htm), signed 2007-12-14). Cause tag `no_last_trade_print` still fits (the library holds no exchange print for the last trade). I did not verify which computation produced 12-14.
- Not refuted, not independently confirmed: 2007-12-03 is worked out, not read from an exchange print (no MIDAS, halt or Form 25 notice text states it; the Form 25 gives only a signature date). Confidence stays `inferred`.
- Kind (merger), terms ($48.00 cash) and issuer checked against the 8-K and agree with the report.
