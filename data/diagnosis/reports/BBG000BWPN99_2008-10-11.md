# WEN (Wendy's International), case BBG000BWPN99_2008-10-11

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger: each Wendy's share became 4.25 Triarc Class A shares (Triarc renamed Wendy's/Arby's Group, ticker WEN from 2008-09-30), with cash only in lieu of fractions. The library's kind, date, rule and terms are right; the row is uncertain only because its own price check failed: the "last close" it used (5.26) is the acquirer's, not Wendy's, since the symbol WEN was reused by the successor on 2008-09-30.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2008-09-29: merger consummated, Items 2.01/3.01/5.01).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Wendy's became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Triarc; holders got Triarc stock, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K: Wendy's shares trade on NYSE "through the close of business on September 29, 2008"; Form 25-NSE says merger effective 2008-09-29).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K Item 3.03/5.01: each share converted into 4.25 Triarc shares).
- Terms: filing (8-K: 4.25 shares and cash in lieu of fractions; the combined company trades as "WEN" from 2008-09-30).

## 5. What happened

- Merger closed 2008-09-29; each Wendy's share converted into 4.25 Triarc Class A shares plus cash in lieu of fractions; Triarc renamed Wendy's/Arby's Group ([SEC 8-K 2008-09-29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/105668/000095015208007532/l33325ae8vk.htm)).
- Wendy's traded on the NYSE through the close on 2008-09-29; the combined company traded as WEN from 2008-09-30 (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2008-10-01: shares came to evidence other securities; merger effective 2008-09-29, 4.25 Class A shares each ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/105668/000087666108000375/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2008-10-14 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/105668/000095014208001671/form15-12b_101308.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 950590109 under WEN (Wendy's International) runs 2008-03-24..2008-09-26; CUSIP 950587105 under WEN (Wendy's/Arby's Group Class A) starts 2008-10-01, first row $5.26 (fails data, offline `sec.py fails`). The library's `library_last_trade_close` 5.26 equals that second line's first row.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: old shares exchanged for another company's shares at 4.25 per share, a merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Classification and terms are right. `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity` and `merger_at_par` (hence `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`) arise because the last close was read by symbol WEN and picked up the successor's CUSIP 950587105 row ($5.26, dated 2008-10-01, flagged `ftd_close_lagged`) instead of Wendy's own CUSIP 950590109 (last fails row 2008-09-26). So `terminal_value` 5.26 and the sanity gate are polluted by the ticker reuse. Cause tag: `other:last_close_from_reused_symbol`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- No filing facts still open.
