## 9. Verification

Upheld. Re-opened 8-K 0001193125-22-100359 through sec.py. Item 5.03 says the stock continues on the NYSE "under the new ticker symbol 'ENOV' beginning on April 5, 2022", with new CUSIP 194014 502; item 1.01 says "as of April 5, 2022, the Company's common stock began trading under the new ticker symbol 'ENOV'". So the first ENOV day is 2022-04-05 and the last CFX day 2022-04-04. Fails files (offline) show CUSIP 194014106 (CFX) rows 20220401..20220407 and CUSIP 194014502 (ENOV) rows from 20220406, so both runs follow lagging fails dates. The new run (CFX ..04-05, ENOV 04-06) is one day late; the old run (04-07 / 04-08) three days late. Neither equals the filing, so "neither" holds. No library-wrong claim or missed filing is asserted.
