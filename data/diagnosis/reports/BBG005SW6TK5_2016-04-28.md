# BBG005SW6TK5_2016-04-28 (LMCK, old Liberty Media Corp Series C)

## 1. Verdict

On 2016-04-15 Liberty Media Corp reclassified each old Series C share into a basket of three new tracking stocks (Series C Liberty SiriusXM, Series C Liberty Braves, Series C Liberty Media), all stock, no cash. That is a replacement by several securities, not a one-for-one continuation, so the library's row (continuation of BBG00BFHDFR4 by timing, last trade 2016-04-18, 0.0 transfer fill) is wrong on kind, successor, date and value.

- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2016-04-18 for "OLD Liberty Media Corporation Series C Common Stock (531229300)" ([SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000135445716000357/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the holder gave up one security and received three others of the same issuer, stock only. Not a one-for-one continuation (decision 9), so a terminal event valued by its stock package.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The row is `continuation_by_timing_only` (`handoff_continuation`): the finder saw one of the three new lines (BBG00BFHDFR4, new LMCK) take the ticker the day after and linked it. It did not read the 8-K showing a three-way split. Cause: other:tracking_stock_reclassification_basket.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat a reclassification into more than one new security as a replacement, not a continuation; the rule needs a multi-leg stock basket (the contract holds one `price_ticker` and one `stock_ratio`).
- Confirm the Series C ratios and the old stock's last trading day (2016-04-15) in the 424B3 or proxy.
- Ticker history: end old LMCK at 2016-04-15; start new LMCK at 2016-04-18.
- Golden case: yes, once the basket representation is decided.

## 9. Verification

A skeptic re-read the 8-K of 2016-04-19 (0001560385-16-000077), the Form 25 (0001354457-16-000357) and the fails rows for CUSIPs 531229300 and 531229607, and tried to refute each field the report calls wrong. All five survive.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K item 8.01 says each existing share "was reclassified by exchanging each such share, upon the cancellation therefor, for (i) one newly issued share of the corresponding series of Liberty SiriusXM ..., (ii) 0.1 ... Liberty Braves ... (iii) 0.25 ... Liberty Media", with cash only "in lieu of fractional shares". One old share became three securities, so it is not a one-for-one continuation (decision 9). The cash is only fractional-share settlement, so the "no cash" test is not broken. Choosing `merger` over `exchange` with continuation false is a judgment call: the reference table has no row for a same-issuer split into several lines, and `exchange` is defined only for a transfer or a continuation. The library's `exchange` + continuation is wrong either way.
- **successor: upheld.** The library names one successor, BBG00BFHDFR4 (new LMCK), which holds only the 0.25 Liberty Media leg. The holder's package is three lines (LSXMK, BATRK, LMCK), so a single successor is wrong. The Form 25 lists the old Series A, B and C (531229300) as removed, which supports a replacement and not a rename.
- **last_trade_date: upheld, with a caveat.** The 8-K says the Restated Charter was filed "after the market close" on 2016-04-15 and the new stocks "began trading ... on April 18, 2016", so the last old-stock session is Friday 2016-04-15. The fails rows agree: 531229300 under LMCK dated 20160418 is priced $38.26, the 04-15 close (rows are priced at the prior close), and 531229607 (LSXMK) starts 20160418 at $38.26. The library's 2016-04-18 is a fails-row date, not a trade. The Form 25 is dated 2016-04-18, so 04-15 is before its effective date. Caveat: no filing states "last trade 04-15" as an exchange print, so under decision 12 the published date may stay blank; the true date is still 04-15, and the library's internal 04-18 is wrong.
- **value_rule: upheld.** The package is all stock (plus fractional cash), so `stock`, not `continuation`. The report's open check on the Series C ratios is lighter than it said: the 8-K says each share of the existing common stock goes into the "corresponding series" at 1 / 0.1 / 0.25, so the Series C ratio is filing-supported, not only inferred from the Series A figures in Item 2.03. It is still not a Series C-specific sentence. A multi-leg basket does not fit the single `stock_ratio`/`price_ticker` contract fields, so the rule is right and the representation is open.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** Old LMCK ends 2016-04-15, not 2016-04-18 (same evidence as the last trade date). The new LMCK starts 2016-04-18 (8-K: began trading April 18; fails rows under the new CUSIP start 20160418), not the library's 2016-04-19. The 04-19 fails row for the old CUSIP is symbol `LMCKXXXX` (a deleted symbol, price unchanged at $38.26), which is settlement residue and not trading.

Confidence stays `inferred`. No field refuted.
