## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred, because the last trade date is not read from a filing.
- Exit kind: filing (DEFM14A 2007-08-10; Form 25-NSE 2007-09-14 "Acquired by Gerdau Ameristeel Corporation").
- Successor: filing (DEFM14A: cash merger, Chaparral ceases to be independent; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out from the Form 25-NSE filed 2007-09-14 (Rule 12d2-2(a)(3)), the Form 15-12G of the same day reporting zero holders of record, and the last fails row (2007-09-17, priced $85.91, i.e. the 09-14 close).
- Payout rule: filing (DEFM14A).
- Terms: filing (DEFM14A: $86.00 in cash, without interest, per share).

Not backed: the exact last trading day (2007-09-14 is inferred; the merger may have closed earlier that day or week). An exchange print, a Nasdaq halt record, or the closing press release or 8-K stating the last trading day would make it verified. The completion 8-K was not found in the issuer's filings (the issuer filed none after the 09-12 8-K before its Form 15).

## 5. What happened

- Merger agreement announced 2007-07-11; $86.00 cash per share, last full trading day before announcement 2007-07-10 ([SEC DEFM14A 2007-08-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1319048/000119312507179075/ddefm14a.htm)).
- Stockholders adopted the merger agreement on 2007-09-12; Chaparral to be acquired by Gerdau Ameristeel ([SEC 8-K 2007-09-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1319048/000114420407049089/v087571_8k.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-09-14 for the common stock, Rule 12d2-2(a)(3), notice "Acquired by Gerdau Ameristeel Corporation" ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1319048/000135445707000276/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12G filed the same day, holders of record: zero ([SEC Form 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1319048/000116923207003667/d72713_15-12g.txt)).
- Fails rows of CUSIP 159423102 under CHAP run 2007-02-02 to 2007-09-17, last priced $85.91 (offline `sec.py fails`).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The kind and terms are right. The Form 25-NSE carries no suspension date and no MIDAS or halt print exists (2007 is before MIDAS), so the row has `last_trade_date_source` blank, flags `no_last_trade_date`, `no_last_close`, bucket `merger`, dlret_method `needs_last_trade`. Ticker_history was clipped at the filing + 10 days (2007-09-24) instead of the true last trade. A separate seed of CHAP from 2008-01-16 onward (observed_after_delisting, `seeds_outside_history`) is the caller's stale observation of a dead ticker, not a second security. Cause: `no_last_trade_print`.
