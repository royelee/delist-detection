# CIK352363-COMMON_2012-05-14 (LIZ, Liz Claiborne)

## 1. Verdict
Not an ending. Liz Claiborne renamed itself Fifth & Pacific Companies effective 2012-05-14 (ticker LIZ to FNP, CUSIP 539320101 to 316645100) and kept trading on the NYSE; the same company later became Kate Spade & Company (KATE). The library's "exchange / 304" ending is a continued-filings guess and is wrong: there should be no delisting row, and the FNP and KATE lines belong to the same security as LIZ.

## 5. What happened
- [SEC 8-K 2012-05-18, 0001104659-12-038519](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/352363/000110465912038519/a12-12211_38k.htm): item 5.03, effective May 14, 2012 the name changed from Liz Claiborne to Fifth & Pacific Companies by a short-form Delaware merger of a subsidiary into Liz; shares unaffected, still on the NYSE, CUSIP now 316645100, symbol FNP.
- Fails rows (SEC FTD): CUSIP 539320101 as LIZ 20120403..20120514; CUSIP 316645100 as FNP 20120515..20140225; then CUSIP 485865109 as KATE from 2014-02-26 (the library's BBG000C4WJT9).
- No Form 25 for the issuer in 2012-04..2012-07 (EDGAR filings list shows none).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Kept the same security (rename; the same CUSIP-to-CUSIP relabel, same issuer, same exchange).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
Reason `Continued 10-K/Q filings >180d after delist`, flags `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `successor_unknown`. The run saw LIZ observations stop on 2012-05-14 (no observation under FNP), so with no Form 25 it applied the continued-filings fallback instead of following the CUSIP/ticker rename. It also failed to join the KATE line (BBG000C4WJT9, same CIK 352363) to the LIZ placeholder. Cause: rename_not_followed.
