- 2008-12-22: NYSE filed Form 25-NSE under Rule 12d2-2(b), average closing price below $1.00 over 30 trading days, removal at the opening of 2009-01-02 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1367396/000087666108000579/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the two fields the report calls wrong (`terms`, `ticker_history`). Both upheld.

- **terms: upheld.** The library publishes `price_ticker` IAR with `price_date` blank. The 8-K item 3.01 of 2008-11-24 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1367396/000095013408021150/d65369e8vk.htm)) says NYSE suspended trading before the open on 2008-11-21 and that the stock "is now trading over the counter under the trading symbol IDAR". The 25-NSE EX-99.25 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1367396/000087666108000579/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)) confirms suspension "before the opening of the trading session on November 21, 2008", so the last exchange session was 2008-11-20 and the otc_print `price_date` is 2008-11-21. reference.md says the otc_print `price_ticker` is the security's own OTC symbol (IDAR), not IAR. No cash or stock leg exists. Fails rows of CUSIP 451663108 (offline) show IAR to 2008-11-26 and IDAR from 2008-11-28 on the same CUSIP, so IDAR is the same security. Minor: 2008-11-21 is rule-derived; the first IDAR fails row is 2008-11-28 (settlement lag), which does not contradict it.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library has IAR to 2008-11-27, IDAR to 2009-04-01, IDARQ to 2009-06-08 (output/ticker_history.csv; last two from `ftd`). The 25-NSE removes the class from NYSE at the opening of 2009-01-02 for average close under $1.00 over 30 trading days, after the 2008-11-21 suspension. The stock traded OTC from 2008-11-21 (8-K above; the 2009-03-31 8-K 1.03 press release also reads "OTC: IDAR", [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1367396/000110465909021816/a09-9032_18k.htm)). Per CLAUDE.md, ticker_history records exchange listings and a delisting that ends the security clips it, so IAR should end 2008-11-20 and IDAR/IDARQ are OTC, not listings. Caveat: the exact clip date inherits the last-trade evidence (filing-backed here).
- **Side notes, not refuted.** The 25-NSE (filed 2008-12-22) is in EDGAR's list for this CIK, so `no_form25` is a genuine miss. Section 7's wording is muddled but its conclusion holds. "CRSP 551-ish" in section 8 is loose (reference.md maps price to 550/552); the bucket and drop_reason are what matter.
