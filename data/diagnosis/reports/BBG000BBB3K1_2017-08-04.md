## 4. Why this confidence

Verified: each of the five key fields rests on a cited SEC filing, so the label stands.

- exit kind: filing, the [8-K 2017-07-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275283/000119312517235158/d397089d8k.htm) reports the merger completed with BAT.
- successor: filing, the same 8-K shows BAT is the acquirer and RAI shares became cash plus BAT ADSs, so there is no successor security.
- last trade date: filing, the same 8-K and the [25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275283/000114331317000033/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml) say trading was suspended at the merger's effect before the open on 2017-07-25; the trading day before, 2017-07-24, follows from that stated date.
- payout rule: filing, the same 8-K states cash plus BAT ADSs, so cash_plus_stock.
- terms: filing, the same 8-K states $29.44 cash and 0.5260 BAT ADS per share.

## 5. What happened

- The merger took effect before market open on 2017-07-25; each RAI share (other than those owned by BAT or excluded holders) became BAT ADSs representing 0.5260 of a BAT ordinary share plus $29.44 cash; BAT held about 42% before closing ([SEC 8-K 2017-07-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275283/000119312517235158/d397089d8k.htm)).
- RAI shares were suspended from trading on the NYSE after closing; the 8-K cites the BAT ADS close of $69.25 on 2017-07-24 for an implied $65.87 per share ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275283/000119312517235158/d397089d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2017-07-25: shares came to evidence other securities and cash by operation of law on 2017-07-25; removal at the open on 2017-08-07 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3) ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275283/000114331317000033/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2017-08-04, the library's `delist_date` ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275283/000119312517248557/d399643d1512b.htm)).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-read the cited [8-K 2017-07-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275283/000119312517235158/d397089d8k.htm) through sec.py (cached/live SEC).

- **terms: upheld.** Item 2.01 states each RAI share (other than BAT-owned or excluded) converted into "a number of BAT American depositary shares ... representing 0.5260 of an ordinary share ... plus $29.44 in cash". The library's stock_ratio 1.0 is not in the filing; the 0.5260 is per ADS-equivalent ordinary share, and the filing's implied value ($65.87 = 29.44 + 0.5260 x 69.25) confirms 0.5260 ADS per RAI share is not contradicted (0.526 x 69.25 = 36.43; 36.43 + 29.44 = 65.87). The cash is stated in dollars, so USD for the blank currency holds. The wording says ADSs "representing 0.5260 of an ordinary share", so the ratio is ADS-per-RAI-share as the report reads it; the arithmetic of the implied value matches that reading only if one BAT ADS equals one ordinary share's price quote scaled to 1:1 (BTI ADS = 1 ordinary share), which is the standard BAT ADS ratio. Minor residual uncertainty: the "ADS count vs ordinary-share fraction" wording; it does not change that 1.0 is wrong.
- **Other fields the report calls agree** (kind, last trade date 2017-07-24 from "suspended ... prior to the opening of trading on July 25, 2017", cash 29.44) were consistent with the same 8-K; not disputed.
