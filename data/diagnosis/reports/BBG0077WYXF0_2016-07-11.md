## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2016-07-01, items 2.01/5.01/3.01: merger completed, shares cancelled for cash).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: CPG became a wholly owned subsidiary of TransCanada; no successor line).

- Terms: filing (8-K and Form 25 notice: $25.50 per share).

## 5. What happened

- Merger became effective at 12:01 a.m. EDT on 2016-07-01; each share converted into $25.50 cash, without interest ([SEC 8-K 2016-07-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1629995/000119312516639518/d221385d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE: security suspended from trading on 2016-07-01; each share converted into $25.50 cash ([SEC 25-NSE 2016-07-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1629995/000087666116001062/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2016-07-12 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1629995/000119312516646358/d211074d1512b.htm)).
- CPG kept filing 10-Qs afterward (2016-08-02, 2016-11-01) because of its remaining registered debt, which is what tripped the continued-filings rule ([SEC 10-Q 2016-08-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1629995/000162999516000054/cpgx-2016630x10q.htm)).
