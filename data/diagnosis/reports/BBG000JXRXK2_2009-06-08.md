## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2009-01-30 item 2.01; Form 25-NSE rule 12d2-2(a)(3)).
- Successor: filing (8-K: Sovereign Virginia became a wholly owned subsidiary of Santander; no continuing public line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: suspended from trading on 2009-01-30, so the last trade was 2009-01-29, a Thursday).

## 5. What happened

- Santander acquired all Sovereign common stock not already owned on 2009-01-30 through a reincorporation merger and share exchange; each share became 0.3206 Santander ADSs (or, by election, ordinary shares at the same ratio)
  ([SEC 8-K 2009-01-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811830/000095012309001677/y74339e8vk.htm)).
- On 2009-01-29 Sovereign asked the NYSE to file a Form 25; Item 3.01 same 8-K.
- The NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2009-02-02, removal effective at the opening on 2009-02-12; rule 12d2-2(a)(3); merger effective before the opening on 2009-01-30; security suspended from trading 2009-01-30
  ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811830/000087666109000094/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2009-02-02 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811830/000095012309001750/y74294e15v12b.htm)).
- Fails rows of CUSIP 845905108 under SOV run through 2009-02-03 only (offline fails index).
- Series C preferred stayed outstanding at the successor, which explains the later 10-K (2009-03-17) and 8-Ks that triggered the library's continued-filings rule.

## 9. Verification

A skeptic re-opened the cited filings through sec.py. All four fields the report calls wrong are upheld.

- **exit_kind (merger, not exchange): upheld.** 8-K 2009-01-30 (0000950123-09-001677), item 2.01: Santander "acquired all of the Common Stock not already owned"; each share was exchanged for 0.3206 Santander ADSs (or ordinary shares at the same ratio), and Sovereign Virginia became a wholly-owned subsidiary. This is a different company's securities, so it is not a continuation or a transfer. The Series C preferred was converted one for one into Sovereign Virginia preferred (item 3.03), which explains the later 10-K (2009-03-17) and the library's continued-filings guess. Form 25-NSE 0000876661-09-000094 exists (filed 2009-02-02, NYSE, class "Common Stock", rule 12d2-2(a)(3)), so the library's `no_form25` is wrong.
- **last_trade_date (2009-01-29): upheld.** The EX-99.25 notice says the merger became effective before the opening on 2009-01-30 and the security "was suspended from trading on January 30, 2009". 2009-01-30 was a Friday, so the last trading day was Thursday 2009-01-29. The date is not after the Form 25 effective date, and the library's blank is therefore wrong. Fails rows of CUSIP 845905108 end 2009-02-03 (15 rows, 2009-01-02 to 2009-02-03, last price 2.47). Those rows are settlement lag and do not show trading after the suspension.
- **value_rule (stock): upheld.** 8-K item 2.01 and the 25-NSE both state 0.3206 Santander ADS per common share, with no cash. The ordinary-share election is at the same ratio, so the package is the same. The library's `transfer` (0.0 fill) is wrong.
- **ticker_history (should end 2009-01-29): upheld.** This follows from the last trade date. The Form 25 effective 2009-02-12 and the suspension on 2009-01-30 show the NYSE line did not continue.
- **Caveat, not one of the four fields.** The filings never name the ticker "STD". The ADS is stated in words only. `price_ticker=STD` is outside knowledge, and the report already lists it as an open check. The price date of 2009-01-30 is worked out from the effective date.
