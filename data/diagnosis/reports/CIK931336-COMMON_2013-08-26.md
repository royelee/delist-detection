# CIK931336-COMMON_2013-08-26 (DF, Dean Foods)

## 1. Verdict
The 2013-08-26 "ending" is not an ending: it is a 1-for-2 reverse split (new CUSIP, same ticker DF) that the library read as an exchange transfer from a continued-filings guess. The security kept trading on the NYSE until the 2019 bankruptcy; its real ending is 2019-11-11 (dropped, bankruptcy, valued at the first OTC print). The library's row is wrong.

## 5. What happened

- Fails rows: old CUSIP 242370104 under DF to 2013-08-26; CUSIP 242370203 under DF from 2013-08-28 and on to 2019-11-14 (fails).
- Chapter 11 petition 2019-11-12 [SEC 8-K 2019-11-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/931336/000110465919062024/tm1922535-1_8k.htm).

- Fails rows: same CUSIP under DFODQ from 2019-11-15 to 2020-02.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
2013: kept the same security (reverse split, new CUSIP, same holders, same ticker). 2019: lost (bankruptcy, then OTC).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The run only knew CUSIP 242370104 (it ends 2013-08-26); with no Form 25 and the issuer still filing, the continued-filings rule (reason "Continued 10-K/Q filings >180d", flags no_form25, delist_date_approx) invented a 304 transfer. The new CUSIP 242370203 of the same issuer was never added. Cause: continued_filings_guess.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Confirm the first OTC session symbol and date (public OTC records or press release).
- Golden case: yes, a reverse split with a CUSIP change misread as an ending.

## 9. Verification
Skeptic pass; each field the report calls wrong was re-checked against SEC filings and fails rows. All four are upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K of 2013-08-15 (Items 5.03, 8.01) states a 1-for-2 reverse split effective 4:15 pm ET on 2013-08-26 and says the stock "will continue trading on the NYSE under the trading symbol DF" under a new CUSIP 242370203 ([SEC 8-K 2013-08-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/931336/000119312513335750/)). The only cash is for fractional shares, which is not consideration for surrendering the old share, so the exchange is not a merger (reference.md, decision 9). Fails rows: 242370104 under DF to 2013-08-26, 242370203 under DF from 2013-08-28 to 2019-11-14. The 2013 row is not an ending. The 2019 ending (8-K 1.03 of 2019-11-12; Form 25-NSE of 2019-11-27 citing the Chapter 11 filing) is `dropped` / `bankruptcy`.
- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The Form 25-NSE notice says the NYSE determined on 2019-11-12 to suspend the stock and that "trading in the Common Stock were suspended prior to the market open", so the last exchange trade was 2019-11-11 (decision 12). Fails rows under DF to 2019-11-14 are settlement rows and do not contradict that. Minor error in the report: the notice schedules the removal for the opening of business on 2019-12-09 under Rule 12d2-2(b). The 2019-11-27 date in the report's `effective_date` is the Form 25 filing date, not the effective date. The last trade date is earlier than either date, so the corrected `last_trade_date` is unchanged.
- **value_rule: upheld.** For a bankruptcy followed by OTC trading the decision table gives `otc_print` (decision 11). Fails rows show the same CUSIP 242370203 under DFODQ from 2019-11-15 (28 rows to 2019-12-31), so the stock did trade OTC. The symbol and date of the first OTC session (2019-11-12) are not confirmed. The rule is sound and the term `price_ticker` is `inferred`.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The security (same issuer, ticker and class) continued on CUSIP 242370203 to 2019-11-11, so the library's range ending 2013-08-26 with one CUSIP is wrong. Small slip in the report: the new CUSIP's first fails row is 2013-08-28, not 2013-08-27.

Not refuted. Open: the first OTC session and symbol (not on SEC), as the report already says.
