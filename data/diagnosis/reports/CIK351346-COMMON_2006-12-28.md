# BMET (Biomet Inc, CIK 351346): case CIK351346-COMMON_2006-12-28

## 1. Verdict

A real cash merger, but on a different date than the library's row. Biomet's common shares were acquired by LVB Acquisition (Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, KKR, TPG) for $46.00 cash per share, and they traded on Nasdaq until the close on 2007-09-25. The library's 2006-12-28 ending comes from a Form 25 that withdrew only the Preferred Share Purchase Rights, so the row is wrong in kind, date and value rule.

## 5. What happened

- On 2006-12-18 Biomet filed a Form 25 and a Form 15 (15-12B) that "relate solely to the withdrawal from listing of the Preferred Share Purchase Rights ... no effect upon the continued listing of the Common Shares" ([SEC Form 25 2006-12-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/351346/000110465906082100/a06-25962_225.htm), [SEC Form 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/351346/000110465906082101/a06-25962_31512b.htm)). The same day Biomet signed the merger agreement with LVB ([SEC 8-K 2006-12-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/351346/000110465906082097/a06-25962_48k.htm)).
- The 2007-01-12 8-K Item 3.01 is a separate notice (not read in detail; the library read it as a deficiency). Biomet kept trading and filing through 2007.
- Shareholders approved the amended and restated merger agreement on 2007-09-05; the merger closed 2007-09-25, each share converted into $46.00 cash ([SEC 8-K 2007-09-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/351346/000090342307000915/biomet8k_0925.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-09-25, effective that day ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/351346/000135445707000287/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Biomet filed Form 15-12B on 2007-10-05 ([SEC Form 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/351346/000090342307000966/biomet-15.htm)).
- The earlier 8-K Item 5.01 of 2007-07-17 describes the $46.00 cash tender offer and the merger to follow ([SEC 8-K 2007-07-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/351346/000119312507156544/d8k.htm)).
- Fails rows (CUSIP 090613100): 38 rows 2007-01-08 to 2007-09-26, last price $45.99 (offline fails data).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the shares were converted into cash by a private-equity buyer. A merger, not a continuation and not a drop.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder matched the 2006-12-18 Form 25 (filed against Biomet and Nasdaq) to the common security without noticing its class text read "Common Shares; Preferred Share Purchase Rights" while the notice states it relates solely to the Rights. Its 2006-12-28 date is the filing plus 10 days. The real Form 25-NSE of 2007-09-25 was not used. The 8-K 3.01 of 2007-01-12 then gave a compliance-failure (570) reading, and the same filing wrongly sets the ticker_history end. Cause tag: `other:rights_only_form25_matched_to_common`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Form 25 parsing: when the notice or the Form 15 says it relates solely to rights (or the 10-K/8-K shows the common continues), do not match it to the common stock; take the later Form 25-NSE of 2007-09-25.
- Corrected row: merger, last trade 2007-09-25, cash 46.00 USD; ticker_history for BMET extends to 2007-09-25 (this also removes `seeds_outside_history` only for dates up to then; the 2008-01-16 seed is a stale observation after the delisting).
- Open check: what the 2007-01-12 8-K Item 3.01 said (not read).
- Golden case: yes, a rights-only Form 25 that precedes the real merger by nine months.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check against SEC filings (sec.py grep/filings/fails). All four fields upheld.

- **exit_kind (dropped -> merger): upheld.** The 2006-12-18 Form 25 (0001104659-06-082100) lists "Common Shares; Preferred Share Purchase Rights" as the class, but its text says "This Notification relates solely to the withdrawal from listing of the Preferred Share Purchase Rights ... shall have no effect upon the continued listing of the Common Shares". So it is not a common-stock delisting. The 8-K of 2007-09-25 (0000903423-07-000915, Items 3.01/5.03/9.01) says LVB completed the merger on 2007-09-25, Biomet became a wholly-owned subsidiary, each Common Share was converted into $46.00 cash, and Nasdaq filed a Form 25 the same day. A 25-NSE (0001354457-07-000287) is in the filing list on 2007-09-25. Cash merger, not a drop. The 2007-01-12 8-K 3.01 (0001104659-07-002383) was a Nasdaq Staff Determination of 2007-01-11 (late filing), which the report left unread. That is a deficiency notice, not a delisting: the stock kept trading (fails rows run to 2007-09-26), so it does not rescue the library's 570 reading.
- **last_trade_date (blank -> 2007-09-25): upheld.** The 8-K says the Common Share was withdrawn from Nasdaq "as of the close of business on September 25, 2007". Fails rows for CUSIP 090613100: 38 rows 2007-01-08..2007-09-26, last price $45.99, consistent with trading to 09-25 and a near-$46 close. The date source is the 8-K 3.01 text (an allowed exchange-print source) and does not exceed the Form 25 effective date (filing 09-25 + 10 days). Minor nit: section 5 says the 25-NSE was "effective that day"; the statutory effective date is 10 days after filing. This does not change the last trade date.
- **value_rule (otc_print -> cash): upheld.** The same 8-K gives $46.00 per share cash, "without interest", with no stock leg. Cash terms: 46.00 USD.
- **ticker_history (ends 2006-12-28 -> ends 2007-09-25): upheld.** It follows from the ending being wrong. The security traded as BMET until 2007-09-25 (fails rows to 2007-09-26 with no later price). The 2006-12-28 end rests only on the rights-only Form 25.
