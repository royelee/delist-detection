# CIK886835-COMMON_2020-10-12 (Superior Energy Services, NYSE: SPN)

## 1. Verdict

A real delisting for a listing deficiency: the NYSE suspended Superior Energy Services' common stock after the close on 2020-09-17 (market cap under $15M), and it traded OTC as SPNX from 2020-09-18. The library's kind (dropped / guidelines) and rule (otc_print) are right. It names the wrong OTC symbol (SPNV, which is a different security), publishes no last trade date, and has the wrong exchange ticker in ticker_history.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.

- Successor: filing (no successor line; the 8-K and notice name none).

## 5. What happened

- 2020-09-17: NYSE notified the company that its 30-day average market cap was below $15M and suspended trading about 4:00 p.m. ET; the company did not appeal and expected OTCQX trading from 2020-09-18 as "SPNX" ([SEC 8-K 2020-09-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886835/000119312520248926/d19531d8k.htm)).
- 2020-10-02: NYSE filed Form 25-NSE to remove the common stock on 2020-10-13 under Rule 12d2-2(b) ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886835/000087666120000875/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows under CUSIP 868157306: SPN to 2020-09-21, then SPNX from 2020-09-24 (fails data, offline).
- 2020-12-07: Chapter 11 filed; existing equity to be discharged without distribution ([SEC 8-K 2020-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886835/000119312520311144/d63085d8k.htm)). 2021-02-02: plan effective, old equity cancelled ([SEC 8-K 2021-02-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886835/000119312521026071/d27671d8k.htm)). This is after the delisting and does not change it.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: delisted for a deficiency, then OTC. The shares stayed the same security and traded OTC; value is the first OTC print.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The security's NYSE ticker in the run is SPNV, taken from fails rows. The CUSIP traded under the temporary symbol SPNVD in 2019-12 and under SPN from 2019-12-27; the ftd-derived history labels it SPNV from 2019-10-01. SPNV is also the ticker of Supernova Partners (CUSIP 86846V108), so the otc_print price_ticker SPNV points to an unrelated security. The library never found the OTC symbol SPNX. The last trade date is unpublished because it carries `last_trade_date_unconfirmed` although the 8-K and notice state 2020-09-17. Cause tag: `terms_misread` (wrong price_ticker), with a ticker_history error.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish price_ticker SPNX (from the 8-K or the post-delisting fails symbol under the same CUSIP), and publish last_trade_date 2020-09-17 from the Form 25 notice / 8-K.
- Fix ticker_history to SPN (NYSE) to 2020-09-17, not SPNV.
- Optional: confirm the first SPNX OTC print on 2020-09-18 (qlib_practice's job, not a diagnosis fact).
- Golden case: yes, a well-sourced NYSE-to-OTC drop with a wrong ticker in the history.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: terms
- Upheld: ticker_history
- Refuted: ticker_history (sub-claim 'SPNV was never its ticker'): Fails rows show SPNV on Superior's old CUSIP 868157108 from 2019-10-01 to 2019-12-23. The library is wrong only from 2019-12-27, when the line traded as SPN.
- Refuted: last_trade_date (verdict text, not in the wrong list): The source row already publishes 2020-09-17 (ex99_notice). Only the internal unconfirmed flag is set, so 'unpublished' is wrong.

terms is upheld. The 8-K 0001193125-20-248926 names the OTCQX symbol SPNX. Fails rows under CUSIP 868157306 run SPN to 2020-09-21, then SPNX from 2020-09-24. Superior's own SPNV rows end 2019-12-23, and SPNV is Supernova from 2020-12-15, so price_ticker SPNV is wrong. ticker_history is wrong for 2019-12-27 to 2020-09-17. The report's fix should keep SPNV (unconfirmed) for 2019-10-01 to 2019-12-23. Section 9 was appended to the report and a verification object added to the record JSON.
