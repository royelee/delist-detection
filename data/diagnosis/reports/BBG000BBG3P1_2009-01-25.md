## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred. The label in section 3 was `verified`; it is corrected, because two key fields do not rest on a filing.
- Exit kind: filing (the 8-K of 2008-12-02 reports the NYSE suspension for a sub-$1.00 price; the Form 25-NSE of 2009-01-15 removes the common stock).
- Successor: fails rows (CUSIP 885218800 kept trading as THMR, then THMRQ, so no successor); the 8-K of 2009-05-01 only reports the later Chapter 11.
- Last trade date: filing (the 8-K of 2008-12-02 says suspended before the open on Friday 2008-12-05, so the trading day before, 2008-12-04).
- Payout rule: worked out from the fails rows (the same CUSIP trades OTC after the suspension, so the holder is valued at the first OTC print); no filing states a payout.
- Terms: fails rows for the OTC symbol THMR (first seen 2008-12-11); the price date 2008-12-05 is worked out as the session after the last exchange trade, not read from a print.

Not backed: the payout rule and the terms (THMR and its start date) rest on fails rows and a worked-out date. A filing or exchange print showing the first THMR trade date would make it verified.

## 9. Verification
Skeptic pass: the filings were re-read through sec.py. All three fields the report calls wrong are upheld, and the drop_reason claim also holds.

- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The 8-K filed 2008-12-02 (0001193125-08-246652, item 3.01) says NYSE Regulation notified the company on December 1, 2008 that the stock "should be suspended from trading prior to market opening on Friday, December 5, 2008". The Form 25-NSE of 2009-01-15 (0000876661-09-000070, EX-99.25) repeats it: suspended "before the opening of the trading session on December 5, 2008". Dec 4, 2008 was a Thursday and a normal session, so the last exchange trade is 2008-12-04 (decision 12). It is before the Form 25 effective date. The library's 2008-12-01 is the notice date, not a print. The report's own 8-K link and rule hold. [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/892535/000119312508246652/d8k.htm), [SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/892535/000087666109000070/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)
- **drop_reason (checked as well, upheld).** The EX-99.25 states the ground: average closing price under $1.00 over 30 consecutive trading days, not cured within six months (Listed Company Manual 802.01C), removal under Rule 12d2-2(b). That is a price deficiency (550), not an SEC order (573). The "SEC REVOKED 2012" reason matches neither filing. The report left open why it attached, which stays open.
- **terms: upheld, with a caveat.** The library's `price_date` of 2008-12-02 is before the suspension and cannot be the session after the last exchange trade, so it is wrong. 2008-12-05 follows the skill's rule (first OTC session after the last trade). The 8-K says the stock was expected to be quoted on the OTC Bulletin Board and/or Pink Sheets. Fails rows for CUSIP 885218800 show TMA through 2008-12-10 and THMR from 2008-12-11 (last $0.10, then first $0.15), so THMR is the same security's OTC symbol and the library's TMA is the dead NYSE symbol. Caveat: the TMA rows after 2008-12-04 are fails-settlement lag, not trading. The first THMR print date is not shown by any filing or fails row (the rows start 2008-12-11), so `price_date` stays worked out and not read from a print. This is why confidence stays `inferred`.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** `output/ticker_history.csv` has TMA 2007-12-17 to 2008-12-01 (source observation). The last exchange session was 2008-12-04, so the range should end then. The library's end rests on the last observation, not on the delisting.
- **Not refuted, not newly established.** The report's claim that the common stock's Form 25 effective date is 2009-01-25 matches the library's `delist_date`. The notice itself gives removal at the opening of January 26, 2009 (10 days after filing, so effective 2009-01-25). The 2009-05-01 Chapter 11 and the THMRQ symbol were not rechecked, since they do not affect these fields.
