## 1. Verdict

A real merger: on 2020-01-03 each Avon share became 0.300 Natura &Co Holding ADS (each ADS = two Natura shares), or 0.600 Natura shares at the holder's election. The library's kind, last trade date and stock-only rule and ratio are right; its only problem is that its own price check failed, so it filled assumed par and marked the row uncertain.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified: all five key fields rest on a cited SEC filing, so the label stands.

- exit kind (merger): [SEC 8-K 2020-01-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/8868/000095015720000022/form8-k.htm) (items 2.01, 5.01) and the 25-NSE filed 2020-01-06, which states the merger took effect 2020-01-03.
- successor (none): the same 8-K and 25-NSE show Natura is the acquirer and Avon survives only as a subsidiary, so no successor security continues Avon.
- last trade date (2020-01-03): the 8-K says AVP would be suspended before the open on 2020-01-06 and the 25-NSE gives that suspension date, so the trading day before, 2020-01-03, is read from the filings, not from MIDAS.
- payout rule (stock): the 8-K states each share became 0.300 Natura ADS, with cash only for fractions.
- terms (0.300 NTCO): the 8-K conversion terms; the 25-NSE repeats them.

## 5. What happened

- The two-step merger closed on 2020-01-03; each Avon share converted into 0.300 Natura ADS (two shares each) or 0.600 Natura shares, with cash for fractional ADSs; Series C preferred had been converted to common beforehand ([SEC 8-K 2020-01-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/8868/000095015720000022/form8-k.htm)).
- The same 8-K says AVP common would be suspended from the NYSE before the open on 2020-01-06 and was being formally delisted (items 1.02, 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01, 5.03).
- The NYSE's Form 25-NSE filed 2020-01-06 states the merger took effect 2020-01-03, the conversion terms above, and that the security was suspended from trading on 2020-01-06 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/8868/000087666120000004/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Avon filed Form 15-12B for its common stock and for its notes on 2020-01-24 ([SEC 15-12B common](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/8868/000095010320001015/dp119635_1512b-common.htm)); the filings after the end that the library saw came from the notes, not from a surviving listing.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: shares were exchanged for another company's ADSs, not one-for-one into the same holders' claim in a continuing line of Avon. A merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It mostly did not. The end-of-era resolver relabelled a continued-filings ending from the 8-K 5.01 (`resolved_from_continued_filings`), and the LLM terms failed the sanity check against the fails-based close (`terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`), so the value fell to assumed par (`assumed_par_after_failed_gate`, `merger_at_par`). The terms themselves match the 8-K. Likely the check compared the per-ADS stock leg with a mismatched price (the ratio is per ADS, two shares); not verified here. Cause tag: `library_right_but_uncertain`.
