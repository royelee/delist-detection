# BBG00B6WH9G3_2020-07-10: Match Group (old, CIK 1575189), MTCH

## 1. Verdict

A real merger, not a continuation: on 2020-06-30 old Match Group merged into a subsidiary of "New Match" (the renamed IAC/InterActiveCorp, CIK 891103), and each old Match share became one New Match share plus either $3.00 cash or 0.03370 extra New Match share (default: the stock election). The library's timing-only continuation (successor BBG00VT0KNC3, no value, last trade 2020-07-01) is wrong on kind, date and value, and it also attached IAC's Form 25 (CIK 891103) instead of old Match's own.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2020-07-06, Introductory Note / Item 2.01: Match merged into Match Merger Sub, holders received New Match stock plus an election).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: New Match is the payer; not a one-for-one line).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K Item 3.01: Form 25 suspends trading before the open on 2020-07-01, so the last trade is 2020-06-30).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: non-electors treated as additional stock election).
- Terms: filing (8-K: 1 share + 0.03370 share or $3.00). The price date is worked out as the next session.

## 5. What happened

- The Transaction Agreement (2019-12-19, amended) closed 2020-06-30. Old IAC was renamed Match Group, Inc.; IAC Holdings became IAC/InterActiveCorp; old Match merged into Match Merger Sub ([SEC 8-K 2020-07-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575189/000110465920080741/tm2023781d3_8k.htm)).
- Old Match holders (other than IAC and subsidiaries) received one share of Old IAC Class M (the new Match common) plus, at election, $3.00 cash or 0.03370 further share (value based on a $88.9466 10-day VWAP); non-electors got the stock ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575189/000110465920080741/tm2023781d3_8k.htm)).
- Item 3.01: Nasdaq filed Form 25 on 2020-06-30 to suspend old Match stock before the open on 2020-07-01 (same 8-K).
- Old Match's own Form 25-NSE: accession 0001354457-20-000292, effective 2020-07-01 ([SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575189/000135445720000292/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)). The library used 0001354457-20-000290, which is IAC's (CIK 891103).
- Form 15-12B filed 2020-07-10 by Match Merger Sub as successor ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575189/000110465920082741/tm2024344d1_1512b.htm)); this is the library's delist_date.
- Fails rows: CUSIP 57665R106 (old) last row 2020-07-01; new CUSIP 57667L107 from 2020-07-02 (offline fails data).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: old shares were exchanged for another company's shares plus a cash option; the 1:1 continuation test fails because cash and extra shares are part of the exchange.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason string "Continuation (timing:cik)", flags `handoff_continuation`, `continuation_by_timing_only`: a ticker handoff (MTCH to MTCH) was read as a continuation from timing. The delisting also took IAC's Form 25 and CIK 891103, and its last trade is the last sighting. Cause tag: `wrong_exit_kind`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Treat the handoff as a merger with the election package (stock 1.0337 default); last trade 2020-06-30; ticker_history end 2020-06-30; use Form 25 0001354457-20-000292.
- Check: first print date of the new MTCH line (price_date assumed 2020-07-01; fails first show 2020-07-02).
- Golden case: yes, a same-ticker holdco-style separation with an election.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check against the cited filings (8-K 0001104659-20-080741; Form 25-NSE 0001354457-20-000292 under CIK 1575189; Form 25-NSE 0001354457-20-000290 under CIK 891103). Result: all six fields upheld, one with a scope note.

- **exit_kind (merger): upheld.** The 8-K says old Match holders received, "in exchange for each outstanding share of Company Common Stock", one share of the new Match common plus, at the holder's election, $3.00 cash or 0.03370 share. Cash is offered inside the exchange for the old share, so the operator ruling (2026-10-03, BHI) makes it a merger, not a continuation. Old Match merged "with and into" Match Merger Sub.
- **successor: upheld.** The new Match line (renamed Old IAC Class M, CUSIP 57667L107) is the payer, not a one-for-one continuation, so the library's continuation successor link BBG00VT0KNC3 is wrong as a continuation.
- **last_trade_date (2020-06-30): upheld.** 8-K Item 3.01: Nasdaq filed Form 25 on 2020-06-30 "to suspend trading ... prior to the opening of trading on July 1, 2020", so the last trade is 2020-06-30. Form 25-NSE 0001354457-20-000292 has effectiveness date 2020-07-01, so 06-30 is not after it. The library's 2020-07-01 is a last sighting; fails rows are dated one day after the close they price.
- **value_rule (stock, ratio 1.0337): upheld.** 8-K: holders "who did not make an election were treated as having made an additional stock election", so the default package is 1 + 0.03370 = 1.0337 new shares. The cash alternative (1 share + $3.00 USD) is the holder's choice, and the report states both. The price_date 2020-07-01 remains an unconfirmed assumption, as the open check says.
- **issuer: upheld, scope note.** The contract's security issuer (1575189) is already right in the source row (`issuer_ids`). What is wrong is the internal delisting row: `library_cik` 891103 and accession 0001354457-20-000290, which the EDGAR index lists under CIK 891103 (IAC). Old Match's own filing is 0001354457-20-000292 (CIK 1575189, effective 2020-07-01). The report's "wrong" applies to the internal row only.
- **ticker_history (ends 2020-06-30): upheld.** It follows from the last trade date; the library's range ends 2020-07-01, one session after the suspension.

Refuted: none. Not checked: the first print date of the new MTCH line (no filing states it).
