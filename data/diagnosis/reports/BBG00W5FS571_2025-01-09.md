## 5. What happened

- On 2024-12-24 a BC plan of arrangement closed: Old BEPC (renamed Brookfield Renewable Holdings Corporation) holders other than the Brookfield Group received New BEPC shares one for one ([SEC 8-K12B 2024-12-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1791863/000119312524285923/d795477d8k12b.htm)).
- New BEPC is the successor issuer under Rule 12g-3; board and officers initially identical ([SEC 8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1791863/000119312524285923/d795477d8k12b.htm)).
- Old BEPC shares were to be suspended on the NYSE on 2024-12-30 and New BEPC shares to start trading on the NYSE as BEPC the same day; the NYSE would file a Form 25 ([SEC 8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1791863/000119312524285923/d795477d8k12b.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2024-12-30 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1791863/000087666124001225/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows (library cusip_history): old CUSIP 11284V105 through 2024-12-27, new CUSIP 11285B108 from 2024-12-30.
