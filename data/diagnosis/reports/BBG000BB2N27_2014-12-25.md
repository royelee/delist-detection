## 4. Why this confidence

Verified. It was inferred until 2026-10-03, when the acquirer's own closing 8-K was read. That 8-K states QSR's first trading day, which is the price date.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2014-12-12, items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01: the arrangement and merger completed, each share converted).
- Successor: filing (the same 8-K: holders receive cash and RBI shares; RBI is the acquirer, no continuing THI line).
- Last trade date: filing (the Form 25-NSE: merger effective 2014-12-12, suspended from trading 2014-12-15, so the last trade is 2014-12-12).
- Payout rule: filing (the 8-K's default package, cash plus RBI shares).
- Terms: filing. C$65.50 and 0.8025 are in the 8-K and the Form 25-NSE. The symbol and the price date are in RBI's 8-K of 2014-12-12 (CIK 1618756, [0001193125-14-441411](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1618756/000119312514441411/d836919d8k.htm)): the common shares "were approved for listing on (a) the New York Stock Exchange ('NYSE') under the symbol 'QSR'" and "will begin trading on the NYSE and the TSX on December 15, 2014". The price security BBG0076WG2V1 is a look-up in the library's tables.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the share was exchanged for cash plus another company's shares, a merger and not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Terms match the filing except the currency: `cash_currency` is blank though the cash is Canadian dollars. Its check (`terms_gate=failed`, flag `llm_gate_failed`) most likely compared C$65.50 plus the stock leg with a USD close, so the value fell to assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause tag: `currency_missing`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish `cash_currency=CAD` and make the gate convert it (or skip it for a non-USD cash leg). The fill then becomes a measured value once qlib_practice prices QSR.
- Steps 2 and 3 of the CAL fix (BBG000BDXVW8_2010-10-14) also apply:
  - Publish price_sec_id BBG0076WG2V1 for QSR. The acquirer is RBI (CIK 1618756), whose QSR line is in the run.
  - QSR's ticker_history starts 2014-12-17, two sessions after the first trading day the filing states. Taking the ticker from ticker_history on 2014-12-15 would find nothing, so the history should start on 2014-12-15.
- These steps alone do not make the gate pass. Fails data has no 2014-12-15 close for QSR (its first row, 2014-12-17, carries the 12-16 close), and the cash leg is in CAD.
- Golden case: yes, a cash-and-stock election deal with a non-USD cash leg.
