## 1. Verdict

A 1:1 holding-company reorganization: on 2021-03-01 each Apache share became one APA Corporation share, and APA Corp took the ticker APA on Nasdaq. The library's kind (exchange, continuation, successor BBG00YTS96G2) is right, but its ending is the "continued filings" guess with no last trade date; the date should be 2021-03-01.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (the 8-K12B of 2021-03-01: Apache became a wholly owned subsidiary of APA Corporation, a reorganization).
- Successor: filing (the same 8-K12B: APA Corporation, CIK 1841666, successor issuer under Rule 12g-3).
- Last trade date: worked out (the Nasdaq Form 25-NSE says Apache reorganized as APA Corp on 3/2/2021, so the last Apache print is taken as the trading day before, 2021-03-01; APA Corp's fails rows under its own CUSIP start 2021-03-02 and agree, but no filing states the last trading day).
- Payout rule: filing (the 8-K12B: automatic one for one conversion, same class, so a continuation with no value).
- Terms: filing (the same 8-K12B: one APA share per Apache share, no cash).

Not backed: the last trade date, which rests on a date worked out from the Form 25 notice and on fails rows, not on a filing or an exchange print that states the last day. Evidence that would make it verified: a filing stating the last day Apache shares traded (for example an 8-K or press release saying "ceased trading at the close on 2021-03-01"), or an exchange print for that day.

## 5. What happened

- Apache became a wholly owned subsidiary of APA Corporation at the effective time on 2021-03-01; APA replaced Apache as the Nasdaq-traded company under ticker APA ([SEC 8-K12B 2021-03-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1841666/000119312521063695/d127090d8k12b.htm)).
- Each Apache share converted automatically into one APA share with the same rights; holders became APA holders one for one in the same class (same filing).
- The filing establishes APA as successor issuer under Rule 12g-3(a) (same filing).
- Nasdaq's Form 25-NSE for Apache, filed 2021-03-04, states "ON 3/2/2021 - APACHE CORPORATION REORGANIZED AS APA CORPORATION" ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6769/000135445721000304/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); with the 10-day rule it is effective 2021-03-14, the library's delist_date.
- Apache filed a Form 15-12B on 2021-03-19 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6769/000119312521087202/d133109d1512b.htm)).
- APA Corp's common stock has its own CUSIP 03743Q108 in the fails data from 2021-03-02; Apache's was 037411105 (library cusip_history, fails rows).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Mostly it did not: kind, continuation and successor agree. It is uncertain because the Form 25 effective date (2021-03-14) is not an exchange print date, so `last_trade_date` is blank (`no_last_trade_date`, `no_last_close`), and the reason string is the generic "Continued 10-K/Q filings ... successor by same ticker" (`continued_filings_rule`), not the 8-K12B-based reorganization. The old security's range runs to 2021-03-14 and overlaps the successor's. Cause tag: `no_last_trade_print`.
