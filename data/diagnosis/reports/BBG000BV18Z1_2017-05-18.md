The library's row is wrong on its date: it ends the security at the bankruptcy filing (2017-05-18, a guessed last trade, no Form 25) when the real ending is the 2017-07-31 effective date. ## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason `Bankruptcy (8-K item 1.03 filed 2017-05-18)`, bucket liquidation, CRSP 470, flags `no_form25`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`. The resolver anchored the ending to the filing day rather than to the effective date when the old stock stopped trading, and read the case as an OTC move though the stock stayed on the NYSE (`otc_print` with no date; Shumway fill -0.30). Cause tag: `resolver_no_bankruptcy_branch`... more exactly the ending date came from the 1.03 filing: `other:bankruptcy_ending_dated_at_filing_not_effective_date`.

## 9. Verification

Checked by a second pass against the cached SEC filings and fails rows. Both fields the report calls wrong are upheld; one factual gap in the report's description of the payout is noted.

- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The old common did not stop trading at the 2017-05-17/18 filing. The 10-K filed 2017-06-13 still lists maintaining the NYSE listing as an open goal ([SEC 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/98222/000156459017012484/tdw-10k_20170331.htm)). Fails rows of old CUSIP 886423102 under TDW run 2017-05-01..2017-06-30 (44 rows) and 2017-07-03..2017-07-31 (14 rows, last $0.96), then one row under the deleted symbol TDWXXXX on 2017-08-02. The new CUSIP 88642R109 first appears 2017-08-01 (TDWZZZZ) and trades as TDW from 2017-08-02 (first $25.00). The 8-K of 2017-07-31 (items 1.02, 3.02) states the Old Common Stock was cancelled on the Effective Date ([SEC 8-K 2017-07-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/98222/000119312517242513/d431632d8k.htm)). No Form 25 or Form 15 appears for 2017-03..2018-12. So the internal 2017-05-18 date is wrong and the real end is the 2017-07-31 effective date. Caveat: the exact last exchange day (07-31 vs 07-28) rests on fails rows only, and the contract's blank `last_trade_date` is itself policy-correct under decision 12 (no exchange print); the "wrong" status refers to the guessed internal date and the clipping at the filing, not to publishing a date.
- **value_rule: upheld.** `otc_print` is wrong because the stock stayed on the NYSE and was never moved to OTC (same filings and fails rows as above), and decision 11's OTC print applies only after a move off the exchange. Correction to the report: holders of Old Common Stock received more than equity warrants. The 8-K item 3.02 says pre-emergence stockholders received 1,500,000 shares of New Common Stock plus 2,432,432 Series A Warrants (exercise $57.06) and 2,629,657 Series B Warrants (exercise $62.28), pro rata. So the payout is new stock plus warrants, per-share ratios not extracted, and `unknown` remains the right rule until the ratios are computed from the old share count; the report's "cancelled for equity warrants" and its `consideration = other (equity warrants)` omit the stock leg. This does not change the verdict that the library's rule is wrong.
- **Unchanged and not contested:** exit_kind `dropped` / `bankruptcy`, continuation false, no successor (post-bankruptcy equity is a new security).
