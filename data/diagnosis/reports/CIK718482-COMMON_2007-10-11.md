- Exit kind: filing (Form 25-NSE EX-99.25: shares came to evidence other securities on 2007-10-01, rule 12d2-2(a)(3); 8-K 2007-09-28 stockholder approval).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2007-05-31: acquirer Wachovia, conversion into cash and WB shares).
- Last trade date: worked out (the notice gives 2007-10-01 as the conversion day, not a last-trade statement; 2007-09-28 is the trading day before).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2007-05-31).
- Terms: filing (8-K 2007-05-31: 0.9844 WB shares and $35.80 per share); price date is the first session after close, worked out.
- Not backed: an explicit statement or exchange print of the last trading day. An NYSE notice or press release stating it would make this `verified`.
