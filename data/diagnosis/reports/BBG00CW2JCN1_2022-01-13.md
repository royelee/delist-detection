the new Apollo Global Management (APO). The library's row (merger, stock 1.149 x APO, last trade 2021-12-31) is right;
it is uncertain only because its reason chain went through the continued-filings resolver.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2022-01-03 reports completion of the Mergers; Form 25-NSE says shares were exchanged).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K and notice: AGM is the acquirer, AHL a subsidiary of AGM afterwards).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: suspended from trading on 2022-01-03, so the last trade was 2021-12-31).
- Payout rule: filing (Form 25-NSE: each ATH share exchanged for 1.149 AGM shares).
- Terms: filing (same notice, 1.149; no cash leg).

## 5. What happened

- Merger effective 2022-01-01 (AHL Merger Effective Time 1:01 a.m. ET); AHL continued as a direct subsidiary of AGM
  ([SEC 8-K 2022-01-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1527469/000152746922000003/ahl-20211231.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2022-01-03: each common share exchanged for 1.149 AGM shares; suspended from trading
  2022-01-03 (the notice's "January 01, 2021" is a typo for 2022)
  ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1527469/000087666122000008/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Athene declared a $750 million dividend to its own pre-merger holders, payable after the Mergers; a distribution
  on the old shares by the target's board, not merger consideration in the filing
  ([SEC 8-K 2022-01-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1527469/000152746922000003/ahl-20211231.htm)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2022-01-14 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1527469/000119312522009742/d232731d1512b.htm)).

(8-K item 5.01 filed 2022-01-03); the registrant kept filing after it", i.e. the end-of-era resolver ran, although a
Form 25-NSE (0000876661-22-000008) exists and the last trade came from its EX-99 notice (`ex99_notice`). Cause tag:
`library_right_but_uncertain`.
