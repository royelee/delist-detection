# DTV (The DIRECTV Group) 2009: holdco reorganization into new DIRECTV

## 1. Verdict

Not a cash or stock merger exit. On 2009-11-19 (after the close) The DIRECTV Group merged into a subsidiary of new DIRECTV (CIK 1465112) in the Liberty Entertainment combination; each Group share became one share of new DIRECTV Class A, still traded as DTV on Nasdaq. This is a one-for-one continuation with successor BBG000FL1TC8. The library's `merger` / `stock` row with a blank date is wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2009-11-20, item 2.01: one Class A share per Group share).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K names DIRECTV, ticker DTV); the FIGI link rests on fails rows (CUSIP 25490A101 under DTV from 2009-11-20) and the library's own securities table.
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K gives the effective time 5:15 pm EST on 2009-11-19 and the fails rows show the old CUSIP's last row 2009-11-19 and the new one from 2009-11-20; the Form 25-NSE (effective 2009-11-19) notice text was not parsed for a suspension date.
- Payout rule: filing (1:1, no cash).
- Terms: filing.

Not backed: an exchange print for the last day. The 25-NSE EX-99.25 notice stating the suspension date would make it verified (MIDAS does not cover 2009).

## 5. What happened

- Liberty Media's Split-Off left Liberty Entertainment Inc (LEI) holding 57% of DIRECTV Group; the Mergers combined DIRECTV Group and LEI under new DIRECTV. [SEC 8-K 2009-11-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/944868/000110465909066017/a09-33784_18k.htm)
- The DIRECTV merger became effective 5:15 pm EST on 2009-11-19; holders of DIRECTV Group common received one Class A share per share held; LEI holders received 1.11130 Class A. Same 8-K.
- New DIRECTV Class A trades on Nasdaq as DTV; the Group's common stock was delisted. Same 8-K.
- Form 25-NSE filed 2009-11-19, effective that day. [SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/944868/000135445709000311/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)
- Fails rows: old CUSIP 25459L106 last 2009-11-19; CUSIP 25490A101 under DTV from 2009-11-20 (first row $0.01, then about $31.50). Offline `sec.py fails`.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced 1:1 by a new line of the same holders (the new line also issued shares to LEI holders, which dilutes but does not change what Group holders received). Continuation per decision 9; no cash was paid in the exchange.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason "M&A 2.01+3.01+5.01 (acquired by external acquirer)" read the 8-K items as an acquisition; the LLM then read stock 1.0 DTV with no price date (flag `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_price`). The "acquirer" is the holdco itself. Cause: `holdco_not_linked`. The old line's range end 2009-11-29 is an artifact (no last trade found), overlapping the new line from 2009-11-23 (`ticker_overlap:DTV`).

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Date the last trade 2009-11-19 and clip the old ticker_history there.
- Confirm the 25-NSE EX-99.25 text states the suspension date.
- Good golden candidate (well-sourced holdco case with a dilutive co-merger).

## 9. Verification

Result: upheld on all five fields (exit_kind, successor, value_rule, terms, ticker_history). Re-read the [8-K of 2009-11-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/944868/000110465909066017/a09-33784_18k.htm), the [25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/944868/000135445709000311/) header and the fails rows (offline `sec.py fails`).

- **exit_kind (merger -> exchange): upheld.** The 8-K says holders of DIRECTV Group common stock "received one share of DIRECTV Class A common stock for each share of DIRECTV Group common stock held", with no cash leg and no fractional-share cash in the matches read. That is 1:1, no cash in the exchange (decision 9, reference.md). The 1.1113 ratio belongs to LEI holders only, so dilution does not change what Group holders got. Item 5.01 and 2.01 wording ("change of control") describes the holdco layering, not a third-party purchase: DTVG One merged into DIRECTV Group, which survived. New DIRECTV Class A trades as DTV on Nasdaq from 2009-11-20, so the holder kept the same ticker. The continuation reading stands; a merger reading is not supported.
- **successor (none -> BBG000FL1TC8): upheld.** CUSIP 25490A101 ("DIRECTV CL A COM STK") has fails rows from 2009-11-20 (first $0.01, then DTV from 2009-11-23 at about $31.50), while old CUSIP 25459L106 has its last row 2009-11-19 (125 rows, last $31.03). The FIGI link is `inferred` from the fails rows and the library's own securities table (ticker_history row BBG000FL1TC8 starting 2009-11-20); not a filing statement of the FIGI. Same caveat as the report's section 4.
- **value_rule / terms (stock 1.0 x DTV, no date -> continuation): upheld.** Ratio 1.0 is right but the rule is not an exit; there is no price date because none is needed. The library's `terms_gate=failed:no_acq_price` fits a rule read as an exit when there is none. The "acquirer" ticker DTV is the successor of the same holders.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** Library keeps CIK944868-COMMON DTV to 2009-11-29 while BBG000FL1TC8 carries DTV from 2009-11-23 (25490A from 2009-11-20): overlapping ranges, as flagged `ticker_overlap:DTV`. The old line's fails rows end 2009-11-19, and the 8-K gives the DIRECTV effective time as 5:15 p.m. EST on 2009-11-19, after the close, so the end 2009-11-19 is supported. The report's text "BBG000FL1TC8 DTV from 2009-11-23" matches the library's DTV row.

Not refuted but still open: the 25-NSE EX-99.25 text was not read (it matched only its header, effectiveness 2009-11-19), so last_trade_date 2009-11-19 stays `worked out` (8-K effective time plus fails rows), not an exchange print. Confidence `inferred` is appropriate. Minor: the report's `last_trade_date` status "missing" is also upheld (library blank).
