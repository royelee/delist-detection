## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2011-04-05 items 2.01/3.03/5.01: merger completed, Qwest a wholly owned subsidiary).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: holders receive CTL shares; no continuing Qwest line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: merger effective before the opening on 2011-04-01, suspended 2011-04-01, so last trade 2011-03-31).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01: conversion into CTL shares).
- Terms: filing (8-K and Form 25 notice both state 0.1664 CTL share per Qwest share).

## 5. What happened

- Merger Sub merged into Qwest on 2011-04-01; each Qwest share converted into 0.1664 CTL shares, cash in lieu of fractions ([SEC 8-K 2011-04-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037949/000110465911018718/a11-9685_18k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2011-04-01: merger effective before the opening on 2011-04-01, security suspended 2011-04-01, removal at the opening on 2011-04-12 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037949/000087666111000164/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2011-04-14 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037949/000110465911020159/a11-10122_11512b.htm)). Qwest kept filing because of its bonds.
