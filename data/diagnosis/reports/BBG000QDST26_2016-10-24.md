## 5. What happened

- Merger closed 2016-10-14; each ITC share converted into $22.57 cash and 0.7520 Fortis common shares, about $3.4 billion cash in total ([SEC 8-K 2016-10-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1317630/000110465916150305/a16-19905_18k.htm)).
- ITC requested NYSE delisting; trading was suspended prior to the NYSE open on 2016-10-14 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- Form 25-NSE filed 2016-10-14 by the NYSE ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1317630/000087666116001345/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); the library dates the delisting 2016-10-24 (filing plus 10 days).
- S-8 POS and POS AM deregistrations were filed the same day.
