## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (Liberty Broadband 8-K, Item 2.01, completion on 2020-12-18).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: none, the acquirer survives).
- Last trade date: worked out from the closing date 2020-12-18 and the library's MIDAS print; no filing states "ceased trading on D".
- Payout rule: filing (same 8-K, stock conversion).
- Terms: filing (0.580 Series C); price date is the next trading day, worked out.

Not backed: the exact last-trade day by a filing. A Form 25 EX-99.25 notice or the joint press release stating the last trading day would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- On 2020-12-18 the combination closed. Each GCI Liberty Series A share was converted into 0.580 of a Liberty Broadband Series C share ([SEC 8-K 2020-12-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1611983/000110465920138229/tm2038584d3_8k.htm)).
- GCI Liberty's preferred was exchanged for new Liberty Broadband preferred (LBRDP), same 8-K.
- Form 25-NSE filed by the exchange on 2020-12-21 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/808461/000135445720000814/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- LBRDK traded in December 2020 (fails rows: CUSIP 530307305, LIBERTY BROADBAND CORP COM SER C).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old share was exchanged for another company's stock, a merger.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The LLM-read terms name LBRDA as the acquirer ticker, while the filing says Series C (LBRDK); `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_price` and `assumed_par_after_failed_gate` follow, so the 0.0 fill is a consequence. Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish price_ticker=LBRDK (Series C); the ratio 0.580 stands.
- Open check: a filing statement of the last trading day (the joint press release, EX-99.1, was not read).
- Golden case: yes, a stock-only merger where the acquirer has several share classes.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: terms

Liberty Broadband 8-K 0001104659-20-138229 says each GCI Liberty Series A share converted into 0.580 of a Liberty Broadband Series C share (LBRDK). The cover page lists Series A (LBRDA) and Series C (LBRDK) as separate classes. So the library's LBRDA price ticker is the wrong class and the ratio 0.580 is right. The closing date of December 18, 2020 is confirmed. The "cash in lieu of fractions" wording was not surfaced by my grep, but it does not change the rule or terms.
