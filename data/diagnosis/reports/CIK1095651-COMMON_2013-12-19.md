## 5. What happened

- iStar announced the NYSE ticker change from "SFI" to "STAR" beginning December 19, 2013 ([SEC 8-K 2013-12-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1095651/000110465913090155/a13-25922_18k.htm)).
- Fails rows for CUSIP 45031U101: SFI 2013-12-02..2013-12-19, then STAR 2013-12-20 through 2017-06 with no gap and the same name (fails-to-deliver data, via sec.py fails).
- The issuer kept filing: 10-K 2014-03-03 and 2015-03-02, quarterly 8-Ks ([SEC 10-K 2014-03-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1095651/000109565114000007/star-12312013x10k.htm)). No Form 25 or Form 15 found in the window.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same CUSIP, new ticker (rename).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The run's observations stop at SFI on 2013-12-19, and no observation carries STAR. With no Form 25, the continued-filings rule (304, `continued_filings_rule`) invented an ending on the last sighting (`delist_date_approx`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`). The rename was not followed. Cause: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Drop the ending; extend ticker_history with STAR from 2013-12-19 (the fails rows under the CUSIP show it).
- The security's later history was not examined (a delisting after 2017, if the run covers one, is a separate case).
- Golden case: yes. A rename with a no-Form-25 continued-filings fallback should yield no ending.
