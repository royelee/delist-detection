## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (report says wrong: library `price_ticker=BTU`, should be `BTUUQ`). Verdict: **upheld**.

- Re-read the 8-K of 2016-04-13 (accession 0001064728-16-000173, Item 3.01) through sec.py: "the Company's common stock is expected to begin trading on the OTC Pink Sheets marketplace under the symbol BTUUQ on April 14, 2016." The NYSE suspended trading on April 13, 2016, so the last exchange trade was 2016-04-12, as the report says.
- reference.md, `otc_print` row: `price_ticker` is "the security's own OTC symbol if known", and `price_date` is "the session after the last exchange trade". The filing names the OTC symbol (BTUUQ), so it is known. The library's `price_ticker=BTU` is the exchange symbol, which has no prints after the suspension. Its `price_date=2016-04-13` matches the rule, so the report is right to leave the date alone and to flag the 8-K's 2016-04-14 only as an open check.
- Security class: the 8-K and the Form 25-NSE (0000876661-16-000954, filed 2016-05-04, Rule 12d2-2(b), removal at the open on 2016-05-16) both concern the common stock, so BTUUQ is the right class.
- The agreeing fields (exit_kind, drop_reason, last_trade_date, value_rule, issuer 1064728) rest on the same filings and nothing found contradicts them. These were not re-derived further.
- Uncertainty: I did not check when BTUUQ actually first printed (the 8-K only expects it). This is already an open check in section 8 and does not affect the `price_ticker` claim.
