# GXP (Great Plains Energy) -> Evergy, 2018

## 1. Verdict

A real merger: on 2018-06-04 each Great Plains Energy share became 0.5981 of a share of the new holding company Evergy (EVRG, trading from 2018-06-05). The library's kind, date and stock ratio are right; it publishes no price security (`price_ticker` blank, `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`), so the stock leg cannot be priced and the row falls back to assumed par.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2018-06-04 Item 8.01 and the NYSE Form 25-NSE notice).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: GPE merged into Evergy; shares converted, not carried over one for one).
- Last trade date: worked out from the 8-K (merger effective 2018-06-04, EVRG trades from 2018-06-05) and the library's MIDAS date; no filing states "last traded on".
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: conversion into Evergy shares only).
- Terms: filing (8-K and Form 25 notice: 0.5981; 8-K: EVRG begins trading 2018-06-05).

Not backed: the last trade day by a filing statement. An exchange print (MIDAS is the library's source) or an NYSE notice naming the day would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- On 2018-06-04 GPE merged with and into Evergy (formerly Monarch Energy Holding); each GPE share was converted into 0.5981 Evergy common share. Evergy common stock begins trading as EVRG on 2018-06-05 ([SEC 8-K 2018-06-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1143068/000119312518182966/d454249d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2018-06-06 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3): shares came to evidence other securities; each GPE share was exchanged for 0.5981 Evergy share; removal at the opening of 2018-06-18; no cash right stated ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1143068/000087666118000661/)).
- No cash leg is stated in either filing.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the GPE share was converted into shares of another company at a ratio other than one for one, in a merger of two utilities. A merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The ending, date and ratio (0.5981, from the LLM) are right. The acquirer ticker was not extracted (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`), so `price_ticker` is blank, the payout gate failed (`payout_gate_failed:25`) and the value fell to assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause tag: `terms_not_extracted`.
