## 5. What happened

- Closing 2022-04-08: Discovery amended its charter, renamed itself Warner Bros. Discovery, and reclassified each Series A, B and C share into one WBD common share ([SEC 8-K 2022-04-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437107/000119312522103051/d328161d8k.htm)).
- Same 8-K, item 3.01: Nasdaq suspended the Discovery series before the open on 2022-04-11; WBD common began trading that day; delisting effective 2022-04-18, ten days after the Form 25 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437107/000119312522103051/d328161d8k.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE filed under the same CIK ([SEC 25-NSE 0001354457-22-000231](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437107/000135445722000231/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The WarnerMedia Spinco merger was a separate step: Spinco holders received about 0.2419 WBD shares per Spinco share; Discovery holders did not take part in it (same 8-K, item 2.01).
- The 8-K's 2.01 item (acquisition of Spinco) is what the library read as a "completed acquisition".

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason: "Completed acquisition (8-K item 2.01 ...); the registrant kept filing after it", flags `resolved_from_continued_filings`, `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`, `merger_at_par`. The end-of-era resolver took item 2.01 (the issuer's acquisition of Spinco) as the issuer being acquired, although the registrant is the surviving WBD and item 3.03 states the 1:1 reclassification. Cause: `holdco_not_linked` (a same-issuer 1:1 reclassification, successor WBD in the run but not linked).
