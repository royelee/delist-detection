## 5. What happened

- 2016-08-08: Monster, Randstad North America and Merlin Global Acquisition signed the merger agreement; a $3.40 cash tender offer began 2016-09-06 ([SEC 8-K 2016-11-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1020416/000119312516755173/d279397d8k.htm)).
- The merger closed 2016-11-01 under DGCL 251(h) without a stockholder meeting; shares were listed on the NYSE as MWW; the parties asked NYSE to file Form 25 (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2016-11-01 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3): each share was converted into $3.40 cash and the security was suspended from trading on 2016-11-01; removal at the open on 2016-11-14 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1020416/000087666116001386/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The library's own 2008-11-20 row (Form 25, Nasdaq to NYSE, MNST to MWW) is the earlier listing move; the stock kept trading under MWW afterwards (ticker_history shows MWW from 2008-11-19).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check through sec.py (cached/live SEC) of the four fields the report calls wrong. All four upheld.

- **exit_kind (merger, not exchange): upheld.** The 2016-11-01 8-K (0001193125-16-755173, items 1.02, 2.01, 2.04, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01, 5.02, 5.03, 9.01) says Merger Sub's $3.40 cash tender offer was followed by the Merger; each share was converted into cash, a change in control occurred and Monster became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Randstad North America. The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-16-001386, Rule 12d2-2(a)(3), class "Common Stock", NYSE) EX-99.25 says each share was converted into $3.40 cash. `filings` for CIK 1020416 over 2009-01-01..2017-03-01 returns only this one Form 25/25-NSE, so there is no 2009 Form 25 and the library's 2009 ending rests on the continued-filings guess.
- **last_trade_date (2016-10-31): upheld.** The EX-99.25 states the security "was suspended from trading on November 1, 2016". 2016-11-01 was a Tuesday, so under decision 12 the last trade was Monday 2016-10-31, before the Form 25 effective date. The date is derived from the notice, not printed in it. Fails rows under MWW (CUSIP 611742107) run 2010-01-04..2016-11-07 (22 rows 2016-10-03..11-07, last price $3.41, near the $3.40 offer); rows after the suspension are settlement lag and do not contradict the end date.
- **value_rule (cash) and terms ($3.40 USD, no stock leg): upheld.** The 8-K gives "$3.40 per Share in cash ... without interest" and the Form 25 notice "$3.40 in cash". One class (common stock, par $0.001), no stock or election leg, so `cash` with no price security is right. The filings do not name the currency; USD is inferred from a US company on the NYSE (minor).
- **ticker_history (MWW continues to 2016-10-31): upheld.** Fails rows for MWW run to 2016-11-07, so the same CUSIP kept trading under MWW after 2009-06-08. The 8-K says the shares were "listed and traded on the NYSE under the trading symbol MWW" until the Effective Time. The 2009 clip is wrong.
