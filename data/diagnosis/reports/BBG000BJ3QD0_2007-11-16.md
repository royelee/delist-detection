## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (SC TO-T/A No. 6 and SC 14D9/A, Form 15 with one holder of record).
- Successor: filing (same; cash for all shares, none remains).
- Last trade date: worked out. No filing states it. The Form 25-NSE notice (filed 2007-11-06) has no suspension date in its text; the merger was expected "on or about November 6"; fails rows run to 2007-11-29 at about $76.98 (settlement of fails, not trading).
- Payout rule: filing (SC TO-T/A No. 6).
- Terms: filing ($77.00 per share, net in cash, without interest); currency USD is implied by the US offer.
- Not backed: the last trade date. An exchange print, a Nasdaq halt record or a press release stating the final session would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Offer for all shares at $77.00 net in cash, Offer to Purchase dated 2007-08-08 ([SEC SC TO-T 2007-08-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1183920/000095012307011022/y38052tsctovt.htm)).
- Offer expired 2007-10-31 with 94% tendered (including guaranteed delivery); remaining shares to be converted into $77 cash in a short-form merger expected on or about 2007-11-06 ([SEC SC TO-T/A 2007-11-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1183920/000095012307014674/y41555a6sctovtza.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-11-06 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1183920/000135445707000346/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); the library's delist date 2007-11-16 is that filing plus 10 days.
- Form 15 filed 2007-11-06 reports 1 holder of record ([SEC Form 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1183920/000095012307014926/y41555de15v12g.htm)).
- Press coverage says Siemens completed the acquisition in early November 2007 ([web](https://www.auntminnie.com/industry-news/article/15584686/siemens-completes-dade-behring-deal)).
- Fails rows of CUSIP 23342J206: 41 rows to 2007-11-29, last price $76.98.
