# WW (Weight Watchers / WW International), case BBG000DY6735_2013-12-31

## 1. Verdict

The 2013-12-31 "ending" is not an event: it is the last day of the caller's observations, turned into an exchange transfer by the continued-filings fallback (no Form 25 was found near it). The security kept trading for 11 more years. Its real ending is the 2025 chapter 11: Nasdaq delisted the old common stock (suspended at the open on 2025-05-16, so the last trade was 2025-05-15), and the plan cancelled it on 2025-06-24 in return for a pro rata share of 900,000 of 10,000,000 new shares. The library's row is wrong on kind, date and value.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2025-05-09, Item 3.01: Nasdaq delisting for the chapter 11 filing).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2025-06-25, Item 3.02: new shares go to First Lien claim holders and old equity; Old Common Stock cancelled).
- Last trade date: filing, but a planned date (8-K 2025-05-09 says Nasdaq "will suspend trading ... at the opening of business on May 16, 2025"); the Form 25-NSE (2025-07-03) states no trading day, so no exchange print confirms it.
- Payout rule: worked out from decision 11 (bankruptcy then OTC); the 8-K 2025-06-25 cover shows the old stock under WGHTQ on OTC Markets.
- Terms: worked out. The 900,000 / 10,000,000 split is in the 8-K; the per-share ratio divides by the 80,280,308 shares outstanding on 2025-04-29 (10-Q cover), not the count at the effective date. The OTC print itself was not looked up.
- To reach verified: an exchange or OTC print showing 2025-05-15 as the last Nasdaq trade, and the plan's or transfer agent's per-share distribution.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. The old shares were replaced by a small slice of equity in the reorganized company, which the spec treats as a new security, so no continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reasons `continued_filings_rule` plus `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed` and `issuer_from_todays_ticker_map`. The observations given to the run end at 2013-12-31 (the observation_map's last row for this security), so the finder looked for an ending near that date, found none, and the 10-K/Q fallback labelled it an exchange transfer. The real 2025 ending lies 11 years after the last sighting. Cause: `continued_filings_guess`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- The security is still alive after the observation window; the row should be dropped or replaced by the 2025 ending (dropped / bankruptcy, last trade 2025-05-15). If the caller's observations stop in 2013, the honest answer is "no ending observed", not an exchange transfer.
- The 2018 NYSE to Nasdaq Form 25 (2018-10-12) is a transfer of the same security and must not clip history.
- ticker_history needs WTW to 2018 and the WW rename date (not checked here); the 2012 "WW NASDAQ" row is unsupported.
- Open: first WGHTQ print date, last Nasdaq trading day, per-share new-share ratio at the effective date. No market prices are claimed here.
- Golden: not worth adding in this form; a case with observations through 2025 would be.
