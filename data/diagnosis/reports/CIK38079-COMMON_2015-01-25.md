# FST (Forest Oil) CIK38079-COMMON, delisting 2015-01-25

## 1. Verdict

Not a merger at par. Forest Oil combined with Sabine Oil & Gas LLC on 2014-12-16 (Forest the legal survivor, renamed Sabine Oil & Gas Corporation). The NYSE judged the result a "back door listing" that failed initial listing standards, suspended the stock, and it began trading on OTCQB as FSTO on 2014-12-17. Holders kept their shares and lost the exchange listing: a drop with value at the first OTC print. The library's merger / assumed-par row is wrong; its date is right.

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2014-12-22 item 3.01; Form 25-NSE EX-99.25).
- Successor: filing (same registrant, renamed; no successor registration).
- Last trade date: filing (the NYSE suspended trading on 2014-12-16, so the last trade was 2014-12-15; agrees with MIDAS).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2014-12-22: common stock began trading on OTCQB as FSTO on 2014-12-17).
- Terms: filing (no consideration was paid to holders; the rule needs only FSTO's first print).

## 5. What happened

- NYSE told the company on 2014-11-05 that its 30-day average close was under $1.00 (Rule 802.01C); the company learned on 2014-12-04 ([SEC 8-K 2014-12-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38079/000115752314004761/a50999218.htm)).
- The combination with Sabine Oil & Gas LLC closed 2014-12-16 ([SEC 8-K 2014-12-16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38079/000003807914000101/item201-8xkx1214.htm); its item 2.01 itself reports the Arkoma asset sale, which the library read as the acquisition).
- NYSE suspended trading and began delisting for failing initial listing standards (Rule 102.01) after the closing; from 2014-12-17 the stock traded on OTCQB as FSTO; the registrant was renamed Sabine Oil & Gas Corporation ([SEC 8-K 2014-12-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38079/000119312514450831/d841411d8k.htm)).
- NYSE's Form 25-NSE (filed 2015-01-15, removal 2015-01-26) cites the back-door listing, Rule 102.01 and the sub-$1 price, and a suspension decided 2014-12-16 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38079/000087666115000019/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 346091705 under FST to 2014-12-19, under FSTO from 2014-12-22 (same CUSIP; fails data).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost the listing, kept the claim: delisted for a deficiency, then OTC. Not a merger (no cash or stock paid to holders) and not a continuation (the exchange listing ended).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder matched the 25-NSE, but the end-of-era resolver read the Dec 16 8-K item 2.01 (an asset-sale report that also mentions the Sabine closing) plus the DEFM14A as a completed acquisition (reason "Completed acquisition ... the registrant kept filing after it"), ignoring the 3.01 suspension text and the 25-NSE rationale. Bucket merger, no terms, assumed par 0.0, flags last_trade_date_conflict;merger_at_par. Cause tag: `wrong_exit_kind`.
