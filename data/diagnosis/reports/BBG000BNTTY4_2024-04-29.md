## 5. What happened

- The merger closed on 2024-04-19; each share converted into $63.00 in cash, without interest
  ([SEC 8-K 2024-04-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/773141/000095014224001115/eh240470925_8k.htm)).
- The company asked the NYSE to suspend trading before the open on 2024-04-19 (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- The NYSE's 25-NSE says the merger with Clear Line, Inc. (Sekisui House affiliate) took effect 2024-04-19, each share was exchanged for USD 63.00 cash, and trading was suspended 2024-04-19
  ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/773141/000087666124000286/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12G filed 2024-04-30
  ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/773141/000095014224001229/eh240475874_1512g.htm)). Later company filings (8-Ks in May 2024, a Form 25 on 2024-06-03, a 15-12G on 2024-06-13) are listed in EDGAR's index; I did not read them, and they most likely concern its debt securities (unverified).
