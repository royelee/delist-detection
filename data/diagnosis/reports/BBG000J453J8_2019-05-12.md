# BBG000J453J8_2019-05-12 (CCO, Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings)

## 1. Verdict

A 1:1 holding-company reorganization, not an ending: on 2019-05-01 Old CCOH merged into its parent (Clear Channel Holdings, Inc., renamed Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc.) and each Class A share became one share of the new common stock, which kept the symbol CCO on the NYSE. The library's row (exchange, continuation false, no successor, ticker_history ending 2019-05-01) is wrong: it is a continuation and CCO still trades.

## 5. What happened

- iHeartMedia's plan became effective 2019-05-01; the Outdoor Group separated from iHeart and Old CCOH merged into Clear Channel Holdings, Inc., which was renamed Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. Each Old CCOH Class A share converted into one share of common stock ([SEC 8-K12B 2019-05-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1334978/000119312519135029/d735979d8k12b.htm)).
- The new common stock began trading on the NYSE on 2019-05-02 under CCO, the same symbol (same 8-K12B).
- The NYSE's Form 25-NSE says each old Class A share converted into one new share, the filing is only for the old shares and does not affect the new shares' listing; the old security was suspended on May 2, 2019 ([SEC 25-NSE 2019-05-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1334978/000087666119000443/ruleprovisionnotice.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 18451C109 under CCO to 2019-04-12, then CUSIP 18453H106 under CCO from 2019-05-03 (fails, offline).
- The cash iHeart-side distributions were to iHeartCommunications, not to CCOH holders for their shares.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced 1:1 by a new line of the same holders (reorganization). Continuation, successor named.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder matched the Form 25-NSE (filed 2019-05-02, effective that day) and the end-of-era resolver saw the 8-K12B, giving crsp 304 exchange_transfer with `successor_unknown` (flags `resolved_by_current_ticker_map`, `bankruptcy_before_merger`; reasons `resolved_from_continued_filings`, `issuer_from_todays_ticker_map`). The successor line (new CUSIP, same ticker, same issuer CIK) was not linked back to this security, so the history was clipped at 2019-05-01. Cause tag: `holdco_not_linked`.

## 9. Verification

Result: upheld on both fields (value_rule, ticker_history). Checked by re-reading the cited filings through sec.py and the fails data.

- **value_rule (library `transfer`, report `continuation`): upheld.**
  - The 8-K12B (0001193125-19-135029) says that in the Merger Old CCOH Class A Common Stock "converted into an equal number of shares of common stock" of the Company, and that pre-Merger Class A holders "owned the same percentage" afterwards. The text shows no cash in the exchange.
  - The only cash in the filing is iHeart-side: iHeart Operations preferred stock sold for cash and the proceeds distributed to iHeartCommunications. That is not consideration for CCOH Class A shares, so reference.md's "no cash in the exchange" test is met.
  - The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-19-000443) says each old Class A share "was converted into one share of Common Stock" (new). It says the filing "does not affect the continued listing on the NYSE" of the new shares, and that the old security was suspended May 2, 2019.
  - Under decision 9 (1:1 holdco reorganization, same holders and claim) this is a continuation with a successor link, not an exchange transfer with a value rule of "transfer".
  - The bankruptcy was iHeart's. Old CCOH was not a debtor (8-K12B explanatory note), so the "post-bankruptcy equity is a new security" caveat does not apply.
- **ticker_history (library ends 2019-05-01, report says CCO continues): upheld.**
  - The 8-K12B says the new common stock "began trading on the NYSE at the opening of the market on May 2, 2019 under the symbol CCO", the same symbol, with the same CIK 1334978 under Rule 12g-3.
  - Fails rows (offline) show CUSIP 18453H106 under CCO from 2019-05-03 (first close $4.45). The old CUSIP 18451C109 last appears 2019-04-12.
  - Minor correction: the new CUSIP's CCO rows run to 2019-08-29, not 2019-07-31 as section 2 says. This does not change the conclusion.
  - The old line ends 2019-05-01, but the ticker did not end, so ticker_history should extend. Whether it stays under the same sec_id depends on the new CUSIP carrying composite FIGI BBG000J453J8. That is still unchecked (the open check in section 8), but a composite FIGI is per issuer and share class, so it is likely.
