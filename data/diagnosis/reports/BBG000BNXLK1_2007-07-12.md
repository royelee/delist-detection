# MEL (Mellon Financial) -> The Bank of New York Mellon, 2007

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger: the Mellon/Bank of New York merger became effective 2007-07-01 (a Sunday) and each
Mellon share became one share of The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation (BK). The library's row (merger, stock,
ratio 1.0, BK) is right; it is uncertain only because the security's later "seeds" (MEL observed from 2008-01-16)
fall outside its history, and because the last trade date is worked out rather than read.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (the NYSE Form 25-NSE notice: merger automatically effective 2:00 A.M. 2007-07-01).
- Successor: filing (same notice: shares converted into New BNY Mellon common, an acquirer-side line).
- Last trade date: worked out (effective Sunday 2007-07-01, so the last trading day was Friday 2007-06-29; the library's source is the EX-99.25 notice, which states the effective date rather than a last-trade day).
- Payout rule: filing (the notice's one-for-one conversion, no cash).
- Terms: filing for the 1.0 ratio; BK's first session 2007-07-02 comes from fails rows (BK, new CUSIP 064058100, first row 2007-07-03 priced at the 2007-07-02 close).
Not backed: the last trade day is not stated in a filing. An exchange print or a press release naming the last day would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- The merger became effective at 2:00 A.M. on 2007-07-01; each Mellon share was converted into one share of (New) The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation ([SEC 25-NSE 2007-07-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64782/000087666107000586/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2007-07-12 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64782/000119312507154049/d1512b.htm)).
- Fails rows: Mellon CUSIP 58551A108 last row 2007-06-27 (close $43.11); new BK CUSIP 064058100 rows from 2007-07-03, first price $44.25 (fails rows).
- The old Bank of New York CUSIP 064057102 ended 2007-06-25 (fails rows), so BK's old and new lines differ.
