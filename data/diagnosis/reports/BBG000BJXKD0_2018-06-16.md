## 4. Why this confidence

Verified. It was inferred until 2026-10-03, when the acquirer's own 8-K12B was read.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2018-06-04 items 3.01/5.01: each Westar share converted into the right to receive one Evergy share; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Evergy is the new parent, Westar the surviving subsidiary).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: suspended from trading June 5, 2018, so the last trade was June 4).

- Terms: filing. The ratio is in Westar's 8-K. The price security BBG00H433CR2 is a look-up in the library's tables; its EVRG history starts 2018-06-05, the same day.

## 5. What happened

- The run already holds Evergy as BBG00H433CR2, EVRG from 2018-06-05 (output/ticker_history.csv).
- Evergy's 8-K12B: the stock "will begin trading under the symbol 'EVRG' on June 5, 2018" ([SEC 8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1711269/000119312518182964/d598560d8k12b.htm)).
- Fails rows for Evergy (CUSIP 30034W106) begin 2018-06-05 with no price. The row dated 2018-06-06 carries the 2018-06-05 close, $54.25.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old shares became another company's shares, in a combination with a second company. A merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Exit kind and date are right, but the row came through the end-of-era resolver (`resolved_from_continued_filings`, 8-K 5.01) with no acquirer ticker: `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`, so the price security is "?", the payout gate failed (`payout_gate_failed:25`), and the value fell back to assumed par (`merger_at_par`). Cause tag: `terms_not_extracted`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

Steps 2 and 3 of the CAL fix (BBG000BDXVW8_2010-10-14) apply. Here the LLM read no acquirer ticker (`no_acq_ticker`), so the lookup must start from the acquirer's name.
- **Find the acquirer.** Evergy, Inc. is CIK 1711269, the 8-K12B's filer and the successor registrant. Take its common line on 2018-06-05 from the security master: BBG00H433CR2.
- **Publish and price by the security.**
  - Publish price_sec_id BBG00H433CR2 and price_ticker EVRG. Its ticker_history value on 2018-06-05 is right here.
  - The gate prices CUSIP 30034W106's 2018-06-05 close, $54.25, from the row dated 2018-06-06. Skip the priceless first row.
  - Then 1.0 × $54.25 against WR's last close of $54.00 is a 0.5% gap, so the gate passes and `assumed_par_after_failed_gate` goes away.
  - `resolved_from_continued_filings` still keeps the row in uncertain.csv.
- **Payout gate.** The regex's $25 cash read (`payout_gate_failed:25`) is a separate misread. It does not matter once the stock leg passes.

Golden case: yes, a merger of equals via a new holdco at 1:1 with the acquirer in the run.
