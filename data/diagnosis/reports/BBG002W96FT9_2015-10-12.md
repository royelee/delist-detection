# GOOG (Google Inc. Class C), BBG002W96FT9, 2015-10-12

## 1. Verdict

A 1:1 holding-company reorganization: Google Inc. became a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. and each Google Class C share converted automatically into one Alphabet Class C share, trading as GOOG again from 2015-10-05. The library's contract row (exchange, continuation, successor BBG009S3NB30, last trade 2015-10-02) is right; it is uncertain only because its evidence is thin (a `no_evidence_default` rule plus a ticker overlap).

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (Google 8-K 2015-10-02, items 1.01/3.01/3.03: holding-company reorganization).
- Successor: filing (Alphabet 8-K12B 2015-10-02: successor issuer under Rule 12g-3).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K item 3.01: Nasdaq suspends trading after the close on 2015-10-02; Form 25-NSE effective 2015-10-02).
- Payout rule: filing (each share converted into an equivalent corresponding Alphabet share).
- Terms: filing (none; no cash leg).

## 5. What happened

- Google implemented the Alphabet Merger on 2015-10-02; each share of each class converted automatically into an equivalent corresponding Alphabet share ([SEC 8-K 2015-10-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000119312515336550/d56649d8k.htm)).
- Nasdaq was to suspend Google shares after the close on 2015-10-02; Alphabet GOOGL and GOOG were to start trading on 2015-10-05 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- Alphabet filed an 8-K12B establishing itself as successor issuer ([SEC 8-K12B 2015-10-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000119312515336577/d82837d8k12b.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE for Class A and Class C, effective 2015-10-02 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000135445715000182/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Google also filed Form 15 and 15-12G on 2015-10-02 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000119312515336646/d12352d1512g.htm)).
