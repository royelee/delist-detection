## 9. Verification

Upheld. Re-read through sec.py: 8-K 0001193125-11-314493 states the Restated Certificate "became effective at 5 pm Eastern Standard Time (EST) on November 16, 2011", reclassifying Class A and B share for share into one Common class. In November the US is on EST, so 5 pm is after the 4 pm close; the last Class A session is 2011-11-16. The 25-NSE 0001354457-11-000248 was filed and effective 2011-11-16 for SunPower (CIK 867773), consistent with that date and giving no other. The truth row (data/diagnosis_truth.csv, 2011-11-16, inferred) matches. The verdict is "truth", so no missed filing is required. The library's blank internal_last_trade_date is therefore wrong.
