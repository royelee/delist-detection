# CIK1308161-COMMON_2013-07-01 (NWS-A, News Corp)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending. On 2013-06-28 old News Corporation (CIK 1308161) separated its publishing business into a new News Corporation and renamed itself Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc. The old Class A holders kept their shares (now FOXA). The library's merger row (assumed par, stock 0.25 x NWSA) is wrong; there should be no ending, and the ticker history should continue under FOXA.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason: "Completed acquisition (8-K item 2.01 filed 2013-07-03 ...); the registrant kept filing". The end-of-era resolver read the Separation's 8-K 2.01 as a merger; there was no Form 25. The LLM stock term (0.25 x NWSA) failed the sanity gate and the row fell to assumed par. Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- A 2.01 describing a Separation or spin-off is not a merger; follow the rename and drop the ending.
- Check the CUSIP change and the spin-off ratio in the 8-K or Form 10, and the first FOXA day.
- Golden case: yes, a spin-off plus rename misread as a merger.

## 9. Verification

Re-checked with the skill's sec.py helper: the issuer's filing list 2013-06-01..2013-12-31, the 8-K of 2013-07-03 (0001193125-13-281456, full text) and the fails rows of both CUSIPs.

Overall: the verdict (not a merger; rename plus spin-off, no ending) is upheld. Four fields hold, two do not.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K names the registrant "Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc., formerly known as News Corporation" and Item 5.03 records the by-law amendment "to reflect the Company's name change". Item 2.01 is a distribution, not an acquisition: one new News Corp Class A share for every four old Class A shares held on 2013-06-21, with cash only in lieu of fractions. Holders kept their old shares. No Form 25, 25-NSE, 15, 8-K12B or 8-K12G3 appears for CIK 1308161 from 2013-06 to 2013-12 (the only S-4 is dated 2013-11-06). The reference's rename rule applies, and the library's merger label rests only on the 2.01 item code.
- **last_trade_date: refuted as stated.** The report says the library publishes "2013-07-01, unconfirmed". The source row's `last_trade_date` is blank (the 2013-07-01 is only the internal `delist_date`, with flags `last_trade_date_unconfirmed` and `last_trade_not_exchange_print:none`). A blank is already what the corrected answer says. The only error is that a row exists at all, which is exit_kind. Fails rows confirm trading went on: CUSIP 65248E104 as NWSA to 2013-07-01, then NWSAXXXX 2013-07-02..03 at $29.40/$29.21, then CUSIP 90130A101 as FOXA from 2013-07-03 at $29.21.
- **successor: refuted.** The library's `successor_sec_id` is blank and the report's own corrected value is blank (its table lists "none" against "none"). The status should read "agree". The only successor is a ticker (FOXA) for the same issuer, which is a rename and not a successor_sec_id.
- **value_rule: upheld.** The library publishes `stock` with a failed terms gate. Holders surrendered nothing and received nothing for their old shares, so there is no payout rule for an exit. With no ending, `transfer` (or no row) is right.
- **terms: upheld, with a correction to section 4.** The library's `stock_ratio` 0.25 on NWSA is real: it is the spin-off distribution ratio, one new News Corp share per four held (8-K Item 2.01), so section 4's "spin-off ratio not read" is inaccurate; it is in the filing. It is still not merger consideration, because holders received it as a dividend-like distribution and kept their shares. Publishing it as terms is wrong. The library's price ticker NWSA is the distributed (new) News Corp stock, not the acquirer of a target.
- **ticker_history: upheld, with a caveat.** The row clips NWS-A at 2013-07-01 although the security kept trading under FOXA. The report's open check stands: the CUSIP changed (65248E104 to 90130A101) and no filing read explains it. This is probably a name-change CUSIP reassignment at the same issuer, but it is not confirmed. If a FIGI for FOXA differs from the old line, the right answer could be a continuation with a successor link rather than one open-ended history. In either case the clip at 2013-07-01 as a merger exit is wrong.
