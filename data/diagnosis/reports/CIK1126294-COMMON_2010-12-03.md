# CIK1126294-COMMON_2010-12-03 (RRI, Reliant Energy / RRI Energy)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending. RRI Energy was the legal acquirer in its merger with Mirant; on 2010-12-03 it renamed itself GenOn Energy and on 2010-12-06 its ticker changed RRI to GEN, still on the NYSE. RRI holders kept their shares. The library's `merger` row, with the 2.835 GEN ratio, uses the terms Mirant's holders received, so it is wrong.

## 5. What happened

- RRI Energy completed the merger of Mirant into its subsidiary on 2010-12-03; each Mirant share became 2.835 shares of RRI/GenOn common stock ([SEC 8-K 2010-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1126294/000095012310111604/c09394e8vk.htm)).
- Name changed from RRI Energy, Inc. to GenOn Energy, Inc. on 2010-12-03; ticker changed RRI to GEN on 2010-12-06; common stock continues on the NYSE (same 8-K).
- Accounting treats Mirant as acquirer ("reverse merger"), legally RRI survived (same 8-K).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 74971X107 under RRI to 2010-12-03 (last $3.53); CUSIP 37244E107 under GEN from 2010-12-06 ("GENON ENERGY INC") (SEC fails, offline).
- No Form 25 for RRI in the window; the 8-K 2010-11-24 and 8-A12B/A relate to the rights agreement amendment.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: the registrant and its common stock survived; holders received nothing.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The end-of-era resolver saw an 8-K 2.01 completed acquisition plus 425 filings and read a merger (reason "Completed acquisition ... the registrant kept filing after it", `resolved_from_continued_filings`), then the LLM terms reader took the Mirant holders' 2.835 ratio. The CUSIP change at the rename split the sightings, so GEN's fails rows were not tied to the security. Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Drop this ending; continue the ticker history under GEN (2010-12-06 onward) on the same sec_id, with the CUSIP change 74971X107 to 37244E107.
- The 2.01 rule must check whether the registrant is the acquirer or the target (here the acquirer, with a name and ticker change).
- Open: a filing stating the new CUSIP.
- Golden case: yes, a reverse-merger rename where the 2.01 and 2.835 ratio belong to the other company's holders.

## 9. Verification

Result: upheld on all five fields. Re-read [SEC 8-K 2010-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1126294/000095012310111604/c09394e8vk.htm), the issuer's filing list for 2010-11-01 to 2011-03-01 and the fails rows for GEN.

- exit_kind (merger is wrong): upheld. Item 2.01 says RRI's subsidiary Merger Sub merged into Mirant, so Mirant became RRI's wholly owned subsidiary. RRI is the legal acquirer, not the target. Item 5.03 changes its name to GenOn Energy, and "our common stock continues to trade on the New York Stock Exchange" under GEN from 2010-12-06. The "reverse merger" wording is accounting only ("Mirant being treated as the acquirer"). No Form 25 or Form 15 appears in the filing list for CIK 1126294 through 2011-03-01 (the 2010-11-24 8-K items are 1.01, 3.03 and 9.01, with the 8-A12B/A for the rights agreement).
- successor: upheld. The registrant is the same filer, CIK 1126294, whose conformed name is now GenOn Energy, Inc. No other issuer takes over RRI holders' shares, so no successor security exists beyond the same sec_id under ticker GEN.
- last_trade_date: upheld. There is no ending. Blank is right, and the library's own value is also blank. The RRI symbol's last day is 2010-12-03 and fails rows show CUSIP 37244E107 under GEN from 2010-12-06 (32 rows to 2011-01-31, "GENON ENERGY INC").
- value_rule (stock): upheld. RRI holders were not exchanged into anything, so `continuation` fits and a stock rule does not.
- terms (2.835 x GEN): upheld. Item 2.01 states the 2.835 ratio applies to "each outstanding share of common stock of Mirant", not to RRI shares.

Not checked: the CUSIP change 74971X107 to 37244E107 (no filing text states it; fails rows only, as section 4 says). It does not affect any of the five fields. The report's `continuation` row (library false, evidence true) is outside the five fields asked for, and the same 8-K supports it.
