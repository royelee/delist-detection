## 5. What happened

- Merger with Dolphin Sub, a subsidiary of AES, completed 2011-11-28; each share converted into $30 cash ([SEC 8-K 2011-11-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/787250/000091412111000413/dp11282011-8k.htm)).
- Item 3.01: DPL asked the NYSE to file Form 25; DPL is the surviving corporation, wholly owned by AES (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2011-11-28, stating each share was converted into $30.00 cash ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/787250/000087666111000497/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2011-12-16 and again 2012-02-27 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/787250/000110465911069806/a11-31795_11512b.htm)), consistent with DPL's debt keeping it a filer.
