## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2014-07-03 items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01: each Forest share cancelled and converted into the merger consideration on 2014-07-01).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: holders receive cash and Actavis shares; no continuing Forest line).
