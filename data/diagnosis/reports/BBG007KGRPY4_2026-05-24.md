## 5. What happened

- Biogen's Purchaser Sub commenced a tender offer on 2026-04-14 for $41.00 cash plus one CVR per share
  ([SEC 8-K 2026-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1492422/000119312526222923/d23709d8k.htm)).
- The offer expired at one minute after 11:59 p.m. ET on 2026-05-13; 105,687,831 shares (about 82.4%) were tendered (same 8-K).
- The merger became effective 2026-05-14; untendered shares were converted into the same $41.00 + CVR (same 8-K).
- Item 3.01: the company asked Nasdaq to suspend trading effective 8:00 p.m. ET on 2026-05-13 (same 8-K).
- CVR: non-transferable, milestone payments (the first $2.00 on SYFOVRE annual net sales of at least $1.5 billion in 2027-2030), up to $4.00 aggregate; Biogen's maximum CVR outlay is about $582 million (same 8-K).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2026-05-14, effective that day; the library's delist_date 2026-05-24 is filing + 10 days
  ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1492422/000135445726000449/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The second milestone's text was not read in full; only the aggregate of $4.00 is stated in the 8-K's introductory note.
