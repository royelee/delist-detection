## 4. Why this confidence
Verified.
- exit kind: filing, 8-K 0001267395-19-000013 (items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01) and the Form 25-NSE notice.
- successor: filing, same 8-K (acquirer is Highlands Holdings, Apollo Funds; no successor registrant).

## 5. What happened
- Merger agreement dated 2018-08-27 with Highlands Merger Sub, an Apollo Funds affiliate ([SEC 8-K 2019-02-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1267395/000126739519000013/form8-kxhighlandsclosingno.htm)).
- Completed before market open 2019-02-15; each ordinary share cancelled for $42.75 cash; the two preference share series stayed outstanding (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2019-02-15 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3): shares exchanged for USD 42.75 cash, suspended 2019-02-15, removal at open 2019-02-26 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1267395/000087666119000137/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Replaced by cash of another company: merger, not a continuation.

It did not: the row is correct. It is uncertain only through `resolved_from_continued_filings` (the registrant kept filing, because the preference shares stayed outstanding, so the resolver took the 5.01 branch). Cause: library_right_but_uncertain.
