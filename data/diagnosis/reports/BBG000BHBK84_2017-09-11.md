- successor: filing, same 8-K (acquirer is DowDuPont; no successor line).

## 5. What happened
- Diamond Merger: each Dow share converted into one DowDuPont share; Orion Merger: each DuPont share into 1.2820 DWDP; both became subsidiaries of DowDuPont. [SEC 8-K 2017-09-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29915/000119312517274845/d426977d8k.htm)
- Form 25-NSE (NYSE), effective 2017-09-01: merger effective 2017-08-31, one DowDuPont share per Dow share, suspended 2017-09-01. [SEC 25-NSE 2017-09-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29915/000087666117000492/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)
- Dow kept filing afterward as a subsidiary, which is why the library's continued-filings rule fired.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Replaced by stock of another company. Not a 1:1 holdco continuation: DowDuPont is the combined Dow and DuPont, so the holders' claim changed (a merger of equals). A reader who treats it as a holdco reorganization would call it a continuation; the filing frames it as mergers.
