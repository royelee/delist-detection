## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The merger and cash terms are right. The date: the library read the notice's "suspended from trading on July 24" as suspended before the open, so last trade = prior trading day (2026-07-23, `last_trade_date_source=ex99_notice`). The 8-K says the suspension followed the close on July 24, so the last trade was July 24. The row is uncertain because of `issuer_from_todays_ticker_map` (issuer 1562476 is correct) and flags `ftd_close_lagged`. Cause tag: `other:last_trade_off_by_one_notice_vs_8k`.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the two fields called wrong, against the filings through the skill's sec.py helper.

- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The 8-K of 2026-07-24 (accession 0001193125-26-316037), Item 3.01, says TMHC requested that NYSE "suspend trading of TMHC Common Stock on the NYSE following the closing of trading on July 24, 2026". The merger became effective July 24 (Item 2.01, and the Form 25-NSE, accession 0000876661-26-000640). The Form 25 notice says only "suspended from trading on July 24, 2026"; it does not say before the open, so the "before the open on D" rule in reference.md does not apply, and the 8-K's explicit "following the closing" reading is the specific one. July 24, 2026 is a Friday and a trading day. The corrected date is not after the Form 25 effectiveness date (header EFFECTIVENESS DATE 20260724), so decision 12's cap holds. Residual doubt: no exchange print (MIDAS or fails rows) was checked, so the date rests on filing text only, as the report's open check says.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** It follows from the last trade date: the library's range ends 2026-07-23, and the last trading day is 2026-07-24.
- Unchecked fields the report marks as agreeing (kind, terms, issuer) were not disputed. The $72.50 cash, the merger with the WXYZ Merger Sub of Berkshire Hathaway, and the USD currency all match the quoted filing text.
