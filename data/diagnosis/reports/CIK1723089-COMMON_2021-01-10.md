# CIK1723089-COMMON_2021-01-10 (APY, Apergy Corp)

## 1. Verdict

Not an ending of the security. Apergy renamed itself ChampionX (ticker APY became CHX on 2020-06-04, same issuer) after the Ecolab/Nalco Champion combination, then moved its listing from the NYSE to Nasdaq (last NYSE day 2020-12-31, Nasdaq from 2021-01-04). The same common stock kept trading, so the library's exchange-transfer row with no successor and a 2021-01-10 end is wrong as a delisting; the ticker history should run on under CHX.

## 9. Verification

Re-checked against the cached SEC filings (sec.py `filings`, `grep`, `fails`) with the aim of refuting each field the report calls wrong.

- **exit_kind: upheld, with a wording caveat.** The 8-K of 2020-12-21 (0001723089-20-000169, item 3.01) says the company will withdraw the principal listing of its Common Stock from NYSE and transfer it to Nasdaq, with the NYSE delisting effective at the close on 2020-12-31 and Nasdaq trading from 2021-01-04. The Form 25 (0001723089-20-000172) is filed by the issuer under 12d2-2(c) for "Common Stock, par value $0.01" (the same class). The 8-A12B of the same day (0001723089-20-000173) registers the same common stock on The Nasdaq Stock Market. This matches reference.md's row "Kept: same CUSIP, other exchange": not an ending, or `exchange` with successor = itself. The library's `exchange` label is therefore not wrong in itself; what is wrong is that the row is published as a standalone ending with `continuation=false` and no successor, a 2021-01-10 end and a blank last trade date. The report's "wrong" is correct for the row as a whole, not for the word "exchange".
- **successor: upheld.** The same registrant (CIK 1723089) files the 8-K, Form 25 and 8-A12B. Fails rows show 15872M104 under CHX, 26 rows from 2020-12-02 to 2021-03-29, so the stock trades through the NYSE to Nasdaq switch. A successor equal to the security itself (or no row) is the right answer; the library's "none" with `continuation=false` is wrong.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library's APY range running to 2021-01-10 omits the CHX ticker. The 8-K of 2020-06-04 cited in section 5 documents the APY to CHX change. The security is still listed, so no clip applies.
- **Correction to section 5 (not a field change).** Section 5 says the old CUSIP 03755L104 shows no rows after 2020-11. In fact its fails rows run 2020-05-01 to 2020-06-05 (APY, 21 rows) and stop at the rename. The CUSIP changed when APY became CHX, in June 2020, not near the exchange move. This resolves the open check in section 8: the new CUSIP is a reassignment at the rename, the same issuer and class, and not a new security. The verdict is unaffected.
- **Not re-verified.** The 2020-06-03/04 rename 8-K itself was not re-opened, but the CUSIP switch in June 2020 and the CHX fails rows are consistent with it.

Result: the verdict stands. The report is upheld on exit_kind (caveat above), successor and ticker_history.
