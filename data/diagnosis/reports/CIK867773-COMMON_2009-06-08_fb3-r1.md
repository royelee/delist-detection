- 25-NSE 0001354457-24-000708 (filer Nasdaq, subject SunPower, 17 CFR 240.12d2-2(b), Common stock): "removal ... effective at the opening of the trading session on September 30, 2024 ... securities were suspended on August 16, 2024 ... final on August 16, 2024". This is a real ending of the common stock in 2024, after the 2011 continuation, so exit_kind and the dates are not the 2011 event.
- 8-K 0000867773-24-000086: Item 1.03, voluntary Chapter 11 petitions filed 2024-08-05. The 8-K 3.01 (0000867773-24-000093) gives the Nasdaq delisting after those filings. drop_reason bankruptcy holds.
- Last trade: suspension on 2024-08-16 gives 2024-08-15. Fails rows for CUSIP 867652406: SPWR 20240801..20240816, SPWRQ from 20240819. The contract row (dropped, bankruptcy, 2024-08-15, otc_print, SPWRQ, price date 2024-08-16) matches. The library's `last_trade_date_conflict` flag is a flag and not a contradiction.
- Same security: output/cusip_history.csv holds both CUSIPs (867652109 to 2011-11-16, 867652406 after) under BBG000FVQ185, so the 2011 event is a rename and the truth row is stale.
