# CIK1115836-COMMON_2014-07-01 (OEH, Orient-Express Hotels Ltd.)

## 1. Verdict

Not a delisting. Orient-Express Hotels renamed itself Belmond Ltd. on 2014-06-30 and its class A shares kept
trading on the NYSE, ticker OEH until 2014-07-28 and BEL from then on. The library's 2014-07-01 ending
(a continued-filings guess) is wrong; no ending row should exist.

## 5. What happened

- Shareholders approved the name change Orient-Express Hotels Ltd. to Belmond Ltd. on 2014-06-30
  ([SEC 8-K 2014-07-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115836/000095010314004621/dp47644_8k.htm)).
- On 2014-07-28 the class A common shares listed on the NYSE changed ticker from OEH to BEL
  ([SEC 10-Q 2014-08-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115836/000111583614000034/oeh-20140630x10q.htm)).
- The issuer filed no Form 25, Form 15 or 8-K12B between 2014-04 and 2014-09 (SEC filing list of CIK 1115836);
  it kept filing 10-Qs.
- Fails rows (SEC FTD): the old CUSIP G67743107 shows OEH to 2014-07-01 and one OEHXXXX row 2014-07-02; CUSIP
  G1154H107 (BELMOND LTD) shows OEHZZZZ 2014-07-01, OEH 2014-07-02..07-28, BEL from 2014-07-29 (54 rows to
  2014-12-31). This looks like a CUSIP reissue on the rename, the only reason the library saw the line end.
  Rests on fails rows; the filings do not state the CUSIP change.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same company, same class A shares, same holders; only the name, ticker (and per the fails
rows the CUSIP) changed.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The CUSIP G67743107 stopped appearing in fails rows on 2014-07-01, so with no Form 25 the finder used the
`continued_filings_rule` (304, `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`) and called it an exchange transfer; it
did not follow the rename and the new CUSIP. Cause tag: `rename_not_followed`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Follow the OEH to BEL rename (ticker and CUSIP switch G67743107 to G1154H107, name Belmond Ltd.), emit no
  ending, and extend `ticker_history` to BEL.
- Open check: confirm the CUSIP reissue date from the 10-K or a transfer-agent notice (fails rows only today).
- Golden case: yes, a rename with a CUSIP switch and no Form 25 (expect no ending).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the three fields the report calls wrong. All three upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K of 2014-07-02 (accession 0000950103-14-004621, Item 5.03) says shareholders approved
  the name change Orient-Express Hotels Ltd. to Belmond Ltd. on 2014-06-30, same registrant, CIK 1115836. The 10-Q of
  2014-08-01 (0001115836-14-000034) says: "On July 28, 2014, the Company changed the ticker symbol of its class A
  common shares listed on the New York Stock Exchange from OEH to BEL." The SEC filing list for 2014-04-01..2014-10-01
  shows no Form 25, Form 15 or 8-K12B, only 8-Ks, 10-Qs and SEC correspondence. A rename is not an ending
  (reference.md, "Rename / ticker change"), so the library's `exchange` / 304 row is wrong. Fails rows for CUSIP
  G1154H107 (BELMOND LTD) show OEH to 2014-07-28 and BEL from 2014-07-29 (prices $14.70 to $12.93), so the shares kept
  trading.
- **value_rule: upheld.** With no ending there is no payout rule; the library's `transfer` rule hangs on an ending
  that does not exist. The report's wording ("at most continuation/transfer") is loose but the conclusion stands.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library clips the history at 2014-07-01. The same security traded as OEH to
  2014-07-28 and as BEL after, so the history should run on under BEL (the 10-Q date for the ticker change; fails rows
  agree).
- **Caveat, not refuted.** The CUSIP reissue (G67743107 to G1154H107) rests on fails rows only; no filing states it.
  reference.md describes a rename as the same CUSIP, so a new CUSIP is unusual, but the filings still show one
  continuing registrant and listing, and the verdict does not depend on the CUSIP.
