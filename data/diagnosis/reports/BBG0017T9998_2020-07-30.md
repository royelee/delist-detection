# CZR (old Caesars Entertainment Corp, CIK 858339), case BBG0017T9998_2020-07-30

## 1. Verdict

A real cash-and-stock merger: Eldorado Resorts (ERI) acquired old Caesars on 2020-07-20 and was renamed Caesars Entertainment (ticker CZR from 2020-07-21). ## 5. What happened

- The merger closed 2020-07-20; each share was converted into approximately $12.41 cash or approximately 0.3085 New Caesars shares worth about $12.41 (10-day VWAP to 2020-07-16), subject to proration ([SEC 8-K 2020-07-21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/858339/000119312520196345/d39509d8k.htm)).
- Elections: 271,242,689 stock-election shares, 382,608,319 cash-election shares; stock electors got about 77.237062% stock and 22.762938% cash (same 8-K).
- Aggregate consideration about $8.46B: about $2.37B stock and $6.09B cash, at $38.00 on 2020-07-17 (same 8-K).
- The company asked NASDAQ to suspend trading and withdraw the listing (item 3.01); ERI's ticker changed to CZR on 2020-07-21 (same 8-K).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2020-07-20 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/858339/000135445720000344/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15-12B on 2020-07-30 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/858339/000119312520204319/d40130d1512b.htm)).

## 9. Verification

Checked against the 8-K of 2020-07-21 (0001193125-20-196345, d39509d8k.htm), the 25-NSE (0001354457-20-000344), the filing list for CIK 858339 and the fails-to-deliver rows for CZR.

- **terms: upheld (the library is wrong).** The 8-K states each share became "approximately $12.41 in cash (the Cash Election Consideration) or approximately 0.3085 shares of New Caesars Common Stock with a value equal to approximately $12.41", at the holder's election and subject to proration. The two legs are alternatives, so the library's 12.41 + 0.3085 x ERI double counts (about $24.8 against a $12.41 deal value). The 8-K's totals ($8.46B at $38.00 on 2020-07-17: $6.09B cash, $2.37B stock) support an aggregate near 8.93 cash + 0.0915 shares. That aggregate is a rough derivation that depends on an assumed share count of about 681.7M, and the 8-K does not state it; the election-leg argument does not depend on it. The first CZR fails row of the new line is dated 2020-07-22 at $38.40, which fits price_date 2020-07-21 for the new CZR. The report's remaining open checks (ERI's FIGI equals BBG0074Q3NK6; a 424B3 aggregate) were not resolved here.
- **ticker_history: upheld (the library is wrong), with a caveat on the exact end date.** The library ends the CZR range at 2020-07-30 (the delist_date). The 8-K says the merger closed at 11:01 a.m. on 2020-07-20, the company asked Nasdaq to suspend trading and withdraw the listing "on the Closing Date", and the 25-NSE was filed 2020-07-20. Old Caesars could not have traded on Nasdaq through 2020-07-30. The report's 2020-07-20 is not proven: the closing was mid-morning, so the old line may have traded part of that day, or the suspension may have started at the open (which would make 2020-07-17 the last day under the "suspended before the open on D" rule). No exchange print or notice naming the day was found, and fails rows under the old CUSIP (127686103) stop at 2020-07-07, so they do not help. The conclusion that 2020-07-30 is wrong stands. 2020-07-20 is a best estimate and 2020-07-17 is possible, so the last trade date needs a print.
- **Side check on last_trade_date (status "missing"): upheld that the library has none.** The corrected value 2020-07-20 carries the same uncertainty as above.
