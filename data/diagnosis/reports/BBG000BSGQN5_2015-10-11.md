A real stock merger: each Ryland share became 1.0191 shares of CalAtlantic Group (the renamed Standard Pacific, which was reverse split 1-for-5 at closing). ## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2015-10-01 items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01: merger completed, holders entitled only to merger consideration).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Ryland merged into Standard Pacific, which survived and was renamed CalAtlantic; no continuing Ryland line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE EX-99.25: merger effective before the opening on 2015-10-01, so the last trade was 2015-09-30).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: 1.0191 surviving-corporation shares per share, cash only for fractions).
- Terms: filing (8-K: ratio 1.0191, the surviving corporation is CalAtlantic, NYSE: CAA, after a 1-for-5 reverse split of Standard Pacific).

## 5. What happened

- Ryland merged into Standard Pacific on 2015-10-01; Standard Pacific survived, renamed CalAtlantic Group, and effected a reverse split of 1 share for each 5 ([SEC 8-K 2015-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85974/000119312515335238/d44597d8k.htm)).
- Each Ryland share became 1.0191 surviving-corporation shares, cash for fractions; the press release names the combined company CalAtlantic Group (NYSE: CAA) (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2015-10-01: the merger became effective before the opening on 2015-10-01 ([SEC 25-NSE 0000876661-15-000446](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85974/000087666115000446/)).
- Ryland filed Form 15-12B on 2015-10-14 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85974/000119312515343570/d58049d1512b.htm)).
- Fails rows under SPF (CUSIP 85375C101) run 2015-09-01..2015-10-01 at about 8.45 to 8.00, a pre-split price (fails rows, offline `sec.py fails`).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: Ryland shares were exchanged for another company's shares at 1.0191, not one for one, so this is a merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The library's merger, date and ratio are right (`reason` "M&A 2.01+3.01+5.01", `last_trade_date_source` midas). It took the acquirer ticker SPF, whose fails prices are before the 1-for-5 reverse split and the rename to CAA, so `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity` fired (1.0191 x 8.00 against 40.83) and the dlret became assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 9. Verification

Checked by re-reading the 8-K of 2015-10-01 (accession 0001193125-15-335238, via `sec.py grep`) and the fails rows (`sec.py fails`, offline).

- **terms: upheld.**
  - The 8-K says Standard Pacific changed its name to CalAtlantic Group, Inc. and "effected a reverse stock split such that each five shares of Standard Pacific common stock ... were combined and converted into one" share of the surviving corporation. It says each Ryland share became "1.0191 shares of Surviving Corporation common stock, with cash paid in lieu of fractional shares", and the press release names the company CalAtlantic (NYSE: CAA). So the 1.0191 ratio is in post-split CAA shares, and the stock rule (no cash leg) is right.
  - Fails rows: SPF (CUSIP 85375C101) ran 2015-09-01 to 2015-10-01, $8.45 first, $8.00 last. CAA (CUSIP 128195104) ran 2015-10-01 to 2015-11-30, $40.00 first. $8.00 x 5 = $40.00, so SPF prices are pre-split. 1.0191 x 8.00 = 8.15 against the library's last_close of 40.83, which is why `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity` fired.
  - The report's correction (price_ticker CAA, price_date 2015-10-01) is supported. The 1.0191 ratio is not wrong; the wrong term is the price ticker.
- Fields marked agree (exit_kind, last_trade_date, successor, value_rule) were not contested. Nothing found that refutes them.
