## 4. Why this confidence

- Exit kind: filing (6-K 2016-02-23 reports completion of the acquisition by ABS Partners C.V., a subsidiary of Activision Blizzard).
- Successor: filing (same 6-K; holders are paid cash, no continuing line).

- Terms: filing ($18.00 per ordinary share, USD, no stock).

## 5. What happened

- The acquisition by ABS Partners C.V. (wholly owned by Activision Blizzard) closed on 2016-02-23 through an Irish scheme of arrangement; holders of record are entitled to $18.00 per ordinary share ([SEC 6-K 2016-02-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1580732/000156459016013040/king-6k_20160223.htm)).
- NYSE filed a Form 25-NSE on 2016-02-23 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3): the merger became effective before the opening on 2016-02-23 and each ordinary share was converted into $18.00 cash; removal at the opening of 2016-03-07 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1580732/000087666116000828/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2016-03-04 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1580732/000119312516492854/d153124d1512b.htm)); this is the date the library uses as `delist_date`.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old shares were exchanged for cash by another company, a merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The library found the 25-NSE (a foreign private issuer, filing 6-Ks rather than 8-Ks), so no 8-K item codes were available; the classifier fell to `no_evidence_default` and never read the Form 25 notice text, which states the merger and the cash. The foreign-issuer deal terms (6-K) were not used for payout extraction. Cause tag: `wrong_exit_kind`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Read the Form 25 EX-99.25 text ("merger ... converted in $X in cash") as merger evidence and extract cash terms from it and from 6-K items for foreign private issuers; publish `cash_currency=USD`.
- The contract's `delist_date` 2016-03-04 is the Form 15 date; the 25-NSE was filed and effective on 2016-02-23. Consider which date the library should publish.
- No other check needed.
- Golden case: yes, a Form 25 under 12d2-2(a)(3) from a 6-K filer with a cash scheme.

## 9. Verification

Re-read the cited filings through the skill's sec.py helper.

- **exit_kind (library blank, evidence merger): upheld.** The 25-NSE (0000876661-16-000828; filed and effective 2016-02-23, NYSE, class "Ordinary Shares", Rule 12d2-2(a)(3)) says the whole class came to evidence other securities or a cash payment, and its EX-99.25 states "The merger between King Digital Entertainment plc and ABS Partners C.V., a wholly-owned subsidiary of Activision Blizzard, Inc. became effective before the opening on February 23, 2016." The 6-K (0001564590-16-013040) confirms completion of the acquisition by scheme of arrangement and the $18.00 entitlement. The class is the ordinary share the library tracks, so this is a replaced-by-cash merger, not a continuation; `unknown` is unsupported.
- **value_rule (library unknown, evidence cash): upheld.** EX-99.25: "Each Ordinary Share of King Digital Entertainment plc was converted in $18.00 in cash, without interest and less any applicable withholding taxes." No stock leg in either filing. Cash with $18.00 USD is the right rule and term.
- Not disputed: last trade 2016-02-22 follows from "effective before the opening on February 23, 2016". The delist_date open check (Form 25 2016-02-23 vs Form 15 2016-03-04) is a genuine open question and does not affect the two fields above.

Result: both fields upheld; no refutation found.
