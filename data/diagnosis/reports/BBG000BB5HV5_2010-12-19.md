## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred. The label fits the rule: four key fields rest on a filing, one rests on a date worked out rather than read.
- Exit kind: filing (the 8-K of 2010-12-09, items 2.04, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01, reports the completed tender offer and short-form merger).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Tyco Electronics is the acquirer and ADC became its subsidiary, no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out (the 8-K says the merger was effected 2010-12-09 and the Form 25-NSE is effective 2010-12-09; neither states a last trading day, so 2010-12-09 is deduced, with 2010-12-08 possible).
- Payout rule: filing (same 8-K: untendered shares converted into the right to cash).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K: $12.75 cash per share, no stock leg).

Not backed: the last trade date, which no filing states and no exchange print (MIDAS covers 2012+, no halt print) confirms. It would be verified by a filing or notice that states the last trading day (a Nasdaq halt or suspension notice, or a press release naming the final session).

## 5. What happened

- 2010-07-12: ADC signed a merger agreement with Tyco Electronics Ltd.; the tender offer began 2010-07-26 at $12.75 cash per share. [SEC 8-K 2010-12-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/61478/000129993310004380/htm_40031.htm)
- 2010-12-08: Tyco completed the offer (86.8% tendered) and accepted shares for payment. Same 8-K.
- 2010-12-09: top-up option exercised and a short-form merger effected; untendered shares converted into the right to $12.75 cash. Same 8-K (items 2.04, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01).
- 2010-12-09: Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE, effective 2010-12-09. [SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/61478/000135445710000318/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml) The notice does not state a suspension date.
- Fails rows for CUSIP 000886309 under ADCT run to 2011-01-10, priced $12.69 to $12.76: settlement of fails after the deal, not trading (fails output).
