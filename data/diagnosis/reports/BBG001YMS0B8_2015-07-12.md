## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2015-07-02, items 2.01/3.01/5.01: merger completed, Kraft shares converted).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: shares converted into Kraft Heinz stock, no continuing Kraft line).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K press release: Kraft shares cease trading at the close on 2015-07-02; Form 25-NSE effective 2015-07-02).
- Payout rule: filing (same 8-K: one KHC share plus the $16.50 dividend).
- Terms: filing (8-K: 1 share and $16.50; press release: KHC begins trading Monday 2015-07-06).

## 5. What happened

- Each Kraft share was converted into one KHC share; in addition Kraft declared on 2015-06-22 a $16.50 special cash dividend payable to holders of record immediately prior to closing ([SEC 8-K 2015-07-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1545158/000119312515244355/d36612d8k.htm)).
- NASDAQ was asked to suspend Kraft trading as of the close on the Closing Date (2015-07-02); Kraft shares cease trading at that close and KHC begins trading Monday 2015-07-06 (same 8-K, Item 3.01 and press release).
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2015-07-02 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1545158/000135445715000117/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); Form 15-12B filed 2015-07-13 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1545158/000095015715000778/form15-12b.htm)).
- Shareholders approved on 2015-07-01 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1545158/000119312515242614/d36618d8k.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old share was exchanged for another company's share plus cash tied to the transaction (the dividend was conditioned on the merger, record date immediately before closing), so a merger, not a continuation (operator ruling: cash that is part of the exchange makes it terminal).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It did not, on kind, date or terms. The uncertainty comes from the failed gates: `payout_gate_failed:72.5` and `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`, leaving `dlret_fill` 0.0 (assumed par, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`), and `cash_currency` blank. Likely the gate could not reconcile a $16.50 cash dividend with a stock leg whose price is unavailable around 2015-07-06 (not checked). Cause tag: `library_right_but_uncertain`.
