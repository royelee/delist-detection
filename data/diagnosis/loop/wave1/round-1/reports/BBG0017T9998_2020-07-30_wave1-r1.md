## 9. Verification

Upheld. Re-read 8-K 0001193125-20-196345 through sec.py. The Introductory Note defines the Closing Date as July 20, 2020 (Effective Time 11:01 a.m.). Item 3.01 says: "On the Closing Date, in connection with the consummation of the Merger, the Company notified NASDAQ ... and requested that the trading of shares of Former Caesars Common Stock on NASDAQ be suspended and that the listing ... be withdrawn." No sentence states a last trading day or a timing word, so the report is right that the library's date is a `closing_day` guess.
- Caveat: the sentence is a request made on D, not literally "suspended on D", and the 11:01 a.m. Effective Time leaves 07-20 prints possible. The 07-17 date rests on operator ruling R8 (decisions file; the truth row for BBG0017T9998_2020-07-30 already carries it), not on an exchange print. The field verdict is "truth" and cites no missed filing, so none needs checking.
- Result: internal_last_trade_date truth 2020-07-17 survives; the library's 2020-07-20 stays wrong under R8.
