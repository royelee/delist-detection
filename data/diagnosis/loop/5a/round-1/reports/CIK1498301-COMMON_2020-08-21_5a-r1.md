- Form 25-NSE filed 2020-08-11, effective 2020-08-21 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498301/000135445720000408/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The earlier report (truth case CIK1498301-COMMON_2020-08-21) reached the same result; the 2020-11-24 8-K 2.01 is an asset sale in bankruptcy, not a merger of the shares.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. The holders keep the same shares but they left the exchange; that is an ending valued at the first OTC print.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Internal row: exchange_transfer, crsp 304, reason "Continued 10-K/Q filings >180d after delist (moved to OTC or spun off)", dlret_method exchange_transfer_zero, successor_sec_id = its own sec_id. The continued-filings fallback labelled an OTC drop an exchange transfer; the pipeline's self-successor rule then removed the row from the contract and left ticker_history unclipped (ASNAQ fails rows continue the range). The bankruptcy (8-K 1.03 / 3.01 citing Chapter 11) is never consulted. Cause: `resolver_no_bankruptcy_branch`.
