## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The library's "...ZZZZ" handling (ftd.py, line_follow.py) treats such a symbol as the first-day marker of a new line. Here it sits on the old CUSIP's residual rows after a reverse split, and the fails symbol HONZZZZ became its own ticker range. Cause: other:ZZZZ-suffixed fails symbol counted as a ticker sighting (should be dropped like XXXX).

## 8. Fix and open checks
A "...ZZZZ" fails symbol must not create a ticker_history/security_history range when the stripped ticker is the security's own; 2026-07-14..07-15 should stay HON. Candidate golden case (reverse split plus new CUSIP, no ending). No open fact checks.
