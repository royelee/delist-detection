## 6. Decision-tree bucket

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The LLM terms extractor (`terms_source=llm`) read the acquirer's ticker as SAN, the ordinary-share symbol, not the ADS the holder receives. Cause tag: `other:llm_acquirer_ticker_ordinary_not_ads`. The terms gate failed (`fail_sanity`), but the published `price_ticker` is still wrong.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- A stock-leg `price_ticker` must be the security the holder receives (the ADS, STD), not the home-market ordinary share.
- Open: the price itself is qlib_practice's. Golden case: already covered by the truth row.

## 9. Verification

Upheld. price_ticker: truth STD is right. Re-read from the cached filings: DEFM14A 0000950123-08-018071 says "Santander American Depositary Shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol STD" and the ordinary shares trade on the Spanish exchanges' Automated Quotation System. The 8-K 0000950123-09-001677 (item 2.01) says each share was exchanged into 0.3206 Santander ADSs. A holder could instead elect ordinary shares at the same ratio, but the default and the stock leg the library prices is the ADS, so STD is the right price_ticker. No missed filing was claimed, and none is needed.
