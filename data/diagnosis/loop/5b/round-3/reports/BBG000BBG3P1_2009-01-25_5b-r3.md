## 9. Verification
Upheld. Re-read the 8-K 0001193125-08-246652 (item 3.01) and the 25-NSE 0000876661-09-000070 EX-99.25 through sec.py. The 8-K says NYSER based the suspension (before the open of Friday 2008-12-05) on the common stock's average closing price under $1.00 over 30 consecutive trading days, not regained within six months, with no appeal and OTCBB/Pink Sheets expected. The EX-99.25 cites Listed Company Manual 802.01C (average closing price below $1.00) as the sole ground, for the common class, removal at the opening of 2009-01-26. Neither filing cites late filings; the NT 10-Q filings are not the stated ground. `drop_reason` = `price` (550) is right and the library's `filings_fees` (580) is wrong.
