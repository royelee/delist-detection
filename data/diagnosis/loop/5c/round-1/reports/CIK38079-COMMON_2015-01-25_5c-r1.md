## 5. What happened
- [SEC 8-K 2014-12-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38079/000119312514450831/d841411d8k.htm): NYSE began delisting proceedings for failing initial listing standards (Rule 102.01) after the Closing; "On December 17, 2014, the Company's common stock began trading over the counter on the OTCQB under the ticker symbol FSTO."
- [SEC Form 25-NSE 2015-01-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38079/000087666115000019/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml): NYSE removal.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Lost the listing, kept the claim: deficiency delisting, then OTC.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The library's contract row names the NYSE ticker FST as the price ticker of the otc_print rule; after the move the security's OTC symbol is FSTO. Cause: `terms_misread`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Truth need not change. No missed filing; the earlier report already cited this 8-K.
