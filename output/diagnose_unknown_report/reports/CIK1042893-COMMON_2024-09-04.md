Not an ending. Dril-Quip (CIK 1042893) acquired Innovex by issuing it new shares, renamed itself Innovex International and kept trading under INVX from 2024-09-09. ## 9. Verification

A skeptic re-opened the cited filings and fails rows and tried to refute each field the report calls wrong.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 424B3 ([SEC 424B3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1042893/000119312524194905/d773596d424b3.htm)) says "each Dril-Quip stockholder will hold the same number of shares of Dril-Quip common stock that such stockholder held immediately prior to completion of the mergers", and that Dril-Quip issues new shares to Innovex holders (52%/48% ownership). So DRQ holders gave up nothing and received nothing; this also rules out a reverse split. The 8-K of 2024-09-06 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1042893/000119312524215264/d802924d8k.htm)) shows the same registrant (file no. 001-13439, CIK 1042893) "formerly named Dril-Quip, Inc.", common stock on the NYSE as INVX from 2024-09-09. The library's merger row rests only on an item 5.01 read, which this evidence contradicts.
- **value_rule: upheld.** The library's `unknown` with an assumed-par fill is wrong because there is no exit and no payout. Wording slip in section 2: the table says "transfer / no exit" while section 3 says `continuation`; `continuation` is the right value.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library clips DRQ at 2024-09-06. Fails rows show CUSIP 262037104 under DRQ through 2024-09-04 (3 rows, last $15.63) and CUSIP 457651107 under INVX from 2024-09-09 to 2024-10-30 (22 rows, first $15.41). The price continuity fits a rename with no split, so the security should continue under INVX.
- **Residual uncertainty (does not change the verdict).** The CUSIP changed. No 8-A12B or MIDAS print for INVX was checked, and the cached 8-K text is cut off before items 5.03 and 3.03. The 424B3 and price continuity still support a rename, so `inferred` stands.
