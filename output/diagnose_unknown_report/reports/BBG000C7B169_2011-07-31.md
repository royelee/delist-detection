## 5. What happened

- Merger of Airplanes Merger Sub into WMG completed 2011-07-20; each share converted into $8.25 cash ([SEC 8-K 2011-07-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1319161/000119312511192305/d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2011-07-21 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3); shares came to evidence only the right to $8.25; suspended from trading July 20, 2011; removal at opening of business August 1, 2011 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1319161/000087666111000323/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Deal agreed May 2011 ([SEC 8-K 2011-05-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1319161/000119312511131590/d8k.htm)); proxy DEFM14A filed 2011-06-13.
