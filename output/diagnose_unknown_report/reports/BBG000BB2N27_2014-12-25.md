## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred (corrected from verified: the terms' price date is worked out, not read, and the rule counts that as inferred).
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2014-12-12, items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01: the arrangement and merger completed, each share converted).
- Successor: filing (the same 8-K: holders receive cash and RBI shares; RBI is the acquirer, no continuing THI line).
- Last trade date: filing (the Form 25-NSE: merger effective 2014-12-12, suspended from trading 2014-12-15, so the last trade is 2014-12-12).
- Payout rule: filing (the 8-K's default package, cash plus RBI shares).
- Terms: filing for C$65.50 and 0.8025 (8-K and Form 25-NSE); worked out for price_date 2014-12-15, the trading day after the last THI trade, since no filing read states QSR's first trading day (the fails rows for QSR only begin 2014-12-17).

Not backed: price_date 2014-12-15. Evidence that would make it verified: an RBI filing or press release stating QSR's first trading day.

## 5. What happened

- On 2014-12-12 the arrangement and merger completed; each THI common share was cancelled and converted into the right to receive the Arrangement Consideration ([SEC 8-K 2014-12-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1345111/000119312514441344/d837647d8k.htm), items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01).
- Default (mixed) consideration C$65.50 cash + 0.8025 new RBI common shares; cash election C$88.50, stock election 3.0879 shares ([same 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1345111/000119312514441344/d837647d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2014-12-15 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(3): shares came to evidence other securities on 2014-12-12, suspended from trading 2014-12-15, removal 2014-12-26; it restates the three options ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1345111/000087666114000652/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2014-12-29 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1345111/000119312514455606/d843513d1512b.htm)).
- Fails rows for QSR (CUSIP 76131D103) begin 2014-12-17 (offline fails data); QSR's first trading day 2014-12-15 is not stated in the filings I read, so price_date is the trading day after the last THI trade.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Terms match the filing except the currency: `cash_currency` is blank though the cash is Canadian dollars. Its check (`terms_gate=failed`, flag `llm_gate_failed`) most likely compared C$65.50 plus the stock leg with a USD close, so the value fell to assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause tag: `currency_missing`.

- Open: confirm QSR's first trading day (price_date) from an RBI filing or press release.
