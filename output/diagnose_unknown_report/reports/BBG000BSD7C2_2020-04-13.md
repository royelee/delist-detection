# RTN (Raytheon Co), case BBG000BSD7C2_2020-04-13

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger: each RTN share became 2.3348 shares of United Technologies (UTC), which renamed itself Raytheon Technologies (ticker RTX) at the effective time. The library's kind, date, rule and ratio are right; it names the price security UTX, a ticker that stopped existing the day the deal closed (RTX is the same CUSIP), and its fill of assumed par should be replaced by the priced package.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (RTN 8-K 2020-04-03 items 2.01/3.01; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (8-K: Raytheon survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of UTC; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K 3.01 and the Form 25 notice: suspended before the open on 2020-04-03, so the last trade was 2020-04-02).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2.01: converted into 2.3348 UTC shares).
- Terms: ratio is from the filing. The price ticker RTX is worked out: the 8-K says UTC changed its name to Raytheon Technologies ("RTX") at the effective time and UTC's later 8-K uses the rtx XBRL prefix; no filing read here states the exchange symbol on 2020-04-03.
To be verified: an exchange or UTC filing stating that RTX began trading on 2020-04-03.

## 5. What happened

- Merger Sub merged into Raytheon; Raytheon survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of UTC; UTC renamed itself Raytheon Technologies at the effective time ([SEC 8-K 2020-04-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1047122/000119312520097237/d827106d8k.htm)).
- Each Raytheon share was converted into 2.3348 UTC common shares (same 8-K, item 2.01).
- Raytheon asked the NYSE to suspend trading before the open on 2020-04-03 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- The NYSE Form 25-NSE (filed and effective 2020-04-03) cites the merger and the 2.3348 ratio; removal from the exchange at the open on 2020-04-14 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1047122/000087666120000291/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- UTC's 8-K of 2020-04-08 carries the rtx XBRL prefix ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101829/000114036120008397/nc10010681x2_8k.htm)).

## 9. Verification

Field checked: terms (price_ticker UTX should be RTX). Result: upheld, with two corrections to the supporting prose.

- Ratio and no cash: upheld. RTN 8-K 2020-04-03 item 2.01 (accession 0001193125-20-097237): each Raytheon share was converted into 2.3348 UTC common shares, plus cash only in lieu of fractions. The library's 2.3348 and blank cash are right.
- Price security: upheld. The same 8-K says UTC changed its name to Raytheon Technologies Corporation ("RTX") at the Effective Time (2020-04-03), so UTC's common is the consideration and UTX is its old name. UTC's 8-K of 2020-04-08 (0001140361-20-008397) cover page lists common stock ($1 par), trading symbol RTX, NYSE, CUSIP 75513E 101. Cached fails data show CUSIP 75513E101 under RTX from 2020-04-03, and UTX (913017109) rows ending 2020-04-08. The confidence stays "inferred": no filing read states the first-trade date of RTX, but nothing contradicts it.
- Correction 1: section 1 says RTX is "the same CUSIP" as UTX. It is not: the CUSIP changed from 913017109 (UTX) to 75513E101 (RTX). Only the FIGI (BBG000BW8S60) is the same line, so the acquirer lookup should follow the FIGI, not the CUSIP (section 8's "same CUSIP" check is mis-worded likewise).
- Correction 2: UTX did not vanish on 2020-04-03; fails rows under UTX continue to 2020-04-08 (settlement lag) and the library's ticker_history keeps UTX until 2020-04-08. Section 7's mechanism is therefore a guess, as it says ("most likely"). The numbers fit it: fails rows around 2020-04-03 carry about $86 (the pre-spin-off close), and 2.3348 x 86 = 200.8 against RTN's last close of 116.96, which would fail the sanity gate. The right price is the post-spin RTX close on 2020-04-03; a fails-based price for that day may still be the stale pre-spin one, so fixing the ticker alone may not make the gate pass.

Other fields ("agree") were not challenged. Verdict: terms wrong (price_ticker), upheld.
