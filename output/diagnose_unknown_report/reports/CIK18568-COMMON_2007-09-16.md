## 9. Verification

Checked by a second reader against the filings cited above, re-read through the skill's sec.py helper.

- **value_rule (library `cash`, report `cash_plus_stock`): upheld.** The DEFM14A (0000950144-07-006264) says each share is exchanged, at the holder's election, for either 2.8 BBVA ADSs or $71.82 cash, subject to proration. It also fixes the aggregate: BBVA issues 196,000,000 shares and pays about $4.6 billion cash. The BBVA 425 of 2007-09-07 (0000950103-07-002233) confirms completion with $4,612 million cash and 196,000,000 shares. A pure `cash` rule therefore misdescribes the deal: the holders as a group received both cash and stock. This matches reference.md's election guidance (give the default or aggregate package the agreement fixes).
- **terms (library cash 71.82, no currency, no stock leg; report 71.82 USD plus 2.8 ADS, aggregate about 47.8% cash / 52.2% stock): upheld.** The election terms (71.82 USD, 2.8 ADS) match the DEFM14A. The aggregate split reproduces from the 425: 4,612M / 71.82 = 64.2M cash shares and 196M / 2.8 = 70.0M ADS shares, so 47.85% / 52.15% (the report's 34.34 cash and 1.462 ratio use 0.478 / 0.522, a rounding difference of about 0.02 per share). The library's row lacks the stock leg and the currency, so it is wrong or incomplete on terms.
- Caveats, not refutations: the 47.8 / 52.2 split is an inference from totals (the report already marks confidence `inferred`). Real proration per share was not found. The last trade date and the ADS price date are unconfirmed (the 8-K12B grep returned nothing under the accession prefix I tried, so I relied on the DEFM14A and 425 and did not re-open the 25-NSE). Neither is a field the report calls wrong.
