## 5. What happened

- Merger with Hercules Offshore effective 2007-07-11; each TODCO share converted into 0.979 HERO share and $16.00 cash; holders could elect all cash or all shares ([SEC 25-NSE 2007-07-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1210697/000087666107000606/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Consideration fixed at about $924.4M cash and 56.6M HERO shares, equal to 0.979 shares plus $16.00 per TODCO share; elections prorated ([SEC DEFM14A 2007-06-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1210697/000119312507127913/ddefm14a.htm)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2007-07-11 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1210697/000119312507153409/d1512b.htm)).
