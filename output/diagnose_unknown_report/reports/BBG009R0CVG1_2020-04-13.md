## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2019-12-27 item 3.01, Form 25-NSE 2020-01-14, 10-K FY2019 "principal market is OTCQX").
- Successor: filing (none appears; the 10-K says the same stock trades on OTCQX).
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K says trading "has been suspended, effective immediately" on 2019-12-27, so I take the trading day before. The filing does not state the last-print day. An exchange print (MIDAS or fails rows) would confirm it.

- Not backed: the exact last NYSE session (2019-12-26 vs 2019-12-27) and the drop reason code (guidelines vs capital, the filing states only a market-cap standard).

- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2020-01-14 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000087666120000026/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The 10-K for 2019 gives OTCQX as the principal market and says the delisting was effective in January 2020 ([SEC 10-K 2020-03-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000156459020007989/lksd-10k_20191231.htm)).
- Fails rows for CUSIP 50218P107 show LKSD from 2019-12-03 to 2020-04-09, with the price falling to $0.06 ([sec.py fails]).
- Chapter 11 filed 2020-04-13; the press release says "OTCQX: LKSD" ([SEC 8-K 2020-04-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000119312520104747/d894205d8k.htm)).
- After 2020-04-13 the stock traded as LKSDQ (fails rows 2020-04-17 to 2021-03-15). The plan of liquidation cancelled all equity on 2021-03-10 with no distribution; Form 15-12B followed ([SEC 8-K 2021-03-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000119312521075200/d149017d8k.htm), [SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000119312521076348/d138454d1512b.htm)).

## 9. Verification

A skeptic re-opened the cited filings (sec.py: `filings`, `grep`, `text`, `fails`) and tried to refute the three fields the report calls wrong.

- **Verdict (the 2020-04-13 bankruptcy is the wrong event).** Upheld. The 8-K item 3.01 filed 2019-12-27 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000119312519324977/d527006d8k.htm)) says the NYSE decided to delist the common stock under Rule 802.01B (average market cap under $15 million over 30 trading days), that "trading in the Common Stock on the NYSE has been suspended, effective immediately", that the company will not appeal, and that it expects OTCQX trading as "LKSD". The NYSE's Form 25-NSE for "Common Stock" is dated 2020-01-14 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000087666120000026/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)), the same class as the observed security. The FY2019 10-K ([SEC 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669812/000156459020007989/lksd-10k_20191231.htm)) gives OTCQX as the principal market and says the delisting was effective in January 2020. The filings list shows no other Form 25 and no 8-A12B in the window. Fails rows for CUSIP 50218P107 show the same CUSIP under LKSD to 2020-04-09 and under LKSDQ from 2020-04-17, so the bankruptcy came after the exchange listing had already ended.
- **last_trade_date: upheld, with a caveat.** The library's guess (2020-04-13, unconfirmed, published blank) is wrong: the NYSE listing ended in December 2019. The report's 2019-12-26 is worked out from "effective immediately" on a filing dated 2019-12-27; it could be 2019-12-27 if the suspension took effect during that session. No filing states the last print, and the fails rows (dated by settlement, 2019-12-03 onward, priced at the prior close) do not resolve the day. Treat 2019-12-26 as the best inference, within one trading day. Under decision 12 the contract may publish it only from an exchange print, so the blank in the contract may itself be correct today; the error is the internal guess and the ending it anchors.
- **value_rule: refuted as "wrong".** The library's rule is `otc_print`, price_ticker LKSD, the same rule the evidence gives (decision 11: a move to OTC is valued at the first OTC print, with the security's own OTC symbol). The rule agrees. Only the term `price_date` is blank in the library (2019-12-27 in the evidence), which the report already lists under `terms` as missing; it is not a wrong rule. The corrected rule is unchanged, and the report's table row should read "agree (price_date missing)".
- **ticker_history: upheld, conditional on the corrected ending.** Today's range LKSD 2016-10-05 to 2020-04-09 runs through the OTC period (fails rows to 2020-04-09) and is clipped by the wrong bankruptcy ending. Since `ticker_history` records exchange listings and a compliance-failure delisting always clips (CLAUDE.md), the NYSE range should end at the last NYSE trade, about 2019-12-26. How to show the OTCQX period stays an open design choice, as the report notes in section 8.
- **Not checked.** The NYSE session of the last print (no MIDAS or EX-99.25 notice available), and the drop-reason code (`guidelines` 570 against `capital` 560); the filing cites only a market-cap standard, and the report says so in section 4.
