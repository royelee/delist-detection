# MIR (Mirant Corp), case BBG000DQV8M1_2010-12-17

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger: on 2010-12-03 each Mirant share became 2.835 shares of RRI Energy, which renamed itself GenOn Energy that day and traded as GEN from 2010-12-06. The library's kind, date, ratio and issuer are right; its price security is wrong (RRI stopped trading on 2010-12-03, so the leg cannot be priced, the gate fails and the row falls back to assumed par).

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- exit kind: filing (8-K 2010-12-07 items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01; Form 25-NSE).
- successor: filing (8-K: RRI Energy is the acquirer, "renamed GenOn Energy, Inc. on the closing date").
- last trade date: filing (Form 25 EX-99.25: securities converted on 2010-12-03; removal at open 2010-12-17).
- payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01: converted into 2.835 RRI Energy shares).
- terms: ratio is from the filing; the ticker GEN and its first session 2010-12-06 rest on fails rows (CUSIP 37244E107 under GEN from 20101206; RRI CUSIP 74971X107 under RRI only through 20101203), not on a filing.
Not backed: the symbol GEN by a filing. A GenOn 8-K or 8-A naming "NYSE: GEN" would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- [SEC 8-K 2010-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1010775/000119312510275719/d8k.htm): on 2010-12-03 Merger Sub merged into Mirant; each share was converted into 2.835 shares of RRI Energy common stock; RRI Energy renamed GenOn Energy on the closing date; NYSE notified under item 3.01.
- [SEC Form 25-NSE 2010-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1010775/000087666110000453/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml): NYSE removes the class at the open on 2010-12-17 (Rule 12d2-2(a)(3)); securities came to evidence other securities on 2010-12-03.
- [SEC Form 15-12B 2010-12-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1010775/000095012310115511/c10049e15v12b.htm) follows.
- Fails rows (offline `sec.py fails`): RRI CUSIP 74971X107 under RRI 2010-11-01..2010-12-03; GenOn CUSIP 37244E107 under GEN from 2010-12-06.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Only the price security. The LLM read the acquirer as RRI (its name in the 2010 merger agreement); after the rename the fails closes under RRI end 2010-12-03, so `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_price`, `merger_at_par` and `dlret_method=assumed_par` followed (`assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause: terms_misread.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Map the acquirer ticker to the post-rename symbol (GEN) or find the acquirer's price by CUSIP/issuer rather than the agreement-time ticker, then publish stock with 2.835 x GEN at 2010-12-06.
- Confirm GEN by a filing (GenOn 8-K / 8-A).
- Golden candidate: yes (well-sourced rename-after-merger case).

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: terms

8-K 0001193125-10-275719 confirms 2.835 RRI Energy shares per MIR share and that RRI was renamed GenOn on the closing date, 2010-12-03. Fails rows show RRI CUSIP 74971X107 under RRI only through 2010-12-03, and GenOn CUSIP 37244E107 under GEN from 2010-12-06. So the library's RRI price ticker cannot be priced on 2010-12-06, and GEN is right. The symbol GEN rests on fails rows, not a filing, so the report's confidence stays inferred. Section 9 was appended to the report and a verification object added to the record JSON.
