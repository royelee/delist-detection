# RESP (Respironics) 2008-03-27: diagnosis

## 1. Verdict

A real cash merger: Philips acquired Respironics and each share became $66.00 cash. The library's kind, successor and
payout terms are right; only the last trade date is missing, so the row is uncertain. The merger closed 2008-03-14,
so the last exchange session was most likely that day (worked out, not stated by a filing).

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2008-03-17 Item 3.01: Merger Sub merged into Respironics on 2008-03-14).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Respironics survives as a wholly owned subsidiary of Philips Holding USA; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: worked out. No cited filing states the last session; the 8-K gives the merger date 2008-03-14 and
  Nasdaq's removal filing 2008-03-17. Fails rows run on to 2008-04-15 (settlement, last $65.96) and do not show the last trade.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: shares converted into the right to receive $66.00 cash).
- Terms: filing (same 8-K, $66.00 net in cash, without interest; the SC TO-T tender offer is at the same price).

Not backed: the last trade date. An exchange print (MIDAS does not cover 2008) or a filing or Nasdaq notice stating the last
trading day would make it `verified`.

## 5. What happened

  ([SEC 8-K 2008-03-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/780434/000095012308003040/y51594e8vk.htm)).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The kind and value are right (reason `8K_1.01`, bucket merger, CRSP 231). It found no exchange print for the last trade
(`last_trade_date_source` blank, flags `no_last_trade_date;no_last_close`, `dlret_method=needs_last_trade`). The 8-K
Item 3.01 states the merger date but not the last session, so the finder did not use it. Cause tag: `no_last_trade_print`.
The ticker_history end 2008-03-27 comes from the Form 25 fallback date.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish `cash_currency=USD`; keep cash 66.00.
- Last trade date: confirm 2008-03-14 from a Nasdaq notice or press release (a fact, not a price), or accept the merger
  effective date as the last trade when an 8-K 3.01 reports a same-day merger.
- Golden case: yes, a plain all-cash tender-offer merger with a blank last-trade date.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: ticker_history (report says wrong: end date 2008-03-27 too late). Result: **upheld, with a caveat on the
corrected date.**

- The library's range is RESP 2008-01-09 .. 2008-03-27 (`output/ticker_history.csv`). The delisting row shows Form
  25-NSE/A accession 0001354457-08-000048 filed 2008-03-17, so 2008-03-27 is the Form 25 effective date (filing + 10
  days), used because `last_trade_date` is blank (`no_last_trade_date`). It is a fallback, not a trade.
- The 8-K of 2008-03-17 (0000950123-08-003040) says the tender offer's subsequent offering period expired 5:00 p.m.
  on 2008-03-13, Merger Sub merged into Respironics on 2008-03-14, and every share became the right to $66.00 cash.
  After 2008-03-14 no share existed to trade, so a listing end of 2008-03-27 is about 13 days too late. The
  report's direction holds.
- Caveat: the report's "about 2008-03-14" is not backed by a filing. The offer expired the evening of 2008-03-13, so
  the last session may be 2008-03-13 or 2008-03-14; nothing found states which. The report's own confidence
  (`inferred`) fits this. The ticker_history end should be corrected to the confirmed last trade date, not
  asserted as 2008-03-14.
- Other fields (exit_kind, terms $66.00 cash, issuer 780434) were not in dispute and were not re-checked.
