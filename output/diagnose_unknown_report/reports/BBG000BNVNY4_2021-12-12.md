# MDP (Meredith Corp), case BBG000BNVNY4_2021-12-12

## 1. Verdict

A real cash merger in two steps: each Meredith share first became one share of Meredith Holdings (spin-off, one-to-one), then was cashed out: $16.99 (Gray merger, for the old share) plus $42.18 (Dotdash/IAC merger, for the Holdings share), $59.17 in all. The library's kind, date and issuer are right; it publishes only the $16.99 leg, so its terms are wrong and its assumed-par fill hides a roughly flat return.

## 5. What happened

- Gray Merger Agreement: Meredith distributed Meredith Holdings stock to its shareholders one-to-one, then Gray acquired the Company immediately after ([SEC 8-K 2021-12-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/65011/000119312521345548/d234670d8k.htm)).
- Each Meredith share was converted into $16.99 cash (Gray Merger Consideration) (same 8-K, item 2.01).
- Each Meredith Holdings share was converted into $42.18 cash (Dotdash Merger Consideration) shortly after (same 8-K).
- NYSE was asked to suspend trading before the open on 2021-12-02 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- Form 25-NSE filed 2021-12-02 states the Holdings share was exchanged for $42.18 and trading was suspended 2021-12-02 ([SEC 25-NSE 0000876661-21-001683](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/65011/000087666121001683/)).
- An earlier proxy supplement quoted the Gray consideration as $14.51; the completed 8-K states $16.99 ([SEC 8-K 2021-11-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/65011/000119312521335955/d209197d8k.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The LLM read only the Gray leg ($16.99); the Holdings leg ($42.18) came through a spin-off share, so the library's terms miss it. Its own gate flagged this (`payout_gate_failed:42.18`, `llm_gate_failed`, `terms_gate=failed`), and the value fell back to assumed par (dlret_fill 0.0). Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish `cash_per_share` 59.17 and `cash_currency` USD (a spin-off plus cash merger total).
- No open check on the terms; the filing states both legs.
- Golden case: yes, a spin-off followed by two cash mergers where the payout sums two legs.

## 9. Verification

Checked field: terms (report says the library is wrong). Result: upheld.

- Re-read the 8-K filed 2021-12-01 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/65011/000119312521345548/d234670d8k.htm), item 2.01) through sec.py. Each Meredith share was converted into $16.99 cash (Gray Merger Consideration).
- The same 8-K says the Gray merger came right after a one-to-one distribution of Meredith Holdings stock to Meredith shareholders (the Spin-Off). Each Meredith Holdings share was then converted into $42.18 cash (Dotdash Merger Consideration). So a holder received $16.99 + $42.18 = $59.17 per old share, and the Holdings share is a pass-through, not a continuing security.
- The library publishes only 16.99 with no currency (`terms_source=llm`, `terms_gate=failed`, flags `payout_gate_failed:42.18`, `llm_gate_failed`). The omission of the $42.18 leg and the missing currency are both real. The library's own last close of 59.07 is consistent with 59.17, not 16.99.
- Rule applied: reference.md "The payout rule" (terms from the filings; compare term by term; a missing cash leg or a missing currency is a terms error). Currency USD is stated by the filing.
- Minor: the other fields were not challenged by the report.
