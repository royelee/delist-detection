# Case BBG001D9S707_2010-05-25 (DYN, Dynegy Inc, CIK 1379895)

## 1. Verdict

There is no ending on 2010-05-25: that day Dynegy did a 1-for-5 reverse split (CUSIP 26817G102 became 26817G300) and the same stock kept trading as DYN. The real ending is the Chapter 11 of 2012-07-06: NYSE suspended the stock, it moved to OTC as DYNIQ, and old holders got warrants only. The library's 2010 row is wrong.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

2010: kept the same security (reverse split, new CUSIP, same ticker, same holders). 2012: lost (bankruptcy, then OTC); the new DYN is a new security.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The security's CUSIP history ends 2010-05-25 at the reverse-split CUSIP change, so no sighting follows. With no Form 25 found the continued-filings rule (304) made an exchange transfer of it (`continued_filings_rule`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`, `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`). The 2012 Form 25 (issued 2012-07-25) is never reached. Cause tag: `continued_filings_guess` (root: the CUSIP change by reverse split was not followed).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the four fields the report calls wrong. All four upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K of 2010-05-25 (0001379895-10-000012, item 5.02) says holders approved a 1-for-5 reverse split on 2010-05-21, effected 2010-05-25. Fails rows show CUSIP 26817G300 under DYN from 2010-05-26 to 2012-07-11 (420 rows, first $5.02), so the stock kept trading. No Form 25 exists in 2010. The ending is the 2012 bankruptcy: the Form 25-NSE of 2012-07-25 (0000876661-12-000438) says NYSE suspended the common stock before the open on 2012-07-06 after the Chapter 11 filing, removal on 2012-08-06; 8-K item 1.03 of 2012-07-06. Warrants-only recovery for old holders rests on the 2012-10-04 8-K, not re-read here. Fits `dropped` / `bankruptcy`.
- **last_trade_date: upheld.** The Form 25 notice itself states "suspended from trading before the opening of the trading session on July 6, 2012". July 4 was a holiday and July 5 a Thursday, so the last exchange trade is 2012-07-05, before the Form 25 effective date (decision 12). Better sourced than the report's "worked out" label: it comes from the Form 25 notice, an allowed source.
- **value_rule: upheld.** Fails rows show the same CUSIP 26817G300 under DYNIQ from 2012-07-12 to 2012-10-16 (51 rows), so a bankruptcy drop to OTC with decision 11's first off-exchange print applies. The library's `transfer` (0.0 fill) rests on the false 2010 transfer. The first OTC session and print stay unconfirmed, as the report says.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library's DYN range ends 2010-05-25 and its CUSIP history ends 2010-05-25 on 26817G102 (output/ticker_history.csv, output/cusip_history.csv). The same security keeps trading as DYN on 26817G300 to 2012-07-11, so the range should reach 2012-07-05.

Residual doubt: none that changes a field. Not checked: the exact first DYNIQ session, and the warrants' terms.
