# RKT (Rock-Tenn Co, Class A) - case BBG000BPQVR5_2015-07-12

## 1. Verdict

A real merger (Rock-Tenn / MeadWestvaco combination into new WestRock, effective 2015-07-01), with an election. The default package for a Rock-Tenn share (stock election or no election) is 1 WestRock share; only prorated cash electors got $51.7514 + 0.1856 WRK. The library's kind and date are right, but it published the cash-elector package instead of the default 1:1 stock package, so its terms are wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2015-07-02 items 2.01/3.01/5.01; Form 25-NSE notice).
- Successor: filing (8-K: holders receive WestRock shares or cash; no continuing Rock-Tenn line).
- Last trade date: worked out from the 8-K (effective 07-01, WRK first traded 07-02) and the library's MIDAS date; no filing states "last traded on".
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: stock electors and non-electors get the stock consideration).
- Terms: filing (8-K: 1 WRK; cash electors 0.1856 WRK + $51.7514).

Not backed: an explicit last-trade statement; a MIDAS print or a filing saying RKT ceased trading at the 07-01 close would make it verified. Choosing the default package over the cash-elector package is a judgement under the reference's election-deal rule.

## 5. What happened

- Combination effective 2015-07-01; each Rock-Tenn share converted, at the holder's election, into $63.549 cash (5-day VWAP) or one WestRock share; cash was oversubscribed and prorated ([SEC 8-K 2015-07-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/230498/000095015715000751/form8-k.htm)).
- Stock electors and non-electors received the stock consideration (1 WRK); cash electors received 0.1856 WRK + $51.7514 per share; WRK began regular-way trading on the NYSE 2015-07-02 (same 8-K).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2015-07-02: shares came to evidence other securities, with an immediate-cash-payment right ([SEC 25-NSE 0000876661-15-000284](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/230498/000087666115000284/ruleprovisionnotice.htm)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2015-07-14 and 2015-07-20 (EDGAR filing index for CIK 230498).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced by cash/stock of another company (merger). Not a 1:1 continuation: part of the consideration was cash paid in the exchange.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Reason `M&A 2.01+3.01+5.01` and CRSP 231 are right. The LLM extractor took the cash-elector package ($51.7514 + 0.1856) as the terms; `terms_gate=failed` and `llm_gate_failed;merger_at_par` flagged it, and the fill is assumed par. Cause: terms_misread (the election default, 1 WRK, was not chosen).

## 9. Verification

Re-read the 8-K of 2015-07-02 (CIK 230498, accession 0000950157-15-000751) through sec.py.

- **value_rule: upheld.** The 8-K states each Class A share could be exchanged for $63.549 cash or one WestRock share. Because cash was oversubscribed, stock electors and holders who made no valid election received the Stock Consideration (1 WRK). Only cash electors received the prorated package. The reference's election-deal rule says to give the default package the agreement fixes, so `stock` is right and the library's `cash_plus_stock` is wrong.
- **terms: upheld.** The library's 51.7514 + 0.1856 x WRK matches the cash-elector package in the 8-K, not the default. The 8-K's totals (130,378,207 WRK shares against about $667.8M cash) show stock was the bulk of the consideration. The currency (USD) is stated by the filing. Correct terms are stock_ratio 1.0, price_ticker WRK, price_date 2015-07-02. A caveat: the library's package is a real one for a minority of holders, so this is a choice of default under the rule, not a misread number.
