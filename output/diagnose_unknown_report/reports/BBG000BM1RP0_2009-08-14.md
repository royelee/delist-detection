- Fails rows for CUSIP 35039W100 (FCL) run to 2009-08-14 (last price 35.93); these are settlement lag, not trading.

## 9. Verification

Checked fields: last_trade_date, ticker_history. Re-read the 8-K of 2009-07-31 and its EX-99.1 through sec.py, and the fails rows of CUSIP 35039W100.

- **last_trade_date: upheld, with a caveat.** The 8-K (accession 0000950123-09-028243, item 2.01) says that at the Effective Time each Foundation share was converted into 1.0840 New Alpha shares. The EX-99.1 press release is dated 2009-07-31 and says both stockholder meetings were held "today" and the merger is complete, with the combined company trading as ANR. So FCL stopped as a separate line on 2009-07-31. The library's internal 2009-08-14 is wrong: the fails rows for 35039W100 run 20090105..20090814 and are settlement lag. Caveats: no filing states FCL's last trading day or the hour of the Effective Time, so 2009-07-31 is worked out (possibly 2009-07-30). Under decision 12, the published blank is correct while no exchange print exists. What is wrong is the internal 2009-08-14 and the unconfirmed status, not the blank itself. The report's confidence of `inferred` is fair.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The output has FCL ending 2009-08-14 and CUSIP 35039W100 ending 2009-08-14. The press release shows the FCL symbol stopped when the merger closed and the surviving entity trades only as ANR. Fails rows past 2009-07-31 give no evidence of trading. The correct end is about 2009-07-31, not 2009-08-14.
- Not refuted: ratio 1.0840, no cash, 59%/41% ownership split, kind merger (not a continuation), all as stated in the same 8-K.
