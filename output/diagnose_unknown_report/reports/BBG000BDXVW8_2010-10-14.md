## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred, because of one term.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2010-10-01 items 3.01, 3.03, 5.01; Form 25-NSE notice).
- Successor: filing (merger into a UAL subsidiary; no continuing CAL line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: effective before the opening on Oct 1, security suspended Oct 1, so last trade Sep 30; that is worked out from the stated suspension date).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 3.03: 1.05 UAL shares, fractions in cash).
- Terms: ratio from the filing; the price ticker (UAUA) rests on fails rows only. Not backed: the post-merger trading symbol (UAL vs UAUA). A filing or exchange notice naming the symbol UAL began trading under on 2010-10-01 would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- Merger completed; each share of Company Common Stock converted into 1.05 UAL shares, fractions paid in cash ([SEC 8-K 2010-10-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/319687/000119312510222187/d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE: merger became effective before the opening on 2010-10-01; each share converted into 1.05 United Continental Holdings (successor to UAL Corporation) shares; trading suspended 2010-10-01; removal from listing at open 2010-10-14 ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2010-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/319687/000087666110000370/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- UAL Corp fails rows under UAUA appear from 2010-10-08 ([SEC fails data, CUSIP 902549807]).
