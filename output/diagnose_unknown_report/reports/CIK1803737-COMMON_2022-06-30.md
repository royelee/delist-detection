# CIK1803737-COMMON_2022-06-30 (EHAB-WI)

## 1. Verdict
EHAB-WI is Enhabit's when-issued line, seen only on 2022-06-30, the day before Encompass Health's spin-off distribution. It converted to regular-way EHAB on the NYSE on 2022-07-01, so there is no ending. The library's merger/231 ending is wrong: the 8-K item 5.01 it read is Enhabit's own separation, not an acquisition.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Kept the same security. A when-issued ticker is a pre-distribution trading alias of the regular security, not a delisting.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The observation was a one-day sighting under the when-issued ticker, which got a placeholder sec_id because no FIGI matched "EHAB-WI". The end-of-era resolver then read the spin-off's 8-K item 5.01 as a merger; flags `last_trade_date_unconfirmed;no_form25;delist_date_approx;no_last_close`. Cause: `other:when_issued_ticker_read_as_ending`.

## 8. Fix and open checks
- Treat a `-WI` when-issued suffix as an alias of the base ticker (EHAB) when resolving, folding the era into the regular-way security's sec_id (BBG014QJ5BV6); then no ending is produced.
- At minimum, the end-of-era resolver should not read a 5.01 filed by the registrant itself on its separation date as an acquisition.
- Open: confirm BBG014QJ5BV6 is the composite FIGI for EHAB in the run's observations.
- Golden-worthy: yes, a clear filing-sourced when-issued case.

## 9. Verification
Skeptic check of the three fields section 2 calls wrong. All three upheld.
- **exit_kind (merger, 231 -> no ending): upheld.** The 8-K filed 2022-07-05 ([accession 0001140361-22-025027](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1803737/000114036122025027/ny20003604x14_8k.htm)) has item 5.01 "Changes in Control of Registrant", and its text says Enhabit "was a wholly-owned subsidiary of Encompass immediately prior to the Distribution" and that Encompass distributed 100% of its common stock on 2022-07-01, listed as EHAB on the NYSE. That is a spin-off, not an acquisition: no cash or stock consideration, no 2.01 item. `sec.py filings` for CIK 1803737, 2022-06-01 to 2023-12-31, restricted to Form 25, 25-NSE, 15, 15-12B and 8-K12B, returns nothing, so no delisting or successor filing exists. Fails rows for CUSIP 29332G102 under EHAB run 2022-07-06 to 2022-08-17 (11 rows), so the security kept trading. Caveat: the 8-K never uses the words "when-issued", so that the EHAB-WI line converted into regular-way EHAB is market convention plus the dates, not a sentence in the filing. No filing contradicts it.
- **value_rule (unknown -> continuation): upheld.** With no ending there is no payout. reference.md lists `continuation` as "not an exit". The library's `abstain_no_consideration` and no-terms state came only from the misread 5.01.
- **ticker_history (EHAB-WI on a placeholder, 2022-06-30 only -> one line with EHAB): upheld.** output/securities.csv holds both CIK1803737-COMMON (placeholder, "ENHABIT INC WHEN ISSUED") and BBG014QJ5BV6 (CUSIP source, "ENHABIT INC"), same issuer CIK 1803737. output/ticker_history.csv gives the placeholder EHAB-WI 2022-06-30 to 2022-06-30 and BBG014QJ5BV6 EHAB from 2022-07-06. Splitting one common stock in two is the error. Nit: the report's "one line with EHAB" leaves the merged range's start unstated. EHAB's first fails row is 2022-07-06, but regular-way trading began 2022-07-01 (8-K), so the range could start at 2022-06-30 or 2022-07-01 and need not wait for the fails rows.
- Not re-checked because the report did not call them wrong: issuer, drop_reason, terms. The report's `successor` (BBG014QJ5BV6) and `last_trade_date` unknown claims are consistent with the above. Open check from section 8 (that BBG014QJ5BV6 is the right composite FIGI) is unchanged, though securities.csv shows it as a CUSIP-confirmed row for the same issuer.
