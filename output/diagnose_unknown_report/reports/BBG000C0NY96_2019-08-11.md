# BBG000C0NY96 (TCF Financial, legacy) 2019-08-11

## 1. Verdict

A real stock-for-stock merger of equals: legacy TCF Financial (CIK 814184) merged into Chemical Financial (CIK 19612), each TCF share became 0.5081 Chemical shares, and Chemical renamed itself TCF Financial and took the ticker TCF. The library's kind, date, rule and ratio are right; its price symbol (CHFC) is the acquirer's pre-merger ticker, which no longer traded on the price date, and the 0.0 is assumed par from a failed gate.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Stock rule and ratio were extracted correctly, but the price leg names CHFC, a symbol that stopped trading when Chemical became TCF. The price check then failed (`terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`), leaving assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (the only field the report calls wrong). Result: **upheld**, with one caveat on section 7.

- Price leg symbol. Chemical's 8-K cover (0000019612-19-000187, [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19612/000001961219000187/a8-kmergerlongform.htm)) lists the common stock under symbol TCF on Nasdaq, and Item 2.01 says Chemical changed its name to TCF Financial on 2019-08-01. Fails rows (offline `sec.py fails`): under CHFC (CUSIP 163731102) rows run 20190705..20190802 at $41.51 to $42.04. Under TCF the legacy CUSIP 872275102 has rows only 20190702..20190712 (about $20.95), and the new CUSIP 872307103 has rows 20190801..20190830, starting at $42.04. So on the price date 2019-08-01 the acquirer's stock trades as TCF, not CHFC. The library's `price_ticker=CHFC` names the pre-merger symbol. Output: `output/ticker_history.csv` has BBG000BFK8Y6 under TCF from 2019-08-01, and `output/securities.csv` has BBG000BFK8Y6 with issuer 19612. Library `price_sec_id` is blank. Upheld.
- Ratio and no cash: the Form 25-NSE notice (0000876661-19-000806) was cited by the report. The library's 0.5081 and no cash agree with the report, and nothing I found contradicts them. Upheld. I did not re-read that notice independently.
- Kind, date, rule: the report marks these as agreeing, and I did not dispute them.

Caveat on section 7 (not a change to a field status). The stale price symbol is a defect, but it may not be the whole reason for `terms_gate_failed:fail_sanity`. The source row has `library_last_trade_close` = 42.04. That is the acquirer's close (CHFC's last rows and the new TCF's first row are both $42.04). Legacy TCF's last price in the fails data is about $20.95, and 0.5081 x 42.04 = 21.36 is consistent with it. So the target's last close appears to be contaminated by the reused TCF symbol, and the gate would fail even with the price leg fixed. A fix should check both the price leg and the last close. I did not recompute the gate, so this is a lead, not a verified cause.
