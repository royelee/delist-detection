## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K items 2.01/3.01/5.01 of 2007-12-03; Form 25-NSE notice).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: GlobalSantaFe merged into Transocean Worldwide; holders get cash plus shares).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K item 3.01: delisting effective as of the close of business on 2007-11-26; Form 25 notice: suspended 2007-11-27).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2.01 and Form 25 notice: shares and cash).
- Terms: filing (0.4757 and $22.46 in both). The price ticker RIG and its date are the acquirer's first session after the close; the filings quoted do not name the ticker, which comes from the library's own terms (not a market price).

## 5. What happened

- GlobalSantaFe merged with Transocean Worldwide by scheme of arrangement; each GSF share was exchanged for 0.4757 Transocean ordinary shares and $22.46 in cash ([SEC 8-K 2007-12-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1038914/000110465907086425/a07-30525_18k.htm)).
- NYSE was asked to delist GSF effective as of the close of business 2007-11-26 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- Form 25-NSE filed 2007-12-13: the merger became effective before the opening on 2007-11-27; the shares were suspended from trading 2007-11-27 ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1038914/000087666107000940/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- After the ending the issuer filed only deregistration amendments (S-8 POS, S-4 POS, POS AM) on 2008-01-16 ([SEC S-4 POS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1038914/000110465908003050/a08-1656_1s4pos.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old shares were exchanged for cash plus another company's shares, a merger.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The merger row is right. Two small issues: `cash_currency` is blank (US$ stated in the filings), and the row is uncertain only through `security_uncertain` (`seeds_outside_history:1 from 2008-01-16`): the caller's observations of GSF dated 2008-01-16 to 2009-06-08 (stale snapshots) are mapped `after_delisting`, which is correct. Flags `observed_after_delisting` and `acquirer_close_lagged` are informational. Cause tag: `library_right_but_uncertain`.
