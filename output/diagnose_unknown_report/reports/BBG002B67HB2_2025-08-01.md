# BBG002B67HB2_2025-08-01 — UNIT (Uniti Group Inc.), Windstream merger

## 1. Verdict

Uniti merged with Windstream into a new parent (New Uniti, CIK 2020795) that is the Rule 12g-3 successor issuer. Old UNIT common became 0.6029 New Uniti common, with no cash to Uniti holders, and the shares kept trading on Nasdaq under UNIT with a new CUSIP. I read this as a holding-company continuation (not one for one; judgement call, see section 6), not a terminal merger; the library's merger / stock row with no price ticker is wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2025-08-01 items 2.01, 3.01, 8.01) plus a judgement that 0.6029 for one with no cash to holders is a continuation.
- Successor: filing (8-K 2025-08-01 "Successor Issuer"; 8-K12B of CIK 2020795).
- Last trade date: fails rows (old CUSIP's last row 2025-08-04, new CUSIP from 2025-08-05); the 8-K states no date.
- Payout rule: worked out from the continuation call.
- Terms: filing (8-K: 0.6029 New Uniti common; $370.7M cash went to Windstream holders, not Uniti holders).
- Not backed: an exchange print or notice stating the last old-CUSIP day, and a spec ruling on a non-1:1 exchange. A Nasdaq notice or filing stating the last day would make the date verified.

## 5. What happened

- Windstream merged into New Uniti, and a merger sub merged into Uniti, which survived as a subsidiary and became Uniti Group LLC ([SEC 8-K 2025-08-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1620280/000095010325009717/dp232456_8k-wizard.htm)).
- Each Uniti common share became 0.6029 New Uniti common; cash only in lieu of fractions (same 8-K).
- Windstream's pre-closing equityholders also received $370,659,503.47 cash from Uniti (same 8-K); Uniti holders received none.
- New Uniti stock continues on Nasdaq as UNIT, new CUSIP 912932 100 (same 8-K, Item 3.01).
- New Uniti is the Rule 12g-3 successor issuer (same 8-K, "Successor Issuer"; 8-K12B of CIK 2020795, accession 0000950103-25-009718, filed 2025-08-01); Uniti filed a Form 15-12G on 2025-08-04.
- Fails rows: CUSIP 91325V108 last $4.91 (rows to 2025-08-04); CUSIP 912932100 first $8.09 (2025-08-05); 8.09 / 4.91 is about 1/0.6029, so the claim carried over (fails rows, not a market price lookup).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security in substance: replaced by the successor line at 0.6029 for 1, same ticker, same exchange, no cash to these holders, no bankruptcy. It is not one for one, so this is a judgement: the ratio acts like a reverse split, but Uniti holders also share the new parent with Windstream holders. If the operator rules that only 1:1 exchanges continue, this is a stock merger valued at 0.6029 × UNIT.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It read the 5.01/2.01 pair as an acquisition by an external acquirer and found no acquirer ticker (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`); the acquirer is the successor parent with the same ticker UNIT. Cause tag: `holdco_not_linked`.

## 9. Verification

Checked the three fields section 2 calls wrong against `reference.md` and the 8-K of 2025-08-01 ([accession 0000950103-25-009717](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1620280/000095010325009717/dp232456_8k-wizard.htm), grepped through sec.py). The facts in the report hold: 0.6029 New Uniti share per Uniti share, cash only in lieu of fractions, Nasdaq UNIT continues. The rule applied does not hold.

- **exit_kind: refuted.** The report calls this a continuation, but the reference defines one as "same holders, same claim, one for one" (decision 9, CRSP's rule). This is neither. (1) It is 0.6029 for 1, not one for one; the report itself calls this a judgement call. (2) The holders are not the same: the 8-K says Windstream's pre-closing equityholders received about 35.42% of New Uniti common, $575M of New Uniti preferred and about 6.9% in warrants, and that pre-Closing Uniti holders end with about 62% of the common. The Uniti holders were diluted into a combined company. (3) The 8-K calls the consideration the "Uniti Merger Consideration" and says the exchange "is a taxable transaction for holders of Uniti Common Stock". The decision table sends "replaced by stock of another company" (8-K 2.01, 5.01, 3.01) to `merger`, `continuation=false`; the 1:1 holdco row is for a new line of the same holders. The library's `merger` stands. The $370.7M cash went to Windstream holders, so the "no cash" test is not the issue; the ratio and the changed holders are.
- **value_rule: refuted as stated.** Since this is a merger, `continuation` is wrong. The library's `stock` rule with ratio 0.6029 is right in kind; only the price terms are missing. Corrected: `stock`, `stock_ratio` 0.6029, `price_ticker` UNIT (the New Uniti line, new CUSIP 912932100), `price_date` 2025-08-04, formula 0.6029 × price(UNIT, 2025-08-04) / last_close − 1. This is the report's own fallback in section 6 and the section 8 alternative. The `price_ticker`/`price_date` gap is a terms gap (`terms_not_extracted`), not a wrong rule. Reading the old row's `price(?, ?)`, the old line's `UNIT` ticker equals the new line's, so a price lookup must use the date, not the ticker alone.
- **ticker_history: refuted.** The old security (CUSIP 91325V108, CIK 1620280) ended on 2025-08-01 when its shares were cancelled in the merger, so clipping its ticker_history at 2025-08-04 is right (the 08-04 date is the last fails row, three days after the last trade; the clip date is a minor point). New Uniti (CIK 2020795, CUSIP 912932100) is a different security that took over the ticker UNIT, not a continuation of the old one. If anything the ticker belongs on the successor line as a ticker takeover. Extending the old security's history is not warranted.
- Not checked and left as the report has them: `last_trade_date` (unknown), `successor` (a link to New Uniti is still useful as the acquirer, not as a continuation), `terms` (missing price_ticker, agreed).

Overall: the report's verdict ("holdco continuation, not a terminal merger") is refuted. The library's `merger` / `stock` row with ratio 0.6029 is right; what it lacks is the acquirer ticker UNIT and price date, and a confirmed last trade day. Suggested cause tag: `terms_not_extracted` (the acquirer is the same-ticker successor parent), not `holdco_not_linked`. Confidence: moderate; if the operator rules that a non-1:1 exchange with changed holders can be a continuation, this would flip, but nothing in `reference.md` supports that.
