## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2008-11-26 reports the merger completed; Form 25-NSE 2008-11-24).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: ImClone survives as a Lilly subsidiary; holders get cash only).
- Last trade date: worked out from the 8-K's stated Effective Time (8:28 AM Eastern, 2008-11-24, before the open), so the last session was Friday 2008-11-21. No filing states the last trading day, and the Form 25 notice here gives no suspension date.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K and SC TO-T/A: $70.00 net in cash).
- Terms: filing ($70.00 per share, cash, USD implied by a US tender offer; no stock leg).

Not backed: the last day by a printed source. An exchange print (MIDAS, a Nasdaq halt record) or a Nasdaq notice naming the last day would make it `verified`. The Form 25 effective date 2008-11-24 is not after any plausible last trade, so decision 12 holds.

## 5. What happened

- Tender offer expired at midnight 2008-11-20; about 85.4M shares (95.5%) tendered and accepted; Lilly planned a short-form merger on 2008-11-24 at $70.00 cash ([SEC SC TO-T/A 2008-11-21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/765258/000095013708013866/c47316a7sctovtza.htm)).
- The merger took effect at 8:28 AM Eastern on 2008-11-24; each share became $70.00 cash ([SEC 8-K 2008-11-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/765258/000095013708014111/c47985e8vk.htm)).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2008-11-24, effectiveness date 2008-11-24 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/765258/000135445708000337/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12G filed 2008-12-05 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/765258/000095013708014262/c48075e15v12g.htm)).
- Fails rows under CIK-linked CUSIP 45245W109 run to 2008-12-29 with a last price near $69.99 (fails settle after the end; not trading evidence).
