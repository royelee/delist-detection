## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2018-06-04 items 3.01/5.01: each Westar share converted into the right to receive one Evergy share; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Evergy is the new parent, Westar the surviving subsidiary).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE: suspended from trading June 5, 2018, so the last trade was June 4).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K: share-for-share conversion, no cash).
- Terms: ratio from the filing; the ticker EVRG and price date 2018-06-05 rest on the run's own ticker_history (EVRG, from 2018-06-05), not on a filing I read.
- Not backed: the acquirer ticker in a filing. A filing naming EVRG and its first trading day would make it `verified`.

## 5. What happened

- The Westar and Great Plains Energy mergers became effective 2018-06-04. Each Westar share was converted into one Evergy share; each Great Plains share into 0.5981 ([SEC 8-K 2018-06-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/54507/000119312518182972/d598447d8k.htm)).
- The Form 25-NSE says each Westar share was exchanged for one Evergy share and trading was suspended on June 5, 2018 ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2018-06-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/54507/000087666118000660/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2018-06-18 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/54507/000119312518195800/d612506d1512b.htm)).
- The run already holds Evergy as BBG00H433CR2, EVRG from 2018-06-05 (output/ticker_history.csv).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old shares became another company's shares, in a combination with a second company. A merger, not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Exit kind and date are right, but the row came through the end-of-era resolver (`resolved_from_continued_filings`, 8-K 5.01) with no acquirer ticker: `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`, so the price security is "?", the payout gate failed (`payout_gate_failed:25`), and the value fell back to assumed par (`merger_at_par`). Cause tag: `terms_not_extracted`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Resolve the acquirer as Evergy (BBG00H433CR2, EVRG) from the 8-K text or from the run's security that starts the day after the last trade; publish price_ticker and price_sec_id.
- Open check: confirm EVRG's first trading day (2018-06-05) from a filing.
- Golden case: yes, a merger of equals via a new holdco at 1:1 with the acquirer in the run.
