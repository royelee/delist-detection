# CMCSK (Comcast Class A Special common), 2015-12-11

## 1. Verdict

Comcast reclassified every Class A Special share (CMCSK) into one Class A common share (CMCSA) on 2015-12-11: a 1:1 reclassification, no cash, same issuer. This is a continuation into the CMCSA line (BBG000BFT2L4). The library's exit kind (exchange) and last trade date are right, but it leaves `continuation=false`, no successor, and labels the ending a guessed "continued filings" fallback.

- Successor: filing (same 8-K: new shares listed as CMCSA, same CUSIP as Class A common).

## 5. What happened

- Shareholders approved on 2015-12-10 reclassifying each Class A Special share into one Class A common share; effective at the close on 2015-12-11 ([SEC 8-K 2015-12-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000095010315009516/dp61838_8k.htm)).
- Nasdaq ceased trading of CMCSK at the close on 2015-12-11; the new shares trade as CMCSA on the same CUSIP as the existing Class A, "indistinguishably" (same 8-K).
- No cash was paid; the reclassification had no effect on economic interests (same 8-K, Item 3.03).
- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE on 2015-12-11 under Rule 12d2-2(a)(2) for Class A Special Common Stock ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000135445715000245/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2015-12-22 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000095010315009675/dp61980_1512b.htm)) relates to the eliminated class; Comcast itself kept filing.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced 1:1 by a line of the same holders (same issuer, same claim, no cash): a continuation with successor CMCSA (decision 9). Not a merger and not a loss.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The row came from the Form 25-NSE, but the finder's reason is "Continued 10-K/Q filings >180d after delist" (CRSP 304, exchange_transfer, `successor_unknown`, `continued_filings_rule`): the issuer kept filing because it is the same company, and no successor link was made to the CMCSA security although it is in the run (BBG000BFT2L4). The 8-K 3.01/3.03/5.03 reclassification text was not recognised as a 1:1 same-issuer reclassification. Cause tag: `holdco_not_linked` (a same-issuer class reclassification left as a dangling transfer).

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Set `continuation=true`, `successor_sec_id=BBG000BFT2L4`, `value_rule=continuation`; the ending is not an exit (no return).
- Rule fix: an 8-K 3.01 + 3.03 stating "reclassify each share of class X into one share of class Y" of the same issuer, with Y in the run, is a continuation.
- No open fact checks. No market price needed.
- Golden case: yes. A same-issuer share-class reclassification, well sourced; a rule fix should flip it.

## 9. Verification

- **value_rule (upheld).** Re-read the 8-K filed 2015-12-15 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000095010315009516/dp61838_8k.htm), Item 3.01): shareholders approved on 2015-12-10 an amendment "to reclassify each issued share of Class A Special common stock into one share of Class A common stock"; the Reclassification became effective at the close of business on 2015-12-11; no Class A Special shares remain outstanding; Nasdaq trading of CMCSK ceased at the close on 2015-12-11; the new shares trade as CMCSA "will use the same CUSIP ... used by shares of Class A common stock". That is a one-for-one, same-issuer exchange with no cash term in the quoted text, so by reference.md decision 9 it is a continuation (not an exit), and the library's `transfer` value_rule (exchange_transfer_zero fill, no successor) is wrong. The successor sec_id BBG000BFT2L4 itself was not re-verified here, only that the successor is the CMCSA Class A line.
