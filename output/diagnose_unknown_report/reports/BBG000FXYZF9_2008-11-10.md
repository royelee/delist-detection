# WM (Washington Mutual, Inc.) - case BBG000FXYZF9_2008-11-10

## 1. Verdict

A real bankruptcy drop: the holding company filed chapter 11 on 2008-09-26 after the FDIC seized its bank, the NYSE
halted and then suspended WM, and the stock moved to the pink sheets as WAMUQ. The library's kind and rule
(`dropped`/`bankruptcy`, `otc_print`) are right; its last trade date (2008-09-29, the suspension date) is not an exchange
print and probably a trading day or more late, and its price ticker is the dead symbol WM instead of WAMUQ.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K item 1.03, chapter 11 on 2008-09-26; 8-K item 3.01 NYSE suspension).
- Successor: filing (nothing in the 8-Ks or Form 25 names a successor; the notice cites JPMorgan's purchase of the bank only).
- Last trade date: worked out. The 8-K states a trading halt at the NYSE open on 2008-09-26 and a suspension effective 2008-09-29, but not the last print. Not backed: the exact last exchange trade (09-25, 09-26 or 09-29). It would be verified by an exchange print (MIDAS is empty here) or a Form 25 notice that states the last day.
- Payout rule: filing (chapter 11 plus NYSE suspension; decision 11 values a move to OTC at the first off-exchange print).
- Terms: fails rows for the OTC symbol (WAMUQ from 2008-10-03 on CUSIP 939322103); the price date follows from the last trade date above. No cash or ratio applies.

## 5. What happened

- Chapter 11 petition filed 2008-09-26 in Delaware after the FDIC was appointed receiver of Washington Mutual Bank on 2008-09-25 ([SEC 8-K 2008-09-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/933136/000089375008000495/form8k.htm)).
- NYSE Regulation notified the company on 2008-09-29 that it suspended the listings of the common stock, effective immediately, after a "regulatory trading halt" at the NYSE open on 2008-09-26; the stock traded as low as $0.15 before it ([same 8-K, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/933136/000089375008000495/form8k.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2008-10-31 to remove the common stock at the opening of business 2008-11-10 under Rule 12d2-2(b) ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/933136/000087666108000444/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 939322103 under WM through 2008-10-02 (last $0.14), under WAMUQ from 2008-10-03 (first $0.12) - the same CUSIP, so the stock kept trading OTC (SEC fails-to-deliver data).
- FINRA later told the company its OTCBB quotation would end on 2008-12-22 for a late 10-Q ([SEC 8-K 2008-12-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/933136/000090951808000909/mm12-1008_8k.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. The holders kept the same shares, now of a bankrupt company delisted by the NYSE; value is the first OTC print.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The row is flagged `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`; its date (2008-09-29) is the suspension date from the notice, not a printed last trade, so the `otc_print` leg is anchored to 2008-09-30 under the dead ticker WM and the return falls back to a Shumway fill (-0.30). Cause tag: `no_last_trade_print`.

- Open: whether WM printed on the NYSE on 2008-09-26 after the halt or only through 2008-09-25; the first WAMUQ print date (web, not in SEC).
