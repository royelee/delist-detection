## 1. Verdict

A real merger: CenturyLink acquired Level 3 on 2017-11-01, and each LVLT share became $26.50 cash plus 1.4286 CenturyLink (CTL) shares. ## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2017-11-01 items 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01: merger completed; shares converted).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: converted into cash and CTL stock; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE, effectiveness 2017-11-01, security suspended on the merger date, so last trade is the prior trading day 2017-10-31; MIDAS agrees).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K and Form 25 notice state cash plus CTL shares).
- Terms: filing (8-K: $26.50 cash and 1.4286 CTL shares per share).

## 5. What happened

- The Initial Merger closed 2017-11-01; each share converted into $26.50 cash and 1.4286 CTL shares ([SEC 8-K 2017-11-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/794323/000119312517329395/d459582d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE: shares converted into 1.4286 CTL shares and $26.50 cash; effective 2017-11-01 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/794323/000087666117000651/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2017-11-13 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/794323/000119312517341100/d495301d1512b.htm)).
- Level 3 kept filing afterwards (post-effective amendments on 2017-11-01 and later debt reports), which is what triggered the continued-filings branch.
