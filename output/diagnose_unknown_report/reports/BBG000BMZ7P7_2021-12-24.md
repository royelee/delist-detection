## 5. What happened
- [SEC Form 25-NSE 2021-12-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/54480/000087666121001748/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml): each KCS share exchanged for USD 90.00 and 2.884 CP common shares; suspended from trading December 14, 2021.
- [SEC 8-K 2021-12-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/54480/000119312521356252/d25745d8k.htm): two-step merger completed; surviving KCS renamed and its stock deposited in an independent voting trust pending STB approval.
- [SEC 8-K 2021-12-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/54480/000119312521354084/d151013d8k.htm): items 5.07, 8.01 (shareholder vote results).
