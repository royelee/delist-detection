## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2014-10-20, items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01: the Merger became effective 2014-10-17).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: each URS share converted into the right to receive the Merger Consideration; no continuing line).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K item 3.01: trading suspended before the opening on 2014-10-17, so the last trade was 2014-10-16).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K and DEFM14A: sum of 0.734 AECOM shares and $33.00).
- Terms: filing (DEFM14A and 8-K: 0.734 and $33.00; election and proration equalise value).

## 5. What happened

- The Merger became effective 2014-10-17; each URS share converted into $33.00 plus 0.734 AECOM shares, valued at $53.991 on the five-day average ([SEC 8-K 2014-10-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/102379/000119312514376437/d806601d8k.htm)).
- NYSE trading in URS was suspended before the open on 2014-10-17 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- Terms were fixed in the merger proxy ([SEC DEFM14A 2014-09-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/102379/000104746914007703/a2221475zdefm14a.htm)).
- NYSE filed the Form 25-NSE on 2014-10-20, removal effective at the open on 2014-10-31, because the shares came to evidence other securities ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/102379/000087666114000559/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old security was exchanged for cash plus another company's shares: a merger.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

It did not, apart from the blank currency. Its LLM-read terms (33.00, 0.734, ACM) match the filings, but the payout gate against its fails-based close failed (`llm_gate_failed`), so `dlret_method=assumed_par` filled 0.0 and the row is uncertain (`assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Why the gate failed is not shown by the filings (possibly a missing or stale ACM or URS fails price). Cause tag: `library_right_but_uncertain`.
