## 1. Verdict

A real cash-or-units election merger into Energy Transfer Equity (ETE), effective 2012-03-26. The library's kind, last trade date, successor and issuer are right; its cash rule of $44.25 is the cash election only and omits the equal alternative of 1.00 ETE common unit, so the row is right but its terms are incomplete.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2012-03-27 items 2.01, 3.03, 5.01: merger effective 2012-03-26 12:59 pm ET).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: Southern Union survives as an indirect subsidiary of ETE; holders get cash or ETE units).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: suspended from trading 2012-03-27, so last trade 2012-03-26).
- Payout rule: worked out. The agreement gives each holder a free choice; the aggregate result was 54% cash, 46% units, so no single package is fixed. Cash is the majority outcome, the unit is what non-electors received.
- Terms: filing ($44.25 and 1.00 ETE unit, election results in the 8-K).
Not backed: one value rule for an election deal. Would become verified if the contract can carry both alternatives or the operator rules which package represents the election.

## 5. What happened

- Merger effective 2012-03-26 at 12:59 pm ET; each share converted into $44.25 cash or 1.00 ETE common unit at the holder's election, max 60% cash and 50% units; election results 54% cash (67,985,929 shares), 46% units (56,981,860 shares), so no proration ([SEC 8-K 2012-03-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/203248/000020324812000027/suform8k032712.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE filed 2012-03-27; security suspended from trading 2012-03-27 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/203248/000087666112000125/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2012-04-06 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/203248/000020324812000051/suform15_040612.htm)).
