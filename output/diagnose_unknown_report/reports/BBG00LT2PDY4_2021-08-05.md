## 9. Verification
Checked by re-reading the cited 8-K (0001104659-21-095902) and the 25-NSE (0001354457-21-000847) through sec.py.

- **exit_kind (merger): upheld.** The 8-K (Item 7.01, 2021-07-26, CIK 1496048) states BAM completed its acquisition of all BPY units and that each outstanding share of the registrant Class A Stock (BPYU, Nasdaq) is automatically exchanged for $12.38 cash, 0.0913 BAM class A limited voting share and 0.0657 BPY preferred unit ($25 liquidation preference). Holders received cash plus another company securities, so merger, not continuation (reference.md decision table). Class matches: the 25-NSE (filed and effective 2021-07-26) covers Class A Common Stock of the same CIK; the Series A preferred (BPYUP) is a separate class. The 8-K delisting wording is forward-looking, but the same-day 25-NSE confirms it.
- **value_rule (cash_plus_stock): upheld.** Terms match the 8-K digit for digit (12.38 USD, 0.0913, 0.0657). The unidentified preferred-unit ticker is a pricing gap already listed as open, not an error in the rule.
- Not challenged: last_trade_date 2021-07-26 and terms status are consistent with the same two filings.
