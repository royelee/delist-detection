# BBG001BP9474_2015-02-27 (EGL, Engility Holdings) diagnosis

## 1. Verdict

A 1:1 holding-company reorganization tied to the TASC merger: each old Engility share became one share of "New Engility" (renamed Engility Holdings, same NYSE symbol EGL, new CUSIP 29286C107). That is a continuation, not a transfer with no successor. The library's `exchange` kind is right, but it says `continuation=false` with no successor and leaves the ending uncertain. Old holders also got an $11.434 special cash dividend, which I treat as a distribution outside the exchange (see section 6).

## 5. What happened

- The Engility/TASC mergers closed on 2015-02-26. New East Holdings was renamed Engility Holdings and became successor issuer to Predecessor Engility under Rule 12g-3; each Predecessor share became one New Engility share, and the new stock trades on the NYSE as EGL ([SEC 8-K12B 2015-02-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1544229/000119312515069445/d880987d8k12b.htm)).
- Holders of record on 2015-02-25 who held through closing were entitled to a special cash dividend of $11.434 per Predecessor share (same 8-K12B).
- The 424B3 calls one New Engility share "the Engility merger consideration" and describes the special dividend as paid by Engility immediately before the Engility merger, to holders of record, in addition to it ([SEC 424B3 2015-01-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1544229/000119312515016426/d814513d424b3.htm)).
- Engility stockholders approved the merger on 2015-02-23 ([SEC 8-K 2015-02-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1544229/000119312515058368/d879098d8k.htm)).
- Fails rows: CUSIP 29285W104 under EGL to 2015-02-27 (last $46.45), then EGLXXXX 2015-03-02 and 03-03; CUSIP 29286C107 under EGL from 2015-03-04 (SEC fails data, `sec.py fails`).
- No Form 25 for the old security was filed in this window (SEC filings list for CIK 1544229).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced 1:1 by a new line of the same holders (holdco reorganization): continuation. The holder gave up one old share and got one new share. The $11.434 dividend was declared on the old shares by Predecessor Engility and paid before the exchange. The operator's BHI 2017 ruling asks what the holder received for surrendering the old share, so the dividend stays a distribution. This is the weakest point: the exception applies if the transaction documents make the payment part of the consideration. The 424B3 keeps them separate ("merger consideration" is the share only), but the merger agreement requires the dividend. The dividend is about 28% of the price, so a reviewer may want to confirm the ruling covers a dividend paid on the old shares.

The successor line (CUSIP 29286C107) is never added to the run. Cause tag: `successor_not_in_run`.

## 9. Verification

Checked by re-reading the 8-K12B (0001193125-15-069445), the 424B3 (0001193125-15-016426), the CIK 1544229 filing list for 2015-02-20 to 2015-04-30, and the fails rows for symbol EGL, 2015-02 to 2015-03 (`sec.py`). The two fields below are the ones the report calls wrong.

- **value_rule (library `transfer`, report `continuation`): upheld.**
  - The 8-K12B says each Predecessor Engility share was converted into the right to receive one New Engility share, and that New Engility is the Rule 12g-3 successor issuer.
  - The 424B3 defines "Engility merger consideration" as that one share. It describes the $11.434 special dividend (estimated at $11.48 in the 424B3) as a separate entitlement, paid by Engility to holders of record before the Engility merger takes effect. It says Engility stockholders "will only be entitled to the Engility special cash dividend and the Engility merger consideration".
  - Under the BHI ruling in reference.md the dividend is not consideration for surrendering the old share, so this is a continuation.
  - The library's `transfer` (an exchange move that keeps the same shares) is wrong either way. If the dividend counted as consideration, the row would be a merger with cash.
  - Caveats the report understates:
    - This case differs from BHI. The dividend is paid by the old issuer before the exchange, not by the successor afterwards, and entitlement requires holding through closing.
    - The merger agreement requires the dividend (Pre-East Merger Parent Special Dividend, a closing covenant).
    - Lazard's fairness opinion treats the share and the dividend together.
    - These are what the ruling's "part of the consideration" exception turns on. The 424B3's wording keeps them separate, so the report's reading survives, but a reviewer should confirm it.
    - The deal is also a TASC merger. Engility holders got 1:1, but they ended with about 48.5% to 49% of New Engility, so this is not a pure holdco reorganization.
- **ticker_history (library `EGL 2012-07-23 .. 2015-02-27`, report `wrong`): refuted as stated.**
  - Fails rows show CUSIP 29285W104 under EGL ending 2015-02-27 (last $46.45), and the new CUSIP 29286C107 under EGL starting 2015-03-04. The library's range matches the old line's own sightings and ends where the old security ended. It is not wrong for this `sec_id`.
  - For a continuation, the new line is the successor's own history, not an extension of this security's. The defect is that the successor line is absent from the run, which the report already lists under `successor`, so it is not a second error in this row's `ticker_history`.
  - Whether OpenFIGI gives New Engility the same composite FIGI (which would extend this `sec_id`) was not checkable here, and the report itself says the new `sec_id` is unknown.
  - Reclassify as a missing successor history, not `wrong`.
