## 9. Verification

Re-checked the 8-K of 2009-01-02 (0001144204-08-071879), the Form 25-NSE of 2009-01-16 (0000876661-09-000078, effective 2009-01-16, "Common Stock (New)", Rule 12d2-2(b)) and the fails rows of CUSIP 74955W307 through the SEC helper.

- **Facts behind the verdict: confirmed.** The 8-K says the NYSE notified RHD on 2008-12-31 under Rule 802.01B (30-day market cap under $25M), trading suspended before the open on 2009-01-02, and the press release says the stock trades on the Pink Sheets from 2009-01-02 "under the symbol RHDC". The 25-NSE is dated and effective 2009-01-16.
- **terms: upheld.** The library's `price_ticker` is RHD with no date. The filing names RHDC as the OTC symbol, and fails rows show 74955W307 under RHD to 2009-01-07 and under RHDC from 2009-01-08 (last RHD row $0.27). `price_date` 2009-01-02 is the session after the last NYSE trade (2008-12-31). The ticker and the date are both wrong or missing.

Once the ending is the NYSE removal, the exchange listing ends 2008-12-31 and the history should clip there. The caveat is that the library's own RHD/RHDC split on 2009-01-08 comes from fails symbols; the report's evidence column ("RHDC OTC to 2010-01-29") mixes OTC trading into a table that tracks exchange listings. The 2008-12-31 end stands; the OTC span is not the history's job.
