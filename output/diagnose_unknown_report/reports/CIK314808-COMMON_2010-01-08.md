# CIK314808-COMMON_2010-01-08 (ESV, Ensco International Inc)

## 1. Verdict

A 1:1 holding-company reorganization: on 2009-12-23 each Ensco International (Delaware) common share became one Ensco plc (UK) American depositary share, still trading on the NYSE as ESV. That is a continuation, not a transfer to nowhere. The library's exit kind (`exchange`), last trade date and zero value are right; the successor link is missing.

## 5. What happened

- The redomestication merger took effect 2009-12-23; each Ensco Delaware share became one ADS representing one Class A ordinary share of Ensco UK, trading on the NYSE as ESV ([SEC 8-K 2009-12-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314808/000095012309072895/d70532e8vk.htm)).
- Ensco UK is the successor issuer under Rule 12g-3 ([SEC 8-K12B 2009-12-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314808/000095012309072898/d70526e8vk12b.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE (2009-12-29): shares exchanged one for one, suspended from trading 2009-12-23 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314808/000087666109000511/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- The library's own fails rows give CUSIP 26874Q100 through 2009-12-22 and G3157S106 for the new line from 2012-05 (cusip_history).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The row came from the continued-filings fallback, resolved from the 8-K12B (`resolved_from_continued_filings`), and labelled an exchange transfer with `successor_unknown`. The successor security (CLASS-A) exists in the run but starts in 2012 because no observation covers 2009-2012, so no link was made. Cause tag: `successor_not_in_run`.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: **value_rule** (library `transfer`, report `continuation`). **Upheld.**

- 8-K 0000950123-09-072895 (Items 2.01/3.01): the merger was effective 12:01 a.m. ET on 2009-12-23. Each Ensco Delaware common share "was converted into the right to receive one American depositary share ... one Class A Ordinary Share" of Ensco UK. The ADSs trade on the NYSE as ESV, the same symbol. No cash leg appears in the exchange (the only "cash" hits are in unrelated indenture guarantee text).
- 8-K12B 0000950123-09-072898: "Pursuant to Rule 12g-3 ... Ensco UK is the successor issuer to Ensco Delaware."
- Form 25-NSE 0000876661-09-000511 (EX-99.25): each share "was exchanged for one (1) American Depositary Share ... on a share for ADS basis", suspended from trading 2009-12-23. The Form 25 is an exchange of the class by operation of law (Rule 12d2-2(a)(3)), effective 2010-01-08. This agrees with the report's last trade date of 2009-12-22.
- Under reference.md (decision 9 and the decision table) a 1:1 holdco reorganization with no cash in the exchange is a continuation with a successor link, so the rule is `continuation` (not an exit, no value), not `transfer`.
- Caveat: the 8-K12B and 8-K both name Ensco UK as the new registrant. The report's tie of the successor to the run's CIK314808-CLASS-A, first seen 2012-05, was not rechecked. It is a `successor` field claim, not a `value_rule` claim.
