A real merger: Flextronics acquired Solectron on 2007-10-01; each share converted, at the holder's election, into $3.89 cash or 0.3450 Flextronics shares (50%-70% of shares in stock, prorated). The library's kind, date and successor are right and its cash leg is right, but it publishes cash only and omits the stock alternative. The row is uncertain only because later stale snapshot observations of SLR (2008+) fall outside the history.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (Form 25-NSE notice and the 2007-06-04 8-K: merger effective 2007-10-01).
- Successor: filing (8-K: holders receive cash or Flextronics shares).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25 notice: suspended from trading 2007-10-02, so the last trade was 2007-10-01).
- Payout rule: filing for the election; but the agreement fixes no single package, only the 50%-70% stock band, so the rule that applies to a given holder is worked out, not stated.
- Terms: filing (8-K: $3.89 or 0.3450 FLEX).
- Not backed: a single default or aggregate package. A filing stating the final proration (the Flextronics merger completion 8-K or press release) would make this verified.

## 5. What happened

- The merger agreement: each share converts to 0.3450 Flextronics shares or $3.89 cash at election, stock between 50% and 70% in aggregate ([SEC 8-K 2007-06-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/835541/000089161807000346/f30897e8vk.htm)).
- The exchange's Form 25-NSE: merger effective 2007-10-01, security suspended from trading 2007-10-02 ([SEC Form 25-NSE 2007-10-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/835541/000087666107000802/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Form 15-12B filed 2007-10-18, common stock holders of record 0 ([SEC Form 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/835541/000103570407000704/d50571e15v12b.htm)).
- Fails rows of CUSIP 834182107 under SLR run to 2007-10-09 only (settlement tail), none after.
- Observations of SLR from 2008-01-16 in observation_map are the caller's stale snapshots (status after_delisting).

Mostly it did not: kind, date and cash are right. It read the election deal as cash only (`reason` cites merger filing 425, `dlret_method` cash_only, terms_source 8K_1.01) and dropped the 0.3450 FLEX alternative and the currency. The uncertainty flag comes from `security_uncertain` (`seeds_outside_history`) and `observed_after_delisting`, caused by stale post-delisting observations. Cause tag: `terms_not_extracted`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish `cash_currency=USD`, and the stock alternative (0.3450, FLEX) with the election note.
- Open: the aggregate proration actually applied (Flextronics completion 8-K); not a market price.
- The security-level uncertainty is the caller's stale snapshots; no library fix needed.
- Golden case: possible (cash-or-stock election, last trade day before suspension), after the proration is confirmed.

## 9. Verification

Fields checked (the report's wrong-field list was blank; the checked fields are its two "missing" rows, value_rule and terms, plus the date and kind they rest on).

- **value_rule (library `cash`, report: election with a stock alternative): upheld.** The 2007-06-04 8-K (0000891618-07-000346) says each Solectron share converts, at the holder's election, into "either, but not a combination of (i) 0.3450 of a share of Flextronics stock, or (ii) a cash payment of $3.89 per share", with stock capped at 70% and cash at 50% in aggregate and prorated. A pure `cash` rule omits the stock alternative. The agreement fixes no single package, so the "inferred" confidence is fair.
- **terms (3.89 with no currency and no ratio; report: 3.89 USD, 0.3450 FLEX): upheld.** The 8-K and the Form 25-NSE notice (0000876661-07-000802) both give $3.89 cash and 0.345 (0.3450 in the 8-K) of a Flextronics ordinary share. The "$" amount is USD. Price ticker FLEX is right (Form 25 names NASDAQ: FLEX).
- **last_trade_date 2007-10-01: upheld.** The Form 25-NSE says the merger became effective October 1, 2007 and the security "was suspended from trading on October 2, 2007", so the last trade was the day before, 2007-10-01.
- **exit_kind merger, continuation false: upheld.** Shares were replaced by cash or another company's stock, a terminal merger.
- **Caveat.** Which leg a holder received is not read from a filing; the final proration is unchecked. This is the report's own open check and does not change the verdict.
