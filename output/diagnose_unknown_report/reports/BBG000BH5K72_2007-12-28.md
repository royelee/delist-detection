## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (Form 25-NSE notice, 2007-12-18: merger effective 2007-12-13, each share converted into $60.00 cash).
- Successor: filing (same notice; cash, no successor security).
- Last trade date: worked out from the merger effective date 2007-12-13 in the Form 25 notice; no filing states the last trading day.
- Payout rule: filing (DEFM14A 2007-11-07 and the Form 25 notice).
- Terms: filing (DEFM14A: $60.00 cash per share of common or Class B; the unit election is optional).
Not backed: the exact last trade day. An exchange print (MIDAS does not cover 2007) or a filing saying "ceased trading at the close on D" would make it verified.

## 5. What happened

- DEFM14A: holders receive $60.00 cash per share unless they make the unit election ([SEC DEFM14A 2007-11-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29924/000119312507237942/ddefm14a.htm)).
- The merger with Diamond Merger Sub, a News Corporation subsidiary, became effective 2007-12-13; each share converted into $60.00 cash ([SEC 25-NSE 2007-12-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29924/000087666107000947/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Dow Jones filed Form 15-12B on 2007-12-18 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/29924/000089534507000712/pr1512b_dowjones.htm)).
