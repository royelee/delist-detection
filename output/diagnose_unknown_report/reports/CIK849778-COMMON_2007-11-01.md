## 5. What happened

- At the effective time of the merger on 2007-10-22, Cytyc merged into Nor'easter and each Cytyc share was converted into the right to receive 0.52 of a Hologic share and $16.50 cash without interest ([SEC 425/8-K 2007-10-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/849778/000119312507222835/d8k.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE filed 2007-10-22 for the common stock ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/849778/000135445707000322/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)); its effective date is 10 days later, 2007-11-01, which the library used as `delist_date`.
- Form 15-12G filed 2007-10-22 ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/849778/000119312507222916/d1512g.htm)).
- Fails-to-deliver rows for CUSIP 232946103 under CYTC end 2007-10-23 (SEC fails data, via `sec.py fails`).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the share was exchanged for cash plus another company's stock, a merger and not a continuation.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Its terms came from an 8-K item 1.01 read (`terms_source=8K_1.01`) that caught only the cash leg; the stock leg and acquirer were not found (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_price`). With a failed gate and no last close the DLRET is blank (`needs_last_trade`, flags `no_last_trade_date`, `no_last_close`). The last trade date was never found because the Form 25 notice text gives none and MIDAS starts in 2012. Cause tag: `terms_misread` (a missing stock leg).

## 8. Fix and open checks

- Publish value_rule cash_plus_stock with 16.50 USD, 0.52 and price ticker HOLX, price date 2007-10-23.
- Publish last_trade_date 2007-10-22 if the fails-row rule is accepted for pre-2012 dates; otherwise confirm it from the Nasdaq notice.
- The ticker_history end (2007-11-01) and the seeds after 2008-01-16 (`after_unconfirmed_delisting`) follow from the missing last trade date; they should clip at 2007-10-22.
- Golden case: yes, a pre-2012 cash-and-stock merger with the cash leg only extracted.

## 9. Verification

- **value_rule (upheld).** The 8-K/425 of 2007-10-22 (0001193125-07-222835, item 2.01) says each Cytyc share was converted into "0.52 of a share of Hologic common stock and $16.50 in cash without interest". There is no election or proration, so the rule is cash_plus_stock. The library's `cash` is wrong. The security is the common stock, so the class is right.
- **terms (upheld).** Cash 16.50 and ratio 0.52 match the filing. The library has 16.50 only, with no ratio, ticker or price date, and the currency is blank. The `price_date` 2007-10-23 is the report's own inference. The last fails row for CUSIP 232946103 is dated 2007-10-23 (78 rows, 20070109..20071023, last price $50.41). The price date is therefore the day after the last trade only if the last trade was 2007-10-22. The 8-K shows the merger closed 2007-10-22, but it does not say whether trading ended on 10-19 or 10-22, so the price date is probably 10-23 but not confirmed. The finding that the terms are wrong stands.
- **ticker_history (upheld on direction, uncertain on the exact date).** The library ends CYTC on 2007-11-01, the Form 25 effective date. The merger took effect on 2007-10-22 and the last fails row is 2007-10-23, so the line cannot have traded to 11-01. The end date is too late. The proposed 2007-10-22 is inferred, and 2007-10-19 is possible if the conversion came before the open on 10-22. No exchange print was found. The decision-12 rule keeps `last_trade_date` blank without an exchange print, so the clip date is an open check.
