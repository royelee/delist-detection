  "equivalent to 0.5409" Charter shares, or at election $115 plus 0.4562 equivalent
  ([SEC 8-K 2016-05-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1377013/000095014216003754/eh1600620_8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE: Option A $100 + 0.48908178 New Charter share (= 0.5409 old), Option B $115 + 0.41249604; no election

  ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1377013/000087666116000980/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Charter: actual shares = the stated ratios x 0.9042 (the parent merger ratio); New Charter began trading on Nasdaq as CHTR
  ([SEC 8-K Charter 2016-05-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1091667/000119312516593941/)).
- Fails rows under TWC (CUSIP 88732J207) end 2016-05-11 at $216.12 (the library's close, fails-based).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The LLM read the "equivalent to 0.5409 Charter" figure instead of the 0.48908178 New Charter shares actually issued; the
0.9042 adjustment is stated in the Form 25 notice and Charter's 8-K. The gate failed (`llm_gate_failed`), so the
value fell back to assumed par (`merger_at_par`, `assumed_par_after_failed_gate`). Cash currency is blank. Cause tag: `terms_misread`.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: terms (the report calls the library wrong on stock ratio and currency). Result: upheld.

- Stock ratio. The Form 25-NSE notice (accession 0000876661-16-000980) states Option A as $100 cash plus 0.48908178 of a share of (New) Charter Class A "(equivalent to 0.5409 of a share of Charter ... currently outstanding Class A Common Stock)", with no election meaning Option A. Charter's 8-K (accession 0000950142-16-003754, Item 2.01) states only the "equivalent to 0.5409" figure, so 0.5409 is an old-Charter equivalent, not the shares delivered. The library's 0.540900 (source.csv, terms_source=llm, terms_gate=failed) is therefore the equivalent. The report's 0.48908178 is the filing's issued-share count, and the CHTR price from 2016-05-18 is that of New Charter, so the issued count is the right multiplier.
- Currency. source.csv has cash_currency blank; both filings state dollars ($100 / $100.00), so USD is correct.
- Cash 100.00, price_ticker CHTR, price_date 2016-05-18 (the session after the 2016-05-17 last trade), value_rule cash_plus_stock, and the election-deal default (Option A) all match the filings.
- Not rechecked (agreed by the report, not disputed): exit kind, last trade date, issuer.
- Residual uncertainty: none material. The report's 0.9042 parent-ratio claim was not needed, since the notice gives 0.48908178 directly.
