## 5. What happened

- Liberty Media's split-off of Liberty SiriusXM, then at 6:00 pm on 2024-09-09 a New Sirius subsidiary merged into Old Sirius; each Old Sirius share became 0.1 New Sirius share, cash for fractions; Old Sirius became "Sirius XM Inc." and New Sirius "Sirius XM Holdings Inc." ([SEC 8-K12B 2024-09-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/908937/000110465924098260/tm2423473d2_8k12b.htm)).
- New Sirius is successor issuer to Old Sirius, keeping its CIK and file number; its stock begins trading on Nasdaq as SIRI on 2024-09-10 (same 8-K12B).
- Old Sirius filed a Form 15-12G on 2024-09-10 for the old registration ([SEC 15-12G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/908937/000110465924098268/tm2423473d1_1512g.htm)); no Form 25 exists.
- The CUSIP changed from 82968B103 to 829933100 (cusip_history; fails rows).
