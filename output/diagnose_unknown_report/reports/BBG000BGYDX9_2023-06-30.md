# DBD (Diebold Nixdorf) 2023: bankruptcy drop to OTC

## 1. Verdict

A real ending: Diebold filed a prepackaged Chapter 11 on 2023-06-01, the NYSE suspended trading on 2023-06-02 and delisted the shares (Form 25-NSE, effective at the open on 2023-07-03), the shares traded on OTC Pink as DBDQQ from 2023-06-05, and the old equity was cancelled with no recovery on 2023-08-11. The library's kind (dropped / bankruptcy) and rule (otc_print) are right; its last trade date (2023-05-26, MIDAS) and price terms (symbol DBD, date 2023-05-30) look wrong.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2023-06-05 item 3.01; Form 25-NSE cites 802.01D after the Chapter 11 filing).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2023-08-11: Old Common Stock extinguished; 25-NSE: holders get no recovery).
- Last trade date: worked out from "indefinitely suspended trading on June 2"; the filing gives no exact last print and MIDAS says 2023-05-26.
- Payout rule: filing (OTC Pink trading under DBDQQ from 2023-06-05).
- Terms: filing for symbol and OTC start date; the first print itself is not read.
Not backed: the exact last NYSE trading day. An exchange print or a filing stating the last day would make it verified. Also the web was not searched, and the MIDAS 2023-05-26 date was not reconciled.

## 9. Verification

Checked by re-reading the 8-K 2023-06-05 and the 25-NSE through sec.py, the cached fails rows (cnsfails202305a/b, 202306a), and one web search. Result: all three wrong fields upheld; the corrected last trade date remains unconfirmed.

- **last_trade_date: upheld (library wrong), corrected value still unconfirmed.** The 8-K says only that "the NYSE also indefinitely suspended trading ... on June 2, 2023" ([SEC 8-K 2023-06-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/28823/000002882323000123/dbd-20230602.htm)); the 25-NSE says that "On June 02, 2023, the Exchange determined that the Common Shares ... should be suspended from trading" ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/28823/000087666123000511/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)). Neither says "before the open", so 2023-06-01 is a worked-out value, and 2023-06-02 cannot be ruled out. A web search ([press release 2023-06-02](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230602005336/en)) repeats the same wording and states no last day. The library's MIDAS date 2023-05-26 is hard to square with the filings: the company disclosed its Chapter 11 petitions on 2023-05-30 and 2023-06-01, and the stock was listed until the suspension. The fails rows under DBD continue daily through 2023-06-05 (volumes of 5,495 on 06-01 and 4,100 on 06-02), but their price is a flat $0.25 from the 2023-05-26 row on, so they are stale-priced and do not date the last print either. Either 06-01 or 06-02 would make 05-26 wrong; the exact day needs an exchange print.
- **terms: upheld.** The 8-K quoted above gives the OTC symbol "DBDQQ" and "Effective as of June 5, 2023" for the start of OTC Pink trading. The library's price_ticker DBD (the NYSE symbol, which stopped trading) and price_date 2023-05-30 (a day before the suspension, not the session after the last trade) do not fit the otc_print rule in reference.md. Fails rows corroborate the symbol: DBDQQ appears from the 2023-06-06 row (dated D, priced at the D-1 close, i.e. the 06-05 session) at $0.02. No market price was used.
- **ticker_history: upheld, with the same caveat.** The library ends DBD at 2023-05-26, which follows the MIDAS date. Because the NYSE listing ran until the suspension on 2023-06-02, the end is 2023-06-01 or 2023-06-02, not 05-26. The report's 2023-06-01 is the likelier reading of "suspended on June 2" but is not confirmed.
- **Not challenged:** kind dropped / bankruptcy, rule otc_print and continuation false are confirmed by the 25-NSE (802.01D after the Chapter 11 filings; holders receive no recovery).
