# AWH (Allied World Assurance Company Holdings, AG), delisting 2017-07-27

## 1. Verdict

A real merger: Fairfax bought AWH through an exchange offer (closed 2017-07-06), then a Swiss squeeze-out merger for the remaining 3.9%. Each share became $23.00 cash plus 0.057937 Fairfax subordinate voting shares, and a separate $5.00 special dividend was paid to holders on 2017-07-06. The library's kind is right but its terms are incomplete (cash leg only, no stock leg, no currency) and it has no last trade date.

## 5. What happened

- Fairfax Financial Holdings (Switzerland) GmbH completed the exchange offer; Acceptance Time 2017-07-06; 96.1% tendered; $23.00 cash plus 0.057937 Fairfax subordinate voting share per share; $5.00 special dividend paid to tendering holders and holders of record at the 2017-07-06 close ([SEC 8-K 2017-07-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163348/000110465917044656/a17-17893_18k.htm)).
- The company notified NYSE on 2017-07-06 and said it would file Form 25 on 2017-07-17 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- Form 25 filed 2017-07-17 by the issuer under 12d2-2(c) ([SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163348/000110465917045357/a17-17972_125.htm)); Form 15-12B filed 2017-08-07 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163348/000110465917049916/a17-18926_11512b.htm)).
- Non-tendering holders are cashed out in a Swiss squeeze-out merger; tendering holders got $28.00 cash in total plus 0.057937 Fairfax share ([SEC DEFM14A 2017-08-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163348/000104746917004976/a2232886zdefm14a.htm)).
- The remaining 5.4% were expected to be acquired in Q3 2017 for about $109M cash and $120M of Fairfax shares ([SEC 425 2017-08-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163348/000110465917050270/a17-19905_1425.htm)).
- Fails rows of CUSIP H01531104: symbol AWH 2017-07-13..2017-08-09, then AWHHF 2017-08-10..2017-08-23 (fails data).

## 9. Verification

Skeptic check of the two fields the report calls wrong (value_rule, terms). Re-read the 8-K, the DEFM14A and the Form 25 through the skill's sec.py helper.

- **value_rule (library `cash`, report `cash_plus_stock`): upheld.** The 8-K of 2017-07-12 (accession 0001104659-17-044656, Introductory Note) says each tendered Share received "(i) cash consideration of $23.00, without interest and (ii) 0.057937 of a ... subordinate voting share of Fairfax". The DEFM14A (0001047469-17-004976) defines the squeeze-out "Merger Consideration" as equal to the Offer Consideration. The class is common (Form 25: "Common Shares, par value CHF 4.10"). A stock leg exists, so `cash` is incomplete and `cash_plus_stock` is right.
- **terms (library: 23.00 cash, no currency, no stock leg; report: 23.00 USD + 0.057937 FFH): upheld.** Both numbers match the 8-K and the DEFM14A; the filings state dollars; FFH is the acquirer named in the 8-K. Caveat on the $5.00 special dividend: the report treats it as separate, and that holds on the filings. The 8-K says the Company paid it to all holders of record at the 2017-07-06 close, and the DEFM14A says it was "paid outside of the Offer but was conditioned upon completion of the Offer", with the Merger Consideration equal to the Offer Consideration (cash and Fairfax shares only). The DEFM14A does add it to the cash as "a total of $28.00 in cash and 0.057937 of a Fairfax share", so a reader could argue for 28.00. The reference's exception (documents make the payment part of the consideration for the old shares) is not met, because the company pays it outside the Offer. Upheld with moderate confidence; the report already lists it under open checks.
- **Not disputed, also confirmed:** the 8-K gives no last trade date (Item 3.01 says only that NYSE was notified on 2017-07-06). The Form 25 was filed 2017-07-17; the delisting takes effect 10 days later (2017-07-27) under 12d2-2(d). No exchange print was found, so `last_trade_date` stays blank.
