## 9. Verification

Re-opened the cited filings through sec.py (cache, then live). All four disputed fields are upheld.

- **exit_kind (upheld).** Form 25-NSE 0000876661-18-001112 (filed and effective 2018-10-22, NYSE, class "Common Stock", Commission File 001-05418) states the merger of SUPERVALU INC. and Jedi Merger Sub, a wholly owned subsidiary of United Natural Foods, became effective before the open on 2018-10-22 and each common share was converted into USD 32.50 in cash. Cash for the old share is a merger, not an exchange transfer or continuation (reference.md, decision 9). The 8-K of 2018-10-22 (0001104659-18-063194, items 1.02, 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01, 5.02, 5.03, 8.01, 9.01) and the Form 15-12B of 2018-11-06 agree, and a stream of S-8 POS and POS AM deregistrations the same day. The library's 2017-08-02 exchange row cannot stand: the issuer filed 10-Qs, a DEFM14A (2018-09-18) and a 10-Q on 2018-10-15, so it was still listed and trading in 2018.
- **last_trade_date (upheld).** The notice says the security was suspended from trading on 2018-10-22; 2018-10-22 was a Monday, so the last trade was Friday 2018-10-19 (decision 12). That is not after the Form 25 effective date. Fails rows for symbol SVU, CUSIP 868536301, run 2018-09-06 to 2018-10-23, last row USD 32.49, consistent with trading at the deal price until the end. The library's blank date (internal 2017-08-02, unconfirmed) is wrong.
- **value_rule (upheld).** Cash USD 32.50 per share, no stock leg, no election (same notice). The library's "transfer" is wrong. The terms (cash 32.50 USD, currency stated by the filing) are missing from the library, as the report says.
- **ticker_history (upheld).** output/ticker_history.csv has SVU 2008-01-02..2017-08-02 (source observation) and output/cusip_history.csv only 868536103 (to 2017-08-02). The fails rows show SVU on the new CUSIP 868536301 to 2018-10-23, so the history should run to 2018-10-19 and the new CUSIP is absent. The report's reverse-split explanation (cause of the CUSIP change) is "probably" and was not independently confirmed; it does not affect the corrected fields.
- **Agreed fields (spot check).** continuation false and successor none hold: holders received cash, UNFI's merger sub is the acquirer. Issuer CIK 95521 matches the Form 25 filer.

Result: upheld on exit_kind, last_trade_date, value_rule, ticker_history. Nothing refuted.
