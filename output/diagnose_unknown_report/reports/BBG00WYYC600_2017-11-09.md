## 1. Verdict

Not a delisting: a 1-for-4 reverse stock split on 2017-11-08 gave the same common stock a new CUSIP, and it kept trading on the NYSE as WLL. ## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2017-11-09, item 8.01: 1-for-4 reverse split, stock continues on the NYSE as WLL).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: new CUSIP 966387 409, same ticker, same issuer); matches the library's cusip_history.
- Last trade date: filing (certificate effective 5:00 p.m. ET on 2017-11-08, new CUSIP at the 2017-11-09 open, so the old CUSIP's last day is 2017-11-08).
- Payout rule: filing (no consideration, automatic combination of every four shares into one).
- Terms: filing (ratio 4:1 combination, cash only in lieu of fractions).

## 5. What happened

- Stockholders approved a reverse split at a special meeting; the board set it at 1-for-4 and authorized shares fell from 600,000,000 to 225,000,000 ([SEC 8-K 2017-11-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1255474/000119312517337932/d491660d8k.htm)).
- The certificate became effective at 5:00 p.m. ET on 2017-11-08; every four shares were combined into one, with cash for fractions (same 8-K).
- At the open on 2017-11-09 the stock continued on the NYSE as WLL under new CUSIP 966387 409, on a split-adjusted basis (same 8-K).
- No Form 25 exists for the event; the library's fails rows show CUSIP 966387102 ending 2017-11-09 and 966387409 starting 2017-11-09 (output/cusip_history.csv).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Kept the same security: same issuer, same ticker, same exchange; only the CUSIP and the share count changed. Not a merger, not a drop.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder found no Form 25 and fell back to the continued-filings rule (`continued_filings_rule`, reason "Continued 10-K/Q filings >180d after delist", flags `no_form25;delist_date_approx;last_trade_date_unconfirmed`), so a CUSIP switch read as an exchange-transfer ending. Its successor link is right. Cause tag: `other:reverse_split_cusip_change_read_as_ending`.
