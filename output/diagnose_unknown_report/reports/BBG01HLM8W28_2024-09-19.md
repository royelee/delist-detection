## 1. Verdict

A real merger-type exchange: on 2024-09-09 Liberty Media redeemed each Liberty SiriusXM share for 0.8375 share of New Sirius XM common stock (the split-off, then New Sirius merged with old Sirius XM). The library's kind, date and ratio are right; it lacks the price security (SIRI) and fills assumed par, so the row is uncertain only for want of the acquirer ticker.

- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2024-09-10 item 2.01).
- Successor: filing (same 8-K: shares exchanged for New Sirius stock).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K item 3.01: delisting requested effective 2024-09-09; Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2024-09-09).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K item 2.01: 0.8375 New Sirius share, cash in lieu of fractions).

New Sirius's 8-K12B of 2024-09-10 (filed under CIK 908937, [0001104659-24-098260](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/908937/000110465924098260/tm2423473d2_8k12b.htm)) states the symbol and the price date: "New Sirius common stock is expected to begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol 'SIRI' on September 10, 2024". The price security BBG01KJQM3Y8 is a look-up in the library's tables, by its CUSIP 829933100 from 2024-09-10.

## 5. What happened

- Split-off effective 2024-09-09 4:05 p.m. ET: each Liberty SiriusXM share redeemed for 0.8375 New Sirius share, cash for fractions; at 6:00 p.m. a New Sirius subsidiary merged into Sirius XM ([SEC 8-K 2024-09-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000110465924098251/tm2422514d16_8k.htm)).
- Liberty asked Nasdaq to delist LSXMA/LSXMB/LSXMK effective 2024-09-09 (same 8-K, item 3.01).
- Form 25-NSE filed 2024-09-09 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000135445724000664/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Liberty Media (CIK 1560385) kept filing as FWONA/LLYVA issuer ([SEC 8-K 2024-09-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000110465924101538/tm2424432d1_8k.htm)), which is why the library routed it through the continued-filings branch.
- New Sirius's 8-K12B: its stock "is expected to begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol 'SIRI' on September 10, 2024" ([SEC 8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/908937/000110465924098260/tm2423473d2_8k12b.htm)).
- SEC fails data:
  - The new CUSIP 829933100's first row, 2024-09-10, is a placeholder: SIRIZZZZ, $0.01. The row dated 2024-09-11, under SIRI, carries the 2024-09-10 close of $27.38.
  - Old Sirius XM's CUSIP 82968B103 keeps rows under SIRI to 2024-09-10 ($2.67), then under SIRIXXXX.
  - The library's ticker_history gives SIRI to old Sirius (BBG000BT0093) through 2024-09-10, and to New Sirius (BBG01KJQM3Y8) only from 2024-09-11.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: holders received another company's stock (New Sirius, which also absorbed old Sirius XM); not a 1:1 same-claim reorganization of the same holders, and the tracking stock ceased to exist.

Mostly right. The end-of-era resolver relabelled the ending a merger (`resolved_from_continued_filings`) and the LLM terms read 0.8375 but no acquirer ticker (`terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker`), so it assumed par (`merger_at_par`). Cause tag: `terms_not_extracted`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

Steps 2 and 3 of the CAL fix (BBG000BDXVW8_2010-10-14) apply. The LLM read no acquirer ticker (`no_acq_ticker`), so the lookup must start from the acquirer's name.
- **Find the acquirer.** The 8-K12B is filed under CIK 908937, and the run lists both Sirius lines under that CIK:
  - old Sirius, BBG000BT0093, CUSIP 82968B103 to 2024-09-10;
  - New Sirius, BBG01KJQM3Y8, CUSIP 829933100 from 2024-09-10.

  So "the issuer's common line on the price date" has two answers on 2024-09-10. Take the line whose CUSIP begins at the closing: BBG01KJQM3Y8.
- **Publish and price by the security.**
  - Publish price_sec_id BBG01KJQM3Y8 and price_ticker SIRI.
  - Do not pick the line by ticker_history on the price date. It gives SIRI on 2024-09-10 to old Sirius (BBG000BT0093), the wrong security, because the new line's SIRI history starts a day late. Old Sirius's last close was $2.67, and 0.8375 × $2.67 would fail the gate.
  - The gate prices CUSIP 829933100's 2024-09-10 close, $27.38, from the row dated 2024-09-11. Skip the SIRIZZZZ $0.01 placeholder.
  - Then 0.8375 × $27.38 = $22.93 against LSXMA's last close of $22.29, a 2.9% gap, so the gate passes and `assumed_par_after_failed_gate` goes away.
  - `resolved_from_continued_filings` still keeps the row in uncertain.csv.

Golden case: yes, a tracking-stock split-off into the acquirer's new shares with a missing acquirer ticker.

## 9. Verification

Field checked: `terms` (price_ticker), the only field the report calls wrong or missing. Verdict: **upheld**.

- Ratio 0.8375 and fraction cash only: confirmed in [SEC 8-K 2024-09-10, item 2.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1560385/000110465924098251/tm2422514d16_8k.htm): Liberty Media redeemed each Liberty SiriusXM share for 0.8375 of a New Sirius common share, cash in lieu of fractions, at 4:05 p.m. on 2024-09-09. The merger of old Sirius XM into New Sirius followed at 6:00 p.m. The class is the Series A (LSXMA), the filing's item 3.01 names LSXMA, LSXMB and LSXMK for delisting effective 2024-09-09. This agrees with the library's last trade date 2024-09-09 and ratio.
- Price security: the library's `price_ticker` is blank. The symbol SIRI is stated in a filing: the 2024-09-10 [8-K of Sirius XM Holdings Inc.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/908937/000110465924098372/tm2423473d6_8k.htm) and the [8-K12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/908937/000110465924098260/tm2423473d2_8k12b.htm) cover pages list "Common Stock, $0.001 par, SIRI, The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC". Item 2.01 of the Liberty 8-K says New Sirius was renamed "Sirius XM Holdings Inc." at the merger, so the SIRI shares are the New Sirius shares paid out. This weakens the report's section 4 note that SIRI is only "worked out": it is read from a filing (cover page), though no filing states the first trading day.
- Rule: stock, not cash plus stock, is right (cash only for fractions). The library's `assumed_par` fill after `terms_gate_failed:no_acq_ticker` is a consequence of the missing ticker, as the report says.
- Not refuted: price_date 2024-09-10 (day after last trade, per the reference rule). Remaining caveat: first trading day of the post-merger SIRI shares was not read from a filing, a minor open check. Confidence `inferred` could be raised toward `verified` on the ticker line.
