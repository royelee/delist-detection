# GPOR (Gulfport Energy, BBG000BG09F5) delisting 2021-02-12

## 1. Verdict

A Chapter 11 drop: Nasdaq delisted the common stock on 2020-11-27 after the 2020-11-13 filing; it then traded OTC as GPORQ and was cancelled without distribution at emergence in May 2021. The library's kind, date and rule are right; the only wrong term is the OTC price symbol, which it publishes as GPOR (the Nasdaq symbol, later reused by the new stock).

## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2020-11-16 item 1.03; 8-K 2020-11-20 item 3.01 Nasdaq Rule 5101 delisting).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2021-05-17: Existing Equity Interests cancelled without any distribution).
- Last trade date: filing (8-K 2020-11-20: delisted at the opening of 2020-11-27; the trading day before, 11-26, was Thanksgiving, so 11-25).
- Payout rule: filing (decision 11, move to OTC after a bankruptcy delisting; the 8-K 2021-05-17 confirms no later recovery).
- Terms: otc_print needs no cash or ratio; the symbol GPORQ rests on fails rows (CUSIP 402635304 under GPORQ from 2020-11-30), which is only the symbol, not a price.

## 5. What happened

- Chapter 11 petitions filed 2020-11-13 ([SEC 8-K 2020-11-16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874499/000121390020037077/ea129804-8k_gulfport.htm)).
- Nasdaq notified delisting at the open of 2020-11-27 under Rules 5101, 5110(b), IM-5101-1 ([SEC 8-K 2020-11-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874499/000121390020038501/ea129925-8k_gulfport.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE filed 2021-02-02, effective 2021-02-12 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874499/000135445721000141/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Same CUSIP 402635304 traded as GPORQ 2020-11-30 to 2021-05-28 (SEC fails rows); the new stock, CUSIP 402635502, appears as GPOR from 2021-05-20.
- Plan effective 2021-05-17: all Existing Equity Interests (Class 8) cancelled without distribution ([SEC 8-K 2021-05-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874499/000121390021027082/ea140991-8k_gulfport.htm)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy then OTC. Post-bankruptcy equity is a new security, not a continuation.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: terms

8-K 2020-11-20 (0001213900-20-038501) says Nasdaq delists at the open of 2020-11-27 and the stock is expected to trade on OTC Pink from 2020-11-30. Fails rows for CUSIP 402635304 show GPOR through 20201127 and GPORQ from 20201130 to 20210610. The 8-K of 2021-05-17 confirms the equity was cancelled without distribution. So the library's price_ticker GPOR is the Nasdaq symbol and the security's own OTC symbol is GPORQ. The symbol rests on fails rows, not an exchange print. Other fields were not disputed and not re-tested.
