# SPB (Spectrum Brands Holdings, old line, BBG000P4BQM9), case BBG000P4BQM9_2018-07-16

## 1. Verdict

On 2018-07-13 the old Spectrum Brands Holdings (renamed Spectrum Brands Legacy, CIK 1487730) merged into its majority owner HRG Group, and each old SPB share became one HRG share (HRG was renamed Spectrum Brands Holdings and took the ticker SPB on 2018-07-16). That is a stock-for-stock merger into another issuer, not a same-issuer continuation. The library's last trade date (2018-07-16) is wrong; the true last trade is 2018-07-13. Its exit kind (`exchange`, continuation) is arguable and I judge it wrong (see section 6).

## 5. What happened

- On 2018-07-13 Merger Sub I merged into old SPB, which survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of HRG; each old SPB share (other than those held by HRG) became the right to receive one HRG share ([SEC 8-K 2018-07-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1487730/000119312518217952/d487110d8k.htm)).
- Old SPB shares ceased trading on and were delisted from the NYSE at the closing; HRG common commences trading as SPB on 2018-07-16 (same 8-K).
- Immediately before the merger HRG did a reverse split of about 0.16125 (cash only for fractions) and the HRG shares were renamed Spectrum Brands Holdings ([SEC HRG 8-K 2018-07-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109177/000119312518217953/d497639d8k.htm)).
- NYSE Form 25-NSE for Spectrum Brands Legacy, Inc., filed and effective 2018-07-16 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1487730/000087666118000794/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Old SPB filed Form 15-12B on 2018-07-26 ([SEC 15-12B](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1487730/000119312518227315/d570256d1512b.htm)).
- The library's own cusip_history shows the old CUSIP 84763R101 ending 2018-07-16 and the new line under CUSIP 84790A105 starting 2018-07-16: a CUSIP change, not a rename.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced, as a merger. Holders got stock of another issuer (HRG, a different CIK and CUSIP) with no cash in the exchange. Only decision 9's "same claim, one for one" test points to a continuation. The old SPB was a majority-owned subsidiary and the HRG reverse split was sized so a share is worth about the same, so the return will be near zero; but this is a parent absorbing a subsidiary, not a 1:1 holdco reorganization of one company. Operator judgment: if the spec treats this as a continuation, the successor link is right and only the dates need fixing.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The finder found no Form 25 for the old line (`no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `last_trade_date_unconfirmed`; the 25-NSE sits under CIK 1487730, "Spectrum Brands Legacy, Inc."), so it used the end of fails rows (2018-07-16) and linked HRG's line by timing (`handoff_continuation`, `continuation_by_timing_only`). The internal `library_cik` 109177 is the successor's (`resolved_by_current_ticker_map`), not the issuer 1487730. Cause tag: `wrong_exit_kind`.

## 9. Verification

Re-checked against the old SPB 8-K (CIK 1487730, 0001193125-18-217952), the HRG 8-K (CIK 109177, 0001193125-18-217953) and the filing list for CIK 1487730 (25-NSE 2018-07-16, 15-12B 2018-07-26).

- exit_kind: upheld. The 8-K Item 2.01 says Merger Sub I merged into old SPB, which survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of HRG; each share was "converted into the right to receive one share" of HRG common. HRG is a different issuer (CIK 109177) and the CUSIP changes, so the holders did not keep the same claim in the same company (decision 9). This is a judgment call on a 1:1 parent/subsidiary stock merger, and the report flags it as an open check; the filings support `merger`.
- continuation / successor: upheld. Same grounds. HRG's line BBG000DS5588 is the acquirer and the price security, not a continuation successor. The library's link rests on timing only (`continuation_by_timing_only`).
- last_trade_date: upheld, 2018-07-13. The 8-K says old SPB shares "ceased trading on, and were delisted from, the NYSE" at the closing on July 13, and HRG common "will commence trading ... as SPB on July 16, 2018". The 25-NSE is effective 2018-07-16, so 07-13 is within decision 12. The library's 2018-07-16 is only a fails-row guess.
- value_rule / terms: upheld. Stock rule, stock_ratio 1.0, no cash in the exchange. The HRG 8-K confirms the 0.16125 reverse split was applied to HRG shares before the merger, with cash only in lieu of fractions, so the ratio is on post-split HRG shares and 1.0 is right. price_date 2018-07-16 is the first session after the last trade.
- ticker_history: upheld. Old SPB ends 2018-07-13 and HRG common trades as SPB from 2018-07-16 (library says 07-16 and 07-17).

Overall: all five fields upheld. The only softness is the exit_kind/continuation classification, which is operator policy, not a factual dispute.
