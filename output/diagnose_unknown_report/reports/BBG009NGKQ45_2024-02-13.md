## 9. Verification

Checked by a skeptic against the filings (cache and live SEC through sec.py). All four fields the report calls wrong are upheld.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** The 8-K of 2024-02-14 (item 5.03, 0000950170-24-014928) states a 1-for-80 reverse split effective 5:00 p.m. ET 2024-02-13 under the same Nasdaq Global Select listing and ticker VRM: the same security, so 2024-02-13 is not an ending. The 8-K of 2024-11-26 (item 3.01, 0001193125-24-266124) says Nasdaq determined to delist the common stock after the 2024-11-13 Chapter 11 filing, that trading is suspended at the opening of business on 2024-12-02, and that a Form 25-NSE will be filed. That is a loss by bankruptcy, `dropped`. Same class throughout (common stock, par $0.001). The 8-K of 2025-01-15 (0000950170-25-005647) says all old equity was cancelled and extinguished; the New Common Stock is a new security (convertible noteholders got 92.94%).
- **last_trade_date 2024-11-29: upheld.** "Suspended at the opening of business on 2024-12-02" means the last trade was the preceding trading day (decision 12). 2024-11-28 was Thanksgiving and 2024-11-29 a shortened trading day, so 2024-11-29 is right. Fails rows for 92918V208 show VRM through 2024-12-02 and VRMMQ from 2024-12-03; they lag a day and do not contradict this. Caveat: the issuer's filing list through 2025-03 shows no Form 25 or 25-NSE, so the Form 25 effective date could not be checked. The date rests on the 8-K, as the report says.
- **value_rule otc_print: upheld.** The 8-K cover of 2025-01-15 lists VRMMQ on OTC Markets, and fails rows show 13 rows under VRMMQ from 2024-12-03 to 2025-01-15. Decision 11 and the decision table's "Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC" row apply; `worthless` does not fit, since the stock traded after the delisting. Caveat: `price_date` 2024-12-02 is the session after the last trade only if trading was on the exchange until 2024-11-29; the fails rows show VRMMQ from 2024-12-03 (a one-day lag is possible), so the first OTC session is unconfirmed, as the open checks say. The rule stands.
- **ticker_history: upheld.** The library's end of 2024-02-13 is the reverse split, not the end of the listing. Fails rows at CUSIP 92918V208 under VRM from 2024-11 to 2024-12-02 show the listing continued. OTC trading under VRMMQ is not an exchange listing.

Confidence `inferred` stands. Not independently checked: the first VRMMQ print and whether a Form 25-NSE was filed.
