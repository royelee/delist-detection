## 5. What happened

- IndyMac Bank, F.S.B. closed by OTS, FDIC receiver, 2008-07-11; NYSE suspended the common stock and WIRES units on 2008-07-14, effective immediately, noting the 07-11 close of $0.28 ([SEC 8-K 2008-07-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/773468/000106823808000736/indymac_8-k.htm)).
- Parent announced a Chapter 7 petition on 2008-07-31 ([SEC 8-K 2008-08-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/773468/000106823808000780/indymac_8-k.htm)).
- NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2008-08-07, effective 2008-08-17, citing abnormally low price; suspension 07-14 ([SEC Form 25 notice](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/876661/000087666108000315/ruleprovisionnotice.htm)).
- Fails rows, CUSIP 456607100: IMB to 2008-07-17, IDMC 2008-07-18 to 10-21, IDMCQ from 2008-10-22 (SEC fails-to-deliver data).
- The same CUSIP kept trading after the Form 25, so holders kept the security as an OTC line.

## 9. Verification

Re-opened the 8-K of 2008-07-15, the Form 25-NSE notice and the fails rows of CUSIP 456607100.

- **last_trade_date: uncertain (the library's 07-14 is not supported; 07-11 is likely but unconfirmed).** The 8-K says NYSE notified the company on 2008-07-14 that listing was suspended "effective immediately", citing the 07-11 close of $0.28 ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/773468/000106823808000736/indymac_8-k.htm)). The Form 25 notice says the exchange "on July 14, 2008, determined that the Securities should be suspended from trading immediately" and announced it "immediately and at the close of the trading session on July 14" ([SEC Form 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/876661/000087666108000315/ruleprovisionnotice.htm)). No filing says the stock traded on 07-14, and none says it did not. The closing price cited is 07-11's, which favours 07-11 as the last print. A web search found nothing more precise. The report's own section 4 already concedes this, so "wrong" in section 2 is too strong: 07-11 is the better estimate, not a verified date.
- **terms (price_ticker IDMC, price_date 2008-07-14): upheld only conditionally.** The fails rows confirm the same CUSIP trades as IMB to 2008-07-17 and as IDMC from 2008-07-18 (rows are settlement-lagged, so this does not date the first print). IDMC as the OTC symbol is consistent with the reference ("the security's own OTC symbol if known"). The price_date moves with the last trade date: it is 07-14 only if the last exchange trade was 07-11. The library's 07-15 is consistent with its own 07-14 last trade.
- **ticker_history end: upheld conditionally.** It should follow the last trade date, so it is 07-11 only if that date holds. The library's 07-14 end is the Form 25 suspension date, not a print.
- **drop_reason (not in the list checked, noted for completeness): upheld that sec_order/573 is wrong.** The Form 25 is the NYSE's under Rule 12d2-2(b); the 2011 revocation is unrelated. The NYSE's stated grounds are the bank's seizure and the abnormally low price. The bankruptcy label rests on the Chapter 7 filing of 2008-07-31, after the suspension, so it is a judgment call (574 versus a price or guidelines drop).

Net: the library's published last_trade_date is blank and its date is a guess, so the report's direction is right, but 07-11 and everything built on it stay inferred.
