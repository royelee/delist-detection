A real merger into Tyco International (renamed Johnson Controls International plc): each old JCI share became $5.7293 cash plus 0.8357 new JCI (ex-Tyco) shares (the default package). The library's kind and terms are right; its last trade date (2016-09-06) is wrong: the old stock last traded 2016-09-02, and MIDAS most likely saw the new JCI shares that took over the ticker.

## 5. What happened

- Merger effective 11:59 pm NYC time 2016-09-02; old JCI became a subsidiary of Tyco ([SEC 8-K 2016-09-06](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/53669/000110465916143072/a16-17944_18k.htm)).
- Non-electors and share electors: $5.7293 cash + 0.8357 JCI ordinary shares; cash electors $34.88; aggregate cash about $3.864 billion (same 8-K).
- NYSE suspended old JCI before the open 2016-09-06; the new JCI shares began trading that open under JCI (same 8-K).
- Form 25-NSE filed and effective 2016-09-06 ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/53669/000087666116001250/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: the old shares were exchanged for cash plus another company's shares. A merger, not a continuation; the new JCI line only took over the ticker.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Only the date and currency. `last_trade_date_source=midas` returned 2016-09-06 and the flag `last_trade_date_conflict` fired against the 8-K text: MIDAS is keyed by ticker, and the new JCI traded on 2016-09-06 under the same symbol. The library did not use the filing's suspension date. Cause tag: `other:midas_ticker_reuse_last_trade`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- When a ticker takeover (`ticker_successor_sec_id`) starts within days, do not accept a MIDAS day on or after the successor's first sighting; prefer the 8-K/Form 25 suspension date (2016-09-02). Clip ticker_history at 2016-09-02.
- Publish `cash_currency=USD`; price_date 2016-09-06 (first new-JCI session), not 2016-09-07.
- The failed payout gate (34.88) is the cash-election amount compared with the close, not a misread; assumed par then disappears once priced.
- Golden case: yes. A merger whose acquirer takes over the target's ticker, with an election deal.

## 9. Verification

Upheld by an independent skeptic agent (its section was written back from the workflow's record).
- Upheld: last_trade_date
- Upheld: ticker_history

8-K 2016-09-06 (0001104659-16-143072) says the merger took effect at 11:59pm on 2016-09-02. It also says NYSE suspended old JCI before the open on 2016-09-06, when the new JCI shares began trading under the same symbol. 2016-09-05 was Labor Day, so the last old-stock trade was 2016-09-02. The library's MIDAS date of 2016-09-06 is the successor's session under the reused ticker. ticker_history should end 2016-09-02 for the same reason. The report's terms and currency were not re-checked. Section 9 appended to the report and a verification object added to the JSON record.
