## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the two fields the report calls wrong. Both upheld.

- **exit_kind (blank -> dropped / bankruptcy): upheld.** The Form 25-NSE (0000876661-18-000780) states "Seadrill Limited emerged from Bankruptcy on July 2, 2018", cites 12d2-2(a)(3), an effective ratio of about 0.0037345 New Common Stock per Existing Share, and that the old stock was suspended on July 3, 2018. The 6-K (0001193125-18-211550) states the economic interests in the old shares were extinguished and New SDRL, a different Bermuda company, issued its shares to old holders (Rule 12g-3 successor). A new issuer, a non-1:1 ratio and a bankruptcy make this post-bankruptcy equity, a new security, so not a continuation (reference.md, decision 9). A blank kind (unknown bucket) is wrong. Not a merger: no acquirer consideration.
- **value_rule (unknown -> stock): upheld, with a caveat.** The ratio 0.0037345 is stated in the Form 25 notice, so the library's "unknown / assumed par" is wrong whichever rule applies. Stock (ratio x new-share price) fits the filing. Caveat, already in the report: reference.md's bankruptcy row says value = first OTC print (decision 11), and the fails data shows a single SDRLXXXX row on 2018-07-05, so `otc_print` is not ruled out. The choice is the spec's, not a filing's; confidence stays inferred.
- Not contested: last trade date 2018-07-02 (notice says suspended July 3; fails rows show SDRL through 2018-07-03 settlement). Terms: ratio is from the Form 25; price_ticker and price_date are worked out.
