## 1. Verdict

A real terminal event: Ardagh Group's own exchange offer swapped 84.38% of its Class A shares for Ardagh Metal Packaging (AMBP) shares at 2.5 AMBP per share, then AGSA voluntarily delisted from the NYSE (last trade 2021-10-05). ## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (425/SC TO-I/A press release 2021-10-12: exchange offer completed, voluntary delisting; Form 25 under 12d2-2(c)).
- Successor: filing (same: holders receive AMPSA shares, no continuing AGSA line).
- Last trade date: filing (press release: "the last day of trading of the AGSA Shares on the NYSE was October 5, 2021").
- Payout rule: filing (stock only, in the 2021-09-03 425).
- Terms: filing (425 of 2021-09-03: each tendered share exchanged for 2.5 AMPSA Shares; the 2021-10-12 final results agree: 39,388,152 AMPSA for 15,755,304.7 AGSA).

## 5. What happened

- 2021-09-03: AGSA announced an exchange offer, each validly tendered AGSA share for 2.5 AMPSA shares, to be followed by delisting from the NYSE and deregistration ([SEC 425](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1689662/000110465921112602/a21-25235_8425.htm)).
- 2021-09-22: AGSA declared a $1.25 per share special cash dividend payable 2021-10-12 to holders of record 2021-10-04, a distribution on the old share, not exchange consideration ([SEC 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1689662/000110465921118412/tm2125235d20_6k.htm)).
- Offer expired 2021-10-05 11:59 pm; 84.38% of shares tendered; last NYSE trading day 2021-10-05 ([SEC 425 2021-10-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1689662/000110465921125077/tm2124506d17_425.htm)).
- Form 25 filed 2021-10-06 by the issuer under Rule 12d2-2(c) ([SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1689662/000110465921123497/tm2124506d11_25.htm)); Form 15-12B filed 2021-10-18 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1689662/000110465921127219/tm2125235d26_1512b.htm)).
- Fails rows: ARD (CUSIP L0223L101) 35 rows to 2021-11-08 (settlement fails after delisting); AMBP trades from 2021-09 (fails rows).

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: holders gave up AGSA shares for another company's shares (2.5 AMBP each), so a merger-type terminal event, not a continuation. About 15.6% of shares were not tendered and stayed as unlisted, deregistered AGSA shares; the rule values the tendered package.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

The library's reason is "Merger filing 425 2021-10-12 before the delisting" (CRSP 231). It found the merger from a 425 but the offer is an issuer exchange offer, so no cash or last-trade extraction fired: `last_trade_date_source` blank, flags `no_last_trade_date;no_last_close`, `dlret_method=abstain_no_consideration`, `value_rule=unknown`. The Form 25 is a voluntary 12d2-2(c) notice with no EX-99.25 suspension date; the date sits in the 425/SC TO-I/A press release. Cause tag: `terms_not_extracted`.
