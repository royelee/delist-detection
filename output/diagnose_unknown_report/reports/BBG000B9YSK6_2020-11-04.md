# CBL (CBL & Associates Properties, BBG000B9YSK6), 2020-11-04

## 1. Verdict

CBL filed Chapter 11 on 2020-11-01 and NYSE suspended the common stock on 2020-11-02 for an "abnormally low" price; it then traded OTC as CBLAQ. This is a bankruptcy drop (`dropped` / `bankruptcy`, valued at the first OTC print), not a continued-filings exchange transfer. The library's row (exchange, transfer, 0.0 fill) is wrong on exit kind and value rule.

## 4. Why this confidence

Inferred.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2020-11-02 item 1.03 reports the Chapter 11 petitions; 8-K 2020-11-04 item 3.01 reports the NYSE suspension and OTC trading).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2021-11-02 items 1.02/3.02: old common cancelled, new common is a new security; no Form 25 for the common).
- Last trade date: worked out from the 8-K 2020-11-04 item 3.01 suspension notice by the suspension rule (trading day before 2020-11-02 gives 2020-10-30); no filing states the last day and no exchange print was checked.
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2020-11-04 item 3.01: OTC trading expected from 2020-11-03 under CBLAQ, so the rule is the first OTC print).
- Terms: price_ticker CBLAQ is from the same 3.01 filing; price_date is worked out as the session after the last exchange trade, which depends on the unconfirmed last trade date.

Not backed: the last trade date (2020-10-30 vs 2020-11-02) and the price_date that follows from it. A filing or exchange print stating the last NYSE trading day, or the first CBLAQ OTC session, would make this verified.

## 5. What happened

- 2020-11-01: the REIT, its operating partnership and subsidiaries filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in the S.D. Texas ([SEC 8-K 2020-11-02, item 1.03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910612/000156459020049434/cbl-8k_20201029.htm)).
- 2020-11-02: NYSE notified CBL that the common stock (CBL) was suspended for "abnormally low" price levels (Section 802.01D); OTC trading expected from 2020-11-03 under CBLAQ (common), CBLDQ and CBLEQ (preferred depositary shares) ([SEC 8-K 2020-11-04, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910612/000156459020050276/cbl-8k_20201102.htm)).
- 2021-11-01: plan effective date; old common, old preferred and old LP interests cancelled; 1,089,717 shares of New Common Stock issued to old common holders and certain LP holders ([SEC 8-K 2021-11-02, items 1.02/3.02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910612/000156459021053379/cbl-8k_20211101.htm)).
- 2021-11-02: NYSE filed two 25-NSE forms, for the Series D and Series E depositary shares only (removal from 2021-11-15) ([SEC 25-NSE 0000876661-21-001566](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910612/000087666121001566/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml), [0000876661-21-001567](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910612/000087666121001567/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)). No Form 25 for the common appears in the 2020-09 to 2021-12 filing list, so the library's `no_form25` is real.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC. The NYSE-listed security ended with the suspension; post-bankruptcy equity is a new security (decision 9). Value is the first OTC print (decision 11).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

No Form 25 for the common, so the finder fell back to the continued-filings rule (CRSP 304, transfer, 0.0 fill) because CBL kept filing; the 8-K 1.03 and 3.01 evidence was not used. Cause: `resolver_no_bankruptcy_branch`. Flags: `no_form25`, `delist_date_approx`, `successor_unknown`; uncertain reasons `continued_filings_rule`, `issuer_from_todays_ticker_map`.

- Issuer CIK 910612 is right; only its weak ticker-map source is flagged.

## 9. Verification

Skeptic re-check of the three fields the report calls wrong. Filings re-read through sec.py.

- **exit_kind: upheld.** [SEC 8-K 2020-11-02, items 1.03/2.04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910612/000156459020049434/cbl-8k_20201029.htm) confirms voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in S.D. Texas. [SEC 8-K 2020-11-04, item 3.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910612/000156459020050276/cbl-8k_20201102.htm) confirms NYSE suspended the common (CBL) on 2020-11-02 for "abnormally low" price (Section 802.01D), with OTC trading from 2020-11-03 as CBLAQ. The shares did not stay on an exchange, so an `exchange` transfer is wrong; reference.md's "Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC" row gives `dropped`/`bankruptcy`. Nuance: NYSE's stated reason was price level, so `drop_reason` could arguably be `price`; the Chapter 11 filing supports `bankruptcy`.
- **value_rule: upheld.** Decision 11: a move to OTC is valued at the first off-exchange print, so `otc_print`, not `transfer`. The filing list 2020-10-25 to 2020-11-20 shows no Form 25 for the common, so the library's 304 rests only on the continued-filings fallback.
- **terms: upheld, with a caveat.** `price_ticker` CBLAQ is stated in the 8-K 3.01; the library publishes none, so "missing" fits as well as "wrong". `price_date` stays unconfirmed because it depends on the last exchange trade (2020-10-30 vs 2020-11-02, still open; the press release is dated November 3, the 8-K says NYSE notified on November 2). This does not change the three upheld fields.
- Not checked: no market price or OTC print was looked up, by design.
