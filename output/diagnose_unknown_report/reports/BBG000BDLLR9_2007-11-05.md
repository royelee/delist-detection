## 4. Why this confidence

Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (8-K 2007-11-01 items 2.01-type narrative under 3.01/5.01: merger completed 2007-10-26; Form 25-NSE).
- Successor: filing (8-K 2007-11-01: Merger Sub merged into Bausch & Lomb; all shares converted into cash, no continuing public line).
- Last trade date: filing (Form 25-NSE notice: suspended at the close of trading on 2007-10-26).
- Payout rule: filing (8-K 2007-11-01 and the Form 25 notice: converted into $65.00 in cash).
- Terms: filing (same; $65.00 per share, no stock leg).

## 5. What happened

- Warburg Pincus affiliates completed the acquisition on 2007-10-26 through a merger of Merger Sub into Bausch & Lomb ([SEC 8-K 2007-11-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/10427/000110465907078929/a07-27689_18k.htm)).
- Each common share was canceled and converted into the right to receive $65.00 without interest; NYSE was notified on 2007-10-26 (same 8-K).
- The NYSE filed Form 25-NSE on 2007-10-26: the conversion became effective at the close of business on 2007-10-26 and the security was suspended at the close of trading that day ([SEC 25-NSE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/10427/000087666107000858/xslF25X02/primary_doc.xml)).
- Fails-to-deliver rows of its CUSIP end with the same date as the library's ticker_history (last_end 2007-10-26).
- The caller's observation_map has BOL seeds dated 2008-01-16 through 2009-06-08, all status `after_delisting`; these are stale snapshot rows, not evidence of trading after the merger.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket

Replaced: shares were exchanged for cash of an acquirer-owned vehicle. A merger, not a continuation.
