Cause: the placeholder was never merged into the real FIGI line that shares its CIK and CUSIP.

## 9. Verification
Checked by a skeptic against SEC filings and the SEC fails data (sec.py).
- exit_kind: upheld. The 8-K of 2015-09-28 (0001104659-15-067759), Exhibit 99.1, says SPX Corporation "completed the spin-off of SPX FLOW, Inc. and will begin trading today on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol SPXC". SPX Corp stays the listed registrant, so this is a ticker change and not an ending. The fails rows for CUSIP 784635104 run SPW 20150901..20150928 ($49.44 last) and then SPXC 20150929..20151026 ($12.19, after the spin-off) on the same CUSIP. The library's own cusip_history also keeps 784635104 on BBG000BTGCV5 to 2022.
- successor: upheld. BBG000BTGCV5 (CIK 88205, CUSIP 784635104) is the same security. The placeholder CIK88205-COMMON carries the same CUSIP and issuer, so it duplicates it (ticker_history, cusip_history, securities).
- last_trade_date: upheld, with a correction to the wording. Fails rows are dated D and priced at D-1's close, so SPW's last print was 2015-09-25 and SPXC's first was 2015-09-28 (the press release says trading as SPXC began 2015-09-28). The report's "SPW traded through 2015-09-28" is imprecise. The conclusion stands, because the date is not a last trade of any ending and should not be published.
- ticker_history: upheld. The placeholder's SPW range ends 2015-09-25 and has no SPXC row. The real line's SPW..2015-09-28 then SPXC from 2015-09-29 is the fails-based spelling and is off by about a day from the filing, but the placeholder is the wrong one.
- Open check closed: the SPW to SPXC date is now backed by Exhibit 99.1 (2015-09-28), so confidence can rise from inferred to verified.
