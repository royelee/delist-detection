- Nasdaq filed Form 25-NSE, effective 2025-08-07 (accession 0001354457-25-000781).

## 7. Why the library got it wrong

Its reason reads "each share became one of Class B common stock ... no cash" (`r1_continuation`). R1's own-exchange reading took the stock-consideration clause and missed the Class B Cash Consideration in the same 8-K, so the election's cash was not counted as cash in the exchange. Cause tag: `other:r1_ignores_cash_election`.

## 8. Fix and open checks

- R1's no-cash test should see an election's cash alternative ("right to receive $15.00 in cash") as cash in the exchange; the merger path then gives stock, ratio 1.0, PSKY.
- Open: PSKY's first trading day. Golden case: yes, an R1 case with a cash election.

## 9. Verification

Re-opened 8-K 0001193125-25-175027 (Item 2.01) through sec.py. All six truth verdicts survive; no missed filing is claimed.

- Structure refinement (section 7 is imprecise): the 8-K describes two steps under one Transaction Agreement. The Pre-Closing Paramount Merger converted each Class B share one for one into a New Paramount Class B share with no cash, which is what R1 read. The New Paramount Merger then paid the $15.00 Class B Cash Consideration (prorated; 285,889,212 shares) on New Paramount shares; the rest stayed as one New Paramount Class B share. So R1 did not miss the cash; it read the first step alone.
- Why the truth still stands: both steps are one integrated acquisition by Skydance (change of control, Form 25-NSE effective 2025-08-07), the cash election is fixed by the same agreement and made by holders before closing, and the 8-K itself calls the cash "New Paramount Class B Merger Consideration". reference.md's exception (payment part of the consideration for the old shares, whatever it is called) applies. A post-exchange dividend (BHGE) does not fit, since the cash is a conversion of the share, not a distribution on it.
- exit_kind merger, continuation false, successor blank: upheld. value_rule stock and stock_ratio 1.0 (default package, Class B Stock Consideration = one share): upheld as the election-deal convention. price_date 2025-08-07 (session after the 2025-08-06 last trade): upheld.
- Caveat for the operator: this depends on treating the two steps as one. Treating the Pre-Closing Merger alone as the exchange would give a continuation with a later cash event on the successor.
