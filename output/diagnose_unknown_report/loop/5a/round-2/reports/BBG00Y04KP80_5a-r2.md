## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Kept the same security (reverse split, same company and listing). The 2020 post-bankruptcy CRC equity is a new security.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
Two errors. (1) The 2014-2016 observations resolved by ticker to today's CRC line BBG00Y04KP80, while the CUSIP lookup of 13057Q206 gave BBG0060B3M63: one security got two sec_ids. (2) Because the CUSIP changed at the split, the stage-5 fallback read "continued 10-K/Q filings" as an exchange transfer with a line-follow successor (flags no_form25, last_trade_date_unconfirmed, delist_date_approx), creating a false continuation row and clipping the range. Cause tag: identity_wrong.
