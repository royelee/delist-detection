## 9. Verification

Upheld. I re-fetched the EX-99.25 of 0000876661-07-000873 (ruleprovisionnotice.htm). It says the merger became effective on November 7, 2007 and "this security was suspended from trading on November 8, 2007". Under the reference rule, suspended on D means the last trade was the trading day before D. 2007-11-08 was a Thursday, so the last session is Wednesday 2007-11-07. That is not after the Form 25 effective date, and the notice is an allowed exchange-print source. The earlier truth row said no filing states the last session, which this notice shows to be wrong, so the named missed_filing does show it wrong. The security class (Common Stock, NYSE) matches. No field verdict is refuted.
