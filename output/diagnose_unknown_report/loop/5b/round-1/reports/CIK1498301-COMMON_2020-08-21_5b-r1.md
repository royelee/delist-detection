## 4. Why this confidence
Verified.
- Exit kind: filing (10-K 0001498301-20-000105: Chapter 11, Nasdaq suspension).
- Successor: filing (none; equity not continued).
- Last trade date: filing (suspended at the opening of business on 2020-08-04, so 2020-08-03).
- Payout rule: filing (same 10-K: began trading on OTC Pink); decision 11 gives otc_print.
- Terms: filing (10-K names the symbol "ASNAQ"); fails rows agree: CUSIP 04351G200 is ASNA through 2020-08-04, then ASNAQ from 2020-08-05 (76 rows).

## 5. What happened
- Nasdaq suspended trading at the opening of business on 2020-08-04 and the common stock began trading on OTC Pink "under the symbol ASNAQ" ([SEC 10-K 2020-12-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1498301/000149830120000105/asna-20200801.htm)).
- Form 25-NSE 0001354457-20-000408 followed on 2020-08-11.
- Fails rows (sec.py fails): same CUSIP 04351G200, ASNA rows 2020-07-01..2020-08-04, ASNAQ rows 2020-08-05..2020-12-31.

## 6. Decision-tree bucket
Lost: bankruptcy, then OTC.

## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The library's otc_print price_ticker is the security's listed ticker, not its post-delisting OTC symbol. Cause tag: other:otc_symbol_not_used (price_ticker should be the OTC symbol, found from fails rows under the same CUSIP after the last trade).

## 8. Fix and open checks
- Take price_ticker from the symbol the CUSIP's fails rows carry after the last trade (ASNAQ), not the listed ticker.
- Golden case: no (the truth case already covers it).

## 9. Verification
Upheld. Re-opened 10-K 0001498301-20-000105 through sec.py grep: "at the opening of business on August 4, 2020, trading of the Company's common stock was suspended from Nasdaq and the Company's common stock began trading on the OTC Pink Marketplace under the symbol 'ASNAQ.'" This is the common stock (the class of this sec_id), the date matches price_date 2020-08-04, and decision 11 / reference.md says otc_print `price_ticker` is the security's own OTC symbol if known. The truth value ASNAQ is therefore right and the library's ASNA (the Nasdaq symbol, no prints after the suspension) is wrong. Not verified: the report's fails-row claim (sec.py fails by CUSIP 04351G200 and by symbols ASNA/ASNAQ returned no rows in the cache), but the filing alone supports the verdict. No missed_filing is claimed (truth-side verdict), so none needs checking.
