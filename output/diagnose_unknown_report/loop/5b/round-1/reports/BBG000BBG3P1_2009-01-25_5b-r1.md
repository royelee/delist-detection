## 7. Why the library got it wrong
The library's reason is the "Listing deficiency cited in the 3.01 notice + NT 10-K/Q in the prior year (delinquent filer 580)" rule. The 3.01 text does cite a deficiency, but it is the price standard. The rule appears to read any 3.01 deficiency plus a prior-year NT filing as 580 without reading which standard was breached (the one 580 rule is for filing delinquency). The NT 10-Q was a one-day late 10-Q and is not the stated ground. Cause tag: `terms_misread` does not fit; use `wrong_exit_kind` (drop reason wrong). Missed filing: none, the earlier report was right.
