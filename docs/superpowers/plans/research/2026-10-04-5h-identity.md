**A. A one-letter class ticker's base spelling** (`ftd.FtdIndex._learn`/`_relabel_base`). `X-L` also loads the base
`X`; a row under `X` is keyed by `X-L` only when the ticker's observed names are known, its description agrees with
one of them and names class `L` ("CL C"). `by_symbol(X)` keeps only the rows left under `X`. Guards: HEI-A (HEI
"HEICO CORP", no letter), LEN-B (LEN "CL A"), VIA-B (VIA "CL A"), NWS-A (NWS "CL-B"), BF-A/BF-B (BF is BASF's).

**B. A cross-class CUSIP switch** (`security_master._handoff_joins`, `line_class_letter`, `one_class_issuers`). A
`cusip_handoff` link joins a lettered era to a plain-named era of its issuer when OpenFIGI names the plain era's
confirmed line with the same letter ("MSG NETWORKS INC- A", "STARZ - A") and the issuer's eras name no other letter.
Plus `pipeline._era_renames`: a placeholder whose eras now hold one FIGI (and that no era still holds) is an
`id_changes` rename, across a class label. Guards: HUB-B (its class B was reclassified into Hubbell's plain common,
R1: OpenFIGI's "HUBBELL INC" names no letter), Liberty Interactive's series A tracking stocks (a shared CUSIP never
joins across classes), GOOG/GOOGL, DISCA/DISCK (two letters), the new MSG and new Liberty (another CIK).

**C. A ticker another security's rows fill** (`security_master.foreign_ticker_eras`, `FigiResolver(cusip_span=,
foreign=)`). An era of a known issuer whose rows under its ticker all fail to name the issuer is treated as
unconfirmed (its ticker and name tiers are not asked when a backfill line exists), and for such an era alone the
backfill also takes the one composite of its issuer and class whose confirming CUSIP's trading rows cover its dates.
This is 5a's U4, narrowed: 5a measured 13 other eras whose guard flips, but a flip moves an era only when a backfill
line exists. Measured: UAG alone moves. Guards: IAC 2012 and CBRE 2012 (stale tickers with no rows: plain guarded
eras, no span backfill).

**D. A name-search answer checked against the era** (`TickerResolver.name_period_checks`, stage 2b, never saved).
Among the other CIKs SEC's name index lists under exactly the observed name: `name_in_force` when the answer's CIK
carried no agreeing name from the era's first sighting to its last and exactly one other did (filing by the first
sighting); else `ticker_rows` when the era's own fails rows (at least 3) are not the answer's (guard G's
`_fits_rows`) and exactly one other's they are. A `ticker_rows` era keeps its CIK in force (`_other_issuers`,
`_issuers_in_force`: its observed name is the one its rows refuted). Reported as `issuer_inferred`. Measured over the
697 name-search eras of the run: ABBI and ERA alone. Guards: TCF 2012 (Chemical Financial
took the name in 2019), TGNA 2012 (the 2015 spin-offs), NWS-A 2008 (new News Corp, 2013); the issuer-in-force rule
(5b R5: MRK 2008, SPB, MTCH) is untouched for every other era.

**E. A holding company's CUSIP past the fails data** (`pipeline._own_registration_link`). A CUSIP the texts name,
with one composite of its own, and no row because the fails data ends before the day: the successor is added from
the day after the last trade under the security's ticker. Guard: when the data reaches the day, rows are required.

**F. The ticker tier's later holder** (`pipeline._today_holder_fold`, in the line follow). A SUCCESSOR decision
becomes a FOLD when the line's FIGI came from the ticker tier alone, that candidate holds one of the line's tickers
today, and the new composite's ticker today is another (it left them): CRC (CRC / CRCQQ) and BTU (BTU / BTUUQ), the
two ticker-sourced line successors of the run. Guards: a new composite that holds the ticker (a holdco's new line),
a ticker candidate under another ticker, a CUSIP-confirmed line.

**G. No continued-filings guess for a security with no CUSIP** (`delistings._fallback`,
`SecurityContext.has_cusips`). The continued-filings transfer dated by the last sighting alone (`delist_date_approx`)
is not published for a security that holds no CUSIP; it gets `ended_without_delisting`. Guard: a security with a
CUSIP keeps it (the genuine no-Form-25 endings).

## 4. Decisions

Each: the choice, the alternative, and the cost if wrong.

1. **No resolver cache version bump.** The spec asks for one. A bump re-resolves all 5,060 memo answers, and the
   replay showed that re-resolving needs Form-25 full-text searches the memo never asked (ABBI's, ERA's: 6 and 114
   refused requests), so its blast cannot be measured offline. Stage 2b checks the name-search answers instead, on
   every run, never saved, whatever the memo holds. Alternative: version 5, dropping version-4 name-search answers on
   load. Cost if wrong: a stale name-search memo answer that 2b's two tests do not catch survives (the memo audit
   found 149 of 1,678 whose CIK carried no agreeing name within 30 days of the date; most are abbreviations of the right
   issuer or memo keys this run no longer asks).
2. **ERA keeps its CIK in force.** The issuer-in-force rule (5b R5) reads the observed name; for an era whose CIK its
   fails rows decided, that name is refuted, so its sightings keep the era's CIK (stage 4c and the contract's
   security_history). Alternative: leave R5 alone. Cost: without it, Era Group's security reads old Bristow's 2019
   Form 25 and gets a false 2019 liquidation (measured).
3. **Rule B needs OpenFIGI's own letter.** The first version joined across classes whenever the issuer's eras named
   one letter; the replay showed it folding Hubbell's HUB-B (a 5c `pass` R1 reclassification) and Liberty
   Interactive's series A tracking stocks. Now a CUSIP switch only, and only onto a line OpenFIGI names with the
   era's letter. Alternative: 5a's U3b as written. Cost if wrong: renames whose new line OpenFIGI names without the
   letter stay placeholders (CB Richard Ellis 2011, Washington Post 2013, Warner Chilcott 2009: today's rows).
4. **Rule C's span backfill only for an era whose ticker rows are another security's.** Applied to every guarded
   era it also placed IAC 2012-14 on BBG000BKQG80, which left the IACI 2008 placeholder with a false reverse-split
   continuation (IAC 6 -> 7 mismatches). Cost if wrong: other stale-ticker eras with no rows keep today's pick.
5. **Rule F folds a ticker-tier line into its next composite** (CRC, BTU), R2's placeholder reading for a line
   whose only composite is today's holder's. **Truth conflict:** BTU's truth row (5g's, BBG00GBV88T6_2016-05-14)
   names BBG00GBV88T6 as the 2008-2016 security and its price_sec_id; R2 makes it BBG000FW00S1 (the 2015 split's
   line, BTUUQ). Not edited here (5g owns it): it counts one `sec_id` mismatch (was one price_ticker mismatch) until
   the controller renames sec_id and price_sec_id to BBG000FW00S1. Alternative: no rule F (CRC stays known_wrong).
6. **Truth rulings** (`scripts/apply_5h_truth_rulings.py`, through `diagnosis_loop.write_together`).
   - Applied now: OKE's fixed_by -> 5d (only its worked-out last trade date is left).
   - For the controller after the full run (`--after-run`), because each names a sec_id that only a run of this
     code holds or drops; applied to the committed tables they fail the `D.mismatches.sec_id` floor:
     - ABBI's and ERA's sec_id -> the reports' own issuers' securities. The script reads each era's sec_id from
       output/observation_map.csv. A placeholder-to-placeholder change has no id_changes row.
     - CRC's sec_id -> BBG0060B3M63, shape ending_moved. This is the controller's 5a ruling: the genuine 2014-2020
       line, on which the 2016 event is no ending and the 2020 bankruptcy ends it. CRC's row then scores nothing,
       so it can no longer show the fix (the unit test and the replay's identity diff carry it). It is a strict
       xfail that the loop flips to pass.
   - MSG, LMCA, UAC-C and UAG are renamed by the loop from `contract/id_changes.csv` (not edited).
   - Alternative: rule nothing and let the loop's diagnose agents settle the sec_id mismatches. Cost: four rows
     re-diagnosed for what the reports already say.
7. **Rule G publishes no ending** for WW 2013 rather than the 2013 guess: `ended_without_delisting` (fix) instead
   of a 304 transfer. Alternative: keep the guess. Cost: 1 more fix row each for WW and NCRA (whose truth,
   no_ending, then passes).
8. **ROVI is left for the network run.** The 8-K12B search for "Rovi Corp" is cached and empty (TiVo's 8-K12B says
   "Rovi Corporation"); a search by the R1 target's name ("Titan Technologies Corporation", TiVo's former name), its
   submissions and TIVO's OpenFIGI answer are all uncached, and TIVO's line is gone today, so its FIGI may not be
   found at all. No code: nothing offline could test it.
