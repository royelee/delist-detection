## 2. Rules (built)

- **Stage 9e, `pipeline._distress`** (new; `distress.py` holds the pure readers), for each delisting in the
  liquidation, compliance-failure or unknown bucket with no successor, anchored on its last trade day (else its
  delisting date):
  1. **R6 plan exchange**: the matched Form 25's notice says the class came to evidence new shares
     (`substitutes_new_shares`: the 12d2-2(a)(3) wording and "New Common Stock" / "New" / "(New)"), the ending is
     a bankruptcy (code 470, or the notice says so, which turns an `unknown` row into 470) and the old line never
     traded off the exchange (`otc_symbol_from_fails` reads nothing). The ratio is the notice's stated one (SDRL
     0.0037345), else the one old-share count and the one new-share count to old holders the plan 8-Ks (1.03 or 3.03,
     −5/+10 days of the Form 25) state outside a condition (WOLF 1,306,896 / 156,479,390). The contract publishes
     `stock` on the new line under the same ticker, the ratio as read (a string: the filing's digits), no
     `price_sec_id` (the new line is not in the run), and a `received_close` request; no OTC print request.
  2. **Price drop reason**: a compliance failure (570, 580) whose exchange-filed Form 25 notice, or with no Form 25
     its 3.01 items in [last trade − 30, + 30], cites a price deficiency and no other standard (`price_only`) is
     CRSP 552 (`price`). An issuer's own Form 25 (a voluntary removal) changes nothing.
  3. **OTC symbol** of a liquidation or compliance failure: from the security's own CUSIPs' fails rows after the
     last trade, the exchange symbol when its rows before any other symbol run past 10 days at two or more prices,
     else the first other trading symbol within 30 days; else the first 3.01 8-K in [last trade − 30, + 60] naming
     one (`otc_symbol_from_text`: an OTC venue and "symbol X" in one sentence, not about warrants or preferred only);
     else blank. The `otc_print` price request asks under it (the exchange ticker when blank).
- **Item numbers spaced out** (`evidence.item_sections`, `_ITEM_HEAD`, `classifier._ITEM_HEADING`): "ITEM 1 .0 3"
  is item 1.03; no further digit may follow ("Item 1.031" is not 1.03). Measured: 4 of the 3,794 cached texts carry
  a spaced 1.03 or 3.01 (CBL's two among them).
- **End-of-era branch 5b** (`end_of_era.resolve`, `classifier._liquidation_notice`): read only when branch 6 would
  decide: a 3.01 8-K in the item window whose text announces a liquidating distribution, a liquidating trust or a
  plan of liquidation or dissolution is a liquidation, CRSP 400.

Guards, by real ticker (tests/test_distress_cases.py over tests/fixtures/distress/, plus unit tests): LKSD, MDRX,
LTRPA keep their exchange symbol; CNB, SIVB, PDLI (a month of fails at one price), TDW publish blank; RHD, FST, SPNV,
LKSD, MDRX keep `guidelines`; TSP, PDLI keep `filings_fees`; WLL (a plan that states no ratio, with warrants) keeps
`otc_print`; APA's holdco substitution is untouched; CBL's 3.01 still reads "abnormally low" only, and a mis-tagged
merger 8-K with a spaced 1.03 heading is still no bankruptcy; a deficiency notice, a successor and a change in
control still beat the liquidation branch.

## 5. Decisions

1. **The OTC symbol's last resort is blank** (spec: "else blank"), not the exchange ticker. Alternative: keep the
   exchange ticker where nothing says otherwise (no regression rows). Cost if wrong: a few rows (SIVB, WLL, PDLI,
   CNB) lose a ticker hint the caller could have tried; the price request still carries it.
2. **Fails rows before text.** The first other symbol under the security's own CUSIP is what traded; a 3.01 states
   an intention ("expected to begin trading under"). Alternative: text first. Cost if wrong: none measured (every
   case where both exist agrees).
3. **"Kept trading" is 10 days and two prices**, not `ftd.trades_after`'s 20 rows over 20 days (LTRPA has 8 rows,
   MDRX's first month 8). Fails settling at the last close repeat one price, so PDLI's month at $2.47 is not trading.
   Cost if wrong: a thinly failing OTC symbol read as unknown (blank).
4. **Price only when nothing else is cited.** FST's notice cites the sub-$1 price beside the back-door listing; the
   truth (verified, upheld) is `guidelines`. Alternative: price whenever price is cited. Cost if wrong: a mixed
   notice whose real reason is the price keeps `guidelines`.
5. **The exchange's notice decides, not the company's.** An issuer's own Form 25 (TSP, PDLI: voluntary removals)
   states no exchange reason; their 3.01 texts mention old bid-price notices. Cost if wrong: none in the run.
6. **R6 needs a stated ratio for the stock rule.** WLL's plan gave new shares and two warrant series with no stated
   ratio; it keeps `otc_print` (its symbol blank). Alternative: a ratio-less `stock` row. Cost if wrong: WLL stays
   valued as a print that never happened (as today).
7. **WOLF's ratio from two counts** (old shares outstanding, new shares to old holders, each the only one stated
   outside a condition). The 871,287-share reserve paid "if" approvals arrive is excluded, as the truth note says.
   Cost if wrong: a plan 8-K worded otherwise gives no ratio (the row stays `otc_print`).
8. **A plan ratio is published as a string** (the filing's digits, six significant figures when computed), not the
   contract's six-decimal float, which would turn SDRL's 0.0037345 into 0.003735.
9. **The liquidation branch sits after the deficiency notice** and is read only on the continued-filings fallthrough
   (no extra read elsewhere). Alternative: before branch 5. Cost if wrong: a liquidating issuer also cited for a
   deficiency stays a compliance failure.
10. **CBL's last trade is left to 5d.** With the bankruptcy confirmed, the fallback anchors on the 1.03 filing
    (2020-11-02), so the halt window [10-28, 11-01] misses the 11-02 halt and the internal last trade falls back to
    the 11-04 sighting (unconfirmed, unpublished); before, it was the halt's 11-02. The truth (R8) is 10-30, which
    5d's 3.01 reader gives from "On November 2, 2020 ... had been suspended" — text this sub-plan made readable.
    Mismatch count for CBL's date fields is unchanged (wrong before and after).

Truth rulings (`/tmp/claude/delist_detection/5g/rulings_5g.py`, through `diagnosis_loop.write_together`, two rows
5g owns):
- EPE `drop_reason` guidelines -> price: the NYSE notice and the 8-K 3.01 cite an abnormally low price only
