## 4. Decisions

Each: the choice, the alternative, and the cost if wrong.

1. **Rule A is the merger branches only.** The ruling says "the continued-filings relabel on a row that has a
   matched Form 25"; reader note A's guard keeps the deficiency relabel. I kept the liquidation and
   bankruptcy-before-sale branches too. Alternative: every relabel with a Form 25 (11 compliance failures and EQC
   would be confirmed). Cost if wrong: about 12 rows stay uncertain where the Form 25 already settles them.
2. **Rule B confirms a successor registration, unless the registrant's own filings state another ratio or cash
   (revised in section 7; the first build did not read the ratio).** The ruling names the 8-K12B as the
   confirming filing; reading R1 on it would also refuse SIRI (1-for-10 into the same line, a truth pass). Cost:
   an 8-K12B continuation at another ratio is confirmed when its issuer evidence holds. CHTR (0.9042, 5f's spec rule 6)
   is the one truth case, and it stays uncertain on today's ticker map.
3. **Rule D's stale close is `ftd_close_prior:<n>` above 3 trading days, not `ftd_close_lagged`.** The lagged flag
   marks PNRA's and MRD's right terms and MDP's and PCYC's incomplete ones alike; confirming them would hide MDP's
   and PCYC's missing legs. No row in the run has a close older than 3 days on a failed gate, so only GRUB moves.
   Alternative: count a lagged close as stale (4 more confirmed, 2 of them wrong). Cost: PNRA and MRD stay
   uncertain.
4. **Rule E needs the last ending settled on its own reasons, not a continuation, with its own Form 25, no
   last-trade conflict, no one-for-one no-cash terms, and the seed within a year of the last trade (revised in
   section 7).** An
   ex99_notice day counts as settled, as decision 12 publishes it. Alternative: a measured day only (MIDAS, a halt);
   then almost no 2007 case (the snapshots' first date is 2008-01-16) would move. Cost: a notice day that is wrong
   (FRK's halt) is confirmed with its stale seed.
5. **Rule F requires the R1 one-for-one reading of the window's 8-Ks**, not just a 3.03 item. A 3.03 describes any
   charter change, and the Liberty 2023 3.03s are baskets (5c's I5). Alternative: the item alone (the Liberty rows,
   truth mergers, would be confirmed). Cost: a 3.03 one-for-one whose wording the R1 reader misses (TEAM's scheme,
   SPWRA's uncached text) stays timing-only. A failed read gives no filing and a `resolution_degraded` review item,
   not a flag on the row (the row does not rest on it).
6. **Rule G applies to observed securities only** and uses the security's own walk, not its chain. An added
   acquirer's one range is a price pointer; a predecessor whose successor is closed keeps its own verdict (the
   successor carries the doubt). It also catches DIS and XOM, whose ranges close at the last sighting because the
   run could not confirm them listed today: a real gap in what the tables say. Cost: V.uncertain_securities rises by
   34; its floor must be lowered by hand.
7. **Theme 6 (b), (c) not built** (section 1). Cost: LVNTA stays uncertain on its overlap.
8. **The 9g stage is new (`pipeline._continuation_filings`), between 9e and the rows,** so a degraded read can still
   reach review.csv before the triage; `verdict.decide` takes its answers as `continuations`. Shared functions
   changed: `verdict._security_verdicts` (stale seeds, rule G), `verdict._own_reasons` (was `_ending_reasons`; the
   security part moved into `decide`, which computes each ending's own reasons first), `verdict.decide` (a new
   optional argument), `pipeline._run` (one stage line, one argument). `contract.py` is untouched.
9. **Three old tests now read the guards**: test_verdict.py's outside-history case (an after_delisting seed past a
   settled ending is rule E's target; the test now uses an unconfirmed ending), its two `no_acq_price` gate cases (rule
   D's targets; now a fresh-close and a no-acquirer-ticker failure), and the two-names case (its closed range with no
   ending is rule G's; now open).

## 7. Review fixes (patch 5i-4)

The review of 5i-1..3 found four Important and nine Minor items. All are fixed or answered here; each has a test.

1. **Rule E confirmed MEL and FRK, which the truth set calls wrong.** `verdict_rules.doubted_ending`: an ending whose
   row carries `last_trade_date_conflict` (FRK: the Nasdaq halt day 11-14 against the NYSE notice's 11-16) or a merger
   of one share and no cash (MEL: R1's continuation, published as a merger) no longer settles stale seeds. Measured
   over the 29 rule-E securities: only those two change, 27 stay confirmed. The time bound is `STALE_SEED_DAYS` = 365
   from the ending's last trade (the 29 gaps in the run are 26 to 201 days), so a later era on a recycled ticker
   mis-resolved onto the dead security keeps its doubt. Tests: `test_rule_e_*`, and the MEL and FRK real cases.
2. **Rule B read no ratio (CHTR 0.9042, SIRI 0.1).** Stage 9g now also reads, for each continuation whose reason
   names its successor registration, the registrant's own 8-Ks with 5c's R1 reader, and `Reading.doubt` keeps the
   doubt (`continuation_not_one_for_one:ratio:0.9042`, with the relabel and no-evidence reasons back) when the
   reading states cash or a ratio that is neither one nor a plain split. A missing reading vetoes nothing. Measured:
   over the rule-B rows only CHTR (0.9042) is contradicted; all 11 confirmed rule-B rows stay confirmed.
   **SIRI ruled from its filings:** its own 8-K12B (the cached text) reads "each share of common stock of Old Sirius
   ... was converted into 0.1 of a share of New Sirius common stock", cash only for fractions: a reverse split of the
   same issuer's class into the line the same issuer carries on under. The truth row says continuation (R2: same issuer
   and class, 1-for-10 with a CUSIP change, `pass`), so the confirmation would be right. `verdict_rules.reverse_split`
   exempts a ratio of 1/N or N for an integer N of at least 2, no cash, so the veto does not touch it. SIRI is still
   uncertain, on `issuer_from_todays_ticker_map` (it has no Form 25), as at base. Cost: a merger whose ratio is exactly
   1/N or N, by a successor registration, is not vetoed.
3. **One gate set.** `verdict.GATE_FAILED` is the only copy (verdict_rules.py would import it back from verdict.py, a
   cycle; `unpriced_gate(row, gates)` takes it as an argument instead). 5f adds `terms_gate_skipped` there and the
   integrated code reads it in both places: `unpriced_gate` is False whenever any gate token is not price-side, a skipped
   gate included (`SKIPPED_GATE`). The three probe rows of the review (a skipped gate beside `no_acq_price`, a stale close
   and a payout failure) stay uncertain; the test passes 5f's token in.
4. **GRUB's "NULL" acquirer.** The row's `acquirer_ticker` is blank (the "NULL" is only in the contract's
   `price_ticker`, which the verdict never reads), so `unpriced_gate` takes `no_acq_price` as price-side only for a
   row that names its acquirer (`named_acquirer`: not blank, not NULL, None, N/A, NA, -, any case). GRUB stays
   uncertain (`assumed_par_after_failed_gate`); rule D has no target in today's run, and its stale-close branch has none
   either. 5f blanks the tickers at the source; this is the verdict's own guard.
