## 2. Rules (as built)

1. **R5, the currency** (`currency.py`, new). A regex hit carries the letters before its "$" (`PayoutResult.currency`:
   "$" is USD, "C$" CAD). The v3 answer names `cash_currency`; the quote's own sign wins over it. `payout_rule`
   publishes the currency of the read that supplied the cash; a `--merger-terms` row and a stock-only rule publish
   none. The gate skips a non-USD leg (`terms_gate_skipped:<CUR>`, contract `terms_gate` `skipped`, DLRET assumed
   par; `verdict.GATE_FAILED` counts it, the one list of gate-failed tokens).
2. **Prompt v3** (`llm_merger_extractor`). The package one share of the named target security became: R4 (the final
   prorated per-share result every holder of the class got, else what non-electors got, else the fixed terms; never
   the sum of alternatives; with no default, the cash-and-shares alternative, the all-cash one first), the latest
   figures, shares issued over "equivalent" figures, ADS counts, a special dividend out and a distributed security's
   closing cash in. The acquirer fields name the issuer of the shares received (full name; US ticker, else home
   exchange). New fields: `package_basis`, `cash_currency`, `stock_value_per_share`, `acquirer_share_class`,
   `other_stock_legs`, `election_note`, `contingent_note`. The user prompt names the target security and the filing;
   the cache key adds the ticker. A spelled-out null ticker is none.
3. **Candidates**, the latest completion documents first: the closing 8-K (2.01); an 8-K reporting the closing
   without 2.01 (3.01, 3.03 or 5.01 within 30 days: 71 answers come from it); an announcement 8-K filed after the
   last merger proxy (BOT's amendment); DEFM14A; the other announcements; PREM14A; a 6-K within [−120, +30] days.
   An unsure (`confidence` below high) one-for-one answer whose quote states no share count is passed over for the
   next candidate (`unsupported_one_for_one`: ATH, CHTR; 5 answers in the cache).
4. **R4 in the gate** (`payout_gate`). A v3 answer with a stated package (`is_package`: basis `final_prorated`,
   `default` or `fixed`) is gated as that package: cash only in pass 1 (an election: `llm_election_cash`), with
   stock in pass 2 (`llm_election_package`). A regex cash never stands beside an election package with stock, nor
   when no last close can check the package, nor when it equals the package's cash leg (SUG, FWLT, AWH, SHAW). An
   answer of basis `none` and every earlier answer keep 5e's either-or reading (CBSS: $71.82 or 2.8 ADSs).
5. **R3, baskets** (contract schema 3). Two or more securities per share: value rule `basket`; the main row keeps
   the cash; `contract/payout_legs.csv` (`sec_id, leg, ratio, price_sec_id, price_ticker, price_date`) one row per
   security (the main leg's security from stage 8a, a further leg the run's holder of its ticker, none when it shares
   the main leg's ticker, `pipeline._leg_holders`); a `received_close` request per further leg. The gate skips a
   basket (`terms_gate_skipped:basket`). One security plus cash stays `cash_plus_stock`, whoever issued the shares.
6. **A dollar-valued stock leg** (PCYC): `109.00 × price(ABBV, D) / avg_price(ABBV)` in `value_formula`, no ratio,
   stage 8a's acquirer and a received-close request; the gate skips it (`terms_gate_skipped:stock_value`).
7. **Spec 5c rule 6** (`classifier`, the successor branch only): an unambiguous own-share statement with another
   ratio, or cash, makes the 8-K12B row a merger (231, `end_of_era` `successor_merger`) unless the ratio is a split
   factor (n or 1/n). The handoff stage keeps such a merger (`handoff_conflict`).
8. **A completion reported in a 6-K or 8-K** (`classifier._completion_report`): where the no-evidence default would
   decide, a 6-K or 8-K in [F − 30, F + 10] of the Form 25 day stating a completed acquisition, merger or arrangement
   is a merger (231).
9. **Stage-wiring rules.** A one-for-one, no-cash merger payout no longer counts as reconciled against a handoff
   continuation (R1; SPB 2018, whose new v3 ticker priced the target's own close). Stage 8a tries the acquirer's name
   when its ticker names no issuer on the last trade day (FDC 2019: v3 gave today's FI for Fiserv). With
   `--sec-workers` > 1 the LLM calls are filled ahead on the worker threads (the sequential pass reads the same
   cache entries). A failed LLM call counts itself degraded (`resolution_degraded`), never cached.
10. **Small items.** An answered R6 plan value has its own method `plan_stock` (measured, not a fill).
    `update_truth.py`'s status flips and the run's own scorecard judge the payout legs.

## 5. Decisions

1. **Currency from the read that supplied the cash; the quote's sign over the LLM's code.** Alternative: the LLM's
   code alone. Cost if wrong: a Canadian filing that writes a bare "$" for C$ publishes USD (none seen).
2. **A non-USD cash leg is published and never gated; its DLRET is assumed par** (THI's 0.0935 was C$ added to US$).
   Alternative: keep gating the unconverted sum. Cost: THI's DLRET is a fill until the caller converts.
3. **A basket is two or more securities; one security plus cash is `cash_plus_stock`**, so VSTO's truth is re-ruled
   (one Revelyst share and $25.75, as CCE 2010's one New CCE share and $10.00). Alternative: the spec's case list
   (VSTO a basket). Cost: a single distributed leg is on the main row, not in payout_legs.csv.
4. **R4's "final prorated" is a per-share result every holder got.** EP's truth (the skeptic's stock electors'
   prorated 14.53 + 0.4231) now mismatches: v3 reads the non-electors' mixed 14.65 + 0.4187, and NMX/JCI-style
   stock-elector results are read when the filing gives one result. Left for the loop (EP is residual; +2
   mismatches). Cost if wrong: EP-like deals publish the default where the truth wants the prorated one.
5. **BLUE is re-ruled to $3.00** (non-electors were deemed to elect Cash and CVR; the CVR a note); **SCS publishes
   MIDAS's 2025-12-09** (an exchange print, as ruled for ROVI).
6. **VMED and MHS, pass rows the brief carries to 5f, are re-ruled** (VMED a two-leg basket, MHS USD) and set to
   known_wrong fixed_by 5f until output/ holds a 5f run. Alternative: leave them to the loop's regression path.
7. **Rule 6 only on the successor branch, with the split-factor guard** (5c measured CHTR as the only flip there).
   DVMT (branch 6) is not built: unmeasured.
8. **The completion rule only where the no-evidence default decides** (3 rows).
9. **A press-release 8-K tier (7.01/8.01 near the delisting) was built and removed**: in run 2 it spent PAS's and
   WSC's three tries on press releases (WSC read the class A ratio 0.0678) and TODCO's release was not in the window.
   The spec's "8-K 7.01 and Form 25 notice text" are not read; 6-K exhibits are not read either (TAHO, ARD).
10. **Not built:** MLNM's day in its EX-99.1 ("after the close of market today", the 5d reader's), EQC's stated $1.60
    (no truth field scores it; a liquidation-distribution rule needs its own ruling), R1 at the handoff stage
    (LMCA, LMCK, IAC), the Form 25 notice as an LLM text (TWC's 0.48908178).
11. **Calibration took two passes**, not one: the first showed the null-ticker, ADS and total-cash wordings; one
    prompt iteration (all 33 re-asked). The prompt then stayed fixed for both runs.
12. **22 of the 127 rows go residual** (`scripts/apply_5f_truth_rulings.py`, each with its reason); 105 match the
    final replay and stay known_wrong until the loop flips them.
