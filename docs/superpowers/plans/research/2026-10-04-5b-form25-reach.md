## 1. Code map

How a security's Form 25s are found and owned. Line numbers refer to 9c12f60.

- **Filing list.** `edgar.recent_filings` (edgar.py:714-750) reads `filings.recent` and every older page in
  `filings.files` (edgar.py:732-749). Older filings are seen. The finder reads `submissions(cik)` without
  `fresh_after`, so it never refreshes the list. Only 3 not-listed securities have a list older than last sighting
  + 45 d, each by 1–2 days (FRE, FRC, LNW). Staleness is not a 5b cause.
- **Only one CIK is read.** `DelistingFinder.find` reads `sec.issuer_cik` only (delistings.py:295, 309).
  - An exchange files its 25-NSE under the issuer's CIK (accession prefix 0000876661 is NYSE, 0001354457 is Nasdaq),
    so that filing is on the list.
  - A Form 25 under any other CIK is never seen: the old issuer (SPB: 1487730), a parent (RLGY: 1398987) or the old
    Match Group (MTCH: 1575189).
- **The candidate loop** (delistings.py:319-373), in order:
  1. Floor = first sighting − `FORM25_LOOKBACK_DAYS` 30 (L31, L311). An earlier filing goes to `early` (L320-322).
  2. `alive` = siblings within `_alive_at`, the window [first − 30 d, last own sighting + 400 d] (L132-139, L35-36).
     When no sibling is alive the filing is dropped silently (L327-329).
  3. A raw read failure gives `form25_unreadable`.
  4. A regional exchange is skipped (L337).
  5. `class_kind == "other"` gives `form25_unclassified` (L339-343): 148 rows in the replay, mostly notes and coupon
     receipts.
  6. `match_securities` (form25.py:284-297) matches one sibling of the class kind by elimination, or by class letter
     and name words (`_class_matches` L249-281). An ambiguous class, or a tie (`tied_securities`), gives
     `form25_unmatched` (61 rows; most are holdco or redomicile pairs where the ambiguity is the safe answer).
  7. `_class_conflict` (L150-157).
  8. **`continued`** (L362-364) = `listed_today or seen_after(eff + 5)`. `seen_after` counts *any* sighting: a stale
     observation, or a fails row under an OTC symbol.
  9. Only when `continued`, three skips apply: the own CUSIP switch (U6, L366, `OWN_SWITCH_DAYS`), the secondary
     withdrawal (`withdrawal_kind`, L368-370), and "the next 10-K cover still names the exchange" (L371-372).
- **Grouping** (L377-393).
  - `_group` chains filings within `SAME_EVENT_DAYS` 30 of the group's earliest filing.
  - After a definitive group, a group more than 30 d later is skipped unless the security is sighted after it
    (`IGNORE_AFTER_DEFINITIVE_DAYS`, L33).
  - A group that is `continued` passes `trading_after=True` to the classifier. A continued transfer takes itself as
    successor (L443-447) and never ends the security.
- **Fallback** (L400-412, `_fallback` L513-552). This runs when no group is definitive.
  - `classify_event(form25=None)` lets the classifier pick a Form 25 itself (`_pick_delist_filing`,
    classifier.py:227-253).
  - Its frozen-tail rule (L246-250) drops a Form 25 more than 45 d before the anchor when the *nearest* 8-K (±14 d)
    carries no 2.01/3.03/5.01. The backscan runs only when no 8-K is that near.
  - An early pick must match again in `_early_group` (L481-511) and must not be followed by own-ticker fails rows.
- **Classifier order** (`_classify_resolved`, classifier.py:574-821):
  1. any REVOKED filing, at any distance (L651-664);
  2. a confirmed 1.03 in [anchor − 540 d, anchor + 30 d] (L669-682);
  3. a rename or transfer (L688-696);
  4. SPAC;
  5. the **continued-filings rule**, the end-of-era resolver, when a 10-K/Q was filed more than 180 d after the anchor
     (L720-735). The resolver (end_of_era.py:92-113) appends the "; the registrant kept filing after it" suffix (L97).
  6. Only then the Form 25 branch (L790-821). A matched Form 25 therefore owns nothing whenever the issuer kept filing
     or was ever revoked.
- **`listing_status`.**
  - `cover_exchanges` (listing_status.py:26-30) falls back to `head[:4000]` when the cover has no "name of each
    exchange" caption. RHD's 2009 10-K says "12(b): None" and still yields NYSE from elsewhere on the cover.
  - `withdrawal_kind` (L51-61) works as designed (Apache/Chicago).
- **Flags.** `form25_unmatched`, `form25_unclassified` and `form25_unreadable` are check-severity
  (review_triage.py:234-245). A Form 25 skipped by `alive`, or by the continued skips, leaves no review row.

## 7. Design proposal (minimal; order inside stage 5 unless stated)

**C. `DelistingFinder._continued(ctx, eff, f25, filings) -> bool`.** Replaces the two inline computations at
delistings.py:362-364 and 383-385. True when any of these holds:
- `listed_today`; or
- R7 below; or
- the Form 25 is **not** 12d2-2(b) **and** `ctx.trades_after(eff + 5 d)`.

`trades_after` is a new `SecurityContext` field built in `pipeline._context_builder` (pipeline.py:686-700). It reads
the security's own CUSIPs through `ftd.trading_rows`, under **any** symbol, excluding deleted, unassigned and
digit-bearing symbols (DISCA's "F104PAIROFF"). It needs ≥20 rows spanning ≥20 days with ≥2 distinct prices, the
`_continues_after` idea (pipeline.py:1394) without the own-ticker filter.
- Observations stop continuing a security.
- `seen_after` stays only for the `IGNORE_AFTER_DEFINITIVE_DAYS` test (L381).
- Fixes XMSR, SOV, RHDC, IDARQ and LKSD, and exposes MNI, ASNA and LTRPA.

**R7. A transfer by the issuer's own withdrawal.**
- *In `_continued`:* an issuer-filed Form 25 (form `25` or `25/A`, not `25-NSE`; not (b)) with an issuer 8-A12B within
  10 days is continued.
- *After `_build_delisting`:* a continued group the classifier left UNKNOWN, with such an 8-A12B, becomes 304 with
  itself as successor. This fixes KHC.
